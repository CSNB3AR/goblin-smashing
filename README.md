# Goblin Smashing

A LitRPG / VRMMORPG / deck-building progression story by STR_B3AR (Bobby).

## Repo Map (NovelCrafter-ready structure)

Everything below is split into individual files/entries specifically so it can be imported into NovelCrafter (or any similar codex-based tool) as separate cards, one entry per file, one file per topic.

- **[`story/premise.md`](story/premise.md)** — genre, elevator pitch, core pillars, tone/inspirations, high-level arc structure. Start here.
- **[`style-guide/`](style-guide/)** — non-negotiable craft rules and voice/technique guidance.
  - [`craft-rules.md`](style-guide/craft-rules.md)
  - [`voice-technique.md`](style-guide/voice-technique.md)
  - [`first-person-voice.md`](style-guide/first-person-voice.md): first-person conversion rules and what never changes.
  - [`concrete-detail-and-figurative-language.md`](style-guide/concrete-detail-and-figurative-language.md): concrete detail, sparing figures, filter words, and the limits that protect the voice.
  - [`chapter-reformat-checklist.md`](style-guide/chapter-reformat-checklist.md): how to bring chapters 4 to 10 up to the new format, with `tools/chapter_check.py` (audit) and `tools/build_chapter.py` (docx and md).
  - [`race-dialect-and-voice.md`](style-guide/race-dialect-and-voice.md) — per-race NPC speech/voice rules for the 8 playable races.
- **[`codex/characters/`](codex/characters/)** — one file per character, 26 entries: Tatsuya, his family, supporting cast, antagonists, and in-game characters.
- **[`codex/locations/`](codex/locations/)**: one file per location, 18 entries: real-world Tokyo locations, the championship venue, Eryndal's world/regions, and the northern and border places [`varekhold.md`](codex/locations/varekhold.md), [`varekpass.md`](codex/locations/varekpass.md), [`skaldmere.md`](codex/locations/skaldmere.md), [`ashenveil-forest.md`](codex/locations/ashenveil-forest.md), [`barren-lands.md`](codex/locations/barren-lands.md) and [`khen-mora.md`](codex/locations/khen-mora.md).
- **[`codex/objects/`](codex/objects/)** — one file per notable item/hardware: the Haya EB, the HGO access drive, pod hardware, the goblin signature item set, and the relic [`staff-of-the-mist-god.md`](codex/objects/staff-of-the-mist-god.md) (the Amulet of Seranis was retired; [`amulet-of-seranis.md`](codex/objects/amulet-of-seranis.md) is a redirect stub).
- **[`codex/lore/`](codex/lore/)**: (patrons, the Church of Seranis and the seven world weapons live in [`patrons-and-world-weapons.md`](codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md)) general system plumbing and world mechanics that don't belong to one of the dedicated system folders below: stats, resource pools, leveling, NPC tags, the companion system, the goblin race-roll mechanism, the Karma/Alignment and per-faction Affiliation systems, and real-world safety/regulatory lore (VRSA, full-dive safety). A few entries are thin redirect stubs pointing into the folders below, kept so old cross-references still resolve. Three entries are the Hearthfire-era source of truth: [`hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`](codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md) (release sequence, Season Zero, the Crownfall Siege event and the Siege battle royale, the pro league option, Node Wars, currency, locks, travel), [`lunarest-royal-court.md`](codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md) (the king, queens, princes, Corvith, Shiv's backstory, racial standing, the Fire Festival), and [`timeline-and-beta.md`](codex/lore/timeline-and-beta.md) (the eight-to-nine-month window, ages, the two separate accidents, the beta numbers).
- **[`codex/magic/`](codex/magic/)** — the magic system (now eight books including [`book-of-tides.md`](codex/magic/book-of-tides.md), the Water school): the Wisdom-18 casting threshold and tier system (`overview.md`), a cross-school basic-spell reference (`tier-reference.md`), and one file per named school (Book of Embers, Book of Frost, Book of Tempest, Book of Stone, Book of Radiance, Book of the Veil, Book of the Arcane), including Tatsuya's own Shaman's Veil path.
- **[`codex/combat/`](codex/combat/)** — the Physical Arts system: tiers and the martial-master custom-technique rule (`physical-arts-overview.md`), and one file per weapon category, the Ways (Way of the Fist, Way of the Spear, Way of the Blade, Way of the Axe, Way of the Bow).
- **[`codex/creatures/`](codex/creatures/)** — the bestiary: the Class I-IV threat-tier system (`overview.md`) and one file per class with a representative, expandable creature roster.
- **[`codex/races/`](codex/races/)** — one file per playable race (8), stats/racial ability/resistances/magic-access split out of the shared table that used to live in `codex/lore/`.
- **[`codex/arena/`](codex/arena/)** — the standing PvP-queue hub: the level-5 gate, the teleport-with-zone-lock mechanic, and matchmaking formats.
- **[`codex/pro-scene/`](codex/pro-scene/)**: the competitive layer that develops after the opening (HGO is not an esports story; Siege is a battle royale introduced at Crownfall, with a pro league as an option and Tatsuya on the open-world path): tournament and PvP formats, the pro-guild roster, the guild roster, the ranking ladder, and the Crownfall Siege event's bracket/regional/points structure (`crownfall-siege-event.md`; `winter-bash-tournament.md` is now a redirect stub).
- **[`codex/crafting/`](codex/crafting/)** — the crafting/gathering system: Crafting Points, the six professions, tiered success rates and stations/process (`overview.md`, `professions.md`, `crafting-stations-and-process.md`), buffs and debuffs/resistances (`food-and-consumables.md`, `debuffs-and-resistances.md`), and representative recipes (`recipes-gadget-line.md`, `recipes-spirit-line.md`).
- **[`codex/materials/`](codex/materials/)**: (includes [`monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md`](codex/materials/monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md)) the raw-materials layer crafting draws from, organized per-region (`overview.md`) and per-category (`organic.md`, `inorganic-and-salvage.md`, `magical-and-spirit-tied.md`), maps forward cleanly onto the five-continent Hearthfire expansion.
- **[`codex/subplots/`](codex/subplots/)** — one file per ongoing plot thread, 12 entries, each with a status flag (active / seeded / forward-only).
- **[`outline/`](outline/)** — [`bkc-reuse-map.md`](outline/bkc-reuse-map.md) records what was reused from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles and what was skipped. Beat-by-beat chapter plan, one file per chapter, plus a restructuring-notes file explaining what changed from the old draft and why. `outline/arc-one-open-beta-to-crownfall.md` maps Arc One (Open Beta, Crownfall, Hearthfire), the Battle of Thrash Hill and the split from the Thrash Clan. The king's fall (why it is called Crownfall) and the funeral truce are in `outline/arc-one-open-beta-to-crownfall.md`.
- **[`chapters/`](chapters/)**: drafted chapter prose, Chapters 1-10, **first person, past tense, inside Tatsuya's head**. `outline/first-person-conversion-log.md` records the conversion and every story-level change, with new passages flagged for review. `outline/chapters-1-9-revision-log.md` is the older third-person term-swap log, kept as history.
- **[`chapters-third-person/`](chapters-third-person/)**: the archived third-person chapters as they stood before the first-person conversion.
