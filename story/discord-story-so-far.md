**Goblin Smashing — Story So Far (Chapters 1-9)**

The short version, no spoilers beyond what gets you oriented. Go read the actual chapters for the rest.

Tatsuya Kazehaya is barely getting by, working nights, carrying debts that were never his to carry, and watching the world's biggest game from the outside for years. When he finally scrapes together enough to get in, a stroke of bad luck locks him into the worst possible starting position on a character he can never change. Everyone would call that a dead end. He doesn't.

What follows is Tatsuya figuring out, one hard-earned step at a time, that the position nobody wanted might actually be worth something, if he's smart enough and stubborn enough to make it work. He starts building something in-game that shouldn't exist: real allies, real reputation, real leverage. Back in the real world, the stakes stop being about the game at all, they're about people he loves and a clock he can't stop.

By the end of Chapter 9, the game world and the real world have both started closing in at once, and Tatsuya's no longer just trying to survive either one. He's got a plan.

That's the setup. The rest is worth reading yourself.
