**Goblin Smashing — Story So Far (Chapters 1-10)**

Catching up? Here's everything so far, chapter by chapter.

**Chapter 1 — The Countdown Starts**
Tatsuya Kazehaya works the night shift at a Shibuya charging station, watching the HGO World Championship on the store TV while carrying his late father's debt and his comatose mother's hospital bills. His old rival Eren (now a pro team captain backed by the family Tatsuya's dad owes money to) rubs it in. That night, on impulse, Tatsuya spends money he can't spare on an HGO access drive.

**Chapter 2 — Into the Game**
Tatsuya picks up his drive and finds his way to Sakura Arcade, a rundown cafe with a first-gen VR pod run by Leon, who turns out to have known Tatsuya's father. Using his dad's old technical manual, Tatsuya hacks the outdated pod to accept his license. He only means to preview the game's weakest, joke starting race, Goblin, but a power blip locks it in. No reset. Ever.

**Chapter 3 — Into Bloodhurst I**
Now a goblin, Tatsuya discovers he rolled a rare racial ability and unknowingly triggers a hidden World Quest. Spawning into a goblin war camp, he steps in when two bullies pick on a smaller goblin kid, then takes them both out with a gas-and-flare combo. First real taste of how he actually thinks in this game.

**Chapter 4 — Into Bloodhurst II**
Tatsuya and the kid are hauled in front of the camp's chieftain, Sevish, and his sister Wyx. The clan is hunting for lost knowledge tied to Shiv, a legendary goblin sorcerer, and offers Tatsuya protection in exchange for infiltrating a restricted library to find it. He buys himself three days to think it over. The World Quest goes live for the whole server to see.

**Chapter 5 — Consequences in Silence**
Back in the real world, Tatsuya sells loot for real money and strikes a deal with Leon: legit VRSA safety clearance for the arcade in exchange for the technical help only Tatsuya can provide. A hospital visit to his mother turns into a gut-punch: she has about six months without surgery, and nobody told him. On the way out, he literally runs into a mystery girl who leaves him a note and vanishes.

**Chapter 6 — VRSA Inspection**
Tatsuya reapplies to his old school and finds out his father's own debt is bigger than he thought. Leon's sister Arya enters the picture for real. A VRSA inspector installs mandatory monitors on both pods, meaning every session now has to be actively supervised. Tatsuya tells Leon the truth: he's not doing this for fun, his mom is dying and he needs money fast. He logs in and goes live.

**Chapter 7 — The First Disciple**
Shiv himself shows up, having noticed Tatsuya. As Tatsuya's small stream unexpectedly blows up, Shiv bonds a world-tier necklace to him, evolving him into a Shiv Goblin with a serious stat and ability jump, and personally evolves the goblin kid (now named Ru) into his disciple too. Tatsuya walks out of that tent a completely different character. Then a scout comes in bleeding, reporting an ambush on the road.

**Chapter 8 — Assembling the Assault Team**
The camp mobilizes to defend against the incoming attackers. Tatsuya gets geared up and agrees to lead a strike team, while quietly buying time to log out and think. In the real world, his follower count is exploding (HGO's official channel picked up his stream), and he learns his old teammate Haruto just publicly quit Eren's team, implying Eren stole Tatsuya's own build ideas. Back at the arcade, Arya is waiting at the operator desk instead of Leon, and she knows his handle.

**Chapter 9 — Assembling the Assault Team, Part Two**
Arya's done her homework. She's found guild chat logs proving top Tokyo guilds have already tracked his stream footage back to his in-game location, dangerous leverage, and she trades it for a deal: team up with her for Winter Bash. Tatsuya agrees, on his own terms. Leon sets up to properly coach him over comms going forward. Back in-game, Tatsuya wakes to find the camp's taken heavy losses while he was gone.

**Chapter 10, Part One — Silence and Fire**
Thirty-five defenders dead in the time it took Tatsuya to be away. Ru turns up alive but hurt. Tatsuya confronts Sevish over the costly, repeated pushes that caused it, and the two end up on the same page: the enemy's likely running on fumes after absorbing that many waves, which means there's a real window to hit back. Tatsuya pitches a plan and gets Arya quietly looped into the party. It ends with Leon spotting a name that's been sitting in the viewer list since Tatsuya's very first stream: **PK_Wraith**.
