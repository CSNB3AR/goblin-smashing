# Cue Bank

The beat that brings Tatsuya back into his body after information, a thought or a jolt of feeling. This file overrides the older cue lists (`physical-expression-thesaurus.md`, the cue notes in `craft-rules.md` and `voice-technique.md`) wherever they disagree.

## His signature tell

**Scratching the thin goatee while he is working something out.** That is his thing. Use it when he is actually weighing a decision or turning a problem over, once or twice a chapter at most, never as filler. A touch of the earring or a loc tucked behind the ear is rarer still.

## Capped crutches

Each of these at most once per chapter, and not again in the next chapter: jaw set or tightened, stomach drop or turn, pulse picked up, breath out or held, shoulders up or dropped, knee bouncing, hands flat on a surface, throat tight, heat up the neck, blinking hard, forehead on something.

`tools/chapter_check.py` counts them. Run it on neighboring chapters together to catch repeats across chapters.

## Reach for these first

- **What the scene gives him.** What he sees, hears, smells or touches in the room, and how it is organized. The hum of the fridge, the dark lot past the glass, the cold of the metal, the smell of solder.
- **What he does with an object.** Turns the phone over, squares a stack of receipts, wipes the same spot, pockets the drive, checks the strap on his bag, thumbs the screen dark.
- **A change in how he talks.** Slower, flatter, shorter, a laugh that does not reach anything.
- **Small dry humor**, aimed at himself or at someone who earned it.
- **Stillness.** He stops moving and says nothing for a line.
- **Calming himself on purpose.** Counting the rows on a shelf, reading a label twice, looking at something far away.
- **The eye roll** when Eren talks.

## Facts to keep straight when writing cues

Thin goatee, locs on top with a faded undercut, a hoop earring with a hanging chain, a ring pendant on a thin chain. Tall (a little over six feet), Black, long arms. Builds were drawn on tablets, never graph paper. In the goblin body: smaller frame, clawed fingers, green skin, cold.

## Quick test

If the same cue appeared in the last chapter, or twice in this one, replace it with one from "Reach for these first".
