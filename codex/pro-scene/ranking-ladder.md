# Prestige & Ranking Ladder (resolved)

Named tiers: **Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster**, then a **Top 500** cap above that, not everyone can reach it. Top 500 earns a nameplate icon in tiers (500, 100, 50, 5) with separate role and race boards (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`). This replaces an earlier, superseded set of numeric MMR placeholders entirely.

**Ladder rank and pro/tournament status are related but distinct tracks.** A player can be Grandmaster without being Top 500, and can still carry a pro marker on their profile from actually qualifying into real tournaments, ladder rank alone doesn't confer that marker. Contenders-tier players (a development tier between the ladder and any later circuit, see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`), informally called **Open Contenders**, generally sit around Masters. Haruto Murayama (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`) is the clean illustration: an early Grandmaster, not Top 500, but carries a pro marker from real tournament qualification.

**Two real tiers in canon, in order:** the basic ladder above (Bronze through Top 500) and Open Contenders (competing in what's informally called the **Challenger's League**, the Contenders League's actual competitive circuit). A top tier such as a Siege league is parked and undecided (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`, "If Siege returns"). A player climbs through real competitive results, not ladder rank alone.

See `codex/arena/overview.md` for how ladder rank interacts with the arena's queue system.

## Tournament points, a separate track from ladder rank

Bracket events like Crownfall (see `codex/pro-scene/crownfall-siege-event.md`) run on their own **win-based points system**, no points for a loss, a small completion bonus just for finishing the qualifying stretch. Deliberately not solo-performance-weighted, no individual scoring like KDA feeds tournament standing, a guild-first design choice consistent with the siege objective (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`). This stays fully separate from the named-tier ladder above, a player's Bronze-through-Top-500 standing doesn't convert into or from tournament points.

**Seasonal points, the broader circuit track.** Generalizing what Crownfall's own points system does in isolation, **seasonal points** accumulate from placements across local tournaments, regional LAN events, bracket tournaments and, most of all, fights and wars (Node Wars), feeding the season-long regional-to-national qualifying structure behind each seasonal championship (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`). Regional standing uses the region names locked for Crownfall's qualifying (EMEA, Americas, APAC, China). **Crownfall is the first event to award seasonal points under this system**, seeding the Season One standings. These were previously called HGO Points.
