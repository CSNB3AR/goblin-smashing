# Tochi Representative Qualifier

**Status:** New, forward-plot for Act Two. Not yet on the page.

## What it is

A lighter, intra-school selection tournament, separate from the licensed Inter-High tournament (see `codex/pro-scene/inter-high-tournament.md`, don't conflate the two). Tochi Academy runs this internally to decide who actually represents the school in outside competition. Open only to currently-enrolled Tochi students, no cross-school eligibility rule here, that's Inter-High's mechanism, not this one.

## Why it matters: the real Eren-rivalry climax

This is where the Eren/Tatsuya rivalry, secondhand and Loop-post-distance for all of Act One, finally forces a real, direct confrontation. Tatsuya and Eren end up on a collision course for the same representative slot, or matched directly against each other inside the bracket. The already-locked rivalry file frames the eventual "real" Eren payoff as happening on the competitive stage, this qualifier is a legitimate candidate for that reckoning, not a placeholder skirmish, though it doesn't have to be the rivalry's final, permanent resolution, just the first real confrontation the story's been building toward since Chapter 1.

This is also where the debt pressure and the Tabuchis' "unfinished conversation with Daichi" bait (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`) either surface directly or detonate right after, Eren's own stake in this qualifier isn't just pride, it's tangled up in the family pressure he's already using as leverage.

**Outcome not yet locked**, who actually wins the slot and how Eren reacts afterward stays open until this stretch is actually being drafted.
