# Inter-High Tournament (and the Licensed Tournament-Host Ecosystem Underneath It)

**Status:** New system, first relevant once Haya Storm starts competing past Winter Bash.

## HGO doesn't run every tournament itself

HGO (the platform, owned by Kazuku's Nexus Enterprises) licenses out branded tournament rights to outside sponsors and event hosts, provided the deal cuts HGO in. This means there are multiple, parallel tournament hosts running their own regional and interest-specific scenes at any given time, not one single governing body controlling every competitive event. HGO itself only directly runs the biggest events on the calendar, Worlds (the World Championship, see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`) and other flagship international competitions. Everything below that tier, regional leagues, school circuits, sponsor-branded cups, is somebody else's license, somebody else's broadcast, still running on HGO's platform and rules but not HGO's own production.

## Inter-High, specifically

A licensed, sponsor-hosted tournament organized around Japan's high schools and middle schools. Schools across the country participate, one of many fan-followed regional tournament circuits, not a niche or minor event, just not an HGO-flagship one.

**Eligibility, the part that matters for Haya Storm's roster:** any currently-enrolled high school or middle school student can enter, regardless of which school's official team they nominally belong to, or whether their school even fields an official team at all. A homeschooled student still counts as eligible by age/enrollment status, not tied to a specific institution's roster. This is the mechanism that lets a cross-school team compete as one unit, a Tochi Academy student (Tatsuya), a local Tokyo public-school student (Arya), and a homeschooled student (Pac_Pac) can register and play together under one banner without any of them needing to share an actual school.
