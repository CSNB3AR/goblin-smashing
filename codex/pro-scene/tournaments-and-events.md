# Tournaments & Events

For the release sequence, payouts and currency, see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`. This file covers the formats.

## Competitive formats

- **Season Zero (closed beta) championship format.** Twelve guilds qualify, four per prince's army. A skirmish win is worth 40 points. The finale week cuts the top eight two per day, the final four are set by the weekend, and Sunday is the **Battle of the Snowy Plains**, 300 points. In Chapter 1's broadcast this is the final: 12 Aogiri (Japan) against 20 London Spire (UK), after attrition from 72-hour death locks. Hosted at Singapore arenas and facilities (see `codex/locations/singapore-championship-venue.md`). Prize pool 40 million yen. The broadcast is the **Season Zero Championship**, also called Championship 1.
- **Siege.** Guild arena mode, up to 20 per side, garrison troops fill empty slots (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`). Practice arena unlocks through a guild hall. HGO hosts the flagship tournaments. See `codex/pro-scene/crownfall-siege-event.md` for the Crownfall tournament.
- **Siege Pro League** (after Hearthfire). A national and global guild-versus-guild circuit that HGO picked up because the small 20v20 Siege proved so popular. Seasonal points per match buy rare items, matches pay **power cores** that power the guild hideout (placeholder name Embercores), and the top of each season earns high rewards. **Full loot, no rebound, and the only mode with no 72-hour lock**, so it can be ground. Troops are capped hard (about one or two per player), unlike Crownfall. It does not grant territory (that is Node Wars). Below it sits the **Contenders** development tier. Pro guilds run about 50 members. Full rules: `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`.
- **Node Wars** (after Hearthfire). The main war content. See below.
- **Players bring their permanently-earned items and gear** into guild fights alongside their base class, the deck-building layer (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). World-level items are nerfed for competitive balance but still matter.
- **Locks.** A flat **72-hour death lock** in raids, skirmishes, node wars and Crownfall, with the **Siege Pro League as the one exemption**. Pass-out lock default 24 hours.
- **Character lock at Hearthfire.** Closed-beta characters freeze at their current level once Hearthfire launches. Owners keep playing them as-is or retire them for Legacy Points, which convert to Legacy Silver (locked until Hearthfire) and Legacy items. Open-beta characters are permanent.
- **1v1 Open Ladder:** separate from guild formats, anyone can enter, leaderboard resets each season, the top 100 earn pro tryouts, rare gear and media coverage. See `codex/arena/overview.md`.

## Node Wars, the main war content

After the world maps release, guild fights over **nodes** (towns, resource sites, forts) across the **five continents** are the war the story is about. Territory brings taxes, rules for the zone, infrastructure and NPC ties. Contested zones grant buffs and minor perks. Owning a province or a kingdom is real income, which is why legitimate guilds and the darker real-money side of the game chase the same land (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`). Siege tools (guild halls, the 20-player queue, the troop system) are reused inside node war fights, but the Siege Pro League ladder is a separate competition. Older notes that called this "GvG Wars" describe the same system.

## Esports structure

- **Championship history.** Championship 1 was Season Zero's finale, won by London Spire (40 million yen). Sometimes called the finals of the season-long regional-to-national qualifying structure, which feeds into a single flagship event per season.
- **Crownfall:** December 25th, 10 million yen pool, open-beta guilds only (see `codex/pro-scene/crownfall-siege-event.md`). It replaces the old Winter Bash and does not recur.
- **Season Zero bracket notes.** Kansai Black Lotus (Eren's guild) missed the top eight because Arya's small guild, Crimson Static (see `codex/pro-scene/guilds.md`), took the last qualifying spot by winning the Fire Festival guild event "The Second Dawn" (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). Dosuke AI's guild fell on Saturday of the finale week. None of this needs to land on the page in Chapter 1.
- **World Boss Raids / Server Firsts**, PvE guilds race to clear new content (see `codex/creatures/class-4.md`), first clear earns a cash prize, exclusive loot and a permanent mark on server history.
- **Streamer/Influencer events**, sponsored "all-star" or meme-build exhibition weeks, PR-driven.
- **National/City showcases**, country- or city-branded guilds backed by real sponsors and governments, top players can be drafted, traded or signed to multi-year contracts.
