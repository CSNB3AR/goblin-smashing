# Guilds (background roster, for future-chapter use)

Guilds cap at **120 members**, pay out by contribution (inactive members receive a low base), and drive most of the game's actual economy and world content (Node Wars territory control, World Boss Raids). A guild leader doesn't need pro standing to matter in-world. Pro guilds run about 50. See `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md` for Node Wars and World Boss Raids, and `codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md` for the competitive guild roster.

| Guild | Identity |
|---|---|
| **Shadowspire** | Rogue/assassin, PvP-focused, controls several open-world PK hotspots. |
| **Arcane Sanctuary** | Mage/cleric, world-boss specialists, holds several server-first raid clears (see `codex/creatures/class-4.md`). |
| **Warlord's Court** | Heavy armor, castle-assault focus, the guild most other GvG factions organize against. |
| **Nova Market** | Merchant/crafter guild, controls a meaningful share of the player-driven trade economy rather than fighting for it. |
| **Wyvern Pact** | Mount specialists, controls most of the map's mount and aerial-transport trade. There is no player fast travel (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`), which is why mounts and boats are worth owning. |
| **Crimson Static** | Arya's guild, all-girls, PvP-focused (see `codex/characters/arya.md`). Small now after a roster split cost her most of the guild (Sana and Nao leave), but it won the Fire Festival guild event "The Second Dawn" and took the last Season Zero qualifying spot, the eighth seed, through that win rather than competitive qualifying. She's an established guild leader in her own right even before she has a scene on the page. Infighting and 72-hour death locks sank the run in finale week. |
| **Stillwater** | Japan. Dosuke AI's guild, fourth in Season Zero seasonal points. Name is a proposal, easy to rename. |
