## Crownfall as a 7-man raid (author-stated, latest; supersedes the Siege-heat, 20-player and 5-guild sections below until the author confirms)

**Name hidden on the page.** Crownfall is not named in the opening chapters. Kazuku announces the Hearthfire expansion (the update everyone is planning around) and an unnamed big finish on Christmas Day with ten million yen in prizes, open to every new character, built for guilds. The name Crownfall comes out later (reveal point OPEN, see Open at the bottom of this section). On the page the early chapters now say only "Christmas Day" and the ten million yen.

**Format.** A small-scale, robust **7-man raid** against a world boss, tied to the Thornwatch Forest. Three phases.

1. **Entry and survival.** Everyone enters the forest at least **30 minutes before the raid timer**. When the 30 minutes are up the forest locks. Anyone who tries to enter late gets a poison sickness and is dead in under five minutes, on a **72-hour death clock**. Everyone already inside gets a special buff that makes them immune, because they were there before the boss woke. When the world boss wakes it breathes out a poisonous magical aura that amplifies the forest's magic and makes the beasts there active and aggressive.
2. **The three mythical beasts (a 30-minute window).** Three strong, high-level mythical beasts spawn, **one of each, and only three in total**: one gives a **defensive** buff, one a **cleansing or purity** buff, one an **attack** buff. The ideal run kills all three before the boss fight. The boss shows on everyone's minimap once it wakes. Other parties are hunting the same three beasts, so every beast is its own fight. A party can survive with one buff but not with none. Guilds that skip the beasts and go straight for the boss die fast, because the buffs help against the boss's phases.
3. **The boss fight.** The boss is a **troll or frost giant**, placed at **high elevation** deep in the forest. Heavy melee class with a big mallet, multiple buffs and abilities, a few landscape skills, minions it can summon, and a **frost breath**. It has several phases (the author called it a two-phase fight and also spoke of three phases of attacks; OPEN). The deeper the party goes, the denser the map and the higher the ground.

**Politics in the raid.** Parties meet inside the forest and decide whether to fight the boss together or fight each other. The boss attacks everyone either way, so alliances form and break under pressure, and it weeds out a lot of players depending on where they pull aggro.

**Guild entry (author-stated, latest; corrects the "14 for every guild" wording).** It is a **7-man raid**: each guild enters **7 players, its 7 best**. A guild that has entered is **locked** and cannot add anyone. **Exception: the owners of Thornwatch (London Spire) are allowed 14**, two parties of seven, because they hold the fortress and start close to the forest. They can also respawn early if they die. Because everyone else is limited to seven, a small guild can field a full side, which is why Tatsuya can see himself in it, and a big guild has to pick its best seven. Guild-tagged players cannot kill each other. A big guild battle would not fit him; this does.

Once the sale is over, prices go back to normal and the influx of new players settles, and then HGO drops the rest of the details. That is the in-world reason the announcement is vague and the name is not said. Whether Kazuku says this aloud on the page is OPEN.

**Lore tie-ins (author-stated).**

## Crownfall as a raid and the Thornwatch zone (author-stated)

- Crownfall is a **guild-only raid** against an **open-world boss** that roams the Thornwatch Forest. Only guilds can enter, and only a limited number of players can be in the zone at once, so an unorganized guild gets killed and put on a death timer. The zone is PvP and **full loot**: players drop items on death, so it is high risk and high reward, and the boss can kill players too. Guilds fight each other as well as the boss.
- The raid is worth millions (the announced ten million yen pool) and arrives with an expansion of the map. The announcement in chapter 1 is the raid, not a Siege.
- The boss is a **world-level boss** like the big named world bosses in other MMOs: overpowered, a massive health pool, and it takes multiple kills or a sustained effort by many players. Guilds may get greedy or ally with each other to lock other guilds out of the rewards, so alliances, betrayals and blockades are part of the raid.
- Thornwatch Forest is a **high-level zone with high-level materials**, including the ones blacksmiths and other crafters use, and rare high-level items. The elixir is the biggest drop, but it is not the only one.

## What kind of event it is

- A **Siege battle royale**: 5 guilds per session, 20 players per guild. Each guild starts at an outskirts-like camp at spawn, gathers resources to upgrade it to level 3, and it becomes a fort. Each guild has a guild flag and 60 total lives (3 waves). A session lasts 3 hours. Guilds fight for resources and survive, building creative forts with items and magic. Winners earn shillings and Ember cores. Format details: `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`.
- **Guild-only.** Individuals can earn seasonal points in open beta, but only guilds enter Crownfall, which is why Tatsuya founds a guild. There is no random-teammate mechanic (the old Winter Bash is gone).
- Guilds fill empty slots with troops from their hall, the troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`), so a small guild can still field a full side. **Crownfall does not cap troops**, which is why Tatsuya can bring so many. The Siege Pro League caps them.
- **Open-beta characters only.** Built after the Hearthfire announcement. Closed-beta characters cannot enter, and they freeze at the full launch a week later.
- Hosted by HGO itself, not a licensed outside host.

## Points and payouts

- Win-based points only, none for a loss, a small completion bonus for finishing the qualifying stretch. Standings run on the session result, not on individual scoring (no KDA bonus), keeping the format guild-first. Individual ladder rank stays a fully separate track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`).
- Crownfall is the **Season One opener for seasonal points**, the broader standing track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`). As an elimination-stage guild Haya Storm's core lands a points total high enough to rank above London Spire and Aogiri, Season Zero's champion and runner-up (see `codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md`), a felt underdog-credibility beat even in a losing run.
- **Prize pool: 10 million yen.** The winning guild's payout comes close to the cost of Ohema's surgery (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`). The top three guilds earn seasonal points plus money. Payouts are contribution-based inside each guild.

## Zone and entry (author-stated)

The event is held in the Crownfall zone (author-stated): a neutral trade zone on a plateau off the coast of Lunarest, reached by ship. It is a massive magical forest, the last 10 to 15 acres off the back end of the kingdom, full of magical beasts, and the only high-magic ancient forest near Lunarest. It leads to the elves' domain: a domain that teleports you there, and the elves welcome you. Rumor says the court mage Corvith came from there. Normally the zone is open to everyone. During the event the forest is replicated, and the zone is locked to guilds and their participants. The rules will be set closer to the date.
