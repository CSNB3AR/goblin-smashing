# Crownfall: Season One Opener and the First Siege (Forward Plot, Confirmed Structure)

**Status:** Forward-plot, not yet on the page. Replaces the old Winter Bash (same December 25th date, same countdown from the story's opening, same 10 million yen prize pool). The old file `codex/pro-scene/winter-bash-tournament.md` is now a redirect stub.

Crownfall is the **Season One opener for seasonal points** and the **introduction of the Siege format**, a guild-only open-beta event hosted by HGO in the weeks between the Hearthfire announcement and the launch. It is the gateway event Tatsuya's early arc runs toward. It prepares everyone for the larger-scale conflict that follows: the war the story is about is Node Wars (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`). The event takes place in a **neutral zone** that may tie back to a legendary world quest (a rumor and a hint only, undecided). The standing competitive option built on the same format is the Siege Pro League, active from the start of Season One (see the Hearthfire doc, "Siege and the Siege Pro League").

## What kind of event it is

- A **Siege battle royale**: 5 guilds per session, 20 players per guild. Each guild starts at an outskirts-like camp at spawn, gathers resources to upgrade it to level 3, and it becomes a fort. Each guild has a guild flag and 60 total lives (3 waves). A session lasts 3 hours. Guilds fight for resources and survive, building creative forts with items and magic. Winners earn shillings and Ember cores. Format details: `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`.
- **Guild-only.** Individuals can earn seasonal points in open beta, but only guilds enter Crownfall, which is why Tatsuya founds a guild. There is no random-teammate mechanic (the old Winter Bash is gone).
- Guilds fill empty slots with troops from their hall, the troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`), so a small guild can still field a full side. **Crownfall does not cap troops**, which is why Tatsuya can bring so many. The Siege Pro League caps them.
- **Open-beta characters only.** Built after the Hearthfire announcement. Closed-beta characters cannot enter, and they freeze at the full launch a week later.
- Hosted by HGO itself, not a licensed outside host.

## Qualifying and bracket (to be refit to 5-guild heats)

The text below was written for single-elimination 20v20 sieges and **is to be refit to 5-guild heats**. Heat counts, advancement and the final session format are open (see Open).

- Old structure: ten qualifying sieges per region, four regions in parallel (**EMEA, Americas, APAC, China**), the top 8 guilds per region (32 in all) advancing to a world bracket on Christmas Day (Top 32, Top 16, Top 8, Top 4, Final).
- **Qualifying requires seasonal points.** Open-beta points roll into Season One, and a guild needs enough to be seeded in.

## Physical-attendance requirement

Once a guild reaches the world stage, its core players must attend a regional HGO stadium for the remainder, Tokyo for APAC. This is the mechanism behind two reveals landing in the same stretch: Tatsuya's first public appearance as T-Drag outside a stream overlay, and Eren's first in-person discovery that T-Drag is Tatsuya. Neither gets advance warning, the draw does it.

**Eren's side includes PK_Wraith** (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`). Haruto's public exit from Kansai Black Lotus (Chapter 8) leaves Eren without anyone strong willing to team with him, and PK_Wraith takes the open slot, his vendetta against T-Drag already real since Chapter 10. When the draw puts them in a session with Haya Storm, it lands the identity-reveal beat and PK_Wraith's revenge beat together, coincidence and not anyone's plan.

## Haya Storm's run

Haya Storm (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`) enters as a small guild built around Tatsuya, Arya and **Pac_Pac** (see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`), with Whispers of Shiv troops filling slots (their points credit to Tatsuya without his realizing at first, see `codex/characters/shiv.md`). Arya comes in as his adventuring partner rather than a guildmate, and joins the guild afterward.

- Qualifies as an **underdog seed**, the last guild through on standings.
- Shares a session with Eren and PK_Wraith's side (the reveal beat above).
- Is eliminated before the final by Dosuke AI's guild, with Haruto Murayama and Souma Ikeda (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`, `codex/characters/dosuke-ai.md`). The Dosuke AI pickup of Haruto is forward-plot only and not on the page in Chapter 8.
- A real, earned loss. Haya Storm is an underdog the whole way.

## Points and payouts

- Win-based points only, none for a loss, a small completion bonus for finishing the qualifying stretch. Standings run on the session result, not on individual scoring (no KDA bonus), keeping the format guild-first. Individual ladder rank stays a fully separate track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`).
- Crownfall is the **Season One opener for seasonal points**, the broader standing track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`). As an elimination-stage guild Haya Storm's core lands a points total high enough to rank above London Spire and Aogiri, Season Zero's champion and runner-up (see `codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md`), a felt underdog-credibility beat even in a losing run.
- **Prize pool: 10 million yen.** The winning guild's payout comes close to the cost of Ohema's surgery (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`). The top three guilds earn seasonal points plus money. Payouts are contribution-based inside each guild.

## Announcement and framing (what the story actually shows)

- Crownfall is **announced** inside Kazuku's Hearthfire ad as the Crownfall Siege Castle event in a neutral zone, a taste of the wars to come, with a ten million yen prize pool, alongside open beta, the 72-hour 50 percent discount on drives and the helmet rig, and the note that open beta leads into Season One. Kazuku also mentions a pro league as an option for players who do not want the open-world route. That bundle is the enticing offer that pulls Tatsuya in.
- Tatsuya treats it as money, not sport: the prize pool pays toward Ohema's surgery and the debt, and Crownfall may get the bear's attention.

## Why it is called Crownfall

The name comes from the king's fall. King Aldemar is dying, and he dies around the start of Season One (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`). The December 25th event runs in a neutral zone outside royal jurisdiction, so the coming death does not stop it. Nobody in the story knows yet.

## Zone and entry (author-stated)

The event is held in the Crownfall zone (author-stated): a neutral trade zone on a plateau off the coast of Lunarest, reached by ship. It is a massive magical forest, the last 10 to 15 acres off the back end of the kingdom, full of magical beasts, and the only high-magic ancient forest near Lunarest. It leads to the elves' domain: a domain that teleports you there, and the elves welcome you. Rumor says the court mage Corvith came from there. Normally the zone is open to everyone. During the event the forest is replicated, and the zone is locked to guilds and their participants. The rules will be set closer to the date.

OPEN: the event name (the king's fall) and the zone name are both "Crownfall". Not resolved.

Guild entry (author-stated): a guild needs at least five members to start (OPEN: the author wavered between three and five; five is used). Crownfall needs at least ten participants out of twenty slots, and participants can include allies and troops, not only players. Pac_Pac becomes needed later.
