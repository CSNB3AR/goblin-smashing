# Crownfall Siege Event (Forward Plot, Confirmed Structure)

**Status:** Forward-plot, not yet on the page. Replaces the old Winter Bash (same December 25th date, same countdown from the story's opening, same 10 million yen prize pool). The old file `codex/pro-scene/winter-bash-tournament.md` is now a redirect stub.

Crownfall is a **one-off Siege tournament**, an open-beta showcase held in the week between the Hearthfire announcement and the launch, and it is the gateway event Tatsuya's early arc runs toward. It is **not** the long-term competitive structure. After Hearthfire the long-running guild league is the Siege Pro League, and the war the story is about is Node Wars (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).

## What kind of event it is

- A **Siege**: guilds queue up to 20 players per side and fight over a fortified objective on a siege map (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md` for Siege rules). Guilds fill empty slots with troops from their hall, the troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`), so a small guild can still field a full side. Because it is a new event, **Crownfall does not cap troops**, which is why Tatsuya can bring so many. The Siege Pro League caps them afterward.
- **Open-beta characters only.** Built after the Hearthfire announcement. Closed-beta characters cannot enter, and they freeze at the full launch a week later. It is the debut showcase for characters built for the incoming season.
- Hosted by HGO itself, not a licensed outside host.
- The random-teammate mechanic of the old Winter Bash is gone. Entry is by guild.

## Qualifying structure

Ten qualifying sieges per region, four regions run in parallel: **EMEA, Americas, APAC, China.** The top 8 guilds from each region's standings advance, 32 guilds in all, into a single world bracket.

## World bracket, single elimination, Christmas Day

Top 32, Top 16, Top 8 (quarterfinals), Top 4 (semifinals), Final, run across one "Championship Sunday"-style finale (Crownfall falls on December 25th).

## Physical-attendance requirement

Once a guild reaches the Top 32, its core players must attend a regional HGO stadium for the remainder of the bracket, Tokyo for APAC. This is the mechanism behind two reveals landing in the same stretch: Tatsuya's first public appearance as T-Drag outside a stream overlay, and Eren's first in-person discovery that T-Drag is Tatsuya. Neither gets advance warning, the bracket draw does it.

**Eren's Top 32 pairing includes PK_Wraith** (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`). Haruto's public exit from Kansai Black Lotus (Chapter 8) leaves Eren without anyone strong willing to team with him, and PK_Wraith takes the open slot, his vendetta against T-Drag already real since Chapter 9. Neither can guarantee facing Haya Storm, since seeding factors in qualifying record alongside the draw. When the draw does put them across from Haya Storm, it lands the identity-reveal beat and PK_Wraith's separate revenge beat in the same match, coincidence and not anyone's plan.

## Haya Storm's run

Haya Storm (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`) enters as a small guild built around Tatsuya, Arya and **Pac_Pac** (see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`), with Whispers of Shiv troops filling the queue (their points credit to Tatsuya without his realizing at first, see `codex/characters/shiv.md`). Arya comes in as his adventuring partner rather than a guildmate, and joins the guild afterward.

- **8-2 qualifying record**, 8th seed out of APAC, the last guild through on standings.
- Advances through the Top 32 siege against Eren and PK_Wraith's side (the reveal beat above).
- **Eliminated in the semifinals** by Dosuke AI's guild, with Haruto Murayama and Souma Ikeda (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`, `codex/characters/dosuke-ai.md`). This is where Dosuke AI picking Haruto up as a free agent, seeded in Chapter 8, pays off.
- A real, earned loss. Haya Storm is an underdog seed the whole way.

## Points and payouts

- Win-based points only, none for a loss, a small completion bonus for finishing the qualifying stretch. Siege standings run on the siege objective result, not on individual scoring (no KDA bonus), keeping the format guild-first. Individual ladder rank stays a fully separate track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`).
- Crownfall is the first event to award **seasonal points** that carry into Season One, the broader standing track (see `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md`). As semifinalists Haya Storm's core lands a points total high enough to rank above London Spire and Aogiri, Season Zero's champion and runner-up (see `codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md`), a felt underdog-credibility beat even in a losing run.
- **Prize pool: 10 million yen.** The winning guild's payout comes close to the cost of Ohema's surgery (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`). The top three guilds earn seasonal points plus money. Payouts are contribution-based inside each guild.
