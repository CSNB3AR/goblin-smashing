# Pro Teams (background roster, for future-chapter use)

Regional pro team texture, cross-consistent with what's already established on the page. Each entry is a one-line identity, not a full roster, background texture to draw from when a team needs to actually mean something in a scene rather than just be named. See `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md` for the tournament structure these teams compete in.

| Team | Region | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kansai Black Lotus** | Japan | Eren's team. Aggressive early-snowball comps, style over structure, Haruto Murayama's the only reason it's actually competitive (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`). Knocked out in World Championship qualifiers this cycle, worst record in their pool. |
| **London Spire** | Europe | Reigning World Championship winner this cycle, 40 million yen prize pool. Disciplined, patient drafts, the team everyone else is measured against right now. Roster: **Alistair Rhodes** (Warden, shield support, the team's shotcaller), **Freya Lindqvist** (Ironbreaker tank), **Mateo Silva** (Windrider, ranged DPS, the mechanical star, carries a World-tier off-hand talisman, see `codex/objects/mateo-silvas-talisman.md`, real gear behind the consistency). Names easy to rename. |
| **Aogiri** | International | This cycle's World Championship runner-up, lost the final after pushing off-point when they should have sat and bled the clock (see `chapters/chapter-01.md`). Aggressive, high-variance, the team that beats itself as often as it beats anyone else. Roster: **Ren Kanzaki** (Berserker, the one who pushed off-point), **Hana Otsuka** (Spellforger), **Jin Osei** (Shadowveil). Names easy to rename. |
| **Tokyo Ronin** | Japan | Defensive siege comps, methodical, rarely tilts, the anti-Kansai Black Lotus in playstyle as much as geography. |
| **Sendai ArcStars** | Japan | Spellforger-heavy, slow-scaling drafts that fall apart if rushed early. |
| **Kyoto Kitsune** | Japan | Trickster/illusion-leaning comps, deliberately deceptive drafts, hard to scout. |
| **Osaka Blue Nova** | Japan | Strong macro/rotational shot-calling over individual mechanical skill. |
| **Seoul Thunderstrike** | Korea | Fast, mechanically precise, the region's benchmark for individual skill expression. |
| **Busan Bladewalkers** | Korea | Assassin/rogue-class-heavy, high-risk aggressive picks. |
| **Shanghai Dawnbringers** | China | Large, well-funded org, deep bench, wins through roster depth more than any single star. |
| **Beijing Jade Guard** | China | Tank/support-anchored, grinds matches out rather than closing fast. |
| **LA Titans** | NA | Big personalities, streamer-forward, results inconsistent year to year. |
| **NY Specters** | NA | Stealth/vision-control specialists, known for punishing overextension. |
| **Dallas Oathkeepers** | NA | Newer org, aggressive spending on transfers, still finding an identity. |
| **Berlin Silver Wolves** | Europe | Disciplined, unglamorous, consistently a top-four finish without ever quite winning it all. |
| **Paris Dream** | Europe | Flashy, high-variance drafts, a fan-favorite team that either pops off or falls apart, rarely in between. |
| **Manila Pulse** | SEA | Fast-paced, aggressive early game, the region's most-watched team on Loop clips. |
| **Bangkok Aegis** | SEA | Defensive, methodical, SEA's answer to Tokyo Ronin's playstyle. |
