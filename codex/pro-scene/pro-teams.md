# Pro Guilds (background roster, for future-chapter use)

The competitive units in HGO are **guilds**, not fixed small teams (the file keeps its old name so cross-references resolve). Pro guilds run about 50 members and field up to 20 in a Siege, with garrison troops filling any gaps. The closed beta had only about 5,000 players, so these are the guilds organized enough to matter, with the region tag marking where members are based. Twelve qualified for the Season Zero Championship. Each entry is a one-line identity, background texture to draw from when a guild needs to mean something in a scene rather than just be named. See `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md` for the formats and `codex/pro-scene/guilds.md` for the economy-side roster.

| Guild | Region | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kansai Black Lotus** | Japan | Eren's team. Aggressive early-snowball comps, style over structure, Haruto Murayama's the only reason it's actually competitive (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`). Missed the Season Zero top eight because Arya's small guild took the last qualifying spot (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`), and Eren holds a grudge over it. |

Names easy to rename. |
