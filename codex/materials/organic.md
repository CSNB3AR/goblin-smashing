# Materials — Organic / Beast

**Status:** New system. See `codex/materials/overview.md` for grades and the per-region breakdown these cross-reference against.

## Representative materials

- **Beast Leather / Hides** (Common-Rare) — basic armor, pouches, belts, wraps. **Myrrhwood Hide** (Rare) runs tougher and poison-resistant, a regional specialty.
- **Gas Sacs** (Uncommon-Epic) — harvested from poison-capable creatures or evolved Toxic Breath sources (see `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`). The core Alchemy/Poisoncraft ingredient behind gas bombs, antidotes, and any Toxic Breath upgrade.
- **Bone Shards / Sturdy Beast Bone** (Common-Rare) — daggers, staves, runes, jewelry, Bonecrafting's bread and butter. **Ancient Bone Shards** (Rare-Epic), pulled from ruins or the Grand Library's restricted floors, carry real magic affinity.
- **Monster Claws / Fangs / Sinew** (Uncommon+) — weapon edges, traps, stitching.
- **Herbs** — **Nightveil** (poison base), **Flarebloom** (ignition/explosive base), **Spiritmoss** (mana regeneration, spirit channeling).

## Processed / intermediate forms

Tanned Leather, Carved Bone Runes, Distilled Poison, Enchanted Thread, Spirit-Infused Oil, Flare Cores, each one a real, separately-crafted intermediate step between raw material and finished item, not a single-click conversion.
