# Materials — Magical / Spirit-Tied

**Status:** New system. Shiv-path-exclusive or high-Fortune-gated, distinct from the general organic/inorganic layer (see `codex/materials/organic.md`, `codex/materials/inorganic-and-salvage.md`). Feeds the Runecrafting/Spirit-Binding profession specifically (see `codex/crafting/professions.md`), gated the same way Spirit Magic access itself is gated.

## Representative materials

- **Spirit Shards** (Epic-World-Tier) — magic weapon cores, Bone Surge catalysts, Shaman's Veil upgrades. Dropped by spirit entities or World Quest bosses, not a farmable drop.
- **Whispering Ash / Cursed Ash** (Rare-Legendary) — pulled from high-level cursed enemies or Shiv apparitions. The base component for spirit-binding work and anti-curse items.
- **Shivbone Fragments** (World-Tier, Call of the Bones reward-tier only) — the core material behind any true Shiv-descendant gear. Not obtainable outside the World Quest chain.
- **Legacy Crystals / Ancient Relics** (Epic+) — sourced from old-account cash-outs (see `bible/game-system.md` §3, the Crystal League Legacy Point system) or Grand Library research, reading as hybrid human-elf-goblin tech rather than purely natural material.
