# Materials: Monocrystals & Mono Steel

**Status:** Entry. These sit under the four material grades in `codex/materials/overview.md` (Rough, Standard, Refined, Pristine), which are separate from the item rarity ladder (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). Monocrystals are a creature drop; mono steel is a **Refined-grade alloy** built on Valefrost's Rimeiron.

## Monocrystals

- **What they are.** Pale, faceted, faintly luminescent crystals that grow in a dense cluster along the ridge fur of wind-attuned mountain and forest beasts. Cool to the touch once harvested, warm and almost alive for a short while after a kill. Each crystal carries the affinity of the creature it came from; the standard ones are **wind** (ties to `codex/magic/book-of-tempest.md`).
- **Source creature.** The **Greyvast** (Class III, `codex/creatures/class-3.md`) is the standard wind-crystal drop. A Pristine cluster off the Class IV Stormcaller Roc (`codex/creatures/class-4.md`) is a plausible top end, flagged open.
- **How they are gathered.** Standard post-kill procedure for garrison soldiers, guards and monster-hunting parties: cut the cluster from the base of the neck as soon as the beast drops. Nobody treats them as treasure in the north, they are routine trade goods.
- **Trade value.** Reliable coin. A small cluster pays for a day's supplies, which makes them a natural early substitute for shillings for any guild running short. Sold to smiths, enchanters and dwarven houses.
- **Rarity and grade.** Base rarity **Uncommon** (Rough or Standard grade off a Class III Greyvast), **Rare** grades off prime specimens.
- **Region.** The Ashenveil Forest and the high mountain ranges of southern Valefrost (`codex/materials/overview.md`, `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`).

## Mono Steel

- **What it is.** An alloy made by smelting **Rimeiron** (Valefrost's frost-veined ore) with a wind monocrystal cluster. It keeps Rimeiron's minor Frost resistance and adds the property that matters: a blade or piece of armor made from it holds a Physical Arts augment properly instead of bleeding it, and stands up better against magic-based attacks than plain iron.
- **Where it sits.** Each region's metal has a niche. Skyglass Ore carries Wisdom for staves, Sunforged Iron carries a burn enchant, and mono steel is the augment-holding metal for blades and armor. Refined grade.
- **Who refines it.** The refining step (turning crystals and Rimeiron into steel ingots) is Valefrost dwarven house knowledge (`codex/races/dwarf.md`). Anyone with the Smithing profession at the right tier can work a finished ingot; getting ingots takes a dwarven contact or standing with a house (`codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md`). That makes it consistent with `codex/crafting/professions.md`: smithing is open to all, the refining knowledge is not.
- **Rarity.** Rare ingredient. Finished gear rarity follows the crafter and grade, not the material alone.

## Story hooks

- A cheap early Myrrhwood-to-Valefrost trade good for the goblin trade company (`codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`), and a reason to cultivate a dwarf supplier.
- A mono steel upgrade is a clean, concrete answer to "my blade can't hold the enchant" for Tatsuya's enchant-heavy build.
