# Materials: Monocrystals & Mono Steel

**Status:** New entry. Provenance: adapted from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles (BKC-Files), recreated in HGO terms, not pasted. See `codex/materials/overview.md` for the four material grades (Rough, Standard, Refined, Pristine) these sit under, which are separate from the item rarity ladder (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`).

## Monocrystals

- **What they are.** Pale, faceted, faintly luminescent crystals that grow in a dense cluster along the ridge fur of wind-attuned mountain beasts. Cool to the touch once harvested, warm and almost alive for a short while after a kill. Each crystal carries the affinity of the creature it came from; the standard ones are **wind** (ties to `codex/magic/book-of-tempest.md`).
- **How they are gathered.** Standard post-kill procedure for garrison soldiers, guards and monster-hunting parties: cut the cluster from the base of the neck as soon as the beast drops. Nobody treats them as treasure in the north, they are routine trade goods.
- **Trade value.** Reliable coin. A small cluster pays for a day's supplies, which makes them a natural early currency substitute for any guild short on shillings. Sold to smiths, enchanters and dwarven houses.
- **Rarity and grade.** Base rarity **Uncommon** (Rough or Standard grade off Class II to III beasts). **Rare** grades off Class III. **Pristine** wind monocrystals are a plausible drop from the Class IV Stormcaller Roc (`codex/creatures/class-4.md`), flagged open.
- **Region.** Valefrost and other high mountain ranges (`codex/materials/overview.md`).

## Mono Steel

- **What it is.** Monocrystals refined down into a steel alloy. A blade or piece of armor made from it holds a magical augment properly instead of bleeding it, and the metal stands up better against magic-based attacks than plain iron does.
- **Who refines it.** The **dwarven houses** are the real experts at processing monocrystals, and everyone else works from methods the dwarves passed down. Fits the Valefrost dwarves (`codex/races/dwarf.md`) as the region's signature smithing trade, and makes a dwarf contact valuable to any guild that wants mono steel gear.
- **Crafting.** Smithing input (`codex/crafting/professions.md`). Like every material, the crafter's tier and the input's grade both gate the result.
- **Rarity.** Rare ingredient. Finished gear rarity follows the crafter and grade, not the material alone.

## Story hooks

- A cheap early Myrrhwood-to-Valefrost trade good for the goblin trade company (`codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`), and a reason to cultivate a dwarf supplier.
- A mono steel upgrade is a clean, concrete answer to "my blade can't hold the enchant" for Tatsuya's enchant-heavy build.
