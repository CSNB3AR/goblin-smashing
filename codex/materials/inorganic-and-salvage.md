# Materials — Inorganic / Salvage

**Status:** New system. See `codex/materials/overview.md` for grades and the per-region breakdown these cross-reference against.

## Representative materials

- **Scrap Wood / Planks** (Common) — campfires, basic tools, gadget frames.
- **Copper / Iron Scrap & Ore** (Common-Rare) — metal edges, reinforced belts, simple tools, often salvaged directly from human or elf ruins rather than mined fresh.
- **Rune Stone Fragments** (Uncommon-Epic) — inscription, magic channeling, sourced from the Grand Library's restricted floors or general treasure-hunting, the raw feedstock behind Runecrafting.

## Treasure / catalyst finds

Non-standard, higher-variance drops that boost a craft's quality roll or unlock a hidden recipe rather than serving as a bulk ingredient: Curious Runes, Blue Pearls (a mana-catalyst gem), Mysterious Gems, and fragments tied to older, half-forgotten lore (a "Book of the Dead" family of finds). These lean into the same Fortune-driven "lucky find" logic as Gambler's Gambit, rare, exciting, not something a crafter can reliably farm.
