# Crafting Professions

**Status:** New system, see `codex/crafting/overview.md` for the CP resource, tier structure, and success-rate mechanic these all share.

## The six professions

| Profession | Draws from | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Smithing** | Ores, metals (see `codex/materials/inorganic-and-salvage.md`) | Weapons, armor, the physical gear layer |
| **Bonecrafting / Leatherworking** | Beast hides, bone, sinew, claws (see `codex/materials/organic.md`) | Light armor, daggers, staves, jewelry. Goblin-native, most Bloodhurst gear starts here |
| **Alchemy / Poisoncraft** | Herbs, reagents, monster parts | Potions, buffs (see `codex/crafting/buffs.md`), poisons and gas variants, the raw-material side of the Goblin Gas/Flare family (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) |
| **Enchanting** | Rare ores, magical residue, World Quest-tier drops | Gear enchantments, the mechanism behind a "Rare" or higher item's bonus line |
| **Cooking** | Food ingredients, regional produce | Consumable buffs (see `codex/crafting/buffs.md`) |
| **Runecrafting / Spirit-Binding** | Rune stone fragments, spirit shards, cursed ash (see `codex/materials/magical-and-spirit-tied.md`) | Inscribed runes, spirit-bound gear. **Gated behind an open Spirit Magic path** (see `codex/magic/overview.md`'s Wisdom-18 access gate, or Tatsuya's own necklace-bypass exception, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), same access rule as casting itself, not a separate unlock |

## Goblin-native starting edge

Goblins default into Bonecrafting/Leatherworking and the gadget-flavored branch of Alchemy naturally, consistent with the race's low-Wisdom-high-Fortune profile (see `codex/races/goblin.md`): scrap, bone, and gas over polished magical crafting, at least until a Spirit Magic path opens the Runecrafting/Spirit-Binding profession. This is flavor and starting-tier ease, not a hard lock, any race can eventually level any profession.
