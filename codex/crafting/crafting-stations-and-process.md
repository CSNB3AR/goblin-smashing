# Crafting Stations & Process

**Status:** New system, see `codex/crafting/overview.md` and `codex/crafting/professions.md`.

## Stations

- **Portable Campfire Kit** — the goblin default, works anywhere safe (a camp, a cleared room), covers basic-tier recipes across most professions. Low success chance on anything Tier 4+.
- **Clan Workbench / Forge** — found at Bloodhurst and other settled goblin camps, or in Lunarest's goblin districts. Full Smithing and Bonecrafting/Leatherworking access.
- **Shamanic Altar / Spirit Circle** — unlocked through the Call of the Bones questline or real Thrash clan standing (see `codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md`). Required for Runecrafting/Spirit-Binding output, including anything feeding Tatsuya's own Bone Surge/Ancient Bone Flame Barrage kit.
- **Portable Shaman Kit** — a later quest-reward item, allows field spirit-binding away from a fixed altar.

## Process

1. **Gather and process** raw materials (tan a hide, carve a bone, distill a reagent), a short timed action rather than an instant conversion.
2. **Open the recipe.** Many recipes are discovered through experimentation or clan lore rather than handed over outright, consistent with the "no lore-dump" rule, a player learns a recipe by doing, not by reading a menu.
3. **Commit materials**, plus an optional catalyst (a gem, a rune fragment, a Spirit Shard for anything magic-adjacent).
4. **Roll for success** (see `codex/crafting/overview.md`'s success-rate mechanic). A high-Fortune crafter, goblins specifically, gets a real edge here, a "Lucky Craft" proc that can bump output quality or grant a bonus effect, the same Fortune-scaling logic already established for Gambler's Gambit and Bone Surge (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), applied to crafting instead of combat/loot.
5. **Resolve.** Success produces the item, quality tier affecting its final stats. Failure returns a portion of materials or produces a degraded item, never a total loss, but a real, felt setback.

## Market and binding

Common/Uncommon-tier crafted goods sell instantly through standing buy orders (the same mechanism behind the Minor Elixir sales already on the page, `chapters/chapter-04.md`). Rare-tier and above usually move player-to-player or stay bound if tied to a quest reward. Modular gear (the Goblin Gadget Belt, see `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`) can be upgraded in place rather than replaced outright.
