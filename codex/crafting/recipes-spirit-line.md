# Recipes — Spirit Line

**Status:** New system, representative rather than exhaustive. Gated behind an open Spirit Magic path (see `codex/crafting/professions.md`'s Runecrafting/Spirit-Binding entry), so these are rare, high-investment crafts, not something most characters ever touch. **None of these duplicate Tatsuya's own necklace-exclusive kit** (Bone Surge, Whispers of Shiv, Ancient Bone Flame Barrage all stay unique to his Call of the Bones Necklace bond, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`); these are general Spirit Magic items any character who's opened the path could theoretically craft or use, sharing an aesthetic with Shiv's legacy rather than duplicating his signature abilities under a different name.

## Shivbone Wand / Staff (Rare-Legendary)

**Visual reference, locked:** a gnarled, vine-wrapped wooden staff, dark bark twisting up its length, live green vines and leaf growth threaded through the grain, topped with a faceted, glowing pale-green crystal cradled in a bloom of branch-like prongs, soft green motes drifting off it in the air. Reads as grown rather than forged, living wood and crystal fused together, not metal.

**Recipe:** Ancient Bone Shard x3 (`codex/materials/organic.md`) + Spiritmoss x5 + Rune Stone Fragment + a Spirit Shard as the activating catalyst (`codex/materials/magical-and-spirit-tied.md`).

**Effects:** +Wisdom and +Fortune while equipped. A moderate spirit-damage channel attack with minor knockback. Passive, renamed to avoid collision with Tatsuya's own necklace-exclusive Whispers of Shiv: **Spirit Echo**, a Fortune-scaled on-hit chance to apply a brief, weaker version of Silence (spellcasting only, not the full dual-lock Hushveil applies) or a minor Fear proc. Scales further with an open Shaman's Veil path (`codex/magic/book-of-the-veil.md`) for additional minor spirit-summon or area-whisper effects. Can be soulbound or fitted with modular spirit attachments, the same "Battlegear" modular philosophy already established for the Goblin Gadget Belt.

**A specimen drops as found loot on the page, Chapter 9:** Spellweaver_Lyn's own legacy-tier staff, off PK_Wraith's crew (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`). Tatsuya sets it aside rather than selling it with the rest of the loot, recognizing it as a real match for Arya's mage build, see `codex/characters/arya.md`. Not a contradiction with this being a crafting-tree item, a found specimen and a player-crafted copy are the same object by two different paths, consistent with how gear works everywhere else in Eryndal.

## Whispering Gas Orb (Epic, hybrid gadget/spirit item)

Gas Sac + Whispering Ash + Spirit Shard + a rune. Throwable, a spirit-possessed poison cloud applying Poison, Fear, and a minor Deafen effect (see `codex/crafting/debuffs-and-resistances.md`) in its radius, one of the few items that combines the gadget line's gas-combat identity with genuine spirit magic.

## Spiritlash Whip (Rare+)

Sinew + Ancient Bone + Spirit Shard. A melee weapon with a ranged spirit-lash follow-up that applies base Silence on a critical or high-Fortune proc.

## Echoing Bone Helm / Whispercloak (armor accessory)

Reduces or shortens incoming Deafen effects, doubles as a minor stealth or spirit-scouting aid for the wearer's own awareness. See `codex/crafting/debuffs-and-resistances.md`'s Deafen entry, this is the crafted counter-item that entry references.

## Rune-Primed Flare Launcher

An upgraded Goblin Flare (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) carrying a minor spirit-magic charge, or capable of igniting a gas cloud at range with a bonus effect. A crafted, general-purpose cousin to Tatsuya's own Shiv-Touched Flare, not the same item.

## Portable Spirit Ward Totem

Carved bone plus Whispering Ash. A temporary zone effect cleansing minor debuffs or granting a small buff to nearby allies, a natural fit for supporting a disciple-tier companion (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`) in a real fight.
