# Recipes — Gadget Line

**Status:** New system, representative rather than exhaustive. The goblin-native crafting identity, already seeded on the page from Chapter 2-3 onward (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`, `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`).

## Representative recipes

- **Smoke Bomb** (Common) — Beast Leather pouch + herbs + scrap wood. Creates an obscuring cloud, stealth or escape utility.
- **Gas Bomb** (Uncommon, unlocks a Goblin Gadget Belt ability slot) — Gas Sac + leather pouch + a catalyst. Deploys a Poison-stacking cloud, ignitable for the already-established Gas + Flare combo (`codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`).
- **Ignition Gas Grenade** (Rare) — Gas Sac + Flarebloom + leather + a Flare Core. Throwable, explodes on fire contact for combined Burn + Poison, a crafted, portable version of the same combo Tatsuya discovered accidentally in Chapter 3.
- **Charged Ward** (Rare, OPEN placement): Monocrystal + leather pouch + Common Spirit Dust + a catalyst. Single-use ward that absorbs one burst or lightning hit up to its crafted charge, then breaks (see `codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`). Adapted from BKC-Files.
- **Goblin Gadget Belt upgrades** (modular) — additional pouches/slots accepting crafted bombs, later slots capable of accepting Spirit Shards once the Spirit Magic path opens, hybridizing the gadget line with spirit-tied effects (see `codex/crafting/recipes-spirit-line.md`).
