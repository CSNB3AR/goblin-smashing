# NPC Personality Template

**Status:** PROPOSAL. A reusable card for any gold or named NPC. Fill it in two minutes, then write the scene from it. Never print the placement on the page. NPCs speak of deeds, never of labels. Cell names are defined in `codex/lore/alignment-framework.md`.

```
NAME / ROLE / LEVEL:
PLACEMENT: D&D cell, HGO byname (Road + Hand). Hold: Firm, Settled or Wavering.
TRAITS: (1) one habit of speech or body. (2) one habit of choice.
IDEAL: what they believe, one line. Creed: Order, Freedom, Mercy, Hunger, Balance or Calling.
BOND: the person, place or oath they would lose something for.
FLAW: the weakness a scene can press on.
LINE: the one thing they will not do or will not forgive. It need not be a virtue.
TRUST: the deeds that raise their Affiliation (small, notable, landmark).
BREAK: the deeds that lower it.
PAGE: what the chapters already show (so later chapters do not contradict it).
DRIFT: what could move them, and the signal to watch for. Gold NPCs only.
VOICE: register, vocabulary, a tic. Goblins keep clipped speech. No modern idioms.
```

**Quick placement tests.** An order is wrong and the giver can punish you: obey, bend, or refuse (Road). A stranger is helpless and helping costs you (Hand). A promise turns expensive (Road). Nobody will ever know (Hand). Their own people are hurt: what do they do to whoever did it (Hand).

**Rules of use.** Every card needs a Line and a Flaw. At least one Trait should surprise the cell (a Warden who is funny, a Taker who respects courage). Mob NPCs with only a role title get a one line Practice note, not a card.
