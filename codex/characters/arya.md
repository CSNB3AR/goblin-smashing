# Arya Sakura

**Role:** Leon's younger sister. Future ally/teammate. Surname **Sakura**, shared with Leon, easy to rename.

## Appearance

Red hair, street-biker aesthetic (per the old draft, carried forward), own HGO drive and account.

## Facts

- Enters the story slowly and deliberately. Their actual first contact, at the front desk when Tatsuya first comes looking for Leon, is brief and purely transactional, she points him toward Leon and the tech, nothing more, a beat of mutual noticing since he's around her age. That's it, not a conversation, not the start of anything yet.
- The real curiosity builds afterward, over multiple further visits. She notices patterns: Leon's business changing, an active VR presence around the arcade, being turned down herself a few times when she wanted to use the pods.
- That's what sparks her real interest. She researches Tatsuya independently and watches his gameplay footage before ever actually approaching him with intent.
- She's been playing competitively for years, she has no reason to casually team up with a stranger, so her eventual approach must be earned and specific, not casual.
- Sharp and guarded.

## Guild backstory (background, later chapters)

Her all-girls guild, **Crimson Static** (see `codex/pro-scene/guilds.md`), fractured after a real falling-out among the members, leaving Arya's own crew small heading into the Crystal League expansion. Part of why she's genuinely available when the Winter Bash's random-teammate mechanic pairs her with Tatsuya, not just narrative convenience. Crimson Static went 0-6 in the World Championship qualifiers this cycle (unchanged), but still reached the World Championship itself through a real, international **GvG merit-slot event**: guilds already holding pro-qualified representation can't re-enter it, opening a slot for an underdog guild on pure merit. Crimson Static won into that slot, she's an established guild leader in her own right even before any of this lands on the page.

**The collapse, full sequence (surfaces Chapter 9).** Crimson Static went in as a three-leader guild (Arya plus two others) and got dog-walked in group play, best-of-three King of the Hill, not a single map taken. **Arya never kicked anyone.** She was the only one of the three who actually played it like real competition (best kill/point ratio on the team by a wide margin); the other two, **Sana Domoto** and **Nao Fujita**, were chasing viewership and stream moments over the actual matches, and after the loss, they turned on her instead. Sana ran the real damage: publicly blaming Arya's leadership and her relationship for the loss, claiming she demoralized the team. Nao was more a follower, peer-pressured along, never badmouthed Arya publicly herself, but left alongside Sana all the same. It worked: the guild bled from 80-90+ members down to roughly three who actually knew what happened and stayed.

A third, separate departure around the same time: **Jiro Amano**, an old Crimson Static member with real trade connections, NPCs and players both, does Arya one last favor before he leaves the guild himself, later extending to Tatsuya (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md` and `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md` for the PK_Wraith-loot payoff this sets up). He's not proud of the timing, he let Sana's rumor campaign carry him along even though he could have stayed, but he does the favor anyway before walking away. Distinct from the unnamed auction-house specialist Arya later recruits while actually rebuilding, keep the two separate, don't merge them.

Arya rebuilt under a new guild name with a couple of new recruits, one of them the unnamed auction-house specialist mentioned above, she leans on him for gear logistics. She's currently sitting on a fresh, low-level mage build (level 10-11), most of her discipline points still unspent, deliberately held back since the collapse rather than sunk into a build she can't yet commit to.

**The real pressure underneath it.** Her mother wants her in America for medical school, a real, live option, not a bluff. Arya doesn't want to go, wants to stay in Tokyo, near Leon, and build something real in HGO first. Her entire read on the current moment, the guild, the Winter Bash pairing, all of it, is filtered through needing this to actually work before that decision gets made for her. **The real foundation underneath this, not yet stated on the page beyond what Chapter 9 already implies:** this isn't just preference, it's a live custody situation, Leon won formal guardianship of her years ago on the strength of their late grandfather's legal backing, and without that money now, her parents pushing to bring her to America is the custody fight quietly threatening to reopen. See `codex/subplots/leon-arya-custody-battle.md` for the full mechanism.

**School:** attends a local Tokyo public school, not Tochi Academy, a different background from Tatsuya's.

**Winter Bash's semifinal loss is a real, live stake for her specifically, not just a competitive disappointment.** Going in, she'd privately been ready to concede to her mother and leave for America if the tournament run didn't prove something real. It doesn't end in a win, the team's eliminated in the semis, but reaching that far as an 8th-seed underdog is real enough that it doesn't close the door either, left genuinely open rather than resolved.

**Act Two: the Tochi scholarship.** On the strength of winning Tochi's representative slot (see `codex/pro-scene/tochi-representative-qualifier.md`), Tochi extends a real scholarship to her (alongside Pac_Pac, see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`), enrolling her in the school itself, not just a sponsorship gesture. This is the real turn for her arc, and not only emotionally: it demonstrates she earned this on tournament merit, not charity, and it's the actual legal linchpin in the reopened custody fight (see `codex/subplots/leon-arya-custody-battle.md`), concrete, institutional proof that undercuts her parents' case. Draws real pushback, family and/or agents showing up to contest it, and Leon steps in on her behalf, flatly stating he'll bring in lawyers if it comes to that, a real, dramatized family-pressure conflict, not settled quickly or quietly. See `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md` for the team this scholarship keeps her on.

**Also Act Two: old guildmates resurface.** Sana Domoto and Nao Fujita come back into the picture now that she's a real, publicly visible player, stirring fresh esports-scene drama, callouts and rumor-mongering, consistent with exactly what already cost her the guild once (see the collapse sequence above). A real, personal-stakes thread distinct from the custody fight, running in parallel.

## Shivbone Staff, Chapter 9

Off the PK_Wraith kill, Spellweaver_Lyn's own legacy weapon drops separately from the rest of the sellable gear: a Shivbone Staff (Rare-Legendary, see `codex/crafting/recipes-spirit-line.md` for the item's full recipe/effect entry, +Wisdom and +Fortune, a Spirit Echo passive that applies a light Silence proc). Tatsuya doesn't put it up for sale with the rest of the loot, he sets it aside for Arya specifically, a real, on-page recognition that it matches her build directly, complementing the same Hushveil investment he just talked her into. The other three pieces (Ironhide Chestplate, Vanguard's Greaves, PK Raiding Crest) go to market normally.

## Personality

High-energy, passionate, slightly chaotic. Plays aggressive and loud, gets easily tilted by lag or toxic teammates, then laughs it off. Refuses to read tutorials or guides, forces her way through everything by stubborn trial-and-error. Extremely expressive, her face and hands do half the talking. Fiercely protective of her people, defends them hard, carries lower-skilled friends without hesitation.
