# PK_Wraith

**Role:** Named open-world PvP faction leader. Reconfirmed for the redraft (Chapter 4+), previously an old-draft-only reference.

## Facts

Big, loud, well-known across the open-world PvP scene, and genuinely weak at actual competitive play. His group bullies small guilds and new players rather than testing themselves against anyone who'd push back, farming easy kills and item drops rather than climbing any real ladder. Active and visible on Loop, reacts loudly to server-wide events.

First surfaces in Chapter 4 through Loop chatter reacting to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest announcement, background noise at this point, not yet a direct threat. Forward plot: once the regular, doomed monthly "goblin rally" event stops recurring at Lunarest (because Tatsuya actually completed what it always failed to), PK_Wraith's crew loses their predictable, easy farm target there and pivots to going after the Thrash camp directly, this is how he becomes a real, escalating antagonist later, not just Loop noise.

See `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md` and `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md` for the old-draft party (Rian, Spellweaver_Lyn, Talo, Joss) who answered to him, useful template for how his people talk and coordinate, not yet reconfirmed as characters who'll actually appear.
