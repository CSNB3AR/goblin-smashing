# PK_Wraith

**Role:** Named open-world PvP faction leader. Reconfirmed for the redraft (Chapter 4+), previously an old-draft-only reference.

## Facts

Big, loud, well-known across the open-world PvP scene, and genuinely weak at actual competitive play. His group bullies small guilds and new players rather than testing themselves against anyone who'd push back, farming easy kills and item drops rather than climbing any real ladder. Active and visible on Loop, reacts loudly to server-wide events.

First surfaces in Chapter 4 through Loop chatter reacting to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest announcement, background noise at this point, not yet a direct threat. Also visible reacting to the rally's no-show in Loop chatter early Chapter 6 (`@PK_Wraith`), still background noise at that point, not yet aware of what actually happened.

**Corrected forward plot.** The Call of the Bones World Quest is a long-running, multi-piece hunt (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`) with no near-term completion, so PK_Wraith's pivot to Bloodhurst is **not** gated behind the old "rally event stops recurring" framing, that note is superseded. Instead: consistent with the already-locked fact that Bloodhurst has survived repeated PK raids hunting it for easy farm kills (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), his crew (see `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md`, Rian, Spellweaver_Lyn, Talo, and Joss, now reconfirmed as appearing) hits the camp's eastern patrol line directly.

**Confirmed, Chapter 8: this is a genuine accident, not a hunt.** The crew stumbled onto Bloodhurst while it was already sitting on Lunarest's outskirts, not a hidden location. They have no in-dive way to cross-reference a livestream against a physical location, no idea this is the same camp the "Call of the Bones" World Quest is centered on. After the first two scout kills, they read it as an ordinary lucky free-farm spot and dug in on the high ground commanding the camp's only real chokepoint (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), not realizing they've picked a fight with the actual quest camp until it's already underway.

**Chapter 7:** a scout's ambush report interrupts Tatsuya on his way to the war tent, right after the necklace bond and Ru's blessing, killing two Bloodhurst patrol goblins in the opening clash, four or five attackers, mixed classes. No negotiated window, no resolution, no logout. The chapter ends mid-report, Tatsuya still logged in, wondering aloud whether his own location's been found. The raid's fuller outcome, including whether/how PK_Wraith's own people end up dying in the clash, stays unresolved on the page.

If his own crew takes real losses there, that's the origin point of a personal vendetta aimed specifically at Tatsuya, a real antagonist rather than background Loop noise, and a direct in-game foil to Eren's real-world rivalry. **Resolves off-page during the same real-world gap that covers Arya's deal-break confrontation** (see `codex/subplots/aryas-loop-discovery-and-reputation.md` for the locked Chapter 8 sequence: Leon pulls Tatsuya offline for a real monitor check, Tatsuya returns to find Arya intercepting him before he can log back in), not shown on the page itself.
