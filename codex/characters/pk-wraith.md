# PK_Wraith

**Role:** Named open-world PvP faction leader. Reconfirmed for the redraft (Chapter 4+), previously an old-draft-only reference.

## Facts

Big, loud, well-known across the open-world PvP scene, and genuinely weak at actual competitive play. His group bullies small guilds and new players rather than testing themselves against anyone who'd push back, farming easy kills and item drops rather than climbing any real ladder. Active and visible on Loop, reacts loudly to server-wide events.

First surfaces in Chapter 4 through Loop chatter reacting to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest announcement, background noise at this point, not yet a direct threat. Also visible reacting to the rally's no-show in Loop chatter early Chapter 6 (`@PK_Wraith`), still background noise at that point, not yet aware of what actually happened.

**Corrected forward plot (Chapter 6).** The Call of the Bones World Quest is a long-running, multi-piece hunt (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`) with no near-term completion, so PK_Wraith's pivot to Bloodhurst is **not** gated behind the old "rally event stops recurring" framing, that note is superseded. Instead: consistent with the already-locked fact that Bloodhurst has survived repeated PK raids hunting it for easy farm kills (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), his crew (see `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md`, Rian, Spellweaver_Lyn, Talo, and Joss, now reconfirmed as appearing) hits the camp's eastern patrol line directly in Chapter 6, killing a couple of rank-and-file Bloodhurst goblins in the opening clash. The raid's fuller outcome, including whether/how PK_Wraith's own people end up dying in the clash, doesn't resolve on the page in Chapter 6, Tatsuya is forced to log off before he can personally respond.

**Chapter 7:** the raid gets a real, negotiated window rather than an instant resolution, Sevish grants Tatsuya roughly four to five hours before the camp risks being overrun, and Tatsuya logs off mid-crisis, outcome still unresolved on the page.

**Confirmed forward plot, deferred to Chapter 8, not written yet:** the crew got farmed out across multiple defensive waves against Bloodhurst's warband and stayed anyway, believing the camp was a "free farm spot," consistent with their established pattern of bullying easy kills rather than testing themselves against real resistance. If his own crew takes real losses there, that's the origin point of a personal vendetta aimed specifically at Tatsuya, a real antagonist rather than background Loop noise, and a direct in-game foil to Eren's real-world rivalry.
