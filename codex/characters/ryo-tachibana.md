# Ryo Tachibana

**Role:** Former Kansai Black Lotus-era teammate of Eren and Haruto Murayama. Live candidate for one of Haya Storm's two Act Two 5v5 roster slots. Name easy to rename.

## Facts

Quiet, kept his head down around Eren, who picked on him a lot, not the loud, public kind of bullying, more constant, needling put-downs. Very smart, a genuinely sharp player, but plays an off-meta class, **Wildcaller** (summoner/terrain control, see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`), one of the base list's least-picked options, which is part of why Eren never took him seriously. Attends Tochi Academy.

Recognizes something of himself in Tatsuya once their paths actually cross, both underestimated, both quietly good at something nobody else bothered to respect. A strong candidate for one of Haya Storm's two still-open Act Two roster slots (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`'s roster-composition rule, these two need to actually attend Tochi), and helps keep the expanded roster co-ed alongside Arya. Not detailed further, worked out once that stretch is actually being drafted.
