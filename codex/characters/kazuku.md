# Kazuku

**Role:** Owner of Hero's Glory Online, publicly known only as a billionaire bear mascot figure. Real identity hidden.

## Facts

- Business partner of Daichi Kazehaya, and more than that: the two of them shaped HGO's actual concept together in the years before Daichi's health declined, not just a hardware supply deal. Kazuku received the schematics near the end and used them to build the third-generation pods/helmets that run HGO, but the vision he built them around already had Daichi's hand in it. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`.
- Sat on the third-generation release longer than the tech alone would have required. Two reasons layered together, both kept private, consistent with how little Kazuku reveals about anything: finishing something that was never fully his to finish alone, and refusing to let any of it fall into Tabuchi hands while the schematics were still vulnerable.
- Made a promise to Daichi to look after Tatsuya. "Bring your business here" is primarily public marketing, an open invitation for players and businesses to build a livelihood inside HGO (Yuna is cited in-world as a success-story precedent for what's possible), but it also carries a second, hidden meaning aimed at Tatsuya specifically. Tatsuya has no idea yet, and the reader is never told outright either, this stays implied.
- Company: **Nexus Enterprises**. Has a flagship retail presence in Ginza, downtown Tokyo, see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`, distinct from WattsUp (a separate, unrelated commercial EV-charging sponsor, see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`), never conflate the two.
- **Avatar presence:** appears "in person" at physical event venues (e.g. the World Championship in Singapore) via CG-animated projection, grounded by Singapore's real-world reputation for advanced projection/holographic CG technology, which is how his virtual avatar gets a physical stadium presence without ever revealing his true identity.

## Late-game reveal

Late in the story's arc, Tatsuya discovers his father's connection to Kazuku, the bear mascot's true identity, and that HGO itself was something the two of them shaped together, not just hardware Kazuku bought the rights to. See `story/premise.md`.
