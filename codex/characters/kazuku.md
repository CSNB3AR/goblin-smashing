# Kazuku

**Role:** Owner of Hero's Glory Online, publicly known only as a billionaire bear mascot figure. Real identity hidden.

## Facts

- Business partner of Daichi Kazehaya, and more than that. **Kazuku holds the proof about what happened to Daichi** and is reachable only inside HGO. Dad's letter in the manual says only "speak to the bear" and is marked with a bear paw print; Tatsuya connects it through Kazuku's slogan "bring your business here" (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`). Kazuku's opening Hearthfire advertisement frames the continents as favoring certain races. The two of them shaped HGO's actual concept together in the years before Daichi's health declined, not just a hardware supply deal. While the two were building the third-generation pods/helmets that run HGO, and before Daichi was killed, Daichi handed Kazuku some notes; that stake would have paid off Ohema's debt, so Kazuku owes the family, which is another reason Dad's letter says to find the bear. The vision he built the pods around already had Daichi's hand in it. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`.
- Daichi's partner: Daichi, who owned the first-gen pods, passed some of his work on to Kazuku, which is why the third-gen helmets are so far ahead of the second-gen pods (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`).
- Sat on the third-generation release longer than the tech alone would have required. Two reasons layered together, both kept private, consistent with how little Kazuku reveals about anything: finishing something that was never fully his to finish alone, and refusing to let any of it fall into Tabuchi hands while Daichi's notes were still vulnerable.
- Made a promise to Daichi to look after Tatsuya. "Bring your business here" is **legitimate recruitment**, an open invitation for players and businesses to build a livelihood inside HGO that genuinely works because the game really pays (Yuna is cited in-world as a success-story precedent for what's possible). It is also the main hook for anyone stuck in debt or needing a reset, a way out and a fair start, and Hearthfire's territory is "The New World" that many entrepreneurs will chase (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`), but it also carries a second, hidden meaning aimed at Tatsuya specifically. Tatsuya has no idea yet, and the reader is never told outright either, this stays implied.
- Company: **Nexus Enterprises**. Has a flagship retail presence in Ginza, downtown Tokyo, see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`, distinct from WattsUp (a separate, unrelated commercial EV-charging sponsor, see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`), never conflate the two.
- **Avatar presence:** appears "in person" at physical event venues (e.g. the Season Zero finale in Singapore) via CG-animated projection, grounded by Singapore's real-world reputation for advanced projection/holographic CG technology, which is how his virtual avatar gets a physical stadium presence without ever revealing his true identity.

## Late-game reveal

Dad's letter (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`) points Tatsuya at the bear early as the one who holds the truth, but the full picture comes late in the story's arc: his father's real connection to Kazuku, the bear mascot's true identity, and that HGO itself was something the two of them shaped together, not just hardware Kazuku bought the rights to. See `story/premise.md`.
