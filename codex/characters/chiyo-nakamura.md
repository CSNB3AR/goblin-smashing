# Chiyo Nakamura

**Role:** Landlord, guardian figure, the twins' and Tatsuya's real grandmother in every way that matters.

## Appearance

**Visual reference (locked):** long red hair, poised, business-casual look (blazer, slacks), reads younger than her age.

## Facts

- Former volleyball player, mid-50s, retired, national-championship pedigree at Tochi, once helped lead the Japan national team. Came out of retirement specifically to cover Ohema's old instructor role at Tochi when Ohema fell into her coma.
- Took in and formally adopted Ohema after finding her at an orphanage, a refugee child, raised her as her own daughter.
- **Coached Ohema herself, years earlier**, through Tochi's middle and high school volleyball program, back when Chiyo was still actively coaching there. Ohema later followed in her footsteps to become the school's own volleyball instructor, before Chiyo eventually came out of retirement to cover that same role once more, after Ohema's coma. Three points on the same generational line, not a contradiction.
- **Owns and runs a high-end hotel in Shibuya, the Nakamura Grand** (see `codex/locations/nakamura-grand.md`). This, not the Tochi instructor stipend, is her real business and the source of the family's financial backing. Her own family background is yakuza, she grew up knowing that world as its princess without ever being personally in it, which is where her underworld ties and restraint both come from, she understands exactly what the Tabuchis are and isn't afraid of them, she's choosing restraint to protect other people, not because she has no leverage.
- She shouldn't have to work the hotel counter herself. She's cut staff and taken shifts personally to free up money for the kids and Ohema's bills, and would never once call it a sacrifice, Tatsuya, Momo, and Taji are her real grandkids, Ohema is her real daughter, and there's no version of her that complains about that.
- **Why she is not paying the Tabuchis.** Her money has gone into bills and Ohema's care (all Haya income stopped when every Haya product, the EB included, was pulled from the market), and she has her own money and lawyers. She does not know the details of what was done to Daichi's car, but as a yakuza daughter she wants the Tabuchis to pay for what they did to her family, in her own right. Her refusal is personal as well as practical. Never confirmed on the page.
- **The Tabuchis have threatened arson against the hotel.** Wildly disproportionate, real arson, something they'd almost certainly never get away with, but a genuine threat against her entire livelihood all the same, and exactly the kind of unhinged that makes the ongoing pressure feel dangerous instead of abstract.
- Covers the family's rent and hospital bills. She began paying the Tabuchi fees when she came back into the picture, which the Tabuchis did not plan for. **She does not want to pay them, has her own money, and has hired lawyers.** Her texts to Tatsuya say they called again and that she is handling it, so he should not worry if something turns up at the hotel. She keeps it light so he can focus on his own decision.
- **The family's apartment sits above/attached to the hotel building itself**, ground floor is the hotel, upstairs is home, consistent with her being their landlord.
- Her connection to Tochi (instructor position + Ohema's volleyball legacy + Daichi's tech legacy) is Tatsuya's real path back into the school.
- **She is their grandmother, plainly.** Ohema calls Chiyo "mom" outright, no qualifier. Treat this as settled family language, not a delicate distinction the text needs to keep explaining.

## Future-chapter note (not for Chapter 1)

She's prepared to make the Tochi decision for Tatsuya herself if he stalls much longer, framed explicitly as love and protection, not control, "you're my grandkid, I'm not going to let you or your siblings get hurt while you're stuck." She'll insist on carrying the adult weight herself (money, the Tabuchis) so he can actually focus on the decision in front of him. Not indifference at any point, a real ultimatum from someone who loves him.

## Personality

The warm but iron-fisted matriarch. Looks like a sweet grandma until the old coach voice comes out and silences a room instantly. Runs the household with loving authority, cooks high-protein "athlete" meals, treats the game exactly like a physical sport, correcting posture, lecturing on recovery, forcing proper breaks. The ultimate peacekeeper when the twins clash, a soft, deeply protective bond with Tatsu, a safe place for him to land when the pressure gets too heavy.

## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. OPEN: the earlier wording that Tochi is "connected to all politics" is superseded; OPEN: "his dad finally got him in" versus merit only; OPEN: Ohema "former player" versus "national team coach"; OPEN: first-year versus "maybe second year".
- **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. Eren walked by, saw it, and the friendship grew over a few months. Eren did not know Tatsuya was Daichi's son; Tatsuya did not know his dad knew Eren's dad. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.
- **The loan.** Daichi sold everything and his business money and royalties went to his wife's hospital bills. As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.
