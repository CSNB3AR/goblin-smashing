# Dosuke AI

**Role:** Top HGO pro player, Taji's favorite.

## Facts

His build resembles the original concept Tatsuya and Eren theorized together back at Tochi, which Tatsuya notices and says nothing about. His guild group, with **Renji Fuma** and **Shun Ibaraki** (names easy to rename), was cut on Saturday of the Season Zero finale week, one step short of the Sunday final. He is famous for a role award from the season. He keeps both of them in mind for his own roster once Season One opens, paralleling Haya Storm's own growth, not detailed further now.

**Forward-plot, not on the page in Chapters 1 to 10 (the Chapter 8 Loop mention was cut in the first-person pass), lands at Crownfall:** speculative Loop chatter ties him to picking up Haruto Murayama as a free agent once Haruto leaves Kansai Black Lotus (see `codex/subplots/haruto-exit-from-kansai-black-lotus.md`), a plausible move given how closely his own build already echoes the Tatsuya/Eren original. Confirmed as actually happening by the time Crownfall's bracket runs (see `codex/pro-scene/crownfall-siege-event.md`): Dosuke AI and Haruto team up, with **Souma Ikeda** as a third core player, and this is the exact guild that eliminates Tatsuya's side (Haya Storm) in the semifinals, a real, formidable roster, not a throwaway obstacle.

## Personality

Quiet, soft-spoken, completely in control. Unmatched awareness and micro-management, sees plays five steps ahead. A patient, strict mentor who builds detailed training routines for chaotic or lower-skilled players. Values discipline and strategy over flashy plays, will instantly bench anyone who strays from the plan. Beneath the calm, serious exterior, deeply protective of his team, quiet, intense pride when they execute perfectly.
