# Dosuke AI

**Role:** Top HGO pro player, Taji's favorite.

## Facts

His build resembles the original concept Tatsuya and Eren theorized together back at Tochi, which Tatsuya notices and says nothing about. His team, **Renji Fuma** and **Shun Ibaraki** (names easy to rename), was knocked out at the quarterfinal stage of this cycle's World Championship qualifiers. He keeps both of them in mind for his own eventual 5v5 roster expansion once the Crystal League format shift lands, paralleling Haya Storm's own growth, not detailed further now.

**Forward-plot, seeded Chapter 8, lands at Winter Bash:** speculative Loop chatter ties him to picking up Haruto Murayama as a free agent once Haruto leaves Kansai Black Lotus (see `codex/subplots/haruto-exit-from-kansai-black-lotus.md`), a plausible move given how closely his own build already echoes the Tatsuya/Eren original. Confirmed as actually happening by the time Winter Bash's bracket runs (see `codex/pro-scene/winter-bash-tournament.md`): Dosuke AI and Haruto team up, with a third player, **Souma Ikeda**, filling the roster's remaining 3v3 slot, and this is the exact team that eliminates Tatsuya's squad (Haya Storm) in the semifinals, a real, formidable roster, not a throwaway obstacle.

## Personality

Quiet, soft-spoken, completely in control. Unmatched awareness and micro-management, sees plays five steps ahead. A patient, strict mentor who builds detailed training routines for chaotic or lower-skilled players. Values discipline and strategy over flashy plays, will instantly bench anyone who strays from the plan. Beneath the calm, serious exterior, deeply protective of his team, quiet, intense pride when they execute perfectly.
