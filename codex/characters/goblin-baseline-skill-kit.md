# Goblin Baseline Skill Kit (levels 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 80)

**Status:** design proposal, codex only. It sets what a Thrash line goblin player normally has at six checkpoints, so later chapters, companions and NPC goblins stay consistent. Tatsuya is the glitch case (Shiv Goblin, Arcane Bomber) and is not covered here.

**Tags:** [CANON] already in the repo (file named). [PROPOSAL] invented here, easy to rename. Companions: `codex/lore/dnd-class-mapping.md` (bands, tiers, rarity), `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md` and the five Way files, `codex/races/goblin.md`.

Every entry is exactly one of: Physical Art, spell, passive, racial ability (racial skills included), mastery, item.

---

## 1. Canon the kit stands on

- Goblin base line Courage 4, Power 4, Wisdom 2, Speed 7, Fortune 9, Energy 4; no resistances; magic restricted, not locked (Wisdom 18 gate). [CANON] `codex/races/goblin.md`
- Level 1 shows HP 50, SP 25, MP 25, Goblin Gas, Goblin Flare and a Newcomer Mystery Box. [CANON] `chapters/chapter-04.md`, `chapters/chapter-05.md`
- **Goblin Gas** (racial skill, radius): noxious cloud, poison damage over time stacking to 4x, reduces visibility, can be ignited for more effect, chance to stun; the user is not immune. SP 10, cooldown 30 s. [CANON] `chapters/chapter-05.md`
- **Goblin Flare** (racial skill, emergency signal): a bright flare visible to allies on the mini map within about a mile, damage 2. SP 5, cooldown 30 s. [CANON]
- **Scavenger's Eye** (racial): passively flags which nearby loot is worth picking up; every goblin has it; it does not identify node quality. **Nimble Hands** (racial): pickpocket through doors and barriers, plus a proficiency with arcane tech and gadgets; carries karma and Affiliation costs on NPCs and players; no player pickpocketing in green zones. [CANON] `codex/races/goblin.md`, `codex/materials/blacksmithing-and-farm-resources-book-one.md`
- **Toxic Breath**: Goblin Gas evolves into it at the Hobgoblin rank up. Ru (level 1 to 5, Shiv's blessing) and Zinka (Hobgoblin, level 7) carry it; Wyx (L25) and Sevish (L80) have it listed and never use it. [CANON] `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`, `codex/characters/pk-wraith-raiding-party.md`
- A goblin becomes a Hobgoblin only by leveling steadily through combat without dying. [CANON] `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`
- Only players start with both Gas and Flare; NPC goblins may have one or both and none has combined them. [CANON] `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`
- HP is 50 plus 3 per level; SP and MP were 25 at levels 1 and 3, 32 at level 8; stats get 2 points per level, cap 75. [CANON] `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`
- Three Tier 1 arts per Way is the standard starting kit; Tier 4 and up are rare. Support Tree opens at level 10. Codex Entries come two to four per arc. [CANON]

## 4. Consistency checks

- Level 1 holds exactly two skills on the panel (Gas and Flare); racial passives sit on the race entry. [CANON ch. 5]
- Toxic Breath appears only at Hobgoblin evolution (L5 or later), never on a Goblin; Ru and Zinka fit; Wyx and Sevish hold it unused.
- Gas costs 40 percent of the level 1 SP pool, so a goblin fires it once, then waits 30 s; that is the intended feel.
- No spell, no Tier 7 art and no World rarity appears at any checkpoint.
- Hobgoblin versus Goblin forks at level 5 only; both reach Way Tier 6 by 80.
