# Goblin Baseline Skill Kit (levels 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 80)

**Status:** design proposal, codex only. Nothing here is on the page. It sets what a Thrash-line goblin player normally has at six checkpoints, so later chapters, companions and NPC goblins stay consistent. Tatsuya is the glitch case and the Shiv Goblin branch, and sits in section 8.

**Tags:** [CANON] already in the repo (file named). [SRC] a D&D, Pathfinder or other game source (URL given). [PROPOSAL] invented here. Companion files: `codex/lore/dnd-racial-abilities-mapping.md` (the why behind each racial), `codex/lore/dnd-class-mapping.md` (bands, tiers, rarity, level ladder), `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md` and the five Way files.

## 1. How to read the panels

The page shows a skill as bold bracket lines: a name line with the kind (and Way and tier where there is one), then one effect line. Player panels also show `[STAMINA COST: n]` and `[COOLDOWN: n SECONDS]` (see Goblin Gas in Chapter 5). NPC panels show names, Way, tier and one effect line, no numbers (`codex/characters/thrash-siblings-ability-lists.md`). So in this file:

- The blockquote is the panel text, ready to lift.
- The italic line under it is **codex only**: cost, cooldown, band codes (C and D, defined in `codex/lore/dnd-class-mapping.md` section 2.2) and rarity.
- Every entry is exactly one of the six categories: Physical Art, spell, passive, racial ability (including racial skills), mastery, item.

**Canon the whole kit stands on**
- Goblin base line is Courage 4, Power 4, Wisdom 2, Speed 7, Fortune 9, Energy 4, the weakest line in the game. Resistances are flat zero. Magic is restricted, not locked. [CANON] `codex/races/goblin.md`
- Level 1 Tatsuya shows HP 50, SP 25, MP 25, two skills (Goblin Gas, Goblin Flare) and a Newcomer Mystery Box. [CANON] `chapters/chapter-04.md`, `chapters/chapter-05.md`
- HP is 50 plus 3 per level. SP and MP were 25 at levels 1 and 3 and 32 at level 8. Stats get 2 points per level and cap at 75. Goblins grow Fortune faster. [CANON] `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`, `codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`
- A goblin becomes a Hobgoblin "only by leveling steadily through combat without dying". Ru went Goblin to Hobgoblin and level 1 to 5 by Shiv's one-time blessing and gained Toxic Breath. Zinka is a Hobgoblin at level 7. Wyx is Level 25, Sevish Level 80. [CANON] `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`, `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`, `codex/characters/pk-wraith-raiding-party.md`
- "Only players start with both Goblin Gas and Goblin Flare." NPC goblins may have gas, flare or both, and none has used the combination. [CANON] `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`
- Codex Entries arrive "two to four per major arc, never as an upfront catalog dump". So the kit is lean on purpose. [CANON] `codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`
- Physical Arts: three Tier 1 techniques per Way as the standard starting kit; Tier 4 and up are rare. The teaching proposal puts Tier 1 to 3 with instructors and Tier 4 and up with masters under gates. [CANON] overview; [PROPOSAL] `outline/research/martial-teaching-and-mastery-proposal.md`
- Support Tree opens at level 10 (utility supplements like Tinker, Field Medic, Scout; or one Post). [CANON] `codex/lore/merchant-license-auction-and-jobs.md`

## 2. The ladder at a glance [PROPOSAL, pools extrapolated from canon data points]

HP is 50 plus 3 per level. SP is 25 plus about 1.4 per level above 3 (25 at L1 and L3, 32 at L8). MP stays tiny without a bond or Wisdom: a goblin's mana pool is very small [CANON] `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`.

| Level | Form | HP | SP | New entries (kind) | Gate or source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Goblin, Small, about four feet | 50 | 25 | Goblin Gas, Goblin Flare (racial skills). Scavenger's Eye, Nimble Hands (racials). Meditation (common baseline). Newcomer Mystery Box (item). If a role was picked: the Way's three Tier 1 arts. | Race and creation |
| **5** | **Hobgoblin** (offered), or stay Goblin | 62 | 28 | Hobgoblin: Toxic Breath (Gas evolves), Drill Training (racial), Dusk Eyes (racial). Goblin who declines: Gutter Step (racial), Dusk Eyes. Both: first Way Tier 2 art. | Evolution offer: clean record, canon anchor Ru 1 to 5 and Zinka at 7 |
| **10** | Support Tree opens; advanced class slot I | 77 | 35 | Way Tier 3 chain. Goblin Rally (passive). Hobgoblin: Line Advantage, Rank Resolve. Small goblin: Underdog's Bite. Support Tree pick (Tinker for the belt, Scout, Field Medic). | Level 10 gate; instructor for the Tier 3 art |
| **25** | Veteran. Advanced class slot II | 122 | 56 | First Rare art taught by a master: Upheaval or the Way's Tier 4. Warband Rally (Rally grows). | Master, trust, proof (teaching proposal) |
| **50** | Elite. Advanced class slot III | 197 | 91 | Way Tier 5 art. Hold the Line (passive). Toxic Breath at high Mastery. | Master; Mastery near 75 percent in the Way |
| **80** | Warband leader. Advanced class slot IV | 287 | 133 | Way Tier 6 signature (Tier 7 held back). Clan technique if the clan teaches it. | Master and clan standing |

The numbers for L10 and above are projections. Arya at L10 shows HP 88 and SP 44, so race and stats move them. The ladder is a guide, not a rule.
