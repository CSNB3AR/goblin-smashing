# Tatsuya Kazehaya

**Role:** Protagonist / POV character (locked third-person POV, always)
**Age:** 18
**In-game username:** TDRAG
**In-game character:** Goblin (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md` for how)

## Appearance

Ghanaian and Japanese (father Japanese, mother Ghanaian). Dark brown skin, black hair in wild locs, scar over right brow from a childhood car accident. 188cm (6'2"), lean, tall build. **The height is from Ohema** (a former volleyball player, see `codex/characters/ohema-kazehaya.md`), not Daichi, who was the shorter of his two parents. His technical aptitude and analytical mind trace to Daichi instead.

## Personality

Current: hype, competitive, smartass, deep game IQ, deflects pain through humor and calculation. Built off Marcus Carter ("Miracle") and Scump energy, hype but always respectful of real skill, understands mechanics deeply. Thinks in numbers and odds. Doesn't monologue pain, deflects, calculates, goes quiet, goes still when the math is bad.

**Character arc note (important):** He was NOT always like this. As a kid, and going into Tochi Academy, he was shy, isolated, had no friends. The loud, hype, deflecting personality is armor built after his father's death and the Eren betrayal, a transformation, not a static trait. This should inform flashback material and any scenes showing his younger self.

Was one of roughly 200 people in Japan who won sweepstakes access to HGO's pre-launch beta (not money, not connections, a QR code scan that pulled his name).

## Physical/behavioral signature (for consistency across chapters)

- Watches exits before people.
- Goes still (not reactive) when the math is bad.
- Hands flat on the nearest surface when working through something.
- When scared, goes practical fast, stops feeling, starts listing.
- One breath through the nose when something lands funny.
- Does not smile where people can see it.

## Employment

Works the register at WattsUp (see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`), Gato's charge station, roughly a year now, since dropping out of Tochi. Does not quit this job in the current outlined stretch of the story, it stays an ongoing, working-class anchor.

## Geographic grounding

Essentially never travels outside Shibuya ward. His day-to-day world (WattsUp, the Nakamura Grand, Tochi Academy, Sakura Arcade) sits within it, see `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`. This keeps any real-world search or errand (like the cafe/pod search in Chapter 2) naturally hyperlocal rather than a citywide odyssey, and makes even a trip to the far side of the ward (Sakura Arcade reads "cut off," "the other side of town" relative to WattsUp and home) feel like a real departure for him. **One clear exception:** the Nexus Enterprises flagship store in Ginza (see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`), downtown, a genuine departure from his usual radius, he's been down there maybe twice in his life, both as a kid on some errand of Chiyo's.
