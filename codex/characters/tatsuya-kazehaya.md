## Appearance

Ghanaian and Japanese (father Japanese, mother Ghanaian). Dark brown skin, black hair in wild locs, scar over right brow from a childhood car accident about eleven years ago, the same crash that paralyzed his father (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`). 188cm (6'2"), lean, tall build. **The height is from Ohema** (a former volleyball player, see `codex/characters/ohema-kazehaya.md`), not Daichi, who was the shorter of his two parents. His technical aptitude and analytical mind trace to Daichi instead.

**Goblin form, visual reference locked (from Chapter 3):** green skin, sharp angular features, yellow eyes, pointed ears, clawed fingers, a mouth of small sharp teeth. Messy, tousled sandy/light-brown hair, swept back, a real departure from his real-world locs. Much shorter than his real height, per the established goblin body-adjustment beats in Chapter 3. Book-cover art also shows him in a ragged brown cloak with a "NOOB" branded VR-style headband, that headband is stylized cover framing, not an in-story item, his actual Chapter 3 attire is the plain patched rags described on the page.

## Personality

Current: shut down, bitter, practical, a smartass when cornered, deflects pain through humor and calculation. He **avoids HGO**. It is the game he and Eren theorized together before Eren took the credit, so every broadcast, jacket and clip is a sore spot. He is not a fan, does not follow guilds, and did not plan a build for years. He still absorbed some knowledge and resents that it surfaces automatically. Money (Ohema's deadline, the debt), the Crownfall prize pool and a cheap drive are what pull him in, not the game. Warmth toward the world only starts after the world quest and Shiv, and it is about people and the world, never rankings. Thinks in numbers and odds. Doesn't monologue pain, deflects, calculates, goes quiet, goes still when the math is bad.

**Character arc note (important):** He was NOT always like this. As a kid, and going into Tochi Academy, he was shy, isolated, had no friends. The deflecting, smartass, shut-down personality is armor built after his father's death and the Eren betrayal, a transformation, not a static trait. This should inform flashback material and any scenes showing his younger self.

## Why HGO

**Why he stayed away.** HGO is the thing he and Eren theorycrafted together. The WattsUp Corporation (the company behind the charge stations, not Gato) sponsors HGO the way other small companies buy exposure on a big closed-beta game with high stock, which is why the finale is on the store screen. Tatsuya keeps his back to it. Only the ¥10M Crownfall prize, the 72-hour discount and the closed-beta moment move him.

**He does not decide until the letter.** Kazuku's ad, the discount (drive and rig) and the ¥10M Crownfall prize are not enough on their own. At the register he shrugs HGO off. The decision comes only after the Haya EB fails on the way home, he goes to Dad's manual to repair it, and Dad's letter falls out (text in `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`).

**Money first.** Tatsuya is stuck in debt and Ohema's six-month deadline is running. HGO, "The New World," is a game that genuinely pays, and the money situation does not go away because Dad now matters more to the plot. He is not the only one who sees it: anyone smart will try to make it big or find a business idea for the game, so he earns his place with connections, products and tech (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`, `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`).

**The letter and the bear.** The letter warns him off giving the Tabuchis the manual and tells him to speak to the bear. Kazuku's slogan "bring your business here" clicks: the bear is Dad's business partner. He thinks of the first-gen pod Dad built, the Tabuchis' pressure now makes sense, and HGO looks like the only option. Only the business partner holds the proof, so he cannot go in doing nothing. Plan: bring one of Dad's inventions into HGO, become a top player in a top guild, reach Crownfall, and earn the bear's attention. Dad's letter also carries a hint of the game and of his disability, a lead, not an answer: if Tatsuya meets the bear, much of what weighs on him will sort itself out, and Dad wishes he could be there when Tatsuya experiences the world he helped create. The letter also hopes Tatsuya can do more with the schematics than Dad did, without pressure. That line pulls Tatsuya toward Tochi, where Daichi learned everything he knew and where parts of the archives still hold his work.

**Third, the pull he cannot name.** HGO was also the world his father spent his last years working toward with Kazuku, a place where a body that could not move freely would not be the ceiling (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`). Some part of Tatsuya is drawn to the PvE and exploration side for that reason, even if he would currently describe it as just liking the grind. It surfaces gradually, never as a stated realization early on. See `codex/characters/kazuku.md` and `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`.

## Build direction (forward-locked from Chapter 10)

His build is **Arcane Bomber**, a unique advanced class: an amplifier that roots, stacks debuffs (green poison skulls, 4 stacks then a burst), ignites (fire orange, then blue-white) and finishes. Weapons are crafted, burnable wands, a reusable rune staff, conjured arcane daggers and a short sword on the gadget belt. Root is a gadget-belt snare first, Chain Root (arcane blue chains, about 5 seconds) later. Nimble Hands is the goblin racial that supports it. The full design is in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, and the belt's upgrade path is in `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`. Fire also has a practical crafting function (he needs fire to weld and smelt), so the flame affinity connects combat and profession. The earlier **Clone / Duplicate spell idea is dropped**: any duplicate trick is the doppelganger ring, a battle item.

## Geographic grounding

Essentially never travels outside Shibuya ward. His day-to-day world (WattsUp, the Nakamura Grand, Tochi Academy, Sakura Arcade) sits within it, see `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`. This keeps any real-world search or errand (like the cafe/pod search in Chapter 2) naturally hyperlocal rather than a citywide odyssey, and makes even a trip to the far side of the ward (Sakura Arcade reads "cut off," "the other side of town" relative to WattsUp and home) feel like a real departure for him. **One clear exception:** the Nexus Enterprises flagship store in Ginza (see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`), downtown, a genuine departure from his usual radius, he's been down there maybe twice in his life, both as a kid on some errand of Chiyo's.

- **Voice (author-stated).** Hype, chaotic good, dry humor, real emotion, never monotone. He keeps his composure but stands up for himself and is not a punching bag. When he feels empowered he turns cocky and entitled, a jock-type jerk.

- **Why Tatsuya left Tochi (author-stated, replaces the earlier version).** Eren got close to Tatsuya because Tatsuya was already reading up on HGO and had interest at school. Once Eren saw Tatsuya's value he made his offer: he wanted Tatsuya's brain for the game, which Tatsuya could not access yet. When Tatsuya could not, Eren flipped, used the work on his father's money (his father wanted heavy investment in the game) and became a famous casual bully. Tatsuya felt mocked seeing his own work out there. On top of that his dad had died in the crash and the family money was pulled when the company went under, and his mom was in the hospital, so he felt obligated to work and help Chiyo, not understanding that Chiyo has money and wants him to focus on school (that is why Tochi keeps coming up). Instead of staying bitter he felt he could use his time better, took the WattsUp job and dropped out. About eight to nine months, a full school year; Eren and Arya are second-years and he is still a first-year. Shown in pieces, never dumped.

## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. OPEN: the earlier wording that Tochi is "connected to all politics" is superseded; OPEN: "his dad finally got him in" versus merit only; OPEN: Ohema "former player" versus "national team coach"; OPEN: first-year versus "maybe second year".
- **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. Eren walked by, saw it, and the friendship grew over a few months. Eren did not know Tatsuya was Daichi's son; Tatsuya did not know his dad knew Eren's dad. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.
- **The loan.** Daichi sold everything and his business money and royalties went to his wife's hospital bills. As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.
- **OPEN.** The exact order of Daichi's death versus the loan and the betrayal.
- **Why he got into HGO (author-stated).** His dad was already dead when HGO released. Tatsuya got into HGO because it made him feel connected to his dad, who loved games, not to win. He watched the trailers in the Tochi courtyard for that reason, and later he hates the game until he gets into it himself.
- **Humor and street smarts (author-stated, revised).** Early Bernie Mac flavor: funny, loyal, family-oriented, street smart, honest. He says what is on his mind out loud, even when it hurts someone's feelings, and some of his humor is spoken, not only in his head. It is an occasional drip, never sarcasm in every sentence, and he never turns it on people who did nothing to him. He is hurt about Eren but never butthurt or whiny.
