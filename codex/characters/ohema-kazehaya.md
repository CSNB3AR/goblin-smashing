# Ohema Kazehaya

**Role:** Tatsuya's mother. Currently comatose, offscreen throughout the current outlined stretch, but a constant motivating presence.

## Facts

- Ghanaian. Came to Japan as a refugee child and was taken in and raised by Chiyo Nakamura.
- Former all-star volleyball player at Tochi Academy, later became a volleyball instructor there. Tall, the taller of Tatsuya's two parents, his height is hers.
- Currently in a coma, and it's not stable, it's at a real threshold. Without surgery soon, the prognosis is terminal. Chiyo covers her hospital bills, but the surgery itself is a harder number to reach. **Sharpened, Chapter 4:** the real deadline is roughly six months, without meaningful progress inside that window, the concern isn't only whether her body survives, it's whether there's still a mind left to come back to. Chiyo knows this and has asked the hospital staff to keep it quiet, Tatsuya overhears it secondhand rather than being told directly, which is the real source of his anger, not the news itself.
- **This is Tatsuya's real, concrete driving motivation**, not an abstract desire to prove himself or get back at Eren. Any moment where money (prize pools, discounts, a shot at HGO) lands in front of him should tie back to her specifically.
- Calls Chiyo "mom" outright, no qualifier, Chiyo raised her as her own daughter.
