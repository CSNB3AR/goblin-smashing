# Ohema Kazehaya

**Role:** Tatsuya's mother. Currently comatose, offscreen throughout the current outlined stretch, but a constant motivating presence.

## Facts

- Ghanaian. Came to Japan as a refugee child, found at an orphanage, and formally adopted and raised by Chiyo Nakamura (see `codex/characters/chiyo-nakamura.md`).
- Former all-star volleyball player at Tochi Academy, later became a volleyball instructor there. Tall, the taller of Tatsuya's two parents, his height is hers.
- Currently in a coma, and it's not stable, it's at a real threshold. Without surgery soon, the prognosis is terminal. Chiyo covers her hospital bills, but the surgery itself is a harder number to reach. **Sharpened, Chapter 5:** the real deadline is roughly six months, without meaningful progress inside that window, the concern isn't only whether her body survives, it's whether there's still a mind left to come back to. Chiyo knows this and has asked the hospital staff to keep it quiet, Tatsuya overhears it secondhand rather than being told directly, which is the real source of his anger, not the news itself.
- **This is Tatsuya's real, concrete driving motivation**, not an abstract desire to prove himself or get back at Eren. Any moment where money (prize pools, discounts, a shot at HGO) lands in front of him should tie back to her specifically.
- Calls Chiyo "mom" outright, no qualifier, Chiyo raised her as her own daughter.
- Tatsuya and his siblings, Momo and Taji, are her three children, all sharing the same two parents, Ohema and Daichi.

## End of Act One: the surgery gets scheduled, not performed

Sponsorship, placement, and loot-drop income lands by the end of Act One, enough to clear the family's outstanding hospital debt and secure Ohema's surgery financially for real, not a partial payment. **The surgery itself is scheduled here, not performed.** The actual procedure lands toward the end of Act Two. This is deliberate: the financial half of the six-month clock is genuinely resolved, real relief, real proof Tatsuya's accomplished something concrete, but the other half, whether her mind recovers at all, stays a live, unresolved stake carrying into Act Two.

## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. - **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. Eren walked by, saw it, and the friendship grew over a few months. Eren did not know Tatsuya was Daichi's son; Tatsuya did not know his dad knew Eren's dad. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.
- **The loan.** Daichi sold everything and his business money and royalties went to his wife's hospital bills. As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.

- **The Haya EB (author-stated).** It is black, clean, high tech and the one thing Tatsuya keeps in shape. A touchscreen locked to the owner's fingerprint (any owner can register a print), which is why it takes a moment to load: it senses the rider. The helmet is a smart visor that shows routes, like smart glasses, because Daichi was super techy. About twenty were sold before the accident got it pulled. It is his only transport to work and everywhere; WattsUp charges it free, one less expense. Daichi had three major products (the VR pod, the sedan, the EB) and a fourth, partnered project that Tatsuya does not know about. Ohema is in a coma.

- **Illness and timeline (author-stated).** Rare lung cancer. She went into cardiac arrest. The first surgery (a transplant) failed because the donor's blood type did not match, after the family had sold everything counting on it. She was brought back and is in a coma. There is no money for the next surgery. About six months before her brain deteriorates and her memory is lost, leaving her alive but unable to move. The bill is not raised on the page until the hospital visit. Medical terms to be refined later.
