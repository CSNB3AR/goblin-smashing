# Eren Tabuchi

**Role:** Antagonist. Tatsuya's former close friend, now estranged.

## Appearance

**Visual reference (locked):** blonde hair, blue eyes, tan/khaki jacket over a white shirt, easy and entitled bearing.

## Facts

- Son of the Tabuchi family, who back/manufacture the second-generation pods (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`).
- Physical signature: easy posture, entitlement where Tatsuya has calculation, voice drops into a lower register when he's trying to sound threatening.
- Eren now collects on the Tabuchi family debt as leverage over Tatsuya. He is **Kazuto's messenger and blind to the schematics**: he knows his father wants something from the Kazehaya family, not what or why. He also knows nothing about the sabotage of Daichi's car or his father's court plan (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`).
- His guild, Kansai Black Lotus, missed the Season Zero top eight because Arya's small guild took the last qualifying spot (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). **He hates her for it.** He is a second-year (see `codex/lore/timeline-and-beta.md`).

## Origin of the friendship and the betrayal

Eren noticed Tatsuya sitting alone in the Tochi cafeteria reading an HGO magazine, that's how they connected. Simple, organic, not dramatic. They became genuinely close for a while, bonding over HGO before launch and theorizing character builds together on paper, continuing from that first meeting at 15 into Tatsuya's first year of high school.

When launch approached, right at the start of that first month of high school, Eren, with family money and access through the Tabuchi second-gen pod business, got hardware access before Tatsuya could afford any device at all, and the betrayal below had already landed by the time Daichi died and the funeral happened, days or weeks later, in that same brutal first month.

Eren took the build ideas they had developed together and implemented them on his own account without telling Tatsuya, then cut him off completely. He announced he already had an established, developed account, isolated himself from Tatsuya, and became openly dismissive and hostile. Tatsuya never publicly called it out. He absorbed it.

The build dominated the meta, built Eren's reputation, and got Tochi to back a school guild team (funded by Tabuchi money).

**Aftermath at school:** once Eren's build got famous, Tatsuya became a target for mockery specifically because everyone knew he'd been involved in the underlying ideas but was too broke to actually play, a cruel, specific irony. This led to real altercations (Tatsuya was no longer the shy kid by this point), and combined with his father's death, led to him dropping out entirely within that first three-month stretch, giving him an undeserved "bad boy" reputation.

See `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`.
