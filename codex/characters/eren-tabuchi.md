**Visual reference (locked):** blonde hair, blue eyes, tan/khaki jacket over a white shirt, easy and entitled bearing.

## Facts

- Son of the Tabuchi family, who back/manufacture the second-generation pods (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`).
- Physical signature: easy posture, entitlement where Tatsuya has calculation, voice drops into a lower register when he's trying to sound threatening.

## Origin of the friendship and the betrayal

Eren noticed Tatsuya sitting alone in the Tochi cafeteria reading an HGO magazine, that's how they connected. Simple, organic, not dramatic. They became genuinely close for a while, bonding over HGO before launch and theorizing character builds together on paper, continuing from that first meeting at 15 into Tatsuya's first year of high school.

When launch approached, right at the start of that first month of high school, Eren, with family money and access through the Tabuchi second-gen pod business, got hardware access before Tatsuya could afford any device at all, and the betrayal below had already landed by the time Daichi died and the funeral happened, days or weeks later, in that same brutal first month.

Eren took the build ideas they had developed together and implemented them on his own account without telling Tatsuya, then cut him off completely. He announced he already had an established, developed account, isolated himself from Tatsuya, and became openly dismissive and hostile. Tatsuya never publicly called it out. He absorbed it.

The build dominated the meta, built Eren's reputation, and got Tochi to back a school guild team (funded by Tabuchi money).

**Aftermath at school:** once Eren's build got famous, Tatsuya became a target for mockery specifically because everyone knew he'd been involved in the underlying ideas but was too broke to actually play, a cruel, specific irony. This led to real altercations (Tatsuya was no longer the shy kid by this point), and combined with his father's death, led to him dropping out entirely within that first three-month stretch, giving him an undeserved "bad boy" reputation.

See `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`.

## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. - **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.

As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.
