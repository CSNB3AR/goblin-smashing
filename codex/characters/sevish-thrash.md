# Sevish Thrash

**Role:** Goblin chieftain, leads the Thrash clan out of Bloodhurst (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`). Wyx Thrash's brother. First appears Chapter 4 (camp arc).

## Appearance

Visual reference locked (author-sent image, latest wins over the earlier red-orange description). Broad, heavily muscled and much bigger than a standard goblin, with the massive presence of a chieftain. Muted green skin, a short black beard and mustache, long black windblown hair in two thick braids over his chest tied off with red ribbon bows, amber eyes under a heavy scowl, a pointed ear with ring earrings. A burgundy vest hanging open over a white shirt with the collar open and one sleeve rolled, a dark sash and brown belt with a large brass buckle, a small tooth or horn pendant on a cord, and swirling dark markings up his forearm. Pirate-captain bearing, battle-worn. Carries a two-handed battle axe. Hobgoblin, so he is human in height and proportion (as tall as a grown man, a head over the camp enforcers), with the swirling dark markings running up one bare arm to the shoulder. Built like a man who trains every day, thick through the chest and shoulders with corded arms and a narrower waist, and he gives off an aura that can be felt in the air before he speaks.

## Facts

Level 80. Carries a two-handed battle axe (always has). Massive presence, scarred, commands total respect. Gives Kaiseki (Tatsuya's in-game name) his central ultimatum, prove himself within a set window or be treated as an enemy, and later treats the Shiv bond as a genuine honor.

**Structural note:** the "chained up" beat now lands by the end of Chapter 4 itself, after Tatsuya kills two of the clan's goblins in Chapter 3's fight, held as a suspected threat even while being offered the World Quest. See `outline/archive/chapter-03.md` and `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.

**His real, driving goal: reunifying Myrrhwood under Shiv's legacy.** Everything Sevish does, the ultimatum, the library mission, the eventual return home, traces back to this, not stated often in his own voice but the actual throughline underneath his faith in Shiv (see Personality below). The clans across Myrrhwood have fractured and turned on each other since Shiv's disappearance (see `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`), and Sevish genuinely believes, not just as a quest objective, that they can't survive much longer without a unifying figure again. Pays off in `outline/act-three-myrrhwood-expedition.md`.

## Personality

Pure action and personal code. Lives for the physical rush of a fight, treats pain and danger as proof he's alive. Rejects titles, rules, anything bureaucratic, trusts only his own logic and strength. Follows a strict personal honor system, won't kill the helpless, demands fair one-on-one fights, respects anyone strong enough to challenge him. Zero natural interest in long-term plans or politics, the next good fight in front of him is what matters. What actually moves him past that, into the World Quest and everything it costs him, isn't a break from this personality, it's his faith in Shiv and what it means for Thrash and his own family, reinforced by how much weight he gives his younger sister Wyx's own read on things, the sharper strategic mind of the two.

- **Levels (author-stated).** Sevish is not under the troop system, so he cannot level and stays at the level he is held at (80) until he becomes a true recruit and troop of Tatsuya. Only then does he get the troop experience buff and scale. The game developers can update this later, but eventually he and his sister Wyx become troops who can level.
