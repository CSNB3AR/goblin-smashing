# Tatsuya: Weapon and Playstyle

Tags: [CANON] stated, [PROPOSAL] mine. No dashes. Defers to `codex/lore/equipment-and-gear-slots.md` for slots.

## The kit

One-hand mace in the focus hand, a shield strapped to the other forearm. A **short wand** is the allowed swap (a bone wand for a day he wants reach), never a staff (too big and slow for a small goblin who runs) and never a gun (a gun in the casting hand blocks casting, house rule 4). He has 65 HP and low attack power, so the mace is a tool for pushing, tapping and finishing, not a main damage source. Most of his damage rides on someone else.

## The Way [PROPOSAL]

A mace is a crushing weapon, so it sits in **Way of the Axe** (heavy, committed, `codex/combat/way-of-the-axe.md`): Haft Check, Split Strike and Hook Pull transfer cleanly, with Momentum Chain later. The shield work sits in **Way of the Fist** (bash, guard). One Blade art, Slip Step, gives him footing. At the First Crossing (level 10) he chooses which Way leads (tree file, section 11). The martial master rule lets him name his own technique only after real mastery, far off.

## A fight in VR

He is a short goblin in a full-dive body. The shield is cover he can crouch behind, not a wall. He starts a fight at range: Gas on the group, Flare to light it, or a Ward Glyph at his own feet. As enemies close, he plants a Tether Rune and steps behind the shield. A Slowing Thread on the first charger and a Dread Mark on the second give his allies advantage. In melee he blocks, shoves with Shield Bash to open a gap, then taps with the mace, which carries a bone inlay that serves as his focus. When the enemy is slowed and bunched inside the glyph ring, he fires the finisher. When it goes wrong he uses Slip Step and goes small and quick.

## Gear slots [PROPOSAL, deferring to the equipment file]

Main hand mace, off hand shield, chest, belt (Gadget Belt: Gas, Flare, Snare and Smoke pouches), necklace (Call of the Bones), earring (Gambler's Earring), plus feet and one ring when he finds them. Level 8 he has the necklace, earring and chest only.

## How it plays with his abilities

- **Gas and Flare.** Belt pouches, used before melee. Flare is cast from the mace hand's palm and works holding the mace because the mace is his focus.
- **Runes.** Placed while the shield is up. The rune roll stays a gamble: a failed placement leaves him exposed, so he rolls only behind cover or after a slow.
- **Flamonta.** Cast standing at the rune, shield up, one hand free. Under it enemies slow and enemy rolls suffer, so a wall of allies and a small goblin behind a shield becomes a lock.
- **Gambler's Gambit.** Rolls on loot and, upgraded, in combat; any failed roll breaks the item (latest decision), so he rolls it out of danger, not mid block.
- **Ancient Bone Flame Barrage.** The one big cast. Three points, a ring, a pause behind the shield.

## Never

No taunting, no summons, no two-hander, no kiting with a bow. The shield makes him hard to kill, not a tank.
