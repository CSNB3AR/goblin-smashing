# The VRSA Inspector

**Role:** the VRSA inspector who visits Sakura Arcade (see `codex/lore/vrsa.md`). A regulator, not a repair technician. Flat, procedural, deadpan.

## Look (author's picture, `codex/characters/reference/vrsa-inspector.jpg`; author-supplied, latest)

A man in a black suit with a grey shirt and a black tie, long black hair tied low at the neck with side-swept bangs hanging over one eye, thin rectangular glasses, a serious, impassive face. Worn on the page as a black suit (replaces the "branded VRSA polo" in Chapter Eight). Carries a tablet and a stylus and a branded case.

**Flag:** Chapter Ten file ("Part Two") describes him as "tan skin, dark hair, glasses giving off a reflection". The picture shows fair skin, so check that description when that chapter is revised.
