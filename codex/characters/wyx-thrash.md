# Wyx Thrash

**Role:** Goblin chieftain's sister. In-game, first appears Chapter 4 (camp arc).

## Appearance

Visual reference locked (author-sent image, latest wins over the earlier green-hair description). Dark brown skin, long black hair worn loose and high with a curved bone clasp, amber eyes, small tusks at the corners of her mouth, pointed ears with several hoops and hanging bone-tooth earrings. Dark red geometric markings across her forehead, cheek, shoulders, arms, stomach and thigh. A necklace of large fangs and claws, a gray cloth top tied at the front, a spiked bone pauldron and leather wraps on her arms, fur trim, a torn cloak, a belt with a beast skull hanging at her hip and a ragged dark skirt. Reads strong, athletic and sharp, a fighter and not a ranger. Hobgoblin, so she is human in height and proportion, and the red markings sit on her forehead (a diamond glyph), cheek, shoulders, arms, ribs and stomach, and thigh. Strong and full-figured: an athlete's shoulders and defined stomach, a full chest, thick muscled thighs, and a striking face (high cheekbones, straight nose, full lips).

## Facts

Level 25 Hobgoblin. Sharp and quick to challenge, defers to her brother Sevish but runs day-to-day camp discipline. See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md` and `outline/archive/chapter-03.md` for when she enters the story.

## Personality

Natural dignity and quiet authority. A confident, responsible leader who treats the people under her like family, willing to risk herself to protect them. Under the serious exterior, deeply affectionate, romantic, easily teased or jealous about people she cares about. Massive pride in her name and abilities, highly competitive, unwilling to back down. Despises cruelty, judges people by character and effort, not status or species.

**Starts registering real interest in Tatsuya specifically, as a player.** Distinct from how an NPC usually reads (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`, background subplot seed), a competing-interest texture next to the main Yuna/Tatsuya arc, not developed on the page for a long while, becomes more overt during the Act Three Myrrhwood expedition (see `outline/act-three-myrrhwood-expedition.md`). See `codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md` for the light note on how this reads mechanically.

- **Levels (author-stated).** Like her brother Sevish, Wyx is not under the troop system, so she cannot level and stays at her current level until she becomes a true recruit and troop of Tatsuya and gains the troop experience buff.

**Early affiliation (author-stated).** Wyx finds Tatsuya very interesting from the first meeting and develops a very strong emotional feeling toward him. It shows on the page as an early individual affiliation tick (45% to 52% in Chapter 6, then 52% to 56% in Chapter 11), which also shows that affiliation and relationships can grow early and that NPCs develop based on what players do.

**Character model (author-stated).** Wyx is built on Rias Gremory from High School DxD: poised, warm and dignified, a caring leader who protects her people like family, proud and self-assured, composed with a sharper side, deeply affectionate, and a firm believer in Shiv's cause that she will not go against. She calls Sevish "brother", backs the truth with calm authority, and delivers dry, smart remarks without raising her voice. The author is unsure of the exact alignment but reads her as chaotic good.

**The brains (author-stated).** Wyx has always shown unusual intelligence for a hobgoblin and is the voice of reason Sevish trusts, even as a devoted believer in Shiv.

- **Does not use Goblin Gas (author-stated).** She has Toxic Breath but never uses it because she thinks it is not ladylike; she keeps herself presentable. She fights with advanced physical arts and rarely used Thrash clan techniques. She does not understand why Tatsuya's gas-and-flare combination works, she only saw it done.
