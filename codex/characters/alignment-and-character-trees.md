# Alignment and Character Trees

**Status:** PROPOSAL, codex only, nothing here is on the page. Places the cast on the Tree in `codex/lore/alignment-framework.md` and gives each main or recurring character a compact personality block. The blank card is `codex/characters/npc-personality-template.md`. Goblin culture is in `codex/lore/goblin-code-and-clan-alignments.md`.

**Tags:** CANON is in the repo, PAGE is shown in the drafted chapters (Chapters Nine to Eleven are unreviewed drafts), PROPOSAL is invented. Where canon is silent, the placement is PROPOSAL. **Hold** is how firmly someone keeps their cell: Firm, Settled or Wavering. **Ideal** carries a Creed (Order, Freedom, Mercy, Hunger, Balance, Calling). Nobody says a label aloud. Drift applies to gold NPCs only.

## 2. The Thrash camp

### Sevish Thrash (Level 80 Hobgoblin, Berserker)
**Placement:** Chaotic Neutral, good code. Unbound. Firm. PROPOSAL from CANON. Chaotic Good is the warmer alternative.
- **Traits:** Lives for the fight and treats danger as proof he is alive. Laughs short and delighted when someone bold stands up to him, then asks a hard question (CANON, PAGE ch6, ch10).
- **Ideal (Freedom, bound by honor):** a fair fight is the only honest argument, and the strong owe the helpless a clean hand.
- **Bond:** Wyx; his people, whose deaths he counts; Shiv's cause.
- **Flaw:** spends bodies for the cause, and calls it courage ("I am not a coward. We push them" PAGE ch11). No patience for long plans.
- **Line:** harming the helpless; striking a downed foe; abandoning the camp; mocking Shiv.
- **Trust:** frankness to his face; holding your ground; owning a mistake bluntly; fighting beside the clan.
- **Drift:** toward Oathkeeper as he trades pushes for plans.

### Wyx Thrash (Level 25 Hobgoblin, Blade Dancer)
**Placement:** Chaotic Good by the author's read. Freeheart with a lawful lean (the walls rule). Settled. A conscience can choose a code.
- **Traits:** Poised, warm authority and dry remarks without raising her voice. Proud, competitive, easily teased about the people she loves (CANON).
- **Ideal (Mercy, serving a Calling):** protect her people like family and keep Shiv's cause.
- **Bond:** Sevish; the Thrash people; Shiv's creed. Growing personal interest in Tatsuya, as texture only (CANON).
- **Flaw:** pride under the poise. She has asked recruits to die in rallies without counting the cost. Refuses Goblin Gas as "not ladylike."
- **Line:** cruelty to the helpless; using the clan's dead as a bargaining chip.
- **Trust:** judging by character and effort; carrying the names of the dead; a reasoned plan over bravado; dry wit answered in kind.
- **Drift:** her vow hardens as Sevish keeps losing the clan. Her trust falls fast if gambles cost goblins.

### Shiv (spectral goblin sorcerer, formerly Shivaris Jutsuin)
**Placement:** Lawful Good. Oathkeeper. Firm. PROPOSAL, strong CANON. His one Wild act, loving the queen, is the root of his exile.
- **Traits:** Plain and patient; asks before he acts ("I won't take the choice from you" PAGE ch9). Dry and humble about method ("Make yours greater than mine").
- **Ideal (Calling):** goblins are not feral. They can be a people with a table to sit at.
- **Bond:** the goblin clans; the first queen (held for the archive reveal); the Warset.
- **Flaw:** his reach is fading, and he withholds his history so he looks like a riddle to those who need him.
- **Line:** taking away someone's choice.
- **Trust:** an open minded goblin who goes looking; plain speech that echoes his creed.
- **Drift:** none. He is fading.

### Ru (Level 5 Hobgoblin, Disciple of Elder Nykri)
**Placement:** Lawful Good. Oathkeeper. Wavering, growing to Settled. PAGE and PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Watchful; holds the flap and counts his brothers (PAGE ch10). Quiet and exact once he finds his voice; a stutter before the evolution.
- **Ideal (Order, chosen):** a line held by people who trust each other.
- **Bond:** Nykri, the first to stand up for him; the brothers he counts; the Thrash chain of command. Affiliation with Tatsuya 75 percent (CANON).
- **Flaw:** no voice of his own yet and no birth name. Risks too much for others ("Nobody reaches you while I stand here").
- **Line:** abandoning his post or the one who vouched for him.
- **Trust:** being seen and spoken for; clear orders with a reason; reporting unprompted.
- **Drift:** gains a voice. Watch for two orders colliding.

### Zinka (Level 7 Hobgoblin, east line scout)
**Placement:** Lawful Neutral. Warden. Settled. PAGE ch10 and PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Quiet and exact; "she counts what she sees." Sharp with sloppiness and plain about it.
- **Ideal (Order):** a true count is a scout's first duty.
- **Bond:** Wyx, whose runner she is; the east line.
- **Flaw:** no patience for the slow. How she came by Toxic Breath is unexplained.
- **Line:** will not shade a report to please the listener.
- **Trust:** being heard before the order is given; a count checked and found right.
- **Page note:** in the ch11 draft Zinka is absent from the tent gassing and Rott and Nab take her place; the ledger says Zinka and Ru. Unresolved.

### Kesh (Thrash scout)
**Placement:** Lawful Neutral, good leaning. Warden. Settled.
- **Traits:** Runs toward trouble to carry word (PAGE ch9). Honest and bad at sums; counts a tent pole twice.
- **Ideal (Order, simple):** the chief must know.
- **Bond:** Sevish ("chief"); his east line comrades.
- **Flaw:** panics and miscounts under pressure. Speaks of himself by name ("Kesh bring news").
- **Line:** will not leave a report undelivered.
- **Trust:** a short clear order; being believed; a task.

### Rott and Nab, and the Enforcers
**Rott and Nab:** Lawful Neutral, Warden, Settled. Rott is thick and grunting ("Rott go"); Nab is thin and carries the rope unasked. Ideal: do the job you were handed. Flaw: slow to improvise, need the plan said plainly. Line: will not leave their assigned commander. Trust: plain orders, being named.
**Thrash Clan Enforcers** are a role title mob with no cell. Their conduct is Taker in miniature, practiced on the weak where no leader watches. They show that the Thrash code is only as strong as whoever enforces it (PAGE ch4, ch5).

## 3. The player side

### Tatsuya Kazehaya, "Nykri" (Level 8 Shiv Goblin, Arcane Bomber)
**Placement:** Chaotic Good. Freeheart. Settled. CANON read, PAGE.
- **Traits:** Dry, hype humor never turned on those who did nothing to him; says what is on his mind even when it stings. Watches exits before people, goes still when the math is bad, and starts listing when scared (CANON).
- **Ideal (Mercy, with Freedom):** the weak should not be left alone with the strong ("if you don't stick up for yourself now, guys like this never go away" PAGE ch4).
- **Bond:** his father's legacy and his family (Ohema, Chiyo, Taji, Momo); Ru; the goblins now following his name.
- **Flaw:** hides the load (the hardwired pod, the provocation he left out, the real world truth). Turns cocky and entitled when empowered.
- **Line:** he will not be Eren. He will not take credit for another's work or spend people who trust him as a price.
- **Trust:** straight talk; showing up when it costs; keeping people alive.
- **Drift:** toward Unbound if a gambler's Fortune build teaches him people are stakes; toward Oathkeeper as vouches harden into oaths.

### Arya Sakura (Level 11 Elf Spellforger, information broker)
**Placement:** Chaotic Good. Freeheart. Settled, guarded. CANON and PAGE.
- **Traits:** High energy and passionate, tilts at lag then laughs it off. Composed, precise and guarded, with a shooter's calm.
- **Ideal (Freedom, with Mercy):** nobody picks her path for her, and her people are not left behind.
- **Bond:** Leon, her guardian brother; what is left of her guild; the people she carries.
- **Flaw:** gambles everything on one bet; trades in secrets, which some read as treachery; refuses tutorials.
- **Line:** will not abandon or expel one of her own (she never kicked anyone out of her failing guild).
- **Trust:** being told the real stakes; honest numbers; a plan that uses her skill.
- **Drift:** stays Freeheart. Selling secrets for gain could slide her toward Unbound.

### Leon Sakura (owner of Sakura Arcade)
**Placement:** Neutral Good. Hearthkeeper. Settled. CANON and PAGE.
- **Traits:** Deadpan on the surface, sharp underneath; reacts small (an eyebrow, the cigarette taken off the ear and put back). A blunt coach who curses sloppy play then explains it plainly.
- **Ideal (Mercy):** the arcade is a haven, and a kid getting pushed around is his business.
- **Bond:** Arya; the arcade and his grandfather's legacy; a growing partnership with Tatsuya.
- **Flaw:** says he is out and cannot stay out; his secondhand research is sometimes wrong; keeps secrets about the pod and the money.
- **Line:** will not let a player cook in the pod ("I'm going to have to pull you") or let Arya be taken.
- **Trust:** honest numbers; work done right.

### Yuna / Yunaile Kirishima
**Placement:** Neutral Good. Hearthkeeper. Settled. CANON and PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Polite and quick to apologize for small things. Devoted: follows the person she trusts like a shadow, shy about who she is.
- **Ideal (Mercy):** keep her carry alive; support and heal. Treats NPCs as people.
- **Bond:** her brother Kaito; Tatsuya, a slow burn; her fans and NPC friends.
- **Flaw:** a double life behind glasses; apologizes instead of asserting; a family she has not confronted.
- **Line:** will not let Kaito be harmed or her fame be used to hurt someone.
- **Trust:** gentleness; keeping her civilian identity; patience.

### Supporting cast (short)
- **Chiyo Nakamura** (Oathkeeper, Firm): warm and iron fisted. Flaw: carries the weight alone and keeps hard news from Tatsuya. Line: harm to her family; will not pay the Tabuchis.
- **Pac_Pac** (Freeheart, Settled): loud, ride or die. Flaw: everything is a fight. Line: will not abandon his squad.
- **Dosuke AI** (Oathkeeper, Firm): quiet, strict, protective. Flaw: benches anyone who strays from the plan.
- **Haruto Murayama** (Wayfarer, Wavering): silent, stubborn when challenged. Flaw: careless about people. Line: will not look away from a real challenge. Drift toward Hearthkeeper after he exits Kansai Black Lotus.

## 4. Rivals and the hidden hand

### PK_Wraith
**Placement:** Neutral Evil. Taker. Settled, with a Wavering streak of respect. PROPOSAL from CANON.
- **Traits:** Big, loud, well known and weak at real competition. Respects what he watched: four officers died in one clean ambush and he is wary of Nykri even as he hunts him.
- **Ideal (Hunger):** reputation and muscle make the rules; the loudest and best connected freeze out the rest.
- **Bond:** his crew and his name. The grudge against Nykri is his own.
- **Flaw:** swagger over fear; bullies the small and avoids anyone who pushes back. His darker wing, which reaches real world crime, may outgrow him.
- **Line:** none that holds. The nearest is that he will not be seen losing.
- **Trust:** results, loyalty to him, an easy target. **Break:** embarrassment on stream.
- **Drift:** toward Ironhand if his crew hardens into a system.
- **Crew** (Rian, Talo, Spellweaver_Lyn, Joss): players, Neutral Evil by conduct. They farm easy kills, then fight like professionals.

### Eren Tabuchi
**Placement:** Neutral Evil, young. Taker. Wavering. CANON and PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Smug, needling and fake sympathetic. Easy, entitled bearing; his voice drops when he tries to sound threatening.
- **Ideal (Hunger):** credit and rank are owed to those who can afford them.
- **Bond:** his father's approval; his guild's standing; hates Arya for the eighth seed.
- **Flaw:** cannot run the build he took; blind to what his family has done.
- **Line:** none that holds. The nearest is not to be exposed as a fraud.
- **Trust:** flattery, results, family.
- **Drift:** toward Wayfarer if consequences land, deeper Taker if his family's cover keeps him safe.

### Kazuto Tabuchi
Lawful Evil. Ironhand. Firm. Cold, controlling, works through debt, courts and yakuza backed money. Ideal: what the Kazehaya schematics are worth is owed to him. Flaw: impatience turns to threats. Line: none known. Break: Chiyo's lawyers and her name.

### Kazuku (owner of Nexus Enterprises, bear mascot)
**Placement:** Lawful Neutral, good leaning. Warden. Firm. CANON and PROPOSAL. Keep his Hand ambiguous until the late reveal.
- **Traits:** Reveals little about anything. Plays a long game with a kind face.
- **Ideal (Calling):** finish what Daichi began, and shield his son while it is done.
- **Bond:** Daichi and Tatsuya.
- **Flaw:** secrecy, and kindness that steers people without asking.
- **Line:** Tabuchi hands on Daichi's notes.
- **Trust:** Tatsuya earning his way honestly.

## 5. The Crown

### King Aldemar Vaereth
**Placement:** Lawful Evil. Ironhand. Firm. PROPOSAL from CANON. About 97, kept alive by failing elixirs.
- **Traits:** Rules by decree and silence. Afraid of death and doubt.
- **Ideal (Hunger, enforced as Order):** the crown must not pass through doubt, and the past stays buried. He killed Isaveil, said she died ill, and let the court cast Shiv as a curse maker.
- **Bond:** the throne; the queen he killed.
- **Flaw:** doubts his eldest is his son; dependent on Corvith.
- **Line:** none known.
- **Trust:** loyal ministers and silence. **Break:** the truth about Isaveil, kept by the Astralum Archive.

### Prince Dorrin, First Shield of the Crown
**Placement:** Lawful Good. Oathkeeper. Settled. PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Blunt, heavy and hurt. Keeps the duty the king denies him.
- **Ideal (Order):** the shield stands before the realm whether or not the crown looks back.
- **Bond:** the realm; the first queen's line; the guilds that bleed for him.
- **Flaw:** pride in duty; will not claim what he is owed.
- **Line:** will not turn his guilds on the realm's people to win.
- **Trust:** honest service; protecting the weak. **Break:** being used as bait.
- **Open:** whose son he is stays unresolved.

### Prince Ilyan, the Silver Prince
**Placement:** Lawful Neutral. Warden. Wavering. PROPOSAL.
- **Traits:** Agile, dutiful. Runs the army while the king is sick.
- **Ideal (Order):** keep the crown whole and his father alive.
- **Bond:** the king; Corvith; the royal city.
- **Flaw:** trained by the man who "corrected" Shiv, so he inherits that man's blind spots.
- **Line:** will not let the king die by neglect.
- **Trust:** results and proper form. **Drift:** toward Ironhand or Oathkeeper after the king dies.

### Prince Maldren, the Gilded Prince
**Placement:** Lawful Evil. Ironhand. Firm. PROPOSAL from CANON.
- **Traits:** Cunning, patient and cruel; works through proxies.
- **Ideal (Hunger, dressed as Order):** a new order with himself at its head.
- **Bond:** his army and Aogiri's backing.
- **Flaw:** ambition that makes enemies of those he uses.
- **Line:** none known.
- **Trust:** usefulness. **Break:** failure and disloyalty.

### High Magister Corvith Ostrane
Lawful Evil. Ironhand. Firm. A banished High Elf with papal authority who "corrected" Shiv and keeps the king alive on dwindling Azure. Ideal: order and his own indispensability. Flaw: the supply is running out. Line: will not be exposed as a fraud.

## 6. The Thrash clan (group card)

**Placement:** Ethos Oathkeeper, Practice Warden. Settled. CANON and PAGE.
- **Traits:** Fair among its own and slow to look past the camp. Loyal to the dead and to the count of them.
- **Ideal (Order, serving a Calling):** Shiv's law and the reunification of Myrrhwood.
- **Bond:** Shiv, the Warset, the monthly rally, the land they gave up for peace.
- **Flaw:** the Gap. Enforcers bully runts while the camp looks away, and the leaders spend recruits on rallies.
- **Line:** goblins fighting goblins; harming the helpless.
- **Trust:** deeds before a witness; fair fights; carrying the names of the dead; standing your ground.
- **Break:** flattery; boasting of the elder title; wasting goblins; gamer slang.
