# Alignment and Character Trees (placements and personality blocks for the cast)

**Status:** design proposal, codex only, nothing here is on the page. It places the cast and the main factions on the Tree defined in `codex/lore/alignment-framework.md` (the Road, the Hand, the nine cells) and gives each main or recurring character a compact personality block in the D&D manner: two traits, an ideal, a bond, a flaw, a Line they will not cross, and what earns their trust. The blank card is in `codex/characters/npc-personality-template.md`. Goblin culture is treated in depth in `codex/lore/goblin-code-and-clan-alignments.md`.

## 0. Conventions

- **[CANON path]** is a fact already in the repo. **[PAGE chN]** is something the drafted chapters show (file numbering in `chapters/`; Chapters Nine to Eleven are the unreviewed "now" drafts). **[SRC Sn]** is a paraphrase of an outside source, keyed to the list at the end of this file. **[PROPOSAL]** is mine, and where canon is silent the placement is marked PROPOSAL.
- Line labels in each block: **Traits, Ideal, Bond, Flaw, Line, Trust, Break, Page, Drift**. "Ideal" carries one of six Creeds from the framework (Order, Freedom, Mercy, Hunger, Balance, Calling).
- **Hold** is how firmly someone keeps their cell under pressure: Firm, Settled or Wavering.
- Nothing here is printed on the page. NPCs never say a label; they speak of deeds, roads and hands.
- "Martial proposal" means `outline/research/martial-teaching-and-mastery-proposal.md`, whose Values sheets for Sevish and Wyx I reuse and extend.
- Dash rule: no em or en dashes anywhere in this file.

## 2. How to use a block

Each block is a quick-play card and an audit tool.

- The **Trust** line lists the deeds that tick that person's Affiliation up; **Break** lists what costs. Sizes follow the framework (Small 1 to 2, Notable 3 to 4, Landmark 5 to 8, betrayal about twice a Landmark).
- The **Line** is the one thing the character will not do or will not forgive. It is not always a virtue.
- **Page** records what the drafted chapters already show, so a later chapter cannot contradict it by accident.
- **Drift** notes what could move the placement, how, and which signal to watch for. Only gold NPCs drift, and only through an arc (framework 7.8).

## 3. The Thrash camp

### 3.1 Sevish Thrash (Level 80 Hobgoblin, Berserker)

**Placement:** Chaotic Neutral with a good code. **Unbound** (Wild Road, Level Hand that opens toward his own). **Hold:** Firm. [PROPOSAL, read from CANON]

**Why:** canon gives a battle-hungry fighter with little regard for rules, titles or politics and "a strict personal honor system": he will not kill the helpless and demands fair one-on-one fights [CANON `sevish-thrash.md`]. He is not kind, he is honorable: his mercy is a rule about whom he will not hurt, his care goes to his own, and he has spent recruits for the cause. So the Hand is Level that opens to his own and is never Cold toward the helpless. If the author wants him warmer, Chaotic Good is defensible; I would not go lower than Unbound, because he is not cruel.

- **Traits:** (1) Pain is proof: he lives for the physical rush of a fight and treats danger as proof he is alive [CANON]. (2) He laughs when someone strong or bold stands up to him, a short delighted laugh and then a hard question, not anger [CANON; PAGE ch6, ch10]. He is calm, heavy and quiet in speech, not loud [CANON ledger, Chapter Six].
- **Ideal (Freedom, bound by honor):** a fair fight is the only honest argument, and the strong owe the helpless a clean hand [CANON].
- **Bond:** Wyx, whose reason he trusts and rarely argues with [CANON]; his people, whose deaths he counts because "the dead stay dead" [PAGE ch6]; Shiv's cause, the reunification of Myrrhwood [CANON].
- **Flaw:** he spends bodies for the cause. The monthly rally sends the camp's recruits at the walls of Lunarest [CANON `world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`], and in the drafts he bleeds the camp again by pushing down the hill [PAGE ch9 to ch11]. His pride sounds like courage ("I am not a coward. We push them" [PAGE ch11]), and he has no patience for long plans [CANON].
- **Line:** harming the helpless; striking a downed foe; abandoning the camp; mocking Shiv [martial proposal 4.3; CANON `thrash-siblings-ability-lists.md`].
- **Trust:** frankness to his face; standing your ground when he pushes; owning a mistake bluntly; fighting beside the clan; wounds taken honestly [martial proposal 4.3; PAGE ch10, "He says it to my face"].
- **Break:** flattery; hiding behind rules; slipping a trap into a fair duel; flaunting the "elder" title; wasting his goblins [martial proposal 4.3].
- **Page:** asks for words chosen with care and for a witness before he judges [PAGE ch5, ch6]; treats two dead clansmen as a favor owed rather than a crime [PAGE ch6]; calls Wyx's walls rule "the only law I keep" [PAGE ch10]; gives a stranger command of three of his own once the plan is sound [PAGE ch10, ch11].
- **Drift:** toward Oathkeeper as he trades rallies for plans. The signal is Sevish choosing a plan over a push. Tatsuya's counsel cutting his spending of recruits is the arc. [PROPOSAL]

### 3.2 Wyx Thrash (Level 25 Hobgoblin, Blade Dancer)

**Placement:** Chaotic Good by the author's read, kept. **Freeheart**, with a lawful lean on the page. **Hold:** Settled; Road Wavering toward Straight. [CANON for the read; PAGE and PROPOSAL for the lean]

**Why:** canon says the author is unsure of her exact alignment and reads her as chaotic good [CANON `wyx-thrash.md`]. She despises cruelty and judges people by character and effort, not status or species [CANON]. The page shows her stepping out when no one else will ("Brother, I saw all of it" [PAGE ch6]) and also writing a law: she and Sevish are "the walls" and anyone who reaches the outer stakes meets her first [PAGE ch10]. The two fit if her Straight vow is a conscience choice: Chaotic Good means conscience over convention, and a conscience can pick a code. Keep her Freeheart and let the page show the code.

- **Traits:** (1) Poised, warm authority: dry remarks delivered without raising her voice [CANON]. (2) Proud and competitive, unwilling to back down, and deeply affectionate and easily teased about people she cares for [CANON].
- **Ideal (Mercy, serving a Calling):** protect her people like family and keep Shiv's cause, which she will not go against [CANON].
- **Bond:** Sevish ("brother"); the Thrash people; Shiv's creed; and, as background texture only, a growing personal interest in Tatsuya [CANON, not a mechanic].
- **Flaw:** pride under the poise. She and her brother have asked the rank and file to die in rallies, and she has not counted the cost [CANON]. She is particular about what she will do herself: she will not use Goblin Gas or Toxic Breath because it is "not ladylike" [CANON].
- **Line:** cruelty to the helpless; using the clan's dead as a bargaining chip [martial proposal 4.2].
- **Trust:** judging by character and effort; treating her people as family; carrying the names of the dead; asking her opinion before a gamble; a reasoned plan over bravado; dry wit answered in kind [martial proposal 4.2; CANON].
- **Break:** boasting; gamer slang; lording the elder title; mocking her squeamishness; gambling with her people's lives [martial proposal 4.2].
- **Page:** answers a quick tongue with interest (45 to 48 percent, then 48 to 52) [PAGE ch6]; speaks for the clan's history in the tent [PAGE ch6]; makes the walls rule [PAGE ch10]; gets the stranger gear that fits ("we cannot have the one Shiv marked walking out looking like he crawled out of a ditch") [PAGE ch10]; 52 to 56 when Shiv is named [PAGE ch11].
- **Drift:** as Sevish keeps losing the clan, her Straight vow hardens. If Tatsuya's gambles cost goblins, her trust falls fast. [PROPOSAL]

### 3.3 Shiv (spectral goblin sorcerer, formerly Shivaris Jutsuin)

**Placement:** Lawful Good. **Oathkeeper.** **Hold:** Firm. The one Wild act of his life, loving the queen, is the root of his exile. [PROPOSAL, strong CANON]

**Why:** he built a creed and a people: he united the clans "under a shared philosophy", tried to settle things at the negotiating table, and wanted goblins "civilized, something worth building" [CANON `shiv.md`, `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`]. His magic works only with consent: the bond is an explicit accept prompt, and he asks Tatsuya's leave before he touches Ru [CANON `call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`; PAGE ch9]. Wyx says Thrash "held to Shiv's law and kept the peace among our own" [PAGE ch6]. Alternative: Neutral Good if the author wants him looser.

- **Traits:** (1) Plain and patient: asks before he acts, and says "I won't take the choice from you" [PAGE ch9]. (2) Dry and humble about method: "My methods might not be something you can just copy" and "Make yours greater than mine" [CANON `shiv.md`; PAGE ch9].
- **Ideal (Calling):** goblins are not feral; they can be a people with a table to sit at [CANON].
- **Bond:** the goblin clans; the first queen, held for the archive reveal [CANON]; the Warset scattered across Eryndal.
- **Flaw:** his reach is fading, and he has only held the clans back from finishing each other, not fixed them [CANON]. He withholds his history and his method on purpose, so he looks like a riddle to the people who need him [CANON, deliberate].
- **Line:** taking away someone's choice; playing the villain of his own story to look humble [CANON].
- **Trust:** a goblin with an open mind who goes looking [CANON]; plain speech that happens to echo his creed ("bigger problems than fighting each other") without knowing it [CANON].
- **Break:** someone wearing his mark as a title; the kingdom's lies; a goblin spending goblins cheaply.
- **Page:** warns instead of boasting [PAGE ch9]; grants nothing unasked [PAGE ch9].
- **Drift:** none; he is fading. His disciples carry him on. [PROPOSAL]

### 3.4 Ru (Level 5 Hobgoblin, "Disciple of Elder Nykri")

**Placement:** Lawful Good. **Oathkeeper.** **Hold:** Wavering, growing to Settled. [PAGE and PROPOSAL]

**Why:** loyal, collected and self-sufficient; his loyalty "is real and chosen" and works inside Thrash's chain of command [CANON `young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`]. On the page he keeps the tent, sets the line, and reports unprompted [PAGE ch10]. He is the camp's own answer to its bystander custom: he used to say "goblins don't really care about each other" [CANON] and has chosen to be the one who does.

- **Traits:** (1) Watchful: stands at the flap with a strip of the camp in view and counts his brothers [PAGE ch10]. (2) Quiet and exact once he finds his voice: patterned, grave speech after the evolution, after a stutter before it [CANON].
- **Ideal (Order, chosen):** a line held by people who trust each other [PROPOSAL, from PAGE ch10].
- **Bond:** Nykri, the first to stand up for him; the brothers he counts; the Thrash chain of command [CANON; PAGE ch9, ch10]. His Affiliation with Tatsuya stands at 75 percent, already Trusted [CANON ledger].
- **Flaw:** no voice of his own yet. He "did not have the rank to speak" in the tent [PAGE ch6] and has no birth name [CANON]. He risks too much for others: "Nobody reaches you while I stand here" [PAGE ch10].
- **Line:** abandoning his post or the one who vouched for him [PROPOSAL].
- **Trust:** being seen and spoken for; clear orders with a reason; being allowed to report unprompted [PAGE ch10].
- **Break:** being used as bait or fodder; being ordered to hurt a goblin who cannot fight back [PROPOSAL].
- **Drift:** gains a voice and Settles. Watch for a scene where two orders collide, which canon predicts ("Sevish outranks him as long as Tatsuya's standing inside Thrash remains where it is" [CANON]). [PROPOSAL]
