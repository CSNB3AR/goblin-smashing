# Sevish and Wyx: ability lists and fighting styles (author-requested, Chapter Ten)

Both are martial. No magic outside the goblin kit. Tatsuya can inspect them because he is now an ally of Thrash (in Chapter Six their panels were question marks).

## Wyx Thrash, LV 25 Hobgoblin, Blade Dancer (six)
Fighting style: a dual-sabre blade dancer in the Prince of Persia vein; two curved blades crossed at the small of her back under the torn cloak.
1. Twin Sabre Mastery (passive): bonus to damage and speed with a blade in each hand.
2. Dance of Blades (RARE): a chain of spinning strikes that quickens with every hit landed.
3. Winding Step: a quick sidestep that slips out of melee range.
4. Whirling Edge: a full spin that cuts everyone around her.
5. Challenging Howl: enemies within earshot are forced to fight the user (Sevish's version also immobilises; hers is the same roar on a shorter leash).
6. Toxic Breath: never used, she finds it unladylike.

## Sevish Thrash, LV 80 Hobgoblin, Berserker (seven)
Fighting style: a berserker with a two-handed war axe, a Kenpachi type who treats pain as proof he is alive.
1. War Axe Mastery (passive).
2. Bloodlust: damage rises with every kill while his guard falls.
3. Pain Is Proof (RARE passive): every wound he takes feeds his next swing.
4. Rending Cleave: a wide axe arc that hits everyone in front of him.
5. Earthsplitter (RARE): a ground-breaking overhead chop.
6. Challenging Howl: the roar from Chapter Five that immobilised Tatsuya and the watchers.
7. Toxic Breath: long unused.

Page rule: names with a short effect line for the martial ones; no numbers. Tatsuya inspects Wyx on purpose (he wants to know whether she has the Howl before planning) and Sevish second.
