# Sevish and Wyx: ability lists (system-fit, supersedes the first version)

Built against `outline/research/ability-system-audit.md` (rules brief), `outline/research/dnd-martial-ability-research.md` (tabletop sources) and `outline/research/martial-teaching-and-mastery-proposal.md` (teaching). Everything below is a PROPOSAL inside canon rules: Physical Arts only (stamina, a Way, a tier 1 to 7, a cooldown), plus the Thrash clan technique Challenging Howl and the unused goblin kit. No spells. Gold-tag NPCs are fixed at their level and do not level. Page format: names, Way, tier and one effect line; no numbers, costs or cooldowns on the panel.

Pools (proposal, never shown): HP is 50 + 3 per level, so Wyx 125 and Sevish 290. One shared stamina pool each; arts cost a fraction of it. About two arts per round for Wyx, one for Sevish, plus one reaction each.

## Wyx Thrash, LV 25 Hobgoblin, Master of the Blade (Way of the Blade, dual sabres)
Fighting style: a blade dancer. She holds only two sabres, but her strikes land like three or five: two real hits per action, and the rest are ECHOES, afterimage hits that arrive one beat later from another angle (the style reference is Irelia the Blade Dancer; the author asked for "three strikes, five strikes" from two blades). She is defensive with moderate offence: a wall, not a killer.
| Ability | Way, tier | SP / cooldown (proposal) | What it does and its limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Afterimage Cut | Blade T3 | about 25 percent, 30 s | Two real strikes and one echo about a third of a normal hit. Parrying or sidestepping the first cut cancels the echo. |
| Winding Step | Blade T3 | about 20 percent, 30 s | A sidestep that slips out of melee range. Teachable later (see below). |
| Defiant Whirl | Blade T4 | about 40 percent, 60 s | A short guarded stance that ends in a sweep; she cannot move during the guard. |
| Dance of Blades (RARE) | Blade T5 | about 80 percent and all Tempo, once per fight (3 min) | A five-beat flurry: the echo feel at its peak. Unusable below Tempo 4. |
| Challenging Howl | Thrash technique T3 | about 25 percent, 45 s | Enemies in earshot must turn on her for about five seconds. A taunt, no damage, no immobilise. This is the plan's key piece. |
| Tempo (passive) | | none | Hits and dodges add Tempo (0 to 5, decays after about 6 s). Tempo 2 enables echoes, Tempo 4 the five-beat feel. Being staggered resets it. |
Goblin kit (listed, never used): Toxic Breath. Baseline Way of the Blade starters (Clean Draw, Parry Riposte, Guard Break Swing, Flowing Edge) are not shown.
Teaches (gold-tag Master): Winding Step first (Tier 3). See the teaching proposal: standing with her personally at Trusted (about 60 percent plus), level 10, Speed 10, a token (a matched pair of blades he makes himself) and the Edge Test (a spar with no gas or flare, best won by wit inside her rule). Chapters 13 to 16 at the earliest. Dance of Blades much later (Book Two).

## Sevish Thrash, LV 80 Hobgoblin, Berserker of the Axe (Way of the Axe, two-handed war axe)
Fighting style: calm, heavy, slow and readable. His damage comes from big, telegraphed moves with exposed recovery windows, so a level 5 to 8 player can read him. Offensive with moderate defence. He fights at the opponent's measure and never strikes a downed foe (honour), which is also why he does not solve fights for Tatsuya.
| Ability | Way, tier | SP / cooldown (proposal) | What it does and its limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reckless Swing | Axe T2 | about 20 percent, 10 s | A heavier swing; his guard stays open until his next action. |
| Momentum Chain | Axe T3 (canon move) | about 30 percent, 30 s | A chain of swings, each heavier than the last; ends if interrupted. |
| Reaping Arc | Axe T4 | about 40 percent, 45 s | A wide frontal arc with a visible wind-up. His only area hit besides the Howl. |
| Bloodlust | Axe T4 | about 35 percent, 120 s | For about ten seconds every hit returns a little stamina while his guard is lowered. Ends the moment the target is downed. |
| Challenging Howl | Thrash technique T5 | about 60 percent, once per fight | Pins those far below him in place (canon: 6 s on Tatsuya, 12 s on the kid); Power and Courage resist, and equal levels shrug it off; a target that resists is immune for 24 hours (proposal). |
| Earthsplitter (RARE) | Axe T6 | about 90 percent, 3 min | A 3-second wind-up and a 2-second opening; a heavy overhead chop that splits the ground in a line. |
| Pain Is Proof (RARE passive) | | none | Below half health each wound adds a small bonus to his next swing, capped at five stacks, fading 10 s after the last wound; each stack lowers his guard. |
Goblin kit (listed, never used): Toxic Breath.
Teaches: nothing yet. The seed on the page is "Ask me again when you have stood a Howl" (not written). Later (Book Two): the taunt-and-steady form of the Howl, at Allied with him and the clan, Courage 12, a Bone Tally token and The Standing proof.

## Resolved conflicts
- Wyx's weapon is dual sabres (the necklace file's daggers and the Way of the Bow note are stale).
- Wyx's Howl is a taunt (ledger), Sevish's is an immobilise (Chapter Five): two different effects under one clan technique.
- Toxic Breath is the evolved Goblin Gas; neither sibling is built on it.
- The first provisional panels (Rending Cleave, Whirling Edge, War Axe/Twin Sabre "Mastery" passives) are gone: no Way, tier, cost, or they overlapped, or used the word Mastery wrongly.
