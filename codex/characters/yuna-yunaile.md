# Yuna / Yunaile

**Role:** HGO's main broadcast personality and tournament caster. Future slow-burn romance interest.

## Appearance

Pink hair, high energy on-broadcast, the public face of the esports world. **Civilian look (locked):** twin buns, glasses, ties directly to her disguise, this is what she looks like as Yunaile, not on-broadcast Yuna.

## Facts

- Also an idol/singer (tracks like "Shadow Drive" and **"Heart on You,"** performs in HGO's virtual spaces). "Heart on You" is the specific track tied to Momo's fandom, Act One plants Momo playing/obsessing over it at home as an early, organic thread, well before any direct Yuna/Tatsuya connection forms.
- Civilian name: **Yunaile**. Hides her public identity completely outside of broadcasts, glasses, real name only.
- Loop account: @Yuna_N9. Momo's obsession with her plants her in Tatsuya's household long before any direct connection forms (see `codex/characters/momo-kazehaya.md`).
- **Revised for Chapter 1:** her on-air name does surface via her own ordinary broadcast self-intro, background noise Tatsuya registers without fully engaging, not a character beat. What still stays for later, at the hospital: her civilian identity (Yunaile), any real recognition or connection on Tatsuya's part, and the slow-burn romance thread. Don't let the Chapter 1 self-intro turn into more than that.

## Family background

Old-money business moguls, rooted in law but with hands in everything, real estate, contracts, corporate sponsorships. Not tech people (that lane belongs to Daichi's legacy and the Tabuchis). They're now positioning themselves in the VR space because of the "bring your business here" expansion, they see it as the next major economic frontier.

Yuna is the daughter they didn't want in the line of succession. Her grandfather passed the family legacy and wealth directly to his grandson (her younger brother, Kaito), bypassing his own son entirely, a judgment on character the son never forgave.

If Kaito dies before he's old enough to formally accept the grandfather's will, everything reverts to the father. That's the motive. The father and mother are both complicit, an outside doctor brings in medication through channels that bypass the hospital directly, and that medication is making Kaito worse, not better. Kaito visibly improves when his parents aren't present.

Yuna has been kept at Tochi Academy since elementary school. She barely sees her father outside formal occasions and hotel lobby encounters. She knows what her parents are, without hard proof of what they're doing to Kaito.

Her parents don't know how deeply she's actually embedded in HGO, not just as a caster, but as its entire public face. When that becomes impossible to hide, the family's interest in her flips overnight from "inconvenience" to "asset," especially once she's visibly connected to Tatsuya.

**The family's late interest in HGO specifically:** they weren't paying attention to the esports side until Kazuku began treating in-game economic infrastructure as a serious real-world business play (kept intentionally vague). Once that shift became impossible to ignore, the family scrambled to get positioned inside it, and suddenly Yuna, previously in the way, became valuable.

## Hospital meeting (future chapter material)

Yuna visits Kaito regularly to cheer him up and bring him fan mail. Tatsuya bumps into her in the hallway; she drops envelopes. She introduces herself as Yunaile only. She recognizes the Kazehaya name but doesn't explain why. By the end of the conversation she asks if he plays HGO, quietly testing whether he recognizes her. He doesn't connect it in the moment. He pieces it together later, at home, watching TV.

See `codex/subplots/yuna-tatsuya-slow-burn-romance.md` and `codex/subplots/yuna-family-inheritance-plot.md`.

## Personality

Pure soft-spoken sweetness and quiet devotion. Polite, easily flustered, apologizes for the smallest mistakes. Exclusively plays high-utility support and healing roles, focused entirely on keeping her carry alive. Shy about her real identity, prefers to stay low-key. Follows the person she trusts like a shadow, blushes at simple compliments, gets visibly overwhelmed by rough, loud environments.
