# Daichi Kazehaya

**Role:** Tatsuya's father, deceased before the story begins.

## Facts

- Short, the shorter of Tatsuya's two parents. Tatsuya's height comes from Ohema, not him. What Daichi passed down instead was the analytical, technical mind, the tinkering instinct that keeps the Haya EB running and later gets a first-gen pod overridden.
- First-gen pod engineer. Company: Kazuhaya Tech (logo still visible on surviving pods).
- Helped build tech infrastructure at Tochi Academy, part of the family's standing there.
- Sold the original first-gen pod schematics to Kazuku (**not** the Tabuchis) before his death, to pay for Ohema's emergency hospital bills. Trusted Kazuku as a genuine business partner.
- Daichi and Kazuku weren't just a supplier and a buyer. Before Daichi's health declined, the two of them spent years shaping the actual idea of what HGO could be, not just the hardware to run it. Kazuku built and launched HGO after Daichi's death, but the vision underneath it, a full second world, not just a game, has Daichi's fingerprints on it as much as Kazuku's. This is the real weight behind "business partner," not just a schematics sale.
- Lost mobility in his legs in the last several years of his life (a progressive condition, not an accident, exact cause left undefined), spending that stretch increasingly confined to a wheelchair, working from a converted corner of the apartment. Tatsuya grew up with this image of him, late at night, wheelchair pulled up to a desk covered in pod components. He's never connected it to anything beyond "that's just what Dad did."
- What Tatsuya doesn't know yet: the wheelchair wasn't incidental to the work, it's part of why the work became what it did. Daichi's own stated reason, something he said to Kazuku more than once and never quite said to his own family in so many words: he wanted a world where this world and that world wouldn't be so far apart, somewhere a body that couldn't move freely in this one wouldn't be the ceiling in the other. Tatsuya is chasing a version of that without knowing it's his father's line.
- Last words to Kazuku: *"If my son ever gets involved, please look after him."*
- Died under suspicious circumstances, closed-casket funeral, never fully explained. Was in debt to the Tabuchis but refused to hand over the schematics despite pressure, even when it might have cleared the debt.
- His decline was a slow cascade, working more and more hours, disappearing from the family's daily life, before the family was simply told there had been a funeral.
- Tatsuya has never processed the closed casket. It sits as an open wound and an open question.

## The funeral and Kazuto's approach

The funeral itself was held and funded by the Tabuchis, which is how Kazuto got a foothold. At the funeral, Kazuto Tabuchi personally approached Tatsuya, not Chiyo, framed as an offer to help ("do this for your father, it'll cover..."), about the old schematics/computer files specifically. Tatsuya refused any contact, in part because Eren had already betrayed him by that point and he wasn't about to trust anything Tabuchi-shaped. This is the origin of the two years of pressure that follow.

**Only Tatsuya knows this.** Chiyo assumes the pressure is ordinary debt collection, she was never at that conversation and doesn't know the files are the real target.
