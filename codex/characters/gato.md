# Old Man Gato

**Role:** Manager of the WattsUp charge station where Tatsuya works, and its original owner before the corporate buyout below.

## Facts

- Dark skin, gray stubble, sharp eyes behind thick glasses. Gruff but warm underneath.
- Got Tatsuya the job. Tatsuya has worked there roughly a year, since dropping out of Tochi.
- **Energy: Sanford and Son, Redd Foxx.** Not corporate-minded at all, he just follows the sponsorship rule because it's the deal, doesn't need or offer a reason why. Funny in a worn-in, cantankerous way. He's the human explanation for why the screen plays what it plays, not a policy document.
- **Ownership history:** originally owned an independent gas station on the same lot. WattsUp, the global EV-charging corporation (see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`), bought him out once EVs came to dominate the 2045 transportation space, retrofitting the site into a branded, HGO-sponsored charging station. Gato took the buyout money and stayed on as the manager/operator of the corporate location rather than walking away, which is why the place reads upgraded and modern instead of rundown despite his own low-key, unbothered energy about all of it.

See `codex/locations/wattsup.md`.
