# Ms. Sugiyama

**Role:** Recurring hospital nurse, close to the Kazehaya family through Ohema's long-term stay. First real appearance Chapter 4.

## Facts

Warm, familiar presence, knows Tatsuya on sight after however long Ohema's been admitted, the kind of relationship built from years of small hallway conversations, not a formal one. Genuinely fond of him, teases him lightly, the family's real, ordinary anchor point at the hospital.

**Suspects foul play with Kaito** (see `codex/subplots/yuna-family-inheritance-plot.md`), no hard proof, just a nurse's read on a kid who visibly gets worse right around his parents' monthly visits. Doesn't act on it directly yet, this stays background texture, not something dramatized on the page until that subplot actually develops.

In Chapter 4, she's the one Tatsuya overhears (with a colleague) discussing Chiyo's request to keep quiet about Ohema's six-month window, the beat that sets off his anger at Chiyo specifically. Can also reference Dr. Inoue's assessment directly in dialogue without Dr. Inoue needing to appear on the page.
