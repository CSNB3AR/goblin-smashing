## 0. The tree on one page

**Who he is.** A Shiv Goblin: a pseudo caster, an enchanter and debuffer with 65 HP and a low attack power [CANON, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`, ledger]. He makes the people around him stronger and the people across from him slower, weaker and unluckier. He is never the one doing most of the damage.

**The six branches.**

| Branch | What it is | Pool | Where it starts |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. The Weighing Line | Enchantment and debuff spells (Book of the Veil, Shaman's Veil): marks, slows, wards, curses, and the road to Flamonta | MP | Opened by the necklace [PAGE] |
| B. Runes | Rune Priming: glyphs and insignias. Control and wards first, destruction only after training | MP per attempt | Granted by the necklace [PAGE] |
| C. Goblin Kit | Goblin Gas, Shiv-Touched Flare, Toxic Breath, Vile Curse, the Gadget Belt | SP | Racial and bond [PAGE] |
| D. The Kin Line | Shiv's spirit magic as leadership: Whispers of Shiv, Call of Command, Marks, Kinweight, banners | SP and MP | Granted by the bond [PAGE] |
| E. Shield and Mace | Physical Arts: Way of the Axe (mace), Way of the Fist (shield strikes), guard arts, one Blade art for mobility | SP | Trained, not granted |
| F. Passives | Gambler's Gambit, racial passives, Kinweight, grip and footing | none | Mixed |

**The author's four decisions, and where they land.**

| Decision | Where it lands in this file |
|---|---|
| Flamonta is the last of his bigger spells and comes into play hugely | Section 10. Tier 7 capstone of Branch A, three Forms at levels 60, 70, 80, anchored by a primed rune, seeded from Chapter 9 onward by a greyed "Witnessed" node |
| Ancient Bone Flame Barrage is good, but is not his identity | Section 10.4. Branch B, a once-per-fight finisher in ranks. Dormant at level 8, not cast on the page until he can afford and roll it |
| Runes are enchantments and debuffs first, destructive only after training | Section 5 and 10.5. The Ember Turn at level 20 (Kindled Glyph) is the first rune that damages for real |
| A point where he can go back into the tree (respec or branch choice) | Section 11. The Crossing: the necklace "turns a bone" on every tenth level. Free, tree only, level 10 is the first and the biggest |

**Weapon.** One-hand mace (or a short wand) in the focus hand and a shield on the other arm. Not a staff (not mobile), not a gun (a gun in the casting hand blocks casting). Full reasoning in `codex/characters/tatsuya-weapon-and-playstyle.md`.

## 2. Rules of the tree

### 2.1 Currency: skill points (SKP)

[CANON] Skill points exist: he had 1 at level 1 and spent it to equip Gambler's Gambit (`chapters/chapter-04.md`). [CANON] Level-ups give 2 discipline points each, spent on the six stats, and each stat caps at 75 (`codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`). Nothing states how many skill points a level gives, so this part is [PROPOSAL]:

- **1 SKP per level**, plus **2 bonus SKP on every tenth level** (the Crossing, section 11), plus occasional SKP from reading in the Grand Library (the library file already allows "Wisdom or skill points") and from quest steps.
- He is holding **5 SKP** at level 8 (levels 4 to 8, none spent), because he "hates dumping points into something I know nothing about" [PAGE Ch4]. The page never says so; add one line when he first opens the tree.
- Available by level 80: roughly 100 SKP. The tree below costs roughly 145. He cannot buy everything, which is the same forced specialization the stat system already has (`codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`).

**SKP cost by tier** [PROPOSAL]: Tier 1 and 2 cost 1, Tier 3 costs 2, Tier 4 costs 3, Tier 5 costs 4, Tier 6 costs 5, Tier 7 costs 7 per Form. Racial, bond-granted and starter Way techniques cost 0. A node marked "upgrade" costs the stated SKP again.

### 2.2 Tiers, level gates and Mastery

Magic runs Tier 1 to 8 with 8 the ceiling; Physical Arts and goblin kit skills run Tier 1 to 7 [CANON `codex/magic/overview.md`, `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`]. [CANON] Mastery runs 1 to 10, grows by use, about 25% unlocks roughly the first half of a book's tiers, and Tier 6 and 8 stay locked below roughly 75% [CANON `codex/lore/mastery-system.md`]. [CANON] A level 8 character already reached a stretched Tier 5 through setup (the Chapter 11 draft prints TIER 5 for Ancient Bone Flame Barrage), and Arya at level 10 to 11 cannot cast Tier 6.

No file maps level to tier, so [PROPOSAL]:

| Level | Natural Veil tier | Kit and rune tier | Mastery needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 to 11 | 2 | 2 (3 with setup) | 1 to 2 |
| 12 to 19 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 20 to 29 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 30 to 44 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 45 to 59 | 6 | 6 | 8 |
| 60 to 80 | 7 (Flamonta, ABF rank IV) | 7 | 8 |

Tier 8 is never his: it is Shiv's, a "world-level effect" [PAGE Ch9] and the theoretical ceiling [CANON overview]. A **stretch cast** (spending setup or an Amplify to reach one tier above the natural tier) is the one way to touch a higher tier early, and it always carries a roll and a cost.

Mastery lines he trains, each 1 to 10 [PROPOSAL grouping of the canon rule]: **V** (Veil spells), **R** (Rune Priming, treated like a Book of its own), **K** (goblin kit), **M** (Way of the Axe, mace), **G** (Way of the Fist, shield strikes). The Kin Line trains on V.

### 2.4 House rules that keep an amplifier honest

1. **Control and odds before damage.** The first two nodes of every branch change how a fight goes (slow, mark, ward, root), not how much it hurts.
2. **Stack caps and immunity windows.** Poison caps at 4 stacks [CANON]. Every hard control (root, silence-like, forced stop) leaves the target immune for a short window afterward, so nothing chain-locks. [SRC] Albion's mace line works this way: its silencing ground circle makes mobs immune to the silence after a few seconds, https://albiondatabase.com/weapons/elders-heavy-mace (read through a search summary, the page itself refused the fetch).
3. **Resistance and level gap.** Slow, root and stun are partly resisted by Power and Courage; a large level gap can bypass that [CANON `codex/crafting/debuffs-and-resistances.md`]. Slow multiples are capped by the gap (section 10.2).
4. **Hands rule** [PROPOSAL, the author's own rule made explicit]: spells and rune work need a focus in a hand that is not gripping a gun or a two-handed weapon. A shield strapped to the forearm does not block casting; a gun in the casting hand does. This is the in-world reason for "not a gun, not a staff". [SRC] D&D has the same worry in miniature: somatic components need a free hand unless a feat or a holy symbol worn on a shield removes the problem, https://arcaneeye.com/dnd-feat-guides/war-caster-5e/ (a fan guide, search summary only).
5. **Advantage and disadvantage** [PROPOSAL, HGO rolls use a d6 per the Gambit decision]: a roll made with disadvantage is made twice and the lower is kept; advantage keeps the higher; they cancel and never stack. [SRC] D&D's rule, with a d20, https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:D20%20Tests (search summary). Anything the system rolls on a creature's behalf (crit proc, status resist, cast success, a rune roll) can carry it.
6. **No taunt, no summoning.** [CANON] No creature summoning anywhere, ever; conjured daggers and rings do not count [CANON `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`]. He also has no aggro tool: taunts belong to Wyx's Howl and to the Ironbreaker class. His archetype is "not a healer, not a tank, not a hard-CC caster" [CANON]; the shield makes him hard to kill, not a tank.
7. **World-tier items shrink in organized events.** [PAGE draft Ch11] World-tier gear is scaled down in guild events such as Crownfall. Anything the necklace gives is halved in caps there.
8. **No new stat.** Everything maps onto the six disciplines and the existing secondary stats [CANON, archetype file].

### 2.5 Pacing rules for the page

[CANON] Codex Entries arrive "two to four per major arc, never as an upfront catalog dump", through use (`codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`, `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`). So:

- No more than **three** newly named abilities in one chapter, and each is introduced by being used.
- A panel shows the name, the Way or Book, one effect line. Numbers appear only when a gamble is live, or when he checks a cost mid-plan.
- He opens **one branch at a time**, never the whole tree.
- The only two menu moments in Chapters 11 to 20 are the First Crossing (level 10) and the Gambit upgrade (level 10).

## 10. Flamonta, the capstone

### 10.1 What it is
Flamonta is the slow, disadvantage spell Shiv cast on him in Chapter Nine [PAGE]. On the page it was world tier: it dropped him. His version is the player-scaled echo, the last and heaviest of his bigger spells, because it is the purest form of what he is: weigh the enemy down and make its luck fail.

### 10.2 The three Forms [PROPOSAL]

| Form | Lv | Effect | Cost | CD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I, Weighing | 60 | One target. Slow 40 percent for 10 s and its rolls at disadvantage. Needs one primed rune under the target | l | D |
| II, Hushed Ground | 70 | A zone, 6 m. Same, plus lesser enemies stagger and lose reaction. Bosses get half | l | D |
| III, The Long Moment | 80 | The zone for 20 s. Slow up to 75 percent on enemies at or below his level plus 10. Bosses 40 percent. Allies inside gain advantage on their first roll | x | E |

The slow cap is bounded by the level gap (house rule 3), so a world boss never freezes: that is Shiv's alone. In events the numbers halve (house rule 7).

### 10.3 The seed mechanism (Witnessed)
From Chapter Nine his tree shows a greyed node, **Flamonta [WITNESSED]**, listing its needs: Veil Tier 7, Veil Mastery 8, level 60, Unlucky Cast, Stilling Hand, one rune of the Weighing Line. Seeing it on the page early costs nothing and promises everything.

### 10.4 Ancient Bone Flame Barrage sits beside it
It stays a Branch B finisher, a spectacle he can afford once. Flamonta sets it up: slowed targets stand still inside the glyph ring, so a Barrage under Flamonta hits every target and gets advantage.

### 10.5 Destructive runes
Kindled Glyph (20), Bone Mine Glyph (28), Barrage ranks. Everything before level 20 is a ward, tether or enchant.

## 11. The Crossing (respec and branch choice) [PROPOSAL]

On every tenth level (10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80) the necklace "turns a bone": a free, tree only respec. He may refund and rebuy any nodes of Tier 5 and below, and at the First Crossing (10) any node at all, plus one choice: **which Way the mace sits in** (Axe for weight, Fist for shield work) which locks the other's Tier 3 nodes for 10 levels. Stats are never refunded. Flamonta Forms are not refundable once bought. Each Crossing also grants 2 SKP and a Gambit upgrade. On the page it is a quiet bone-click and a menu he does not trust, until it saves him.
