# Toddy

**Type:** Minor real-world character. Tatsuya's coworker at WattsUp (see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`).

## Facts

- Younger than Tatsuya, still juggling morning classes around his shifts. Tatsuya covers closing shifts for him fairly often, established as of Chapter 5, so Toddy can actually make it to class on time.
- Tatsuya reads as a genuine little-brother figure to look out for at work, not a literal sibling, warmth without the weight of the family stuff at home.
- Talks a lot about HGO rumor-mill content he's picked up secondhand, notably the worse-case version of World Quest item camping (guilds locking a player down and ransoming the account back for real money, see `codex/lore/world-quests.md` if/when that file exists, otherwise `chapters/chapter-05.md`). Never confirmed how much of it is actually true.
- Has no idea Tatsuya is TDRAG, the goblin behind the World Quest content he'll eventually stumble across on Loop/the Companion App. Chapter 5 flags him as the person most likely to find Tatsuya's stream first, completely by accident, which is part of why Tatsuya isn't in a hurry to go public yet.
