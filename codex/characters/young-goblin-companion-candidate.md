# Young Goblin (Companion Candidate) — "Ru," formerly "Runt"

**Role:** A younger, smaller goblin in Bloodhurst, being picked on by two other camp goblins when Tatsuya first arrives. First appears Chapter 3. Went by "Runt," a nickname from the rest of camp, through Chapter 5. Renamed **"Ru"** by Tatsuya in Chapter 6, in the moment, an accidental collapse of everyone repeating "Runt" at him rather than a deliberate pick. His actual birth name is still unresolved, still his own to withhold, being renamed by Tatsuya isn't the same as that mystery being answered, "Ru" is simply the name to use going forward. Distinct from the already-named later squad (Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`), an earlier, separate companion thread.

## Facts

Tatsuya steps in defensively on the kid's behalf when he sees the hazing, which is what actually starts the fight (the two bullies turn on him instead). Later, when Sevish questions everyone, the kid explains that Tatsuya's intervention wasn't typical goblin behavior, "goblins don't really care about each other," and that he'd been the one who first stood up for someone else on the kid's own behalf. Set up as a future companion, earned gradually per the standing pacing rule (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`), not assigned or resolved in this chapter.

**First real companion:** he's the first companion Tatsuya recruits under the new companion/troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`), already with Tatsuya when he logs back into the tent in Chapter 6, rather than found again separately.

**Founding blessing, Chapter 6 (supersedes the earlier "earned gradually" framing for this specific companion).** Rather than leveling up to Hobgoblin over time through the standard system, Ru is granted an instant Goblin to Hobgoblin evolution (level 1 to level 5) directly by Shiv himself, in the same scene as Tatsuya's own necklace bond, an explicit one-time exception (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md` for why this doesn't change the rule for any other companion). Grows visibly taller in the transformation, past even the standard Hobgoblin baseline, taller than Tatsuya himself. Becomes the first "Shiv disciple," title granted the same way, not earned through a gradually-forming bond.

**Ability kit, granted with the blessing:** **Toxic Breath** (his own baseline Goblin Gas evolving into a gut-charged toxic cloud), his one mechanical gain from the blessing beyond the evolution itself and the title. Call of Command, Shiv-Touched Flare, and Rune Priming, all originally slated for Ru in the first telling of this scene, ended up granted to Tatsuya instead as part of his own necklace bond (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), a deliberate author revision, not a continuity slip.
