# Young Goblin (Companion Candidate)

**Role:** A younger, smaller goblin in Bloodhurst, being picked on by two other camp goblins when Tatsuya first arrives. First appears Chapter 3. Not yet named, distinct from the already-named later squad (Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`), an earlier, separate companion thread.

## Facts

Tatsuya steps in defensively on the kid's behalf when he sees the hazing, which is what actually starts the fight (the two bullies turn on him instead). Later, when Sevish questions everyone, the kid explains that Tatsuya's intervention wasn't typical goblin behavior, "goblins don't really care about each other," and that he'd been the one who first stood up for someone else on the kid's own behalf. Set up as a future companion, earned gradually per the standing pacing rule (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`), not assigned or resolved in this chapter.

**First real companion:** he's the first companion Tatsuya recruits under the new companion/troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`), leveling up alongside Tatsuya with a real shot at ranking up to Hobgoblin over time rather than staying a static NPC.
