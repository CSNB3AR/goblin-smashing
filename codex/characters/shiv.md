# Shiv

**Role:** Spectral goblin spirit, quest-giver for the "Call of the Bones" World Quest, historical founder-figure of organized goblin clan culture.

## Appearance

Ash-and-smoke form, wide-brimmed hat, burning green eyes.

## Facts

Strong character from the old draft, well-written voice, worth preserving almost entirely as-is. Grants Tatsuya (in-game name TDRAG) a legendary bound item, the first piece of "Shiv's Warset" (1 of 7): the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), which negates the goblin Wisdom penalty and unlocks the Shaman's Veil spirit magic path, plus Gambler's Gambit.

Worldbuilding tied to Shiv: goblins are historically barred from the capital; there's a hidden "Astralum Archive" beneath the palace that Shiv wants Tatsuya to eventually reach (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`).

**Power level: genuinely world-quest-tier, not player-scaled.** On manifesting, his presence alone hits any nearby player with an extreme, unavoidable slow effect, roughly four times slower, functionally unable to move or act. Not a spell that can be resisted or a normal debuff, a raw consequence of standing near something this far above a level-one character. Confirms his gold tag (see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`) isn't just ceremonial.

**A separate, active effect, distinct from the passive slow above, named Flamonta.** When Shiv actually wants privacy for a conversation, he can cast a real, active spell (system-named **Flamonta**, a world-level effect, distinct from the passive proximity slow) that fully freezes every other NPC in the immediate zone mid-motion (not merely slowed) and visually shades the area, thin gray light, a low crawling mist, an isolated pocket where only he and whoever he's speaking to can act or move. On the player it's cast near, Flamonta doesn't just slow, it physically drops them, hard enough to read as a "failed spell check" tier effect on-page, a system warning cascade before the status line itself. First shown Chapter 7 ("The First Disciple"), when he pulls Tatsuya and the young goblin companion aside inside Tatsuya's own war tent. A nearby player (as opposed to an NPC) caught in the same zone would only take the passive four-times slow above, not a full freeze, though this hasn't been dramatized on the page yet since Tatsuya's always alone with him so far. **Forward-plot seed:** Tatsuya eventually crafts a minor item that replicates a small-scale version of this freeze effect, see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`, not built yet.

**Characterization note:** Shiv reads uncannily lifelike on the page, real personality, real timing, body language that doesn't feel like a scripted quest-giver. Tatsuya notices this himself and wonders, briefly, whether Shiv is actually AI-driven at all or something the game isn't telling players about, a small character beat rather than a plot thread, keep it available for a reader to wonder about without ever resolving it outright.

## Backstory (partial reveal, Chapter 7)

Shiv tells Tatsuya part of his own history directly, in his own words, inside the frozen tent: he was once a human noble of Lunarest's own royal court, real name **Shivaris Jutsuin**. He loved the Queen, an affair the king's own court mages didn't forgive once it came out, punishing him with what they called a correction rather than a curse. That correction is what changed him into a goblin, closer to his old human shape than most goblins around him, a hybrid rather than a clean transformation, but **he was not the first goblin, the race already existed before him and his curse just remade him into one of them.** What he actually was first at is unifying the clans: he's the one who brought Myrrhwood's fractured, warring goblin clans together under a shared philosophy for the first time, the "we have bigger problems than fighting each other" idea Tatsuya himself echoed without knowing where it came from. That's his real historical weight, not being the original of the race, being the first to get it to act like one people instead of scattered feral clans. He was driven out of the capital for what he'd become, and goblins, beastfolk, and anything else that read as demi-human have been hunted as feral ever since. **His actual motivation for unifying the clans, stated on the page:** everywhere he went afterward, as a goblin, he was treated as a monster first and anything else second, if he got a second look at all. That mistreatment is what decided it for him, he wasn't going to spend what was left of his life proving them right. Goblins weren't feral, they could be civilized, something worth building, and that conviction is the entire reason Myrrhwood exists at all, not just circumstance or survival. He's plain about the court not being righteous in doing it either, that kingdom was rotting from the inside long before he gave them an excuse to point elsewhere, a light touch rather than a grim indictment, he doesn't dwell on it. **Deliberately partial:** he doesn't explain how he actually held the clans together on that philosophy alone, or how the "whispers" mechanic (see Whispers of Shiv, `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`) actually works underneath. That's held back for Tatsuya to piece together gradually across later chapters, tied to the Whispers of Shiv subplot questline and the wider Shiv's Warset hunt (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`), not resolved in one scene.

**Why now, stated plainly.** Shiv tells Tatsuya his reach is fading, thinner every year, and that he's spent what's left of it keeping Myrrhwood's fractured clans from finishing each other off rather than actually fixing anything. Goblins have never truly unified, and if the feral clans keep tearing at each other they'll never be taken seriously by anyone. What he's asking Tatsuya's permission for isn't small, it stretches across all five continents, not just Myrrhwood, tied directly to the wider Shiv's Warset hunt (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`).

**Ru's founding blessing, a flagged one-time exception.** In the same Chapter 7 scene, Shiv personally and directly grants the young goblin companion (see `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`) an instant Goblin to Hobgoblin evolution and the "First Disciple of Shiv" title, explicitly marked in dialogue as his own one-off act, not the normal Whispers of Shiv recruitment flow. See `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md` for how this doesn't reopen the standard gradual-earning rule for every other companion.
