# Shiv

**Role:** Spectral goblin spirit, quest-giver for the "Call of the Bones" World Quest, historical founder-figure of organized goblin clan culture.

## Appearance

Ash-and-smoke form, wide-brimmed hat, burning green eyes.

## Facts

Strong character from the old draft, well-written voice, worth preserving almost entirely as-is. Grants Tatsuya (in-game name TDRAG) a legendary bound item, the first piece of "Shiv's Warset" (1 of 7): the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), which negates the goblin Wisdom penalty and unlocks the Shaman's Veil spirit magic path, plus Gambler's Gambit.

Worldbuilding tied to Shiv: goblins are historically barred from the capital; there's a hidden "Astralum Archive" beneath the palace that Shiv wants Tatsuya to eventually reach (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`).

**Power level: genuinely world-quest-tier, not player-scaled.** On manifesting, his presence alone hits any nearby player with an extreme, unavoidable slow effect, roughly four times slower, functionally unable to move or act. Not a spell that can be resisted or a normal debuff, a raw consequence of standing near something this far above a level-one character. Confirms his gold tag (see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`) isn't just ceremonial.

**A separate, active effect, distinct from the passive slow above.** When Shiv actually wants privacy for a conversation, he can cast a real, active spell that fully freezes every other NPC in the immediate zone mid-motion (not merely slowed) and visually shades the area, thin gray light, a low crawling mist, an isolated pocket where only he and whoever he's speaking to can act or move. First shown Chapter 6, when he pulls Tatsuya and the young goblin companion aside inside Tatsuya's own tent. A nearby player (as opposed to an NPC) caught in the same zone would only take the passive four-times slow above, not a full freeze, though this hasn't been dramatized on the page yet since Tatsuya's always alone with him so far. **Forward-plot seed:** Tatsuya eventually crafts a minor item that replicates a small-scale version of this freeze effect, see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`, not built yet.

## Backstory (partial reveal, Chapter 6)

Shiv tells Tatsuya part of his own history directly, in his own words, inside the frozen tent: he was once a human noble of Lunarest's own royal court, real name **Shivaris Jutsuin**. He loved the Queen, an affair the court's own mages didn't forgive once it came out, punishing him with what they called a correction rather than a curse. That correction remade him into the first of what the world now calls goblins, closer to his old human shape than most goblins who came after him, a hybrid rather than a clean transformation. He was driven out of the capital for it, and goblins and beastfolk have been hunted as feral ever since. **Deliberately partial:** he doesn't explain how he actually held Myrrhwood's clans together on that philosophy alone, or how the "whispers" mechanic (see Whispers of Shiv, `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`) actually works underneath. That's held back for Tatsuya to piece together gradually across later chapters, tied to the Whispers of Shiv subplot questline and the wider Shiv's Warset hunt (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`), not resolved in one scene.

**Ru's founding blessing, a flagged one-time exception.** In the same Chapter 6 scene, Shiv personally and directly grants the young goblin companion (see `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`) an instant Goblin to Hobgoblin evolution and the "First Disciple of Shiv" title, explicitly marked in dialogue as his own one-off act, not the normal Whispers of Shiv recruitment flow. See `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md` for how this doesn't reopen the standard gradual-earning rule for every other companion.
