# Jarvis ("Jarvy")

**Role:** The Tabuchi family's butler.

## Appearance

**Visual reference (locked):** dark hair, glasses, formal attire, a composed, professional bearing.

## Facts

Present alongside Eren from Chapter 1 onward, driving him on family business (as in the WattsUp confrontation), though driving isn't his household role, just this scene's function. Silent, unresponsive to Eren's jabs, never acknowledges Tatsuya directly.

**Old-draft naming note:** the old draft (see `bible/old-draft-chapter-bible.md`) used the name Shinji Takamura for a Tabuchi family butler/retainer figure who sent Tatsuya threatening messages. That's a superseded, distinct old-draft plot beat, not this character, Jarvis is the confirmed current-canon name and role.
