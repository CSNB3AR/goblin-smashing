# Wind Mist (Hushveil): full mechanics (author-stated, latest)

One place for everything. Skill name: **Wind Mist**, in the wind tree. "Hushveil" is the older name and survives as a nickname. Inventions are marked PROPOSAL. Her build is in `codex/characters/arya-build-and-mist-kit.md`.

## What it is

A conductor fog. It fills a small area (a room, a tent) around a point she picks.

**Original form, against enemies inside:**
- **Blinds** them: they cannot see out of the fog.
- **Mutes** them: anything using SP or stamina (Physical Arts) or MP is blocked. No spells, no Arts.
- PROPOSAL: sound is cut too, as a line of flavor, since "mute" reads that way. The author's rule is the resource block, so use that first.

**For the caster:** a live radar. She knows where everyone inside is, ally and enemy, as moving marks. This is why she can hold the mist without seeing the tent.

**As a conductor:** lightning conducts through it and does damage. Without the passive **Protective Lightning** that lightning would hit allies and her. With it, she can use lightning directly.

**Buffs inside for allies:** an attack buff OR a mana regeneration buff, so mana users are not draining while they fight. PROPOSAL: she picks which one at cast, since the author wrote "or". A caster with the MP buff can keep up Tatsuya's rune costs.

## The two casting forms

| | Sporadic | Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Up | 15 seconds | As long as she holds it |
| Then | 30 second cooldown | No cooldown, but she pays |
| Cost | One cast cost | MP every 2 seconds |
| Her body | Free to move | She cannot move |
| Weakness | Gaps | A hit breaks it |

PROPOSAL, numbers: the continuous tick is **2 MP every 2 seconds** (1 MP per second). It fits the author's "10 MP gives Tatsuya a ten second timer" exactly. A full 72 MP holds about 72 seconds, less the opening cast. PROPOSAL: a sporadic cast costs about 8 MP.

**Interruption.** Any hit on her breaks the continuous form. She is a level 10 caster with 88 HP, so she must be placed safe: in the trees behind the line, not in the tent. Whether the mist travels with her is OPEN; I read it as fixed on the point.

**Cooldown ambiguity (OPEN).** The old note says 15 s up, 30 s cooldown, then 15 s remaining after it drops (cooldown from cast). The author's latest line says "up 15 seconds, then a 30 second cooldown", cooldown after. I use the author's latest: gap of 30 s after the drop. The raiders' shot-caller then has a 30 second window, not 15. Rian waiting for it is more believable that way.

## The whistle calls

They cannot talk in person during the fight (only the director has voice chat; see the ledger voice rule). So she whistles.

- **Who:** Arya whistles to Tatsuya. He does not whistle back; he acts.
- **Low mana warning:** three whistles, slow, spaced apart, not run together.
- **At 10 MP:** three whistles again, which gives Tatsuya a **ten second timer**: the mist lasts about that long (5 ticks).
- Reading: the author wrote both a "running out" warning and "at 10 MP" with three whistles. Same pattern, urgency by position. Confirm.
- PROPOSAL, extras: one short whistle means she is recasting, two means someone is breaking in. Only add these if a scene needs them; the author gave only the three-whistle pattern.

## Interaction with Tatsuya's rune dice

- His rune work uses an **automatic dice check**. It is **NOT Gambler's Gambit**, so a fail does not break the die.
- Each try costs mana. The rune cooldown between tries is **6 seconds** (ledger, settled).
- He rolls **two crit fails**, then in the last second gets a number, even a four. **A weak rune is enough.**
- Timeline against the mist: a sporadic 15 s mist covers about two fails (6 s cooldown each) and the third try. The third lands at the edge of the ten second timer, and the whistle is what makes the last second feel earned.
- If she is on continuous cast the timer is the whistle, not the 15 s.
- The mist blocks the raiders' MP, so they cannot cast a counter to the rune, and the rune is not seen.

## Shiv-Touched Flare and the gas

The flare is a palm fireball, 4 damage (from his fingers, no ratchet box). It ignites the Goblin Gas. PROPOSAL: allies inside get the attack buff, and the flare is small enough that the conductor lightning is not needed for it. Lightning through the mist is optional; fire in the gas is the point. If she uses a lightning spell too, Protective Lightning is why Tatsuya and the goblins live.

## Chapter Eleven, an order of use (PROPOSAL)

1. **Setup.** She is in the trees and casts the mist sporadically so the setup is quiet. Tatsuya fails twice at the rune; she holds on.
2. **The window.** Rian keeps everyone in the tent while the mist is up. He thinks the gap is his.
3. **Runes land.** The third rune holds; the gap falls, she recasts the mist (continuous now), and they are blind and muted.
4. **Gas.** Ru and Zinka breathe in from both sides. The Elf and Beastfolk are stunned and die in the tent; the Dwarf and Human resist and die outside.
5. **Flare.** Tatsuya ignites the gas. Radar tells Arya who is moving.
6. **Mana.** She whistles three slow; then at 10 MP three more. Tatsuya finishes inside the ten seconds. Her title comes later.
