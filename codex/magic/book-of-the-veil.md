# Book of the Veil (Dark)

Spirit/death-adjacent, tied to loss, echoes, things that persist past where they should have ended. Rare specifically because it's rarely taught formally, it tends to find casters through circumstance (a bond, a haunting, a debt to the dead) rather than a school choosing to specialize someone into it.

## Tatsuya's path: Shaman's Veil

Tatsuya's affinity is Book of the Veil, tied directly to Shiv and the spirit-magic path Shaman's Veil opens (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), not a separate choice layered on top of it. Shiv himself reads as a spirit/death-adjacent figure, an echo, not a living caster, and the magic he passes down carries that same texture. This is consistent with the archetype's already-locked warlock-adjacent flavor (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, Shaman's Veil "leans sorcerer/warlock-flavored, not priest/cleric").

**Ancient Bone Flame Barrage itself reads Fire on the surface** (it's a literal flame effect), but the path delivering it, and anything Tatsuya grows into past it, carries the Book of the Veil affinity underneath, spirit-touched fire rather than a pure Book of Embers caster's version of the same spell. The distinction matters for how NPCs and other players read him: a Fire caster and Tatsuya can throw visually similar effects and still register as completely different kinds of caster to anyone who actually understands magic. See `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md` for the ability's mechanical depth (the three-rune-point activation requirement) and Ru's Rune Priming ability as the combo piece.

## Skill tree (Tatsuya's own, not a generic school tree)

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Ancient Bone Flame Barrage — where Tatsuya starts once Shaman's Veil actually opens. |
| 3-4 | The rune/mine tree (channel, arm, detonate), where the rune mechanics tied to the barrage above become genuinely usable. |
| 5-6 | Ancient Bone Flame Barrage's own chain-reaction elaboration (see `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`), growth here means an existing spell getting more dangerous, not a new spell unlocking. |
| 7 | *(unclaimed, no known Umbral caster has reached it)* |
| 8 | *(theoretical)* |

Unlike the other six schools, the Book of the Veil's Tier 1-2 and Tier 5-6 entries point back to the same named spell rather than a fresh one each band, this is Shaman's Veil's own structure, not a general Book of the Veil tree other casters would climb the same way. **Shaman's Veil scales purely on Wisdom.** Fortune has no relationship to it at all, a deliberate, locked separation, not an oversight. The Call of the Bones Necklace's own +5 Fortune bonus is a separate, ordinary item-stat grant, unrelated to the magic path it also happens to unlock.
