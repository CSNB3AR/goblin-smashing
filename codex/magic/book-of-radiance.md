# Book of Radiance (Light)

Healing and buff-leaning, the temple/school-trained school almost by default. Reads communal, a caster who thinks about a fight in terms of the whole party rather than themselves.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Minor Mend — closes shallow wounds, the school's entry spell, every temple-trained caster's first lesson. |
| 3-4 | Warding Circle — a shield that protects everyone standing inside it for its duration. |
| 5-6 | Cascading Renewal — a healing effect that jumps between multiple wounded targets in sequence. |
| 7 | Dawnbreak — a battlefield-wide restorative/purifying effect, rare, most casters never get here. |
| 8 | *(theoretical)* |

## The Lumenara line (Seranis-aligned)

A short healer line that runs alongside the main tree above, taught by the temples that honor **Seranis** (see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`). It fits the existing tiers rather than adding new ones. Every spell is Wisdom-gated like the rest of the school (Wisdom 18 to open casting, see `codex/magic/overview.md`).

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Lumenara: a soft guiding light. Lights old etchings and sealed marks, and living things lean toward it. A utility spell, the line's entry cast, no healing. |
| 3-4 | Lumenara Pura: targeted, sustained healing on one patient. It pushes back resource drain and slowly works poison-type ailments out of the body over its duration. It costs the caster stamina as well as mana, so it cannot be held indefinitely. |
| 3-4 | Lumenara Aegis: a protective barrier over the caster or one ally that turns aside projectiles and bolts. A single-target cousin of Warding Circle, and its limit is the caster's own reserves. |

Rules that apply:
- **Cleanses come only from healer classes or towns.** Pura is a healer-class cleanse and works the ailment out slowly, it does not erase a stack instantly. Antidotes still take time, so a poisoned fighter with no antidote and no healer in reach is still in real trouble.
- **No free reset.** Pura drains the caster each time and can fail if the caster is weak, hurt, interrupted or out of reserves.

Provenance: adapted from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles (BKC-Files), where Lumenara, Lumenara Pura and Lumenara Aegis are the church healer's doctrine spells. Names and roles are kept, the divine-tier rating there is not.
