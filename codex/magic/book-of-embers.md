# Book of Embers (Fire)

Aggressive, immediate, burst-damage-leaning. Casters who land here tend to come out of combat schools or self-taught trial-and-error rather than formal instruction, temperament matters more than pedigree. Reads hot-tempered and direct on the page: fast cast times, short cooldowns, little in the way of subtlety.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Ember Dart — a small, aimed flame projectile, the standard opening combat spell. |
| 3-4 | Flame Wall — a standing barrier of fire, area denial rather than a single target. |
| 5-6 | Chain Combustion — one ignition point sets off a sequence of linked flame bursts. |
| 7 | Inferno Front — reshapes a stretch of terrain into an actively burning zone, rare, most casters never get here. |
| 8 | *(theoretical, no player has reached it)* |
