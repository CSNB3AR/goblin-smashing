# Book of Frost (Frost)

Control-first, built around slowing a fight down rather than ending it fast. Common among casters trained in structured military or guild academies where patience gets drilled in early. Reads calculating, a caster who wants the fight to happen on their terms and is willing to wait for it.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Frost Bolt — a slowing cold projectile, the school's entry combat spell. |
| 3-4 | Binding Rime — an icy field that roots anyone standing in it. |
| 5-6 | Shattering Chain — a frozen target that, on breaking, spreads the freeze to whatever's nearby. |
| 7 | Absolute Zero Field — a battlefield-reshaping cold zone, rare, most casters never get here. |
