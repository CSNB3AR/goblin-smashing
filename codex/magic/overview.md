# Magic — Overview

Two separate questions govern any caster: **whether** they can cast at all (race and Wisdom, this file and `codex/races/overview.md`), and **what kind** of magic they lean into once they can (school, one file per school in this folder). A third question, **how much** a caster can actually do, is the tier system below.

## Opening casting: the Wisdom-18 threshold

A character needs a minimum of **Wisdom 18** to open tier-1 casting in any school at all, "unlocking vocation" in the loose player-facing phrase. This is a real, deliberate gate, not a soft recommendation: a character who doesn't start close to it (see `codex/races/overview.md` for base Wisdom by race) is practically locked out early on, since clearing it means pouring leveling points (`codex/lore/leveling-and-progression.md`) into Wisdom at the expense of everything else across many levels, a real opportunity cost, not a free unlock. This is on top of, not instead of, the racial access tiers in `codex/races/overview.md` (fully open / partial / restricted / fully locked). A fully-locked race (Beastfolk, Dwarf) can't open a school at Wisdom 18 or Wisdom 800, the two gates are independent.

## Tiers, functional only

Not a named hierarchy (no "circles," "ranks," or similar flavor label), just numbered tiers 1 through 8, kept plain and functional. **Tier 8 is the practical ceiling for a player character**, nothing above that is reachable through normal play. Kept vague/flavor-level rather than an exhaustive mechanical breakdown, per the house style rule against dumping stat blocks (`codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`):

- **Tier 1-2** — a single small, controlled effect.
- **Tier 3-4** — real area/duration control, an effect that lingers or covers ground rather than a single instant.
- **Tier 5-6** — multiple effects chained or triggered off each other.
- **Tier 7** — effects that reshape the immediate battlefield rather than just hitting something in it, rare, most casters in the world never get here.
- **Tier 8** — the practical ceiling. Reality-bending in scale, vanishingly rare, world-quest/boss-tier casters only.

Access and power are two different gates. Shaman's Veil (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is what grants Tatsuya access to a casting path in the first place, since goblins are otherwise restricted by the standard Wisdom gate above. Tier progression within that path is a separate, later climb, earned through Wisdom growth and his own alchemy/crafting research (see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`), not granted by the bond itself.

See `codex/magic/tier-reference.md` for representative basic spells at each tier, drawn across schools, a general illustrative reference rather than one school's own tree.

## How a caster ends up in a school

Not a hard racial lock, no race is pre-assigned a school. It comes from background, temperament, and who actually trains a character, if anyone, once they pursue a school seriously. A caster raised in a temple leans Radiance almost by osmosis. A battlefield-taught caster with no formal school often lands Book of the Arcane, generalist by necessity, nobody around to specialize them into anything else. A school isn't chosen from a menu at character creation, it emerges from how a character actually comes into their power.

## Two named mechanics

- **Amplify magic** — a caster can spend extra MP to push a known spell's effect temporarily above the tier they've actually unlocked, at real cost and real risk (see `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), a genuine gamble, not a safe stat purchase. The Bones Speak's own Amplify mode (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is a once-a-day, free, Fortune-scaled version of this same general concept, specific to Tatsuya and the necklace, the two aren't the same system but read as the same family of idea.
- **Dual-casting** — maintaining two spells' effects active at the same time, a real Wisdom-gated difficulty spike rather than something any caster can do casually.
