# Book of Tides (Water)

Flow and displacement, a school about moving things (people, ground, the fight itself) rather than hurting or freezing them in place. Common among casters raised near rivers, harbors and coastal fortresses, and among guild casters who want to break a formation instead of kill it. Reads patient and relentless, a caster who treats a fight like a current that eventually wins. Working name, see Open.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Tide Lash: a whipping jet of water that shoves its target back a step, the school's entry combat spell. |
| 3-4 | Undertow Field: a flowing patch of ground that drags everyone standing in it toward its center, a pull and a drift rather than a root. |
| 5-6 | Pressure Surge: compressed water released in a crushing burst, harder-hitting on anything already soaked or standing in water. |
| 7 | Flood Tide: a wave that rolls across the battlefield, moving people, cover and loose terrain, rare, most casters never get here. |
| 8 | *(theoretical)* |

## How it plays

- **Displacement over damage.** Tides breaks positions. It pulls, pushes and scatters, which makes it a natural partner for Frost (which holds things in place) and Stone (which builds the walls Tides then floods around).
- **Terrain-hungry.** It casts anywhere, but it draws on nearby water: strongest at rivers, coasts and rain, weakest in deserts and dry holds. Sunmarch is a hard place to be a Tides caster.
- **Interactions.** Soaked targets take more from Pressure Surge. Whether soaked targets also take more from Tempest lightning or freeze faster under Frost is left open, so no stacking rule is locked here.
- **Not Hushveil.** The wind-and-water fog in `codex/magic/book-of-tempest.md` stays a Tempest spell. Tides does not take it over.
