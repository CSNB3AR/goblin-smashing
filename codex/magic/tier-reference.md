# Magic Tier Reference — Basic Entry Spells

A general, cross-school illustrative reference: three representative basic spells per tier, not tied to any single school, meant to make each tier concrete rather than an abstract number when one appears on the page. Each school's own file (`codex/magic/book-of-embers.md` and siblings) carries its own deeper, school-specific tree; this file is the quick-reference layer above all of them. See `codex/magic/overview.md` for what each tier band actually means functionally.

## Tier 1 — a single small, controlled effect

- **Spark Touch** — a minor shock on contact, barely more than a static jolt, the kind of first spell every caster's temple or academy actually starts them on.
- **Mend Graze** — closes a shallow cut or bruise, no real combat use, a caster's first taste of Radiance-flavored effects regardless of their eventual school.
- **Chill Draft** — a cold gust, enough to fog a window or discourage an insect swarm, nothing close to combat-viable.

## Tier 2 — still a single effect, real enough to matter in a fight

- **Ember Dart** — a small, aimed flame projectile, the standard opening combat spell across most Fire-leaning schools.
- **Frost Bolt** — the Book of Frost equivalent, a slowing cold projectile rather than raw damage.
- **Static Lash** — a short-range disruptive jolt, Book of Tempest's entry combat spell.

## Tier 3-4 — real area/duration control

- **Flame Wall** — a standing barrier of fire, area denial rather than a single target.
- **Binding Rime** — an icy field that roots anyone standing in it.
- **Warding Circle** — a Radiance ward that shields everyone standing inside it for its duration.

## Tier 5-6 — multiple effects chained or triggered

- **Chain Combustion** — one ignition point sets off a sequence of linked flame bursts.
- **Shattering Chain** — a frozen target that, on breaking, spreads the freeze to whatever's nearby.
- **Cascading Renewal** — a healing effect that jumps between multiple wounded targets in sequence.

## Tier 7 — battlefield-reshaping, rare

- **Inferno Front** — reshapes a stretch of terrain into an actively burning zone, most casters in the world never get here.
- **Mountain's Judgment** — a Book of Stone effect that visibly reworks the terrain itself, walls rising, ground splitting.
- **Eye of the Squall** — a localized storm system a Book of Tempest caster can actually stand inside and fight from.

## Tier 8 — the practical ceiling, theoretical for nearly everyone

- **Absolute Zero Field** *(theoretical, no known player has reached it)*
- **Dawnbreak** *(theoretical)*
- **Null Zone** *(theoretical, Book of the Arcane's own ceiling effect, cancels magic outright across an area)*

Tatsuya's own path, Ancient Bone Flame Barrage and its later chain-reaction elaboration, sits outside this general reference, it's Book of the Veil-flavored but sourced specifically through Shaman's Veil rather than a generic school tree, see `codex/magic/book-of-the-veil.md` and `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`.
