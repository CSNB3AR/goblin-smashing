# Book of Tempest (Storm)

Mobility and disruption, lightning/wind-flavored, favored by casters who came up fast and loose, self-taught duelists, open-world PvP veterans. Reads erratic and hard to pin down, the school with the least patience for a drawn-out fight.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Static Lash — a short-range disruptive jolt, the school's entry combat spell. |
| 3-4 | Gale Step — a short, wind-assisted burst of repositioning, control and mobility over raw damage. |
| 5-6 | Chain Lightning Storm — a jumping bolt that arcs between multiple targets. |
| 7 | Eye of the Squall — a localized storm system the caster can stand inside and fight from, rare, most casters never get here. |
