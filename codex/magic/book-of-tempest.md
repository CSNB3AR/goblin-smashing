# Book of Tempest (Storm)

Mobility and disruption, lightning/wind-flavored, favored by casters who came up fast and loose, self-taught duelists, open-world PvP veterans. Reads erratic and hard to pin down, the school with the least patience for a drawn-out fight.

## Skill tree

| Tier | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Static Lash — a short-range disruptive jolt, the school's entry combat spell. |
| 3-4 | Gale Step — a short, wind-assisted burst of repositioning, control and mobility over raw damage. |
| 5-6 | Hushveil — a wind-and-water fog, dense enough to cut visibility hard in its radius. Base version is a crowd-control utility spell, easy to dismiss as low-value since it deals no damage on its own. |
| 7 | Eye of the Squall — a localized storm system the caster can stand inside and fight from, rare, most casters never get here. |

## Hushveil, advanced upgrade (Silence)

Spending additional discipline points upgrades Hushveil past its base fog into a genuine **Silence** effect within its radius, Wisdom-scaling. In full-dive VR this isn't cosmetic: real auditory loss inside the fog, no footsteps, no incoming-attack cues, plus disabled spellcasting and Physical Arts for anyone caught inside without a melee weapon already drawn, a hard counter to casters specifically, forces melee-range engagement. Costs real discipline points most players consider poorly spent on a spell that looks useless next to direct-damage options, which is exactly why it tends to sit unlearned on paper builds. Arya has the base Hushveil but never the upgrade, having written it off the same way, until Chapter 8.

**Friendly-fire caution, real and unstated until now.** Hushveil's effects don't distinguish ally from enemy inside its radius, allies without a melee weapon already drawn lose spellcasting and Physical Arts the same as anyone else caught in it. This is why Tatsuya's own combo timing around it matters, allies who need to fight from inside the fog (Ru, Rott, Nab) go in with blades already drawn, not relying on abilities the fog itself would strip from them.

**Arya's real build identity: a field-spell-heavy tempest mage, not a direct-damage caster.** Beyond Hushveil, she leans on combining the school's mist/fog utility with Static Lash's disruptive charge to lay down lightning-touched field effects, damage-over-time zones rather than direct-cast bolts, playing to Tempest's control identity instead of fighting it. **A field spell has a real breaking point: she can't hold one up forever.** Sustaining it drains her mana continuously, not just on cast, which is exactly what creates the timing window Tatsuya's own traps and marks are built to exploit, her field spell buys the setup time, his kit lands the actual kill before her mana runs out.
