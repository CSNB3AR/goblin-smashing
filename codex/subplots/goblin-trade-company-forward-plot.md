# Subplot: The Goblin Trade Company

**Status:** Forward plot thread, not drafted yet.

## The plan

Later in the story, Tatsuya builds a goblin trade company in Myrrhwood, using goblin-clan access to local materials to craft artifacts, a real extension of his father's tech legacy and his own tinkering instinct carried into the game itself. He partners with Beastfolk there and eventually earns a standing relationship with a Dwarf NPC willing to craft items for him on a regular basis.

## Ties to established canon

- The Myrrhwood advantage: goblin clan players get above-average advantages there that opponents consistently underestimate, see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`.
- The player-owned economy mechanic (craft houses, territory, upkeep), see `codex/lore/player-owned-economy-and-living-world-npcs.md`.
- Daichi's tinkering legacy, already the throughline for the Haya EB and the pod override, see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md` and `codex/objects/haya-eb.md`.
