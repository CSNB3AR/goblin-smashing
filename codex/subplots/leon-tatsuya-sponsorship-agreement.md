# Subplot: The Leon and Tatsuya Sponsorship Agreement

**Status:** Author-stated (latest). Ledger and codex only. On the page the agreement stays light and "smooth on paper" (Chapter Eight shows the signing, the joke and the fine print, nothing more). Dictation garbles read as: "Tatya" is Tatsuya; "MDOR" is read as Tatsuya (flagged).

## What the agreement is

- An **open, neutral 50-50** agreement. Both sides are equal and **either can walk away at any time**, especially if it harms their brand. Leon did the same with Arya's guild (Chapter Seven: "Some guild drama got in the way, and I had to pull back").
- Leon does **not** dictate what Tatsuya does or how he runs his guild.
- His one ask is **media rights**: when Tatsuya streams or posts clips, he tags the arcade. No ads every five minutes. Leon's thinking is media coverage, because Tatsuya holds the biggest World Quest in the world outside the base game.

## Logo timing and security

Leon knows he cannot put the Sakura Arcade logo on the stream until the arcade has enough in it worth advertising and there is security for Tatsuya. Tagging it at once would show that Sakura Arcade has a pod that runs HGO. People would crowd the arcade, or try to recruit Tatsuya early by going there. So the stream goes out without the tag for now (Chapter Eight: "something we can market once we're ready").

## What Leon is hoping for

That Tatsuya gets the processor cores up early, and that the World Quest and the stream bring in money.

## The stream and the money

- Leon manages the live stream from the booth. Yes, subscribers and donations come in. Nothing gates it to a channel that is a month old, but monthly analytics need a month of streaming history, so starting early helps.
- **The split, as dictated (unclear, OPEN):** the cut is 50-50. Leon's half is used on the arcade, split roughly **25 to infrastructure and refurbishing Sakura Arcade and 25 to the processor cores** (Tatsuya's project). The aim is money that keeps being generated, to bring in newer equipment.

## First conflict of interest (forward plot, not on the page)

Once money is coming in and the streams are going well, Leon asks to bring in **second-line pods** (Kuroshio). Tatsuya outright refuses. If any pods come in, they are **third-line (Nexus) pods**. That is his cutoff, consistent with the rule that he will not use anything the Tabuchis back (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`).

## The split, as negotiated on the page in Chapter Eight (author-stated, latest; refines the money section above)

Leon writes the paperwork **80-20 in Tatsuya's favor**, because Tatsuya carries the quest. Tatsuya asks for **50-50, with a renegotiation after six months**: he put one of Leon's pods at risk to get in, and he wants money going into the processor cores and getting both pods running. Leon's half then runs roughly 25 to the cores and 25 to whatever the arcade needs. Leon asks "You sure about that?", and says most people do not ask for less; it reads as trustworthy partners, not a typical businessman's move. Leon crosses out the numbers and initials the change, and Tatsuya signs under it.

**Later:** the upstairs pavilion becomes the pro team's space, with a security guard once there is popularity and money to pay one. The logo stays off until Tatsuya is in a good position, ideally with his own pod, and the arcade can hire security or more staff or has better infrastructure.
