# Subplot: Leon and Arya Sakura's Custody Battle

**Status:** Background, seeded on the page already (Chapter 9), full mechanism locked here. Reopens as a real, dramatized conflict in Act Two.

## The setup

Leon and Arya's grandfather died roughly four years ago and left them Sakura Arcade. Before he died, he was also the reason Leon actually won formal guardianship of Arya: their parents, living overseas, not abusive but genuinely not equipped to raise her well, tried to take her back, and the grandfather's real legal backing, an actual lawyer, actual resources, is what let Leon win that first custody fight. Arya was young enough that the details never fully registered as a "fight" to her at the time, just an outcome: she stayed with Leon.

## The real, live vulnerability

With the grandfather gone and no money for another legal battle, that first win was never permanent. This is the actual foundation under Arya's already-drafted Chapter 9 dialogue ("Last time I let someone in this close, it cost me everything," the not-wanting-to-go-back-to-America lines), not new information contradicting it, the explanation underneath something the reader's already seen. Her parents pushing for her to come live with them and attend medical school in America (already locked, see `codex/characters/arya.md`) isn't just a preference clash, it's the custody fight, quietly, threatening to reopen.

## Act Two: the fight actually reopens, and how it gets winnable again

Once Tochi extends Arya a real scholarship (see `codex/pro-scene/tochi-representative-qualifier.md`, `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`), tied to Haya Storm winning the school's representative slot, her parents' side pushes back for real, family and/or their own lawyers showing up to contest the arrangement, and Leon flatly states he'll bring in lawyers of his own if it comes to that (already locked in `outline/act-two-haya-storm-rise.md`).

**The scholarship is the actual legal linchpin, not just emotional leverage.** Tochi is one of the most prestigious academies in Japan. Arya being enrolled there, regardless of the exact mechanism, esports merit rather than academics, doesn't weaken the argument, only that she's there matters, is concrete, close-to-undeniable proof that Leon has raised her well. It leaves her parents' lawyers with a genuinely hard case: arguing she should be pulled out of one of the country's top schools, the kind of place most of the actual elite send their own kids, doesn't play well in front of anyone. This is what replaces the grandfather's money as Leon's real leverage this time around, an actual institutional fact, not sentiment.
