# Forward Plot: Tatsuya's Combat Archetype (Arcane Bomber)

**Status:** Forward-looking build/ability lore, codex only. The merged home for his build (the Build direction that used to sit in `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md` now points here). Not on-page in Chapter 4, seeded here so later chapters build toward it consistently. File name kept so old cross-references resolve.

## The archetype

**Arcane Bomber** is Tatsuya's **unique advanced class**, not a base class other players draw from (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). He is never boxed into the base class list. It reads as the evolution of what he already self-describes as "something closer to a brigand than a caster or a brawler, gadgets and misdirection over raw power" (see `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`).

It is an **amplifier**: low raw damage, multiplied by stacked debuffs. Each ability carries a **per-stack bonus** on a debuffed target, with no new stat and no named passive. The loop is **control, stack, ignite, finish**. The scaling stat is Wisdom (the game has no Intelligence stat), and Fortune rides on top as it already does for crits.

Not a healer, not a tank, not a hard-CC caster. A utility amplifier that holds a position with tools (smoke, traps, gas), sets up disruption, and wins fights through setup and combo burst rather than raw sustained output.

**Party engine** (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`): Pac_Pac holds and enrages, Tatsuya roots and stacks debuffs while his wards and enchantments keep Pac_Pac alive, Arya detonates. This is what the build complements.

## Magic path

Shaman's Veil (already established, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) leans sorcerer/warlock-flavored, not priest/cleric. It does not hand Tatsuya finished spells. What it actually does: removes the goblin's Wisdom-restricted magic-access lockout (see `codex/races/goblin.md`) and opens the Arcane Bomber path to him at all, spirit-flavored since it is Shiv's blessing. Real mastery, runework, amplifying, anything past the basics, is not handed over by the bond. He has to separately pursue alchemy and crafting research and materials himself (see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`) to grow into it, an earned progression tied to his own effort, climbing the tier system as he goes (`codex/magic/overview.md`). His school within the path is Book of the Veil (formerly "Dark"), see `codex/magic/book-of-the-veil.md`. He notices the Wisdom-restriction angle himself during morning research early in Chapter 5.

**Debuff stacking, Goblin Gas to Vile Curse.** The Goblin Gas nausea stack (4x, then nauseous) is the seed. The smoke evolves into a stackable poison curse, visually distinct from his fire (green poison skulls, one per stack). At 4 stacks the next ability that lands on the target triggers a burst. Not yet happened as of Chapter 10: Tatsuya is only beginning to clock that stacking debuffs plus fire ignition is pointing at something he has not mapped. **Cleanses** only come from healer classes or towns, and antidotes take time, which is what makes his stacks stick.

## Racial and enchantment support

- **Nimble Hands** (goblin racial, see `codex/races/goblin.md`): picks pockets through doors and grants an arcane-tech proficiency. Karma and Affiliation costs apply, no player pickpocketing in green zones.
- **Enchantment is Rune Priming.** He primes runes onto battlefield terrain (Ancient Bone Flame Barrage's setup, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) and the same skill lets him hand-inscribe a rune insignia directly onto tools, gadgets and weapons ahead of combat. A real, separate craft/prep step, not instant or free. Higher-tier insignias cost more mana to seat and carry a real failure chance, consistent with the crafting success-rate mechanic in `codex/crafting/overview.md`. This is also how he buffs allies' weapons and wards Pac_Pac.

## Weapons (revised from the earlier shuriken, kunai and tanto plan)

- **Wands.** Crafted, burnable, charge-limited, bone-and-wire. Cheap enough to burn through in a fight, which is why buying raw materials (competing directly against his own debt and surgery money, see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`) becomes a felt cost.
- **A reusable rune staff.** The sturdier option, runed ahead of time.
- **Conjured arcane daggers.** Short-lived magical blades, not creatures.
- **A short sword** on the gadget belt as the backup.

Why this instead of a conventional weapon: runic amplification is built around crafted foci, and nobody builds seriously around burnable wands as a primary, a genuinely unclaimed niche rather than a reskinned mage build.

**No summoning of creatures, anywhere, ever.** Conjured daggers and the ring below do not count as summoning (see Open items).

## Control

- **Root.** First a **gadget-belt snare** (a thrown or placed trap), later **Chain Root**: arcane blue chains rip from the ground and root targets for roughly 5 seconds, can catch several targets at once for a Y-shaped group kill range. Roots are **short, conditional and resistible** and are not hard CC in the sense the archetype rules out.
- **Sigils** stay zone-control and crafting oriented, not primary combat, slow to place in active combat unless covered.

## Visual language

Green poison skulls (stacks), fire orange turning blue-white as it scales (ignition), arcane blue chains (root).

## Early PvP learning curve (confirmed forward-plot, Crownfall prep arc)

His first real PvP reps go badly, and the reason is mechanical: zone-control and Crownfall objectives hold for a short window, and a purely trap-and-wait, setup-heavy playstyle cannot keep pace. This is the concrete in-fiction reason he needs the mobile kit above, immediate-use tools rather than only pre-armed traps. The troop system covers some of the gap (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`).

## Future signature abilities

- **Ancient Bone Flame Barrage is the only spell here, with no separate name for its later, more developed form.** A short channel/placement action draws a rune on the ground or wall, a brief arming delay, then it is live, invisible to enemies, shown as a hologram to allies only. An enemy who steps in triggers the flame trap. Costs MP, scales with Wisdom (per `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), has a real cooldown, no spamming multiple markers early. It combos with Goblin Gas (a marker igniting a gas cloud is the same family as the Chapter 3 Gas + Flare combo, higher tier).
  - **Chain-reaction elaboration (later mastery):** multiple markers, or a marker plus a live gas cloud, trigger each other.
  - **Crit tie-in:** no new stat, just Fortune doing what it already does, applied to a target caught in a marked zone or otherwise debuffed.
- **The doppelganger ring** replaces the earlier idea of a Clone spell. See below.
- **A steampunk sidearm**, tool-based, fed by a tank on his back, switching between Goblin Gas (caustic shot) and Flare (fire shot). Details in `codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`.
- **A goblin-built turret**, tied to the future goblin trade company, not his personal kit. A product of the trade company once it exists (see `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`). **Balance niche, locked:** underperforms in fast objective fights such as Crownfall (too slow and setup-heavy for that pace), but genuinely strong in Node Wars and PvE/base-defense content (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`).

## The doppelganger ring

Any duplicate or clone trick in his kit is the doppelganger ring, a battle item, not a spell, unrelated to Shiv's blessing or Shaman's Veil (`codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`). **It drops in the Chapter 10 fight (Part Two, on the page) from Joss, the enchanter, a legacy item, an illusion-mist ring.** Its **MP cost to activate is 60**, against his 32 MP full bar, so it is out of reach for now. Earlier docs planned a Gambler's Gambit proc in Chapter 9, but the ring has never landed on the page, so nothing needed removing from the chapter.

## What we deliberately didn't take from an outside build-concept reference

A separately drafted "Arcane Bomber Goblin" build concept was reviewed and mostly folded in. Explicitly not adopted:
- No new stats (that reference used "Intelligence," "Agility," "Haste," a standalone "Luck"). Everything maps onto the existing six stats. A cooldown-reduction effect, if one ever appears, is gear flavor, not a stat. The class name "Arcane Bomber" is adopted, "Steampunk Arcanist" is not.
- No standalone gambling/misfire mechanic, Fortune and Gambler's Gambit already do this narratively.
- No separate "lane lock" zone ultimate, folded into Ancient Bone Flame Barrage.
- No creature summoning, and no "Ifrit" spell name, that naming is retired.

## Open items

- **Root versus "no hard CC":** default is a short, conditional, resistible root, as written above.
- **Summoning boundary:** default is that conjured daggers and the ring are allowed, creature summons never.
- **Doppelganger ring resource:** the earlier ruling put it on SP at an accelerated drain, the page now shows an MP cost (60 to activate). Treat MP as canon on the page unless the author says otherwise.

Nothing in this file is unlocked or referenced on the page yet. It exists so future chapters stay consistent with this design.

## Staff of the Mist God, troops and enchants (Addendum H)

- **Gun-blade form.** The Staff of the Mist God (see `codex/objects/staff-of-the-mist-god.md`) has a two-handed energy rifle / gadget gun-blade form that suits his shoot-and-move play while he casts through the staff. It also stacks enchantments faster at lower MP cost and raises a fallen player **once every seven days**, which offsets the 72-hour death lock.
- **Troop enchants.** Tatsuya is the first to fill guild slots with NPC troops. Enchants multiply on them and last longer than on players, since troops do not follow the player system, so he can build super-soldiers (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`, owned by another agent). Crownfall does not cap troops.

**Open:** the staff's name and minuses.
