# Forward Plot: Tatsuya's Combat Archetype (Trap-Bomber Brigand)

**Status:** Forward-looking build/ability lore, codex only. Not on-page in Chapter 4, seeded here so later chapters build toward it consistently.

## The archetype

Not a healer, not a hard-CC caster. A "hardpoint"-style objective-control support-through-utility DPS: holds a position, uses tools (smoke, traps, gas) for lane denial and disruption, wins fights through setup and combo burst rather than raw sustained output. Already consistent with his established self-read as "something closer to a brigand than a caster or a brawler, gadgets and misdirection over raw power" (see `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`). Visual/kit identity leans steampunk-gadget, not a conventional blade or staff.

## Magic path

Shaman's Veil (already established, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) leans sorcerer/warlock-flavored, not priest/cleric. Ties to a real mechanical hook: goblins carry the standard Wisdom-restricted magic-access penalty (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`), but unlike some other magic-restricted races, they aren't hard-capped below a usable Wisdom threshold, meaning Tatsuya can genuinely scale into real spellcasting over time rather than being structurally locked out. He notices this himself during morning research early in Chapter 5, not something to land in Chapter 4.

## Future signature abilities

- **Ifrit, the big Shiv-tied signature spell (fire trap ultimate):** the mechanism, spelled out, a short channel/placement action draws an insignia on the ground or a wall, a brief arming delay, then it's live, invisible to enemies, shown as a hologram to allies only. An enemy who steps into the marked area triggers a flame trap. Costs MP, scales with Wisdom (per `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), a real cooldown, no spamming multiple markers at once early on. A real trap-bomber signature move, combos naturally with his existing gas-based kit (Goblin Gas + an Ifrit marker igniting it is the same family of interaction as the already-established Goblin Gas + Goblin Flare combo from Chapter 3, just a higher tier).
  - **Chain-reaction elaboration (later mastery, not day one):** multiple placed markers, or an Ifrit marker plus a live Goblin Gas cloud, can trigger each other. A real, earned combo moment once he's actually built up multiple markers and the resource pool to support them, not available from the start.
  - **Crit tie-in:** no new stat or named passive, this is just Fortune doing what it already does (see `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`), applied contextually, a hit landed against something already caught in a marked zone or otherwise debuffed rolls its existing crit chance/magnitude the normal way, nothing new bolted on.
- **Shadow clone:** a genuine combat-duplicate ability, not a scouting tool, capable of real double-damage-style output in a fight. Limited to one clone at a time, gated by his Wisdom stat. Dark-magic-flavored, fits the warlock-adjacent Shaman's Veil path.
- **Spectral Wolf, a Shiv-granted mount, doubling as the shadow-clone creature above.** Proposed merge, not something explicitly locked yet: rather than two separate spirit-creatures, the wolf is both his actual combat-duplicate ability and something he can physically ride, one creature doing two jobs instead of two redundant ones. First conceived during his Chapter 5 morning-research beat (see `outline/chapter-05.md`).
- **A goblin-built turret, tied to the future goblin trade company, not his personal kit.** Not available early, a real product of the trade company once it exists (see `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`), development time in-world, not a day-one gadget. **Balance niche, locked now so it doesn't need re-deciding later:** underperforms in ranked 5v5 (too slow and setup-heavy for that pace, doesn't undercut his established competitive identity), but genuinely strong in GvG Wars and PvE/base-defense content (both already-established systems, see `codex/lore/competitive-pvp-and-esports-structure.md`), giving it a real reason to exist without breaking the balance already established for his kit.

## What we deliberately didn't take from an outside build-concept reference

A separately-drafted "Arcane Bomber Goblin" build concept was reviewed against everything above and mostly folded in as elaboration on what already existed (the mine/rune mechanic is the Ifrit ultimate, not a new ability). Explicitly **not** adopted, flagged so it doesn't get silently re-proposed later:
- No formal class name ("Steampunk Arcanist" or otherwise), his kit stays its own thing, never boxed into the base class list other players draw from (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`).
- No new stats (that reference used "Intelligence," "Agility," "Haste," a standalone "Luck"), everything maps onto the existing six stats. A cooldown-reduction effect, if it ever shows up, is gear/itemization flavor, not a stat.
- No standalone gambling/misfire mechanic as a separate system, Fortune and Gambler's Gambit already do this narratively.
- No separate "lane lock" zone ultimate, folded into Ifrit itself rather than becoming a second ultimate-tier ability.

Nothing in this file is unlocked or referenced on the page yet. This file exists so future chapters introducing any of it stay consistent with this design rather than inventing something contradictory later.
