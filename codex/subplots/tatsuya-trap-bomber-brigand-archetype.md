# Forward Plot: Tatsuya's Combat Archetype (Trap-Bomber Brigand)

**Status:** Forward-looking build/ability lore, codex only. Not on-page in Chapter 4, seeded here so later chapters build toward it consistently.

## The archetype

Not a healer, not a hard-CC caster. A "hardpoint"-style objective-control support-through-utility DPS: holds a position, uses tools (smoke, traps, gas) for lane denial and disruption, wins fights through setup and combo burst rather than raw sustained output. Already consistent with his established self-read as "something closer to a brigand than a caster or a brawler, gadgets and misdirection over raw power" (see `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`). Visual/kit identity leans steampunk-gadget, not a conventional blade or staff.

## Magic path

Shaman's Veil (already established, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) leans sorcerer/warlock-flavored, not priest/cleric. It does not hand Tatsuya finished spells. What it actually does: removes the goblin's Wisdom-restricted magic-access lockout (see `codex/races/goblin.md`) and opens the arcane-bomber magic path to him at all, spirit-flavored since it's Shiv's blessing. Real mastery, runework, amplifying, anything past the basics, is not handed over by the bond. He has to separately pursue alchemy and crafting research and materials himself (see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`) to actually grow into it, an earned progression tied to his own effort, not an instant unlock, climbing the tier system as he goes (`codex/magic/overview.md`). His school within the path is Book of the Veil (formerly "Dark"), see `codex/magic/book-of-the-veil.md`. He notices the Wisdom-restriction angle himself during morning research early in Chapter 5, not something to land in Chapter 4.

**No summoning magic, anywhere, ever.** Not a later tier, not a different flavor of it, not part of this build at all.

## Base weapons: rune-inscribed shuriken, kunai, and a tanto-style blade (confirmed, Chapter 9+)

Not sword, bow, or staff. Tatsuya's actual base weapons are shuriken and kunai, plus a short, thigh-sheathed tanto-style blade, none of them normally treated as a primary weapon by anyone else in the game, shuriken and kunai exist in the item pool strictly as throwables. **This is Rune Priming's own use extended, not a new ability.** He already primes runes onto battlefield terrain (Ancient Bone Flame Barrage's setup, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`); the same skill lets him hand-inscribe a rune insignia directly onto a metal weapon instead, a real, separate craft/prep step done ahead of combat, not an instant or free effect. Higher-tier insignias cost more mana to seat and carry a real failure chance, consistent with the crafting success-rate mechanic already locked in `codex/crafting/overview.md`. He has to actually prep a stock of runed throwables before a fight, not conjure them out of nothing mid-combat, which is why buying raw materials (competing directly against his own debt/surgery money, see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`) becomes a real, felt cost. Why this over a conventional weapon: runic amplification specifically reads as effective against metal, and nobody's built seriously around shuriken/kunai as a primary weapon, a genuinely unclaimed niche rather than a reskinned sword build.

## Confirmed: no new spell, no summoning, "shadow clone" stays the doppelganger ring

Any duplicate/clone-style mobility trick in Tatsuya's future kit is, and stays, the already-locked doppelganger ring battle item below, not a new spell and not summoning magic. The "no summoning magic, anywhere, ever" rule holds exactly as already written, flagged here again since it's an easy rule to lose track of once a build starts sounding flashier on paper than it's meant to read on the page.

## Early PvP learning curve (confirmed forward-plot, Winter Bash prep arc)

His first real PvP reps go badly, and the reason is mechanical, not a vague "he's new to PvP" gesture: King-of-the-Hill control points hold for a fixed short window, and rotating early costs real setup time he doesn't have, a purely trap-and-wait, setup-heavy playstyle can't actually keep pace with that rhythm. This is the concrete, in-fiction reason he needs the rune-weapon kit above to actually work, mobile, immediate-use tools rather than only pre-armed traps, not a build change for its own sake.

## Future signature abilities

- **Ancient Bone Flame Barrage, the existing fire/area spell (already established in the old draft), is the only spell here, no separate name for its later, more developed form.** As Tatsuya grows into Shaman's Veil, this is the spell that develops into real trap-bomber territory: a short channel/placement action draws an insignia on the ground or a wall, a brief arming delay, then it's live, invisible to enemies, shown as a hologram to allies only. An enemy who steps into the marked area triggers the flame trap. Costs MP, scales with Wisdom (per `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), a real cooldown, no spamming multiple markers at once early on. Combos naturally with his existing gas-based kit (Goblin Gas + a marker igniting it is the same family of interaction as the already-established Goblin Gas + Goblin Flare combo from Chapter 3, just a higher tier).
  - **Chain-reaction elaboration (later mastery, not day one):** multiple placed markers, or a marker plus a live Goblin Gas cloud, can trigger each other. A real, earned combo moment once he's actually built up multiple markers and the resource pool to support them, not available from the start.
  - **Crit tie-in:** no new stat or named passive, this is just Fortune doing what it already does (see `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`), applied contextually, a hit landed against something already caught in a marked zone or otherwise debuffed rolls its existing crit chance/magnitude the normal way, nothing new bolted on.
- **A combat-duplicate ability exists in his future, but it comes from a battle item he picks up/acquires later, not from Shiv's blessing or Shaman's Veil at all.** Now given a concrete form, a doppelganger ring, see `codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`. Unrelated to the necklace, unrelated to spirit magic. Flagged here so it doesn't get folded back into the Shiv-blessing material by mistake.
- **A steampunk sidearm**, tool-based, fed by a tank on his back, switches between his existing Goblin Gas (caustic shot) and Flare (fire shot, amplified) rather than adding new abilities. Full details in `codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`.
- **A goblin-built turret, tied to the future goblin trade company, not his personal kit.** Not available early, a real product of the trade company once it exists (see `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`), development time in-world, not a day-one gadget. **Balance niche, locked now so it doesn't need re-deciding later:** underperforms in ranked 5v5 (too slow and setup-heavy for that pace, doesn't undercut his established competitive identity), but genuinely strong in GvG Wars and PvE/base-defense content (both already-established systems, see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`), giving it a real reason to exist without breaking the balance already established for his kit.

## What we deliberately didn't take from an outside build-concept reference

A separately-drafted "Arcane Bomber Goblin" build concept was reviewed against everything above and mostly folded in as elaboration on what already existed (the mine/rune mechanic is Ancient Bone Flame Barrage itself, no separate spell name attached to it). Explicitly **not** adopted, flagged so it doesn't get silently re-proposed later:
- No formal class name ("Steampunk Arcanist" or otherwise), his kit stays its own thing, never boxed into the base class list other players draw from (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`).
- No new stats (that reference used "Intelligence," "Agility," "Haste," a standalone "Luck"), everything maps onto the existing six stats. A cooldown-reduction effect, if it ever shows up, is gear/itemization flavor, not a stat.
- No standalone gambling/misfire mechanic as a separate system, Fortune and Gambler's Gambit already do this narratively.
- No separate "lane lock" zone ultimate, folded into Ancient Bone Flame Barrage itself rather than becoming a second ultimate-tier ability.
- No summoning magic of any kind, and no separate "Ifrit" spell name, that naming has been fully retired, it was always just Ancient Bone Flame Barrage.

Nothing in this file is unlocked or referenced on the page yet. This file exists so future chapters introducing any of it stay consistent with this design rather than inventing something contradictory later.
