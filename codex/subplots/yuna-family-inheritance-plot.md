# Subplot: Yuna's Family & the Inheritance Plot

**Status:** Background, not yet dramatized. Distinct from, but connected to, `codex/subplots/yuna-tatsuya-slow-burn-romance.md`.

## The setup

Yuna's family are old-money business moguls, rooted in law but with hands in everything, real estate, contracts, corporate sponsorships. Her grandfather passed the family legacy and wealth directly to his grandson (her younger brother, Kaito), bypassing his own son (Yuna's father) entirely, a judgment on character the son never forgave. If Kaito dies before he's old enough to formally accept the grandfather's will, everything reverts to the father. That's the motive.

## The darker thread

The father and mother are both complicit, an outside doctor brings in medication through channels that bypass the hospital directly, and that medication is making Kaito worse, not better. Kaito visibly improves when his parents aren't present. Yuna knows what her parents are, without hard proof of what they're doing to Kaito.

## Connection to the Tabuchi hospital thread

Kazuto Tabuchi ends up at the same hospital as Kaito (see `codex/characters/kazuto-tabuchi.md`). Keep this to the stated hospital overlap itself, don't invent additional connective tissue between the two families beyond that.

## Why it matters to the main plot

Yuna's family wasn't paying attention to the esports side of HGO until Kazuku began treating in-game economic infrastructure as a serious real-world business play. Once that shift became impossible to ignore, the family scrambled to get positioned inside it, and suddenly Yuna, previously in the way, became valuable, especially once she's visibly connected to Tatsuya. Her parents don't yet know how deeply she's actually embedded in HGO as its public face.
