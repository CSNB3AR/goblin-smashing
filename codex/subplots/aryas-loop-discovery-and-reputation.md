# Subplot: Arya's Loop Discovery & Tatsuya's Public Reputation

**Status:** Forward plot thread, background only for now.

## The beat

Once Tatsuya's early gameplay in camp starts showing real growth (his handling of the AI camp companions, PK-raid kills), Arya records and posts footage of it to Loop, which is how his public reputation actually starts, under a tagline worth reusing verbatim when the beat is drafted: **"Nobody roots for the Goblin."** His own haters-to-fans pipeline origin.

## Context that sets this up

Arya's own guild crew is small heading into the Crystal League expansion (her all-girls guild, Crimson Static, fractured after a falling-out, see `codex/characters/arya.md`), part of why she's paying close attention to a total unknown in the first place, and part of why she's genuinely available when the Winter Bash random-teammate mechanic pairs her with Tatsuya.

This should surface through the established "live streaming as organic exposition" technique, chat reactions doing the work of flagging "this is unusual" without narration stating it outright. See `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`.

## The actual first-contact mechanism (trigger landed on the page in Chapter 7, payoff locked for Chapter 8)

Chapter 7 plants the real trigger for how Arya starts watching Tatsuya at all, staying entirely inside Tatsuya's locked POV (nothing here shows her side directly, consistent with the never-leave-Tatsuya's-POV rule): Tatsuya goes live during the Shiv confrontation, with Leon staffing the external monitor as operator. He also gets a lighter, earlier irony beat first: Arya's own handle flashes past in the flood of stream chat once HGO's official channel hosts the stream (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`), one viewer among thousands, no reason yet for her to connect the goblin streamer to Tatsuya. He clocks her name with real alarm; she has no idea it's him. That's not the discovery beat, just a reader-facing irony beat landing before it.

The real discovery mechanism: Leon's external monitor overheats under the sustained streaming load (see `codex/lore/external-monitor-and-full-dive-safety.md`) and he has to step away for the VRSA inspector to look at it properly, a five-to-ten-minute window. Tatsuya gets a background system notification that Leon has stepped away ("[OPERATOR STATUS: LEON TEMPORARILY AWAY. MONITORING CONTINUES.]"), barely registers it, mid-crisis. **This half of the beat is now resolved on the page, Chapter 7.**

## Confirmed forward plot, locked sequence for Chapter 8

Chapter 8 opens still mid-ambush, continuing straight out of Chapter 7's cliffhanger. Leon breaks back in a second time: the monitors need a real check, about ten minutes this time (distinct from, and longer than, the shorter cooldown window in Chapter 7). That's what forces Tatsuya offline. In the real-world gap, at Sakura Arcade, he takes a bathroom break, scrolls his phone, follower/affiliate anxiety, consistent with having already played a long stretch.

**Arya catches him in person, right as he steps out of the bathroom, distracted on his phone, with a plain "Hey, T-Drag."** Not a call, not a text, she's physically in the arcade with him for the whole scene. She's seen his stream, knows his general in-game location from it, offers to help. He declines twice. No pod is free at Sakura Arcade itself, so she offers to run to a different cafe that has one and log in remotely instead, that's what resolves the "how does she actually join" logistics. She escalates into real, non-performative desperation (see `codex/characters/arya.md`'s expanded guild-collapse backstory) and that's what actually moves him. He asks about her build, specifically pushes her toward committing points into the Hushveil Silence upgrade (see `codex/magic/book-of-tempest.md`) as the price of joining, tied directly to Bloodhurst's own hillside chokepoint (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`). She commits.

**This Chapter 8 recruitment is a distinct beat from the still-separate, still-unwritten "Arya breaks her deal with Tatsuya" flag.** The outcome here is her joining the assault party, not a confrontation or a broken agreement, that beat stays owed for later, don't conflate the two. This is also the window Leon being away from the booth (per the mechanism above) gives Arya room to actually watch Tatsuya's gameplay live and start putting things together, feeding both this in-person approach and, eventually, the Loop-posting beat at the top of this file. **PK_Wraith's raid outcome resolves on-page later in this same Chapter 8, not off-page**, Arya's Hushveil is the spell that actually makes the ambush work, and her assist gets credited live on-stream, see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`.
