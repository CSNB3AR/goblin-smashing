# Subplot: Yuna / Tatsuya Slow-Burn Romance

**Status:** Seeded only, not yet active. Confirmed future subplot.

## Pacing rule

Seeded gradually per the established pacing model (King's Avatar, Ace of Diamond), not front-loaded. Chapter 1 only plants Yuna as an ordinary broadcast voice Tatsuya isn't emotionally engaging with, and as Momo's idol obsession bringing her into the household indirectly. See `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md`.

## Planned progression

1. Yuna named/heard as caster (Chapter 1, done).
2. Momo's fandom keeps her present in the household without direct contact.
3. Hospital meeting: Tatsuya bumps into her visiting her brother Kaito, they trade real names for real this time (not just her giving "Yunaile"), she still doesn't connect it to Yuna the broadcast persona in the moment on his end. See `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md` for the full scene sketch.
4. **Revised on purpose, replaces the old "do not compress" rule below:** the actual identity-reveal mechanism is now Act One's Luxor Lakes leveling stretch, not a vague later off-page realization. Arya's in school 9-to-3, so Tatsuya fills that daily window grinding with an in-game friend who goes by "Yuna," not obviously the broadcast persona at first. Over that stretch, each of them separately connects this in-game friend to the person they already traded real names with at the hospital, a real, dramatized dual reveal rather than "he pieces it together later, at home, watching TV." Once both sides know, the friendship deepens specifically because they're both people who'd been hiding something.
5. **New, Act One, mid-Winter Bash:** PK_Wraith engineers a deception targeting this friendship (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`, `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md`), creating a real standstill between them mid-tournament. Resolves by the end of Act One, Tatsuya works out it was a lie, they start reconciling, carrying that thread into Act Two.
6. Act Two: real relationship development continues, in parallel with her family situation darkening (see `codex/subplots/yuna-family-inheritance-plot.md`), gradually, not resolved that arc.

## Do not

Do not let the Chapter 1 broadcast self-intro read as more than background noise. Do not let this subplot outweigh the game/esports throughline. (The old "do not compress the hospital reveal earlier than currently planned" rule is superseded by the Luxor Lakes mechanism above, kept here as a note so the change reads as deliberate, not silently dropped.)
