# Subplot: Haruto's Exit From Kansai Black Lotus

**Status:** Seeded in Chapter 1, payoff lands Chapter 8, reaching Tatsuya entirely secondhand through a Loop post during the real-world interlude, never a direct Eren/Haruto scene.

## The seed

Haruto has heard Eren needle Tatsuya before, more than once, enough that he's started to suspect the team's famous build wasn't really Eren's idea. In Chapter 1 he gets a small, quiet beat of dawning respect toward Tatsuya, not a speech, and a genuine compliment on his way out ("You're too good to be on a roster like that"). See `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`.

## The payoff (old-draft sourced, needs POV rework)

The old draft had this play out as a full Eren-POV chapter: Kazuto strips Eren of financial control over the team, and Haruto confronts Eren and quits the team publicly, calling out the stolen build and Eren's toxic leadership on Loop, hinting the "real creator" should "come out and play." That chapter itself is cut for breaking the locked third-person-on-Tatsuya rule. The underlying beat (Haruto's public exit, the stolen-build callout) is worth preserving, but needs to reach the reader entirely through Tatsuya's own observation or secondhand knowledge (a Loop post, a news mention, someone telling him about it), never a direct scene with Eren and Haruto that Tatsuya isn't part of.

## Timing

Leaves the team around the Winter Bash timing, especially with the 5v5 format shift making his individual skill more valuable on a healthier roster. Matches Kansai Black Lotus's own worst-record qualifier bump-out (`codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md`), a real, felt trigger rather than a vague sense of frustration.

## Landed, Chapter 8

Tatsuya sees the announcement mid-scroll during the real-world interlude. Haruto's statement stays careful and controlled, thanks Tochi and Kansai Black Lotus for the opportunity, still respects it, but says outright he's not willing to keep carrying a roster built around a signature build he's started to suspect wasn't built by the person taking credit for it, without naming Eren directly. Tatsuya reads the subtext instantly, since he's the only person alive who actually knows the build's real origin (see `codex/characters/eren-tabuchi.md`). **New forward-plot, added Chapter 8:** a follow-up Loop post speculatively ties **Dosuke AI** (`codex/characters/dosuke-ai.md`) to picking Haruto up as a free agent, plausible given Dosuke's own build already echoes the original Tatsuya/Eren theorized concept. Not confirmed on the page as a done deal, speculation Tatsuya finds himself agreeing with.
