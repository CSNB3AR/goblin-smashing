# Subplot: Taking Down the Tabuchis, Tochi Politics and the Kazuhaya Brand (forward plot)

**Status:** Author-stated, latest (long dictation). Ledger and codex only; mostly Book Two. Dictation garbles read as: "Aaron" is Eren or Arya by context; "Chio" is Chiyo; "Gen 2/Gen 3" are the second and third hardware lines (Kuroshio, Nexus).

## What Tatsuya can and cannot do now

- He cannot attack the Tabuchis on a feeling. He may suspect they killed Dad, but he has **no proof yet**.
- **Later, when he has money:** he hires an investigator, gets proof, and gets to the car that was impounded after the wreck (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`), and runs a full investigation, because nobody has.
- **Chiyo's stalemate.** She needs top lawyers to hold the Tabuchis off, and that costs money she does not have; that is why it is a stalemate. Once the debt is gone, she sees what Tatsuya did, respects him more, and realizes she should never have held things back: he was old and mature enough to handle it. (This is where the anger at her resolves; see `codex/characters/chiyo-nakamura.md`.)

## How the Tabuchis' company gets beaten

1. Beat Eren in the game.
2. Earn the trust of the **Tochi student council** and get on it.
3. Earn the trust of the **booster club**, the "big heads" and in effect the school's administration (the larger council of adults and money). Chiyo sits on it, and so does Eren's father.
4. Get Tochi to back **Kazuhaya Tech** once it is up and running again.
- He does **not** go after Kazuku. Nexus (the third line) is with Kazuku, which already beats the Tabuchis' second line, and Kazuku is a future business partner. What he will not allow is the Tabuchis getting their hands back in.
- Tochi funding, on the page in Chapter Eight as his hope: Dad helped fund the school for years, until the new money came in.

## The Kazuhaya brand inside HGO (mostly Book Two)

- He cannot bring Dad's business back in the real world, where its reputation is bad. On the global stage of HGO he can: the World Quest is active, so people watch, and he advertises the gear himself, because the best way to sell something is to be seen using it. This is what "bring your business here" pays off.
- He brings back some of Dad's major items and builds the **Kazuhaya Tech brand** in the biggest game with the biggest brand attached. His name goes on it (otherwise it would be plagiarism); a partner builds with him and they title it together. He could trademark it under Dad's name.
- **Blacksmithing and workers.** He goes down his own build and into blacksmithing, but a build can only go so far, so he employs people who are further along. Legendary schematics in the game help him build and brand his own line. He also pursues the automaton and building route.
- **A dwarfling smith.** A half-dwarf or dwarfling (not a playable race) he befriends near the mountains, during the Thornwatch push, becomes a blacksmith for him. The author wavered between this being a troop under the troop system and a real player in his guild "instead of a player playing a dwarf"; the contradiction is OPEN.
- Rare gear is dispersed through goblins who act as a black-market trade group under the guild's goblin name. See the agent-written docs listed in the ledger for materials, elixirs, the core and the market mechanics.
