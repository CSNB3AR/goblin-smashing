Held for the assault chapters (Assembling the Assault Team, Silence and Fire). Not on the page yet; the author said not everything has to go in at once, this records where it comes from and what each chapter can use.

## The rule behind it

Tatsuya has had too many chains of success (the first-login luck, the world quest, the Shiv boost). **Somewhere in the assault he must roll a crit fail**, in combat or in the setup. The Chapter Nine boost is fine because it is a quest-completion payout, but the run of good luck has to break.

## The rune-trap setup (the planned crit fails)

- Every rune trap needs a **dice roll** and every rune costs **mana**.
- He only needs to trap the **entrance**, since the raiders' tent has one way in.
- He rolls **two crit fails** at the start; people outside begin to hear him and he panics. The **third** rune finally goes down, and then they go ahead with the **Goblin Gas** technique, the raiders come out, and the fight proceeds.

## Arya's mist and the timer

- Arya's Hushveil **mist lasts 15 seconds**. He asked for it so the setup is quiet.
- Each time a rune fails, the runes take about **4 seconds to come back off cooldown**, and each try burns mana. After two failures he has mana for **one more** use. The panic is the mist timer plus the mana left for the last rune.
- Arya assists. She asks how long he needs, because she has a meeting or a slot at J-Link (the author said "JSTAR", spelling OPEN) and has to switch seats. He runs off **Arya's timer**.

## Why he has to log off and plan

- Streaming is overheating the pod, so he is forced off for a break (this is the in-world reason he says he needs to meditate). He asks for about **30 to 35 minutes** to plan and get gear, because he does not want to go in with the big party.
- Arya's silences are still needed for that window, so she goes to J-Link to be present and assist fully.
- This opens voice chat and puts **Leon in the chat more**, which brings Arya into the flood and sets up a proper escape scene.
