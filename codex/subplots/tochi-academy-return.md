# Subplot: The Tochi Academy Decision

**Status:** Active, the open decision hanging over Chapter 1 and beyond.

## The setup

Chiyo needs an answer soon on whether Tatsuya reapplies to Tochi Academy (the submission window closes December 23rd and the entrance exams are January 4th). If he doesn't decide, she's prepared to decide for him, framed as love and protection, not control. See `codex/characters/chiyo-nakamura.md`.

## Why it matters

Tochi has the resources and curriculum required to maintain the first-gen pods long-term (ties to `codex/subplots/technical-arc-leon-and-sakura-arcade.md`), and it's also the one place in Japan the Tabuchis have no institutional reach, due to Tochi's real, enforced zero-tolerance policy on family-influence targeting. See `codex/locations/tochi-academy.md`.

**New, concrete pressure, Chapter 8-9:** a real parts lead for Leon's overheating pod (see `codex/lore/external-monitor-and-full-dive-safety.md`) turns out to need knowledge Tatsuya doesn't have. Tochi's tech program is the realistic way to actually learn it, faster and cheaper than a university route he can't afford in either time or money, a genuine, felt reason pulling him back toward the decision rather than pure obligation to Chiyo.

## Resolved facts

If Tatsuya returns, he re-enters as a full first-year, no advanced placement, not ahead of Eren, he's behind, and re-entry means starting over. His path back runs through Chiyo's instructor position, Ohema's volleyball legacy, and Daichi's tech legacy at the school.

## Real deadline mechanic (Chapter 2+)

Chiyo and the twins leave on a six-day school trip to Kyoto (see `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`). She needs the Tochi decision by the time they're back, that's Tatsuya's actual window, not an abstract pressure, to manufacture something real (income, proof, leverage) before the conversation happens for real.

## Why third-gen parts mean Tochi (author-stated, Chapter Seven)

- Third-gen processor cores are **never sold openly**: if anyone could buy one they could break down the schematics and copy it. They go only to partners.
- **Tochi is the only school openly partnered with Nexus Enterprises, Kazuku's company, the maker of the third-gen hardware** (Kazuhaya Tech does not own it; the company is too big, with too much stock at stake, to partner openly with just anyone), so its technology lab is the one place he knows to look. Ch3 has him recognise the core logo stamped on equipment there.
- **Access is gated twice.** The lab needs top grades and standing, and instructors get the access, not the students. He would have to climb. His parents' names are the way in: Ohema coached volleyball there, Daichi built there.
- **Money logic.** He wants steady income to help Mom, with the Tabuchis back and the family in debt. He also needs a reason Chiyo will accept: "I'm playing a game" will not do, so a job on the books at Leon's and visible income are what he can show her.
- Resolved (author-stated): the third-gen maker is Nexus Enterprises and Kazuku, consistent with Ch3's "tied to Kazuku." Kazuhaya Tech built only the first-gen pods and does not own third-gen.

He does not know the lab holds a third-gen core, and the "logo in the tech lab" idea is no longer used in Chapter Seven. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`.
