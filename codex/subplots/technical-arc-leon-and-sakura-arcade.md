# Subplot: The Technical Arc (Leon & Sakura Arcade)

**Status:** Begins Chapter 2.

## The arc

Tatsuya's technical knowledge (inherited from Daichi, already established through the Haya EB) is what gets an incompatible first-gen pod running HGO software at all. Leon applies a pressure beat of his own (a cost, a condition, or a warning) before granting pod access. Over time, Tatsuya learns more at Tochi (once he returns), applies the knowledge, and keeps the first-gen pods running long-term, Leon's business grows as a direct, visible result of Tatsuya's presence.

**Ongoing maintenance, seeded in Chapter 2:** the override that gets the pod running isn't a one-time fix, it degrades the hardware over real time. Tatsuya recognizes this himself, mid-override, straight off the schematics, and knows he'll need to source upgraded components (the same family of parts used inside third-gen helmets) and keep doing hands-on maintenance, same as the Haya EB. This is the concrete mechanical seed for his growing, ongoing role at the arcade, not a single favor traded for access, a real, continuing responsibility he takes on without being asked. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`.

## The second pod, Chapter 5

Leon's business struggling is what opens the door, he mentions it himself, unprompted, before Tatsuya pitches anything. Tatsuya offers to fix the second, dead first-gen pod using what he knows from Daichi's manual, openly admitting he already got the first one running illegally. They strike a real deal: Tatsuya gets guaranteed access, the second pod goes into Leon's commercial rotation once it's actually fixed and cleared by VRSA (see `codex/lore/vrsa.md`), the international VR-hardware safety authority, before Leon can legally advertise it. Leon asks Tatsuya to hold off on the pods' HGO capability being talked about publicly until both are genuinely done, protecting what's left of the family name; Tatsuya agrees without needing to be asked twice. Leon brings a tech in the next morning, Tatsuya commits to coming back to help.

Leon's family backstory lands here too: he and Arya inherited the shop from their grandfather. Their parents are overseas and want Arya to come home with them; she won't leave HGO or her guild behind, and the arcade's ongoing financial struggle is part of why, she's been spending her own money at other cafes since Sakura Arcade can't keep up.

## His own core, the build-it-himself plan (author-stated, Chapter Seven)

- **The idea.** Even once he reaches Tochi, he does not want to mimic a second-gen (Kuroshio) or third-gen (Nexus) core. His idea is to **build his own processor core for the first-gen Kazuhaya pods**, the way Daichi built the pod around his own work.
- **Why not buy.** No first-gen pods or cores are left to find; production stopped. Kuroshio cores can be used to study how a core works and as parts to rework, but he cannot buy them the normal way, and on the black market the prices are insane. A few might be had that way at a huge cost. Nexus cores are a long road away: reaching them means getting as far as Kazuku, which is a big point of the story, so he cannot simply pull one from a third-gen source.
- **Why Tochi.** He cannot get the resources or the know-how to build a core any other way, so the only path is back to Tochi to learn it. This is the felt reason the Tochi decision pulls him.
- **The second pod.** The dead shell at Sakura needs the same kind of core, so one working design would fix both pods.
- **On the page (Chapter Seven).** He tells Leon he builds his own core, that Kuroshio is out because of the black market price and Tabuchi money, and that Nexus is a long road away.

## The Chapter Seven conversation, as written (author-stated beats, latest; supersedes the earlier Leon-scene wording)

- **Tone.** A real two-way conversation. Leon opens honest because he has nothing to lie about (the free pod after five was bait to get people in, see how it works and pay for longer hours; business is slow, helmet cafes are eating him alive, he is buried in legal trouble). Tatsuya holds back and gives pieces. He confesses the override but not everything.
- **Leon's reaction.** He would not normally work with anyone who touched his machine without asking and says so, dry, not cruel ("it was a move"), but it is Daichi's machine and Tatsuya knew how to run it, which changes it: like a tech upgrading his gear for free. He should thank him, not be furious; it would be different if a stranger had broken it.
- **Tatsuya's pitch.** Get cores into both pods and Leon has two working HGO pods where everything is booked out (helmet cafes close to a year, second-gen booked too). Leon sets the price; first-gen is rare and burns through power, so he can charge more. Tatsuya's ask: let him work there.
- **Second-gen as the stopgap.** Tatsuya says he can get cores pretty quickly, second-gen, the fastest route. He dislikes it (body language, jaw tightens) and would rather build his own core fitted to first-gen, and he would rather not put Tabuchi-backed parts in Daichi's machine, but he does NOT say that aloud because it would look like bad business. He has no other choice for now. Building his own needs Tochi: he has Daichi's manual and schematics but not the funding or parts, and Tochi's labs have both (core building is part of the curriculum).
- **Why he must give something back.** Arya and Leon already work the floor full time, so the only way Tatsuya works there is by getting in the pod. A player who just plays must do something for them: clips now, streaming when he is ready, advertising the game and Sakura Arcade's brand. Leon sponsored Arya's guild (top eight in seasonal points) to get the arcade's name out; then two guildmates framed her, the guild split, she still has a following but is trolled on every stream, so Leon cannot sponsor or advertise through her now. NOTE: this lightly pre-empts the Chapter 9 collapse reveal; kept to two sentences, no names.
- **Terms as spoken.** Part-time on the books (floor hours and repairs, cover for Chiyo), one pod permanently his as a sponsorship, he gets both pods running clean, Leon pays him for the second pod once it is up, open to close if he wants with the monitor running and Leon present, quiet until VRSA clearance, come in at nine to nine-thirty.
