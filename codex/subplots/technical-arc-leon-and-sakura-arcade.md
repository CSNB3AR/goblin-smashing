# Subplot: The Technical Arc (Leon & Sakura Arcade)

**Status:** Begins Chapter 2.

## The arc

Tatsuya's technical knowledge (inherited from Daichi, already established through the Haya EB) is what gets an incompatible first-gen pod running HGO software at all. Leon applies a pressure beat of his own (a cost, a condition, or a warning) before granting pod access. Over time, Tatsuya learns more at Tochi (once he returns), applies the knowledge, and keeps the first-gen pods running long-term, Leon's business grows as a direct, visible result of Tatsuya's presence.

**Ongoing maintenance, seeded in Chapter 2:** the override that gets the pod running isn't a one-time fix, it degrades the hardware over real time. Tatsuya recognizes this himself, mid-override, straight off the schematics, and knows he'll need to source upgraded components (the same family of parts used inside third-gen helmets) and keep doing hands-on maintenance, same as the Haya EB. This is the concrete mechanical seed for his growing, ongoing role at the arcade, not a single favor traded for access, a real, continuing responsibility he takes on without being asked.
