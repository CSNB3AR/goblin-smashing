# Subplot: The Technical Arc (Leon & Sakura Arcade)

**Status:** Begins Chapter 2.

## The arc

Tatsuya's technical knowledge (inherited from Daichi, already established through the Haya EB) is what gets an incompatible first-gen pod running HGO software at all. Leon applies a pressure beat of his own (a cost, a condition, or a warning) before granting pod access. Over time, Tatsuya learns more at Tochi (once he returns), applies the knowledge, and keeps the first-gen pods running long-term, Leon's business grows as a direct, visible result of Tatsuya's presence.

**Ongoing maintenance, seeded in Chapter 2:** the override that gets the pod running isn't a one-time fix, it degrades the hardware over real time. Tatsuya recognizes this himself, mid-override, straight off the schematics, and knows he'll need to source upgraded components (the same family of parts used inside third-gen helmets) and keep doing hands-on maintenance, same as the Haya EB. This is the concrete mechanical seed for his growing, ongoing role at the arcade, not a single favor traded for access, a real, continuing responsibility he takes on without being asked. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`.

## The second pod, Chapter 4

Leon's business struggling is what opens the door, he mentions it himself, unprompted, before Tatsuya pitches anything. Tatsuya offers to fix the second, dead first-gen pod using what he knows from Daichi's manual, openly admitting he already got the first one running illegally. They strike a real deal: Tatsuya gets guaranteed access, the second pod goes into Leon's commercial rotation once it's actually fixed and cleared by VRSA (see `codex/lore/vrsa.md`), the international VR-hardware safety authority, before Leon can legally advertise it. Leon asks Tatsuya to hold off on the pods' HGO capability being talked about publicly until both are genuinely done, protecting what's left of the family name; Tatsuya agrees without needing to be asked twice. Leon brings a tech in the next morning, Tatsuya commits to coming back to help.

Leon's family backstory lands here too: he and Arya inherited the shop from their grandfather. Their parents are overseas and want Arya to come home with them; she won't leave HGO or her guild behind, and the arcade's ongoing financial struggle is part of why, she's been spending her own money at other cafes since Sakura Arcade can't keep up.
