# Subplot: The Goblin Camp & "Call of the Bones" World Quest

**Status:** Chapter 3 (scene-by-scene planned, see `outline/chapter-03.md`), the central in-game arc for the early chapters.

## Structure (current, supersedes both the old draft's pacing and the earlier three-chapter spread)

1. First login drops Tatsuya into Bloodhurst (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), the Thrash clan's camp within Lunarest Outskirts, as a goblin (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).
2. He's hazed by two goblin NPCs who read his real-player movement as "acting feral" (see `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`). He roasts them back, it escalates into a fight.
3. He kills both with a Goblin Gas + Goblin Flare combo (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`), his first real ability showcase, first level-up, first (minor) deck-building item.
4. Sevish Thrash (chieftain, see `codex/characters/sevish-thrash.md`) comes out to investigate the commotion, reacts with interest rather than pure hostility, "so we have a fighter," and decides to proceed with the clan's regular (previously always-doomed) rally march now that they have him.
5. **The World Quest activates for real, distinguished explicitly from the standard failed NPC "goblin rally" event** that's looped uselessly since launch, because it requires an actual player-goblin present to trigger it, which has essentially never happened. Working interpretation (flagged, not explicitly specified by the author): the activation check is Fortune-gated, and Tatsuya's racial Fortune bonus is what tips it this time.
6. Shiv gives quest direction: infiltrate a restricted section of Lunarest (library/archive) for intel, real objective is helping the Thrash clan reunify Myrrhwood's fractured, warring clans under Shiv's legacy. Ties to Shiv's Warset (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`), pieces scattered across the world, not just Myrrhwood.
7. Tatsuya asks for three days to decide. Right as he does, he registers the in-game time-dilation gap hitting him for real, hours have passed outside without him noticing.
8. **Same chapter, not deferred:** because he killed two of their own, he's taken into custody regardless of the mission offer, a UI notification marks him a prisoner of the Thrash clan, escorted to a holding tent. The "chained up" beat, previously planned for a later chapter after a real-world return trip, now lands here.
9. Companions (see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`: Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh) still get earned one at a time in later chapters, never assigned as a squad in one scene, the old draft's flagged pacing problem, unaffected by this restructuring.
10. Eventually reaches toward the Astralum Archive (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`), historically off-limits to goblins.
