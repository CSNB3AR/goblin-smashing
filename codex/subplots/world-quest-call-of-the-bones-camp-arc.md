# Subplot: The Goblin Camp & "Call of the Bones" World Quest

**Status:** Not yet drafted, the central in-game arc for the early chapters. See `outline/chapter-03.md` onward.

## Structure (per current outline, supersedes the old draft's pacing)

1. First login drops Tatsuya into Lunarest Outskirts as a goblin (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).
2. An NPC skirmish where his abilities (Goblin Gas combos, see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) accidentally do real damage, drawing the attention of Wyx Thrash and Sevish Thrash (see `codex/characters/wyx-thrash.md`, `codex/characters/sevish-thrash.md`).
3. Sevish gives him a proving ultimatum, prove himself within a set window or be treated as an enemy.
4. **Sustained immersion, not an in-and-out cycle:** he lives in camp for a real stretch, texture, a taste of goblin culture, before choosing to log out, real-world pressure (work, family, the clock on his booking) pulling him back rather than the game pushing him out.
5. He accepts the "Call of the Bones" World Quest via a UI prompt. Shiv (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`) appears and gives quest direction. Grants the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`).
6. When he eventually logs back in, the "chained up" beat lands: held by Sevish's own camp as a suspected outsider during the proving trial, not an external captor. This raises the stakes on trust within the goblin camp itself before he can move toward the World Quest proper.
7. Companions (see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`: Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh) get earned one at a time, never assigned as a squad in one scene, the old draft's flagged pacing problem.
8. Eventually reaches toward the Astralum Archive (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`), historically off-limits to goblins.

## Real stakes

See `codex/lore/world-quests.md` for why "Call of the Bones" activating now creates immediate, real competitive pressure from the wider playerbase, not a quiet, isolated opportunity.

## Old-draft objective note

The old draft's flatter premise ("bring intel on a way into Lunarest") is superseded, any player, goblin included, can already walk into Lunarest freely. A sharper infiltration objective (e.g., restricted-archive intel gathering with real capture/jail risk) is worth developing for whenever this arc actually gets drafted.
