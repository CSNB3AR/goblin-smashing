**Status:** Chapter 3 drafted (see `chapters/chapter-03.md`), the central in-game arc for the early chapters.

## Structure (current)

1. First login drops Tatsuya into Bloodhurst (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), the Thrash clan's camp within Lunarest Outskirts, as a goblin (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).
2. He sees two goblins picking on a smaller, younger goblin (a future companion candidate, see `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`) and steps in defensively on the kid's behalf. The two bullies turn on him instead.
3. Provoked (a natural provoke, not a scripted taunt line, see `chapters/chapter-03.md` for how this plays out), they chase him through camp, a populated chase, not an empty foot race, other goblins clock the commotion and watch without intervening. The enforcers adapt partway through, trying to cut him off through gaps between tents instead of chasing straight, forcing him to actually use his Speed advantage and the camp's tight layout. He kills both with a Goblin Gas + Goblin Flare combo that is genuinely accidental, not a plan he walks in already knowing (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`), his first real ability showcase, first level-up, first deck-building item (the goblin gadget belt, plus a couple of sellable potions, via the Gambler's Gambit racial passive, manifested as the Gambler's Earring, see `codex/objects/gamblers-earring.md`).
4. **Wyx's actual first appearance is mid-chase**, drawn out of her tent by the commotion, watching with open amusement and not intervening, which is why the watching goblins don't intervene either, some start cheering it on. She's already at Sevish's side by the time guards bring Tatsuya and the younger goblin in. Sevish Thrash (chieftain, see `codex/characters/sevish-thrash.md`) and Wyx witness or are told the whole sequence, recognizing real value: someone capable of trickery and evasion, tight-space movement, exactly what the library infiltration mission needs, not just raw toughness.
5. **The World Quest activation, resolved: a keyword/philosophy trigger, not a Fortune roll.** Every goblin has technically had the same shot at this quest, since it's open to the whole race, most players either paid to reroll off Goblin early (making it a practically unplayable race in practice) or never said or embodied anything close to Shiv's own founding philosophy. Defending the younger goblin and justifying the kill to Sevish, Tatsuya reasons out loud, unprompted and without knowing any of the lore, something in the spirit of "we have bigger fish to fry than each other." That's the same ideology Shiv himself unified Myrrhwood's clans around. The camp's AI (Sevish and Wyx specifically, both flagged as advanced/adaptive) recognizes the echo. Tatsuya isn't first to have access, he's first to actually trigger it, and it reads as grounded, basic logic on his part, not mystical insight, he doesn't know why it worked.
6. Sevish gives quest direction diegetically, in his own words, not as a UI dump: the restricted top three floors of the **Grand Library of Lunarest** (see `codex/locations/grand-library-of-lunarest.md`), a five-floor building whose bottom two are public, real objective is helping the Thrash clan reunify Myrrhwood's fractured, warring clans under Shiv's legacy. Ties to Shiv's Warset (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`), pieces scattered across the world, not just Myrrhwood. Sevish also carries a real characterization quirk here: he states the rally's failed three times, a real number from his own persistent point of view (he and Wyx never die in these attempts, only rank-and-file goblins do, so his memory reads it as three retreats), when the actual count is dozens, closer to a hundred-plus, since this is a recurring monthly event that's failed for every new player who's attempted it since the closed beta launched, eight or nine months ago. Stays in his own dialogue framing on the page, never narrated as an aside, keeps the NPCs-never-metagame rule intact. Tatsuya privately clocks that Sevish's offer isn't purely altruistic either, the timing lines up with the incoming Hearthfire expansion, a beat that stays internal, not confronted directly.
7. Tatsuya asks for three days to decide. Right as he does, he registers the in-game time-dilation gap hitting him for real, hours have passed outside without him noticing.
8. Sevish's offer: succeed and he's guaranteed an ally of Thrash, real standing, "shown more in one fight than most of this camp in weeks." The threat sits underneath, unstated outright: refuse, and he's not free to leave, having killed clan members, and if their paths cross again after they leave for Myrrhwood without him, he's treated as an enemy. Tatsuya notices Sevish's and Wyx's gold tags flickering as he decides (see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`), a visible stakes cue.
9. He's taken into custody regardless of the mission offer, a UI notification marks him a prisoner of the Thrash clan, escorted to a holding tent. The "chained up" beat, previously planned for a later chapter after a real-world return trip, lands here instead.
10. Companions (see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`: Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh) still get earned one at a time in later chapters, never assigned as a squad in one scene, unaffected by this restructuring. The younger goblin from this chapter is a separate, earlier companion thread, not one of the named four.
11. Eventually reaches toward the Astralum Archive (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`), historically off-limits to goblins.

## Trigger (author-stated)

The quest starts because two Thrash goblins fight each other, a cardinal sin for the clan that first rejected feral ways, and Tatsuya steps in. The infighting shows Shiv's influence slipping among his deepest loyalists. Letting them fight would have been broken up without triggering the same effects. His intervention, even by coincidence, mirrors what Shiv once did in an uneven fight. Off the page for now.

**The monthly goblin event, Thrash's history and the "three" (author-stated, latest).** Thrash gave its own land to another clan to travel out to Lunarest's outskirts, because Sevish and Wyx are trying to find what Shiv was hiding and unify the clans again as in the old days, so goblins stop being pushed around by other races. They gave up a lot for it. Since the beta began, a goblin event has run every month: they rally the goblins born in the camp, charge the walls of Lunarest as a group, and everyone dies except the two leaders and a few others, who escape. Then it repeats. They only remember three attempts because their memory resets every three, which is how the world quest locks; once it progresses, it is done. Sevish and Wyx cannot die in that outcome: at about 20% HP they go into a rage and retreat, which is why no one has ever killed the gold tags, and players earn experience when they retreat. This time they do not show up, because Tatsuya broke the chain. Wyx is the voice of reason for Sevish and is devoted to Shiv. On the page in Chapter 6 only the sacrifice, the goal, Wyx's devotion and a hint (she says "three that I can count, it feels like more") are shown; the resets, the immunity and the rage retreat stay off the page for now.

## Why Thrash keeps failing, and why Tatsuya is the first to escalate (author-stated, latest)

- **Closed beta.** Nobody played a goblin: the base stats were so weak the race was a joke, so no goblin player ever touched the world quest. A **Goblin March** event ran **once a month** through the whole closed beta (RESOLVED by the author: monthly, not weekly), and it was never escalated. The cadence has an in-world reason: it takes about a month for the camp's goblins to respawn and for new recruits to arrive from the Thrash ship, which carries reinforcements. In game-design terms it is the event's reset cooldown. It must not read as a continuous event. A world quest cannot just be destroyed, and a developer would not burn content nobody has escalated, so it stays fresh. Tatsuya is the first to escalate it.
- **What Sevish and Wyx remember.** They never die, so they keep their memory only for the last three Marches before it resets. They call it three failures; organically they have failed the whole beta. Wyx and Sevish think they have been marching on the walls of Lunarest, and they only know the target from a glimpse (the Grand Library and Archive).
- **How they read Tatsuya.** Sevish lights up at a good fight (the Kenpachi Zaraki model) and realises Tatsuya may be the key to the quest they keep failing. Wyx caught the fight but does not understand the combustion; she only sees that no goblin has made an explosion like that, and thinks of hitting the walls with such power. When Tatsuya logs back in and lays out his plan he will say he would rather sneak in than send a whole mob, because a mob gets farmed by stronger adventurers (players); Sevish understands that stronger adventurers can kill them.
- **RESOLVED (author-stated, latest):** the Goblin March is **monthly**, matching the earlier note; the 'weekly' wording is dropped. Reason: respawn and reinforcement time from the Thrash ship.

## Why the goblins say "three" (author-stated, latest)

Their memory resets after three attempts; they remember only the last three rallies, and that is ALL they know, so on the page Wyx says "We have tried the rally three times" and nothing more. They do not say it feels like more. The players and the reader know it has happened many more times, and Tatsuya will find that out himself from online research: people will be asking about the Goblin event that did not happen this month because he caused things (cause and effect: the goblins did not rally). The reset also exists so the storyline can move; without it there would be a million and one resets. Rule: NPCs must not know what the players know; they only know what they know in their world.

## What Thrash knows about the library (author-stated, latest)

They have NEVER gotten past the walls of Lunarest, so they have not tried the Grand Library and do not know where it is. They only have whispers and a glimpse. On the page Wyx says they could not get past the guard and the adventurers, have never gotten past the walls, and that what they seek lies somewhere inside, in the Grand Library, "or so the whispers say. None of us has seen it." Wyx also names the restricted floors and what to look for; Sevish keeps to a short line about how Tatsuya slipped through and the terms. Sevish talks less than Wyx in the briefing.

## What Thrash actually knows about Shiv, FINAL (author-stated, latest; supersedes the whispers-about-the-library notes)

Their objective is ANY information on Shiv: a lead on where he went, where he rests, or even some of his teachings. The only thing they know is that Shiv was born in the Kingdom of Lunarest, a human, before he was cursed, and that whatever remains of him is there, which is why they marched on Lunarest's walls. They have never gotten past the walls, so they do not know the Grand Library exists as a target and do not know the information sits in its restricted floors; that is far too much knowledge for Wyx. The game tells Tatsuya (the Stage 2 notification: infiltrate the Grand Library of Lunarest, restricted floors) after the briefing, not the goblins. Later Shiv gives his blessing and Tatsuya becomes an elder, which is where much of this lines up.

What they have is an **old notebook of Shiv's**, notes carried down in the Thrash clan for years. All it says is that he had a connection to someone in the Kingdom of Lunarest. (He was once a sorcerer there, so traces may remain, and there are traces of him as a human that lead to Myrrhwood and how he was banished. That deeper story is not on the page.)

He goes out, researches, works out his plan, and once he is back and meets Shiv he automatically has one of the war sets, which is exactly the trace they want.
- **Tatsuya will fabricate, not lie, how he met Shiv.** The goblins will ask how, since he has connected to Shiv through the bones. Off the page for now.
