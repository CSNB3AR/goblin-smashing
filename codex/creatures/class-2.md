# Creatures — Class II

A real fight, usually soloable by a capable player but not a given. See `codex/creatures/overview.md` for the stat-block convention and the four-class system generally.

## Ironback Boar

Identity: a heavily-tusked, armor-hided boar found in Sunmarch's borderlands, charges rather than maneuvers.

- **Stats:** Power very high, Courage high (doesn't break easily), Speed moderate, Wisdom near zero.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Charge, a committed rush that deals real damage but leaves it briefly exposed after impact. Innate — Ironhide, reduced damage from glancing/non-aimed hits.
- **Rarity:** Uncommon.

## Marrow Hound

Identity: a skeletal hound found near old battlefields and burial grounds, spirit-adjacent in origin, hunts in small coordinated packs.

- **Stats:** Speed high, Power moderate, Wisdom low-moderate (enough for basic pack tactics).
- **Abilities:** Innate — doesn't tire, no stamina-based openings to exploit. Activated — Bone Rattle, a short howl that briefly lowers a target's Courage-based resistances.
- **Rarity:** Uncommon.

## Thornback Drake

Identity: a small, flightless drake found in rocky highlands, territorial rather than aggressive by default.

- **Stats:** Power and Courage both solid, Speed moderate, Wisdom low.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Thorn Volley, a short-range spine projectile attack. Triggered — bristles (temporary defense buff) when its territory is entered by multiple players at once.
- **Rarity:** Uncommon.

## Stonehide Golem-kin

Identity: a small, crudely-formed stone construct, not a true golem, found guarding old ruins, slow but genuinely hard to put down.

- **Stats:** Power high, Speed very low, Wisdom irrelevant (mechanical, not truly sentient).
- **Abilities:** Innate — high physical damage resistance, weak specifically to Geomancy-school effects (see `codex/magic/geomancy.md`) and to Physical Arts strikes aimed at visible seams. Activated — Slam, a slow but heavy melee hit.
- **Rarity:** Uncommon.

## Riftling Sprite

Identity: a small, mischievous minor spirit found near unstable magical sites, more disruptive than dangerous alone.

- **Stats:** Speed high, Wisdom moderate, Power low.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Blink Snare, a short teleport-and-grab. Innate — resistant to being pinned down by conventional (non-magical) movement-restriction effects.
- **Rarity:** Uncommon.
