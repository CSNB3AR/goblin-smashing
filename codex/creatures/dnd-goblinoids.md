# Goblin-Kin Creatures: What Is Not the Race

The goblin race and its evolutions (Goblin, Hobgoblin, Goblin Chieftain, and the unique Shiv Goblin branch) are covered in `codex/races/dnd-goblinoid-peoples.md` and `codex/races/race-evolution-baseline.md`. Goblins and hobgoblins are **playable and NPC people**, not monsters. This file keeps only what a goblin fights, rides, hunts with or is mistaken for, and what the D&D picture can lend as combat feel for goblin NPCs.

Tags: **[CANON]** from the codex or chapters, **[PROPOSAL]** invented here.

## 1. Where the line sits

- **The race and its evolutions** are tagged Demihuman. They level, evolve and can be feral or Thrash. They are not wild creatures. [CANON ledger]
- **Feral goblins** are still the Goblin race, just clans barred from towns. An NPC from a feral clan is a Goblin or Hobgoblin with a feral flag, not a monster species. [CANON `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`]
- **Everything below** is wild or tamed creatures that live around goblin camps and are not playable. [PROPOSAL]

## 2. Calibration for goblin NPCs

Class bands: Class I about LV 1 to 8, Class II about LV 6 to 22, Class III about LV 18 to 50, Class IV LV 45 and up. [PROPOSAL, from the class mapping note]

| Stage | Level band | Canon point |
|---|---|---|
| Runt Goblin | 1 to 2 | Ru before the blessing (LV 1) |
| Goblin (enforcer, skulker, gas or flare unit) | 2 to 8 | Thrash Clan Enforcer LV 3 |
| Hobgoblin, young | 5 to 12 | Ru LV 5, Zinka LV 7 |
| Hobgoblin, veteran | 12 to 24 | Drosh, level not stated |
| Hobgoblin captain | about 25 | Wyx LV 25 |
| Hobgoblin warlord | about 80 | Sevish LV 80 |
| Goblin Chieftain | 25 and up | none yet on the page |
| Shiv Goblin | player evolution | Tatsuya LV 8 |

Wyx and Sevish are gold tag NPCs with fixed levels, so they are anchors, not spawns. [CANON `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`]

D&D feel to borrow for goblin fighters [PROPOSAL]:
- **Goblin:** skirmish and run. Hit, slip away, use the shield that does not match.
- **Hobgoblin:** formation fighting. A hobgoblin beside another gets the better of a lone adventurer.
- **Goblin Chieftain:** commands first. A chieftain lets the clan fight and steps in only for the decisive blow.

## 3. Wild creatures near goblin camps (not playable) [PROPOSAL]

| Creature | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Worg** | Mount and hunting beast | D&D goblins ride worgs. In Eryndal a worg is a tameable wolf, never a goblin. Feral clans ride them. Thrash does not. |
| **Black Lands boar** | Mount and food | Cheap, stubborn, loud. |
| **Wolf pack** | Camp threat | A spawn zone is low level, so wolves are the first test off the ship. |
| **Scavenger birds** | Warning system | Their alarm calls feed the camp's watch. |
| **Cave spiders** | Hazard in deep camps | Not goblin kin, only neighbors. |

Keep each creature at a low band. Spawn zones have no high level monsters, which is why goblins rarely evolve. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`]

## 4. Things that might be mistaken for goblin kin [PROPOSAL]

- **Imps and gas sprites:** magical, small, fire or gas flavored. Not Demihuman and not goblins. A Goblin Gas ability is a goblin skill, not a sprite.
- **Ogres and trolls:** big, dumb brutes. A Goblin Chieftain is a goblin who rose to lead, not an ogre. Keep them apart so evolution stays meaningful.
- **Players on a feral clan's side:** still players, still the Goblin race.

## 5. Rules for using these in scenes

1. A goblin NPC is a person first. Treat it like a person unless the scene makes it feral.
2. Worgs and boars are animals. They do not talk, and do not evolve into goblins.
3. Evolution applies to the goblin race only. A wolf does not become a worg by leveling.
4. Name nothing on the page that the author has not accepted from this file.
