# Creatures — Class IV

World-boss/raid tier, server-event scale. Rare, named, remembered, tied to World Boss Raids and Server Firsts (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`). See `codex/creatures/overview.md` for the stat-block convention and the four-class system generally.

## The Hollow King

Identity: an ancient, spirit-bound undead sovereign said to have ruled a kingdom that no longer appears on any map, found deep beneath a ruin in Lunarest's outer reaches. Thematically resonant with Shiv's own spirit-echo nature (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`), a fallen figure remade by loss into something that persists past where it should have ended, but **explicitly unconnected to Shiv or his lore**, a parallel, not a relative.

- **Stats:** Wisdom extremely high (a true world-tier caster), Power high, Speed low, Courage doesn't apply.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Book of the Veil-school effects (see `codex/magic/book-of-the-veil.md`) at Tier 6-7. Innate — commands lesser undead in its domain, comparable in kind to the Bonecaller Wraith's own presence (see `codex/creatures/class-3.md`) but at raid scale.
- **Rarity:** World-tier encounter, no player-earnable rarity applies to the boss itself, drops from it can be World-tier (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`).

## Ashen Wyrm

Identity: a fully mature drake, the adult form of the Class III Emberback Wyrmling (see `codex/creatures/class-3.md`), found nesting in Sunmarch's deep badlands, a genuine server-first target.

- **Stats:** Power extremely high, Courage extremely high, Speed high (true flight, unlike the wyrmling stage), Wisdom moderate.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Book of Embers-school breath effect (see `codex/magic/book-of-embers.md`) at Tier 6-7, plus true flight enabling hit-and-run aerial phases a ground-based party has to account for. Innate — heavy Fire resistance.
- **Rarity:** World-tier encounter.

## Stormcaller Roc

Identity: a colossal storm-wreathed avian, found nesting above the Wraith Isles' ranked-arena peaks, associated in player lore with the region's unpredictable weather.

- **Stats:** Speed extremely high, Power high, Wisdom moderate (enough to actually direct its storm effects rather than a passive weather hazard).
- **Abilities:** Activated — Book of Tempest-school effects (see `codex/magic/book-of-tempest.md`) at Tier 6-7, area-wide, forces a fighting party to deal with terrain/visibility disruption on top of the creature itself. Innate — near-untouchable while airborne, has to be forced or baited to ground.
- **Rarity:** World-tier encounter.
