# Creatures — Class I

Common, low-threat encounters. See `codex/creatures/overview.md` for the stat-block convention and the four-class system generally.

## Ash Wolf

Identity: a lean, soot-gray wolf found across most temperate/wild zones, hunts in small packs, dangerous in numbers rather than alone.

- **Stats:** Power and Speed above baseline, Wisdom near zero, no real Courage past a pack's own numbers.
- **Abilities:** Innate — pack coordination (attacks land harder when 2+ Ash Wolves target the same enemy). Activated — Lunge Bite, a basic pounce-strike.
- **Rarity:** Common.

## Cave Skitterling

Identity: a fist-sized, many-legged cave-dwelling swarm creature, rarely a threat alone, dangerous in the swarm-sized clusters they're actually found in.

- **Stats:** Speed high, everything else near the floor.
- **Abilities:** Innate — swarm resistance (physical attacks that hit fewer than a handful at once do reduced effective damage). Activated — none, purely a numbers threat.
- **Rarity:** Common.

## Bog Croaker

Identity: an oversized wetland amphibian, ambush hunter, mostly a nuisance unless a player wanders too close to its territory.

- **Stats:** Power moderate, Speed low outside of its ambush lunge, Wisdom near zero.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Tongue Snare, a ranged grab that roots a target briefly. Triggered — releases a nauseating musk if killed at close range (a lesser cousin of Goblin Gas's debuff family, see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`).
- **Rarity:** Common.

## Grasping Vine-Beast

Identity: territorial plant-creature, found in overgrown ruins and deep forest, stationary until provoked.

- **Stats:** Power high, Speed near zero (root-bound), Courage irrelevant, doesn't flee.
- **Abilities:** Activated — Root Snare, entangles anyone standing too close. Innate — regenerates slowly while any part of its root system survives.
- **Rarity:** Common.

## Wisp-Light

Identity: a small, drifting spirit-light found in old ruins and graveyards, more a hazard than a fighter, lures players off safe paths.

- **Stats:** Wisdom moderate (spirit-adjacent, not a caster in the mechanical sense), everything else near the floor.
- **Abilities:** Innate — Lure, distorts a player's sense of direction if they follow it too long. No direct combat ability.
- **Rarity:** Common.
