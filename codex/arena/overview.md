# The Arena — Overview

A standing, year-round PvP hub, distinct from the one-off tournament venues (see `codex/locations/singapore-championship-venue.md`) and from open-world PvP. Functions as HGO's neutral home for queued competitive play: the 1v1 Open Ladder and randomized-team matches both play out of here (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md` for the formats themselves). Loosely centered on the Wraith Isles region (already flagged as "ranked arena and tournament zone" in `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), though the arena hub itself is reachable from anywhere via teleport, not something a player has to physically travel to.

## Level-5 queue gate

A player has to reach character level 5 before they can open queue for any arena content at all, 1v1 or randomized team. Below that, the queue option simply doesn't exist on a player's menu. This is a real, hard gate, not a soft recommendation, consistent with how early and fragile a level 1-4 character actually is (see `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`). It's also a real pacing beat for Tatsuya specifically: he isn't arena-eligible yet at the point the story's currently at, PvE (the goblin camp, the World Quest) is what's actually available to him, not a genre choice on his part, a mechanical one.

## Teleport, but zone-locked

Any player who's unlocked the arena (level 5+) can teleport directly into the arena hub from anywhere in the world, no travel required. But the arena hub itself is a **locked zone**: a player who teleports in can queue, spectate, and use whatever's inside the hub itself, but cannot walk out into the surrounding city/region without the right access, a **city pass or visa** for that specific zone. A player who hasn't unlocked or earned access to wherever the arena happens to be regionally tied to is stuck inside the arena hub itself until they teleport back out the way they came. This mirrors the same "guards won't let you in without the right paperwork" logic already established for Lunarest's restricted zones (see `codex/locations/grand-library-of-lunarest.md`), just applied to a standing PvP venue instead of a one-time location.

Functionally, this makes the arena hub a genuine neutral ground, similar in spirit to Winter Bash's host-venue concept (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`) but permanent and always-open rather than a once-a-year event, anyone level 5+ can show up and queue regardless of what regions they've actually unlocked in the wider world.

## Matchmaking

- **1v1 Open Ladder** — anyone level 5+ can enter, resets each season. See `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md` and `codex/pro-scene/ranking-ladder.md` for the reward/ranking structure this feeds.
- **Randomized team queue** — the arena's own casual/ranked team format, distinct from a pre-formed 3v3/5v5 competitive match (see `codex/pro-scene/tournaments-and-events.md`), players queue solo or in small groups and get matched into a team for that session only. The lower-stakes, repeatable way most players actually practice team play before ever trying out for a real roster.
