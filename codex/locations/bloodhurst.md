# Bloodhurst

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. The Thrash clan's goblin camp, a specific site within Lunarest Outskirts (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), not a renaming of the outskirts itself, and not itself in Myrrhwood (Myrrhwood is ship-locked and genuinely separate from the reachable supercontinent, not a short walk, see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`). This is where a new goblin character actually starts, and where Tatsuya first spawns as a result of the goblin glitch (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## Visual reference (locked, from uploaded image)

Foggy, muted color palette, overcast light. Patched canvas tents in irregular clusters, worn and weathered, scattered pottery and salvaged goods piled around them. Two visible campfires, one large and central. A dark plume of smoke rising in the background, distinct from the cooking fires, worth keeping ambient and unexplained rather than narrated outright. Bare, stripped tree trunks in the background treeline. Reads scrappy, lived-in, faintly bleak rather than cozy, consistent with a camp that's been surviving repeated raids without a real win in years.

## Facts

- A defensible hillside/cliffside position, a single narrow path down being the only real way in or out. This is deliberate: the camp has survived repeated raids from a PK player faction hunting them for easy farm kills (see `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md`, PK_Wraith), and the terrain is the reason they've held on.
- **The camp's own defensible terrain becomes the trap, Chapter 8.** The single narrow path down is a road, and anyone holding it from outside can turn it into a kill zone for anything coming down from camp. The only alternative is a roughly 20-meter cliff drop off the hillside, lethal for most goblins, not a real option. This is Bloodhurst's own geography working against it, not the raiders' choice of vantage, PK_Wraith's crew (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`) just happened to make camp in the one spot that commands the chokepoint the terrain already creates.
- Home to the Thrash clan, led by Sevish Thrash (chieftain) and his sister Wyx Thrash, see their entries in `codex/characters/`.
- **Warband scale, updated Chapter 8:** grown to roughly fifty goblins at mixed low levels since the Chapter 5 estimate of twenty-five to thirty, real reinforcement over the time since, on top of the two chieftain-tier "gold tag" NPCs themselves (see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`). Anyone hunting a player under the Thrash clan's protection (Tatsuya, once Sevish names him an ally of Thrash, see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`) has to get through the whole warband first, not just outduel the two named leaders. Relevant to Tatsuya's own risk math in Chapter 5: only an old legacy-tier account would have the raw level to seriously threaten a warband that size, and even then it takes an organized party, not a solo attempt. An attacker who dies to the warband eats the same seven-day death lock a player like Tatsuya risks from a World Quest kill, a symmetric cost that's part of why nobody serious is likely to gamble on it during the reset window. **Concrete on-page confirmation, Chapter 8:** the World Quest system itself surfaces a `[THRASH CLAN DEFENDERS: 50/50]` invasion notification with a real countdown timer the moment Kesh's ambush report lands, the game's own UI backing up the fifty-goblin figure rather than it staying only a narrated estimate. This notification is stakes texture for the wider camp threat, the actual on-page resolution stays the small, targeted tent ambush Tatsuya, Ru, Rott, Nab, and Arya carry out in Chapter 9, not a full camp-wide battle sequence.
- **The near-wipe, Chapter 8-9.** While Tatsuya's offline for the real monitor fix, Sevish commits the full ~50-goblin warband against a second wave of reinforcements answering PK_Wraith's remaining officer. It's a real, costly loss, not a clean win, down to roughly fifteen survivors by the time Tatsuya logs back in. This is the direct trigger for Sevish and Wyx's decision to move the clan back to Myrrhwood (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`), not an abstract timeline landing on its own.
- Where Tatsuya first meets the camp, gets hazed and roasts back, kills two goblin NPCs in the resulting fight, and is taken into custody, all in Chapter 3. See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.
- **Departure for Myrrhwood, real trigger locked Chapter 8-9.** The near-wipe above is what actually decides it, not an abstract pre-expansion deadline. Sevish and Wyx commit to moving the clan back to Myrrhwood now, before the Crystal League expansion launches (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md` for the January 1st date). See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md` for the full deadline/rendezvous mechanic (a ship, a stated location, a real cost if Tatsuya misses it) this sets up for Tatsuya specifically.
