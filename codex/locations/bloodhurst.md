# Bloodhurst

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. The Thrash clan's goblin camp, a specific site within Lunarest Outskirts (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), not a renaming of the outskirts itself and not on the Myrrhwood continent (Myrrhwood only becomes a separate continent once the Crystal League expansion fully launches, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`, nobody is there yet in story-present). This is where a new goblin character actually starts, and where Tatsuya first spawns as a result of the goblin glitch (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## Visual reference (locked, from uploaded image)

Foggy, muted color palette, overcast light. Patched canvas tents in irregular clusters, worn and weathered, scattered pottery and salvaged goods piled around them. Two visible campfires, one large and central. A dark plume of smoke rising in the background, distinct from the cooking fires, worth keeping ambient and unexplained rather than narrated outright. Bare, stripped tree trunks in the background treeline. Reads scrappy, lived-in, faintly bleak rather than cozy, consistent with a camp that's been surviving repeated raids without a real win in years.

## Facts

- A defensible hillside/cliffside position, a single narrow path down being the only real way in or out. This is deliberate: the camp has survived repeated raids from a PK player faction hunting them for easy farm kills (see `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md`, PK_Wraith), and the terrain is the reason they've held on.
- Home to the Thrash clan, led by Sevish Thrash (chieftain) and his sister Wyx Thrash, see their entries in `codex/characters/`.
- **Warband scale (locked, Chapter 5):** Bloodhurst fields a full warband under Sevish and Wyx, roughly twenty-five to thirty goblins at mixed low levels, on top of the two chieftain-tier "gold tag" NPCs themselves (see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`). Anyone hunting a player under the Thrash clan's protection (Tatsuya, once Sevish names him an ally of Thrash, see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`) has to get through the whole warband first, not just outduel the two named leaders. Relevant to Tatsuya's own risk math in Chapter 5: only an old legacy-tier account would have the raw level to seriously threaten a warband that size, and even then it takes an organized party, not a solo attempt. An attacker who dies to the warband eats the same seven-day death lock a player like Tatsuya risks from a World Quest kill, a symmetric cost that's part of why nobody serious is likely to gamble on it during the reset window.
- Where Tatsuya first meets the camp, gets hazed and roasts back, kills two goblin NPCs in the resulting fight, and is taken into custody, all in Chapter 3. See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.
- **Departure for Myrrhwood, locked worldbuilding, not yet stated on the page:** the Thrash clan leaves Bloodhurst for Myrrhwood before the Crystal League expansion launches (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md` for the January 1st date), the two dates deliberately tied. Sevish is meant to state this directly to Tatsuya as a real deadline on his own business in Lunarest, but the Chapter 7 scene where that conversation would happen gets interrupted before it lands, see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.
