# Bloodhurst

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. The Thrash clan's goblin camp, a specific site within Lunarest Outskirts (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), not a renaming of the outskirts itself, and not itself in Myrrhwood (Myrrhwood is ship-locked and genuinely separate from the reachable supercontinent, not a short walk, see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`). This is where a new goblin character actually starts, and where Tatsuya first spawns as a result of the goblin glitch (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## Facts

- A defensible hillside/cliffside position, a single narrow path down being the only real way in or out. This is deliberate: the camp has survived repeated raids from a PK player faction hunting them for easy farm kills (see `codex/characters/old-draft-minor-references.md`, PK_Wraith), and the terrain is the reason they've held on.
- **The camp's own defensible terrain becomes the trap, Chapter 8.** The single narrow path down is a road, and anyone holding it from outside can turn it into a kill zone for anything coming down from camp. The only alternative is a roughly 20-meter cliff drop off the hillside, lethal for most goblins, not a real option. This is Bloodhurst's own geography working against it, not the raiders' choice of vantage, PK_Wraith's crew (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`) just happened to make camp in the one spot that commands the chokepoint the terrain already creates.
- Home to the Thrash clan, led by Sevish Thrash (chieftain) and his sister Wyx Thrash, see their entries in `codex/characters/`.
- **Warband scale (author-stated, latest; supersedes the 30 to 40, "fifty" and "grown to fifty" figures).** About **25 goblins in total**, scouts and patrols included. Roughly 20 are in the camp at any time and visible on the page in Chapter 6. After the near-wipe about **15** remain. The Chapter 9 invasion notification reads `[THRASH CLAN DEFENDERS: 25/25]` and the Chapter 10 count reads "Fifteen out of twenty-five". Anyone hunting a player under Thrash's protection still has to get through the whole warband plus the two named leaders; see `codex/lore/npc-tag-system.md`. Stakes texture only: the on-page resolution stays the small, targeted tent ambush in Chapter 10, not a camp-wide battle.
- **The near-wipe, Chapter 8-9.** While Tatsuya's offline for the real monitor fix, Sevish commits the full ~25-goblin warband against a second wave of reinforcements answering PK_Wraith's remaining officer. It's a real, costly loss, not a clean win, down to roughly fifteen survivors by the time Tatsuya logs back in. This is the direct trigger for Sevish and Wyx's decision to move the clan back to Myrrhwood (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`), not an abstract timeline landing on its own.
- Where Tatsuya first meets the camp, gets hazed and roasts back, kills two goblin NPCs in the resulting fight, and is taken into custody, all in Chapter 3. See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.
- **Departure for Myrrhwood, real trigger locked Chapter 8-9.** The near-wipe above is what actually decides it, not an abstract pre-expansion deadline. Sevish and Wyx commit to moving the clan back to Myrrhwood now, before the Hearthfire expansion launches (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md` for the January 1st date). See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md` for the full deadline/rendezvous mechanic (a ship, a stated location, a real cost if Tatsuya misses it) this sets up for Tatsuya specifically.

## Who lives in the camp, ranks, and why there are only two hobgoblins (author-stated, latest)

- **Ranks.** The clan still gives its people the tools to succeed, but there are ranks and you earn your gear; nothing is simply handed over. They did not come out with farm tools: they came with what they raided with, and it came over on the Thrash ship.
- **Almost everyone is a grunt.** The goblins who keep arriving are low-level recruits. In the goblins' world they are new recruits from the Thrash ship. In the game's logic they are respawns (about a month for the camp to refill, which is why the Goblin March is monthly).
- **Why they never evolve.** A goblin becomes a hobgoblin only by leveling steadily through combat without dying. Sevish never sends the recruits out to fight, and the zone they spawn in has no high-level monsters, so they do not level. Most goblins never live long enough to rise. Sevish and Wyx do not know the mechanics; this is the game's reason, not theirs.
- **Count.** Only **two hobgoblins** are in the camp (Sevish and Wyx). Potentially a **third** when Tatsuya becomes a Shiv Goblin, a **fourth** with Ru. That belongs to the Shiv storyline and is not on the page yet.
- **Tone.** Wyx says it without disgracing the goblins; Tatsuya's "maybe I thought less of goblins" is only a thought, because he is a goblin now.
