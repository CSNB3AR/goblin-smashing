# Sakura Arcade

**Type:** Real-world location, Tokyo — **Shibuya ward**, the far side from WattsUp/the Nakamura Grand, see `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`. Leon's business, where Tatsuya finds VR hardware access.

## Visual reference (locked)

Warm pendant lighting, exposed brick and wood paneling, a real gaming-lounge feel rather than a dingy back-alley cafe. Themed gaming chairs, multiple screens per station. Reads well-kept and inviting on the surface even though Leon's business is struggling underneath, the two first-gen pods live somewhere less polished than the front-of-house setup this reference implies, an older, separate section of the space.

## Facts

- Owned by Leon (see `codex/characters/leon.md`), whose sister Arya (see `codex/characters/arya.md`) is also often present.
- Has two first-gen Kazuhaya Tech pods, but only one is functional, the sole first-gen pod still running in the area, see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`. The other is a dead shell.
- Where Tatsuya's character creation and the goblin glitch happen, see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`.
- **J-Link Shibuya** is approximately two blocks away — a chain location running both second-gen and third-gen pods, larger than Sakura. This is one of the "third-gen cafes in a five-block radius eating us alive" (Leon, ch5). In the chapter 9 scene, Arya logs in from J-Link Shibuya; characters say "J-Link" without further specification since in context the Shibuya branch is implied. J-Link Akiba (Akihabara) is a separate chain location, referenced in the ch2/3 pod-hunting sequence.
- **Scene timing anchor:** the Tatsuya/Arya negotiation scene in chapter 9 takes place around 8:50–8:54 PM. The VRSA inspector came out late (standard for commercial full-dive venues). Leon's closing offer: "We normally close at nine, but play it out."
