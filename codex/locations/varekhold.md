# Varekhold

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. A mountain fortress above a cold coast in Valefrost (which coast is OPEN). Forward material, not yet on the page.

## Facts

- A natural mountain keep above a cold coast, reached by steep switchbacks, with a small hold of roughly 250 to 300 residents when manned: smiths, fishers, a garrison, and the families that feed them.
- It is a **defensible fort site** of the kind Node Wars fight over (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`): one approach road through Varekpass (`codex/locations/varekpass.md`), a harbor below, and a forge that can supply a small guild with mono steel work (`codex/materials/monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md`).
- Valefrost's Dwarves and Frostborn treat it as a border keep, not a capital. No player guild holds it at the start of the story. A guild that claims it inherits a hall with real upkeep: food and fuel run short in winter, and the pass is the only supply line.
- Good guild hall candidate for a small, patient guild: easy to hold, hard to feed.

## Sensory palette

Salt wind, pine resin, cold water, fish oil, forge smoke, steep switchbacks, gulls, distant surf.
