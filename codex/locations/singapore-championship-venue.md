# Championship Venue, Singapore

**Type:** Real-world location, Singapore. Host site for the Season Zero finale, the broadcast Chapter 1 opens on. Single source for the broader structure: `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`.

## Facts

- A major neutral international hub, clearly distant and foreign from Tatsuya's own Tokyo, not just "the city."
- Guilds qualify in from regions worldwide (Japan, NA, UK, China, and others). The Season Zero final, the Battle of the Border Fortress (12 Aogiri against 20 London Spire), is fought in-world at the border fortress, while the crowd, Yuna and the broadcast sit at Singapore arenas and facilities. Background color like a UK guild eliminating a Chinese guild along the way is available texture, not required on the page.
- **Presentation:** a hybrid broadcast, a feed from the in-world battle plus a real, sold-out physical venue where fans gather and the newest hardware (third-gen helmets) is on public display. Big spectacle, real crowds, not just a screen. Convention-style, with attendee rare-drop draws.
- Kazuku's avatar presence at the physical venue is grounded by Singapore's own real-world reputation for advanced projection/holographic CG technology, which is how his virtual avatar gets a physical stadium presence without ever revealing his true identity.
