# Varekpass

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. A garrison settlement at the northern pass in Valefrost. Forward material.

## Facts

- The **chokepoint node** between the Ashenveil approach (`codex/locations/ashenveil-forest.md`) and Varekhold (`codex/locations/varekhold.md`). Whoever holds the pass controls what reaches the keep.
- A small garrison town built on a thin supply line: chronically underdeveloped, near famine in a bad winter, held together with straw and stubbornness. It cannot sustain a long fight and needs reinforcement from the keep, not the other way round.
- Creature pressure: Class II to III beasts such as the Greyvast come down from the Ashenveil and make every shortfall worse (`codex/creatures/`).
- **Node Wars use:** the classic pass fort. Small guilds can win it by cutting its supplies, not by storming it. A good trade-company target: it will pay well for food, fuel and repair goods (`codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`).

## Sensory palette

Wind off the ridge, wet snow on packed road, cold iron, woodsmoke, straw and thin broth.
