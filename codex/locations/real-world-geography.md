# Real-World Geography

Grounding note for the real-world half of the story. All real-world locations should read as real Japanese places, not invented compound names.

## Facts

- WattsUp, the Nakamura Grand, Tochi Academy, and Sakura Arcade are all in Tokyo's **Shibuya ward** (Kanto region). Tatsuya essentially never travels outside the ward (see `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md`), so most real-world travel in the story stays hyperlocal, WattsUp and the Nakamura Grand are a short Haya EB ride apart, and Sakura Arcade sits on the far side of the ward, cut off enough to read as a real trip without ever actually leaving Shibuya. **The one clear exception:** the Nexus Enterprises flagship store (see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`) is downtown in **Ginza, Chuo ward**, a genuine, deliberate departure from his usual radius.
- Any address/pickup reference should read as a real store in a real ward ("WattsUp, in Shibuya"), not an invented compound place name ("WattsUp Shibuya" as if it were its own proper noun).
- The family's school trip destination is **Kyoto**, in the **Kansai region**, roughly a six-day trip given the distance.
- The World Championship is hosted in **Singapore** (see `codex/locations/singapore-championship-venue.md`), a real, distant, foreign location from Tatsuya's Tokyo.
