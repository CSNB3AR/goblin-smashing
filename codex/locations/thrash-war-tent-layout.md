# Thrash war tent: floor plan (author-requested positions, Chapter Ten)

Round tent, wide enough to walk a full circle around the war table. Timber ribs curve up to the center pole (lantern). The back wall faces the cliff: the waterfall hum and cold come through it. Entrance flap in the front wall with bone totems on the poles outside (Chapter Six); a cross rope above the entrance sits low enough that Ru has to bend his head.

Seen from someone entering through the flap, facing in:
- BACK WALL (far side): chest and barrels stacked tidy (Chapter Six). Sevish stands in front of them on the far side of the table, axe in the chair at his right hand. Wyx stands at Sevish's left when she is in the tent.
- CENTER: round war table with the map of the continents and ringed candles; lantern above on the center pole.
- NEAR SIDE OF TABLE (flap at his back): Tatsuya, center. Zinka takes the spot on his right once she enters.
- LEFT WALL: barrels, where Kesh sits; Rott and Nab wait along this wall.
- RIGHT WALL: Wyx's crate (the gear crate).
- FLAP, RIGHT SIDE INSIDE: Ru, shoulder to the canvas, watching the camp through a gap.
