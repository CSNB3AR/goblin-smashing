# The Astralum Archive

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. Hidden beneath the royal palace in Lunarest. Forward plot thread, not yet drafted.

## Facts

Goblins are historically barred from the capital. Shiv (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`) wants Kaiseki (Tatsuya's in-game name) to eventually reach the Astralum Archive as part of the long-term "Call of the Bones" World Quest arc. See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`.

## What the archive keeps

The archive holds the truth about the first queen, **Isaveil Thandrel**, killed about forty-five years ago and unlisted in the royal rolls, and about Shiv (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). Goblins are barred from the capital, and the palace's restricted zones add a second gate.
