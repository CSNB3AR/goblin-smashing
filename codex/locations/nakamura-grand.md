# The Nakamura Grand

**Type:** Real-world location, Tokyo (Shibuya ward). The family's home and Chiyo's hotel.

## Visual reference (locked)

A green-covered modern building, several stories, warm-lit windows and entrance, balconies with plants. Reads as a boutique high-end property rather than a sprawling traditional grand hotel, consistent with "high-end" without needing a huge ornate lobby.

## Facts

- Owned and run by Chiyo Nakamura (see `codex/characters/chiyo-nakamura.md`).
- The family's apartment sits above/attached to the hotel building itself, ground floor is the hotel, upstairs is home.
- Real-world geography: in Tokyo's Shibuya ward (Kanto region), a short Haya EB ride from WattsUp. See `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`.

- **Basement storage and the temp (author-stated).** The family lives in the hotel. The basement storage cage holds Daichi's old gear and parts, moved there a few months ago when Chiyo boxed it up so the room could be Tatsuya's again. Chiyo hired a temp to run the front desk during her six days away; the temp is Arya (Tatsuya does not know yet).

- **High-end and the storage level (author-stated).** The Nakamura Grand is a high-end hotel (stone floor, brass, flowers, quiet). Chiyo texts Tatsuya that she hired someone who will be in later and he should greet her (the temp is Arya, not revealed). The basement storage is a level below the lobby, reached by elevator with Chiyo's key.

- **Arya at the hotel (author-stated, notes only, never on the page).** The temp Chiyo hired for the front desk is Arya. It is her second job. Tatsuya does not know her yet and does not see her until he is home again after the arcade. She has another job to get to, which is also why the desk gives them room to talk more about the game.

- **Hotel money and staffing (author-stated).** The Nakamura Grand is a nice Midtown-style hotel. Chiyo normally has people working it, but she cut staff to save money because she is paying big-time lawyers to fight the Tabuchis, who have big money, while also covering Ohema's hospital costs. The family lives on the third floor. Route in chapter 3: elevator down to the lobby, the storage key from the drawer of Chiyo's desk, the elevator again to the basement level, which sits below the lobby and holds Dad's stored gear.
- **Why Tochi took him on merit (author-stated, how it reads).** He was technically smart and could do what his dad did at a very young age; not as advanced as his dad, but on the same path and on par for his age. Chapter 3 says it plainly after the repair, without a speech.
- **Voice in the repair (author-stated).** He talks like a car mechanic who works with an electrician: load tests, the 12-volt aux rail, jump terminals, internal resistance.
