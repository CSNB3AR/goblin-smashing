# Nexus Enterprises Flagship Store

**Type:** Real-world location, Tokyo. Ginza, Chuo ward, downtown Tokyo. Distinct from WattsUp (see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`), a separate, unrelated commercial chain, not to be conflated.

## Facts

Kazuku's own company (see `codex/characters/kazuku.md`), presenting itself less like a store and more like a showroom, "kind of like an Apple Store." Glass and brushed steel, three stories, third-gen helmets mounted on individual lit pedestals under the HGO compass rose logo, a wall-sized display looping match highlights. Demo pods let walk-in visitors try a helmet for a few free minutes. The pickup counter for HGO access drives sits toward the back, past the demo floor. This is where Tatsuya collects his drive in Chapter 2, not WattsUp, an important, deliberate distinction, WattsUp is a commercial EV-charging sponsor, not a Nexus retail outlet.

## Geography note

Downtown, a real departure for Tatsuya, who otherwise has essentially never left Shibuya ward (see `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md` and `codex/locations/real-world-geography.md`). This is one of maybe two times in his life he's been down here, both as a kid, on some Chiyo errand.
