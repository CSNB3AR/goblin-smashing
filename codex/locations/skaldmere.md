# Skaldmere

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. A small waypost town on the southern approach to the first pass in Valefrost. Forward material.

## Facts

- A palisade wall around roughly a dozen tightly packed buildings, a central road barely wide enough for a cart, an iron fire cradle burning at the gate, and a guard on each side who has seen too many winters to be impressed.
- A **waypost town node**: inn, market stalls along the wall, a repair-grade smith, and rooms for travelers coming out of the Ashenveil with monocrystals to trade (`codex/materials/monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md`). Prices run high for anyone who looks desperate.
- The last safe-ish place before the mountains. Parties stop here to rest, resupply and sell, which makes it a natural meeting point and a good place to hear rumors.
- **Node Wars use:** a modest town node whose taxes depend on traffic. Holding the road matters more than holding the walls.

## Sensory palette

Woodsmoke, cooked fat, livestock not fully mucked out, packed dirt over stone, snowmelt mud at the edges, shuttered stalls at evening.
