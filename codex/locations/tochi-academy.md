# Tochi Academy

**Type:** Real-world location, Tokyo. Major, prestigious sports and academic school running from elementary through academy level.

## Facts

- Tatsuya got in on genuine merit, he tested in, smart and tech-savvy like his father, reinforced by Daichi's tech infrastructure contributions to the school and his own history there (student, instructor or donor-builder is Open).
- He needs to go back: Tochi has the resources and curriculum required to maintain the first-gen pods long-term, and his path back runs through Chiyo's instructor position, Ohema's volleyball legacy, and Daichi's tech legacy. See `codex/subplots/tochi-academy-return.md`.
- Yuna has also been at Tochi since elementary school.
- Has a real, enforced zero-tolerance policy on bullying, hazing, and family-influence targeting students on campus, which is exactly why it's the one place in Japan the Tabuchis have no reach at all. Tatsuya was hazed there and chose not to report it, consistent with his instinct to absorb rather than escalate, even with real institutional backup available to him.
- He only completed about three months of his first year of high school (age 16) before Eren's betrayal pushed him out entirely. OPEN: whether Daichi died before or during Tatsuya's roughly three months at Tochi. The only author-stated anchors are that Daichi was killed about a year ago, months before HGO and the third-gen pods released, and that Tatsuya lasted about three months of his first year at Tochi before the Eren betrayal pushed him out and he has been out a full school year.
- Eren's team funding traces back to the Tabuchis investing in a school guild program once second-gen pods opened HGO access, consistent with Tochi's existing pedigree in competitive sports (baseball, Ohema's volleyball) and tech (Daichi's infrastructure work), the school leans into being competitive at everything.

**Re-entry, resolved:** if Tatsuya returns to Tochi, he comes back as a full first-year. He never completed that year the first time, so there's no advanced placement and no version where he's ahead of Eren, he's behind, and re-entry means starting over.

## Governance: the school council (new)

Tochi's major decisions, including whether to open a real representative qualifier for outside guild competition rather than default to an incumbent, run through an adult council: Chiyo, the Tabuchis, and five other unnamed members. Not a bureaucratic detail to dwell on, just enough structure to make Act Two's representative qualifier read as a real institutional decision rather than an arbitrary event. See `codex/pro-scene/tochi-representative-qualifier.md` for why the council actually opens the slot up now.

- **Why Tochi is safe and what it has (author-stated).** Tochi is the safest place to be: by the prime minister's law it is connected to all politics. It also has the national volleyball and baseball programs. No esports or HGO guild existed there until Eren brought one in. Chiyo wants Tatsuya in school there because she has the money and wants him to focus on school.

## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. OPEN: the earlier wording that Tochi is "connected to all politics" is superseded; OPEN: "his dad finally got him in" versus merit only; OPEN: Ohema "former player" versus "national team coach"; OPEN: first-year versus "maybe second year".
- **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. Eren walked by, saw it, and the friendship grew over a few months. Eren did not know Tatsuya was Daichi's son; Tatsuya did not know his dad knew Eren's dad. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.
- **The loan.** Daichi sold everything and his business money and royalties went to his wife's hospital bills. As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.
- **OPEN.** The exact order of Daichi's death versus the loan and the betrayal.
- **Tochi, later (author-stated).** In chapter 2 Tatsuya has no intention of going back to Tochi; he wants to show Chiyo he can make real money, and if he cannot, she will send him there. His later reason: at Sakura Arcade he finds first-gen pods, the manual lacks the details to fix them, he cannot find a pod to play on, and on the Kyoto trip he decides Tochi is his best bet. Also: he starts to suspect someone is behind the Tabuchis (maybe at Tochi) and questions Daichi's closed casket.
