# Thrash Camp Field Guide (Bloodhurst, as the page shows it)

**Status:** A working guide for the writer. It says what is already fixed on the page, what the camp must plausibly contain, what its people do all day, how it holds a line, and what is safe or unsafe to mention. It does not design Tatsuya's Chapter 11 plan. Companion files: `codex/races/dnd-goblinoid-peoples.md` (the peoples) and `codex/combat/goblin-warfare-and-tactics.md` (the tactics).

## 1. What the page and the image already fix

### The valley (Chapters 4, 5, 6, 10)

- A cliff-walled valley. A waterfall pours down rock at the back into a stream. Everything that is not the track is "cliff and water". [CANON Chapter 4]
- **One dirt track** curves off to the right between the sharpened stakes. Standing at the fire pit and looking up the slope, right is east. The track runs down to a road. [CANON Chapters 4 and 10, ledger "Chapter Six reasoning"]
- The ground by the pool below the fall is stagnant mud. The wind comes down off the rock cold and damp. [CANON Chapter 4]
- **The east side:** the stakes stop a few paces short of the cliff, where the ground breaks into loose rock and falls away into trees. [CANON Chapter 10]
- Night in the game, with the camp lit by torches along the stakes and the burned-down fire pit; smoke hides the stars. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten: night, Wyx's Howl, stream back on"]

### What stands in it

- **Hide-covered A-frame tents on heavy timber frames**, worn dirt paths, a **stone-ringed fire pit** in front with smoke rising, a **cart with hay bales**, **sharpened stakes** along the clearing edge, **two blue banners** on poles, pines, mossy rock walls. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`, "camp image"]
- **One huge tent in the middle** with the hay cart beside it. Four tents huddle around it, plus a smaller pale one. Supply crates are stacked beside the big tent; barrels and crates are the only cover. A narrow lane runs between two tents toward the track. [CANON Chapters 4 and 5, ledger "Chapter 4 chase and camp layout"]
- **A stew pot** boils in the middle of camp with a goblin stirring it with a long spoon. A **water barrel** sits at the edge of the firelight. [CANON Chapter 4]
- **Bone totems** cap the poles on either side of the big tent's flap. Inside: a lantern on the center pole, a chest and barrels tidy against the canvas, a round war table with a map. [CANON Chapter 6]
- **The holding tent** is small, patched, near the eastern edge away from the central fires, with a single oil lamp, a thin bedroll on straw and two guards at the flap. [CANON Chapters 6, 9, 10]
- **Weapons and kit:** enforcers carry blades; line goblins carry clubs and mismatched shields (a tent door, a barrel lid, a cart panel). Wyx keeps a gear crate. [CANON Chapters 4, 6, 10]
- **Defenses in use:** outer stakes, an inner barricade raised on Ru's order, goblins in a thin line along the stakes with gaps where somebody had been a minute ago. [CANON Chapter 10]
- **Numbers:** about 25 at the start, 23 after Tatsuya's kills, 14 and then 10 during the raid. [CANON ledger "Head count, audited"]

### What the image adds [IMAGE]

- The big tent is a long hide A-frame with a plank strip along the ridge, hides stitched in pale patches, and a timber-framed gable end hung with hides and small round pieces mounted on it.
- Two **plank-roofed structures**: one behind the big tent near the stream, one beside the track at the top right.
- **Plank steps** cut into the slope in front of two of the tents.
- A second, darker smoke trail behind the big tent, distinct from the cookfire smoke.
- The hay cart is two-wheeled with one big bale, standing near the front, close to the two blue banners. The banners are narrow with a pale glyph on blue.
- Clusters of sharpened stakes angled outward at the front right and along the right side, with logs piled beside them.
- A small tent at the right edge, with a crate at its front, sits where the holding tent should be.
