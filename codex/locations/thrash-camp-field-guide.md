## 1. What the page and the image already fix

### The valley (Chapters 4, 5, 6, 10)

- A cliff-walled valley. A waterfall pours down rock at the back into a stream. Everything that is not the track is "cliff and water". [CANON Chapter 4]
- **One dirt track** curves off to the right between the sharpened stakes. Standing at the fire pit and looking up the slope, right is east. The track runs down to a road. [CANON Chapters 4 and 10, ledger "Chapter Six reasoning"]
- The ground by the pool below the fall is stagnant mud. The wind comes down off the rock cold and damp. [CANON Chapter 4]
- **The east side:** the stakes stop a few paces short of the cliff, where the ground breaks into loose rock and falls away into trees. [CANON Chapter 10]
- Night in the game, with the camp lit by torches along the stakes and the burned-down fire pit; smoke hides the stars. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten: night, Wyx's Howl, stream back on"]

- **Hide-covered A-frame tents on heavy timber frames**, worn dirt paths, a **stone-ringed fire pit** in front with smoke rising, a **cart with hay bales**, **sharpened stakes** along the clearing edge, **two blue banners** on poles, pines, mossy rock walls. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`, "camp image"]
- **One huge tent in the middle** with the hay cart beside it. Four tents huddle around it, plus a smaller pale one. Supply crates are stacked beside the big tent; barrels and crates are the only cover. A narrow lane runs between two tents toward the track. [CANON Chapters 4 and 5, ledger "Chapter 4 chase and camp layout"]

- **Bone totems** cap the poles on either side of the big tent's flap. Inside: a lantern on the center pole, a chest and barrels tidy against the canvas, a round war table with a map. [CANON Chapter 6]

## 2. What the camp contains, and why

### The checklist D&D gives a goblin camp

- A camp has a guarded perimeter, walls, traps and patrol routes; caves and ruins are very defensible; the leader's tastes show in tents, trophies and belongings; the clan leaves traces (cleared forest, thinned game, old campsites); a den for wolves or worgs needs keepers and a training area; patrols stay near camp while scouts watch the wider ground. [SRC](https://2minutetabletop.com/how-to-run-goblins/) (secondhand DM advice)
- Goblin lairs keep lookouts in trees and wolves on the perimeter. Hobgoblins build military-style camps, and a command post can be a stone fortress, a palisaded command tent or a fully stocked war camp. [SRC](https://dungeonmister.com/lore/monsters/goblinoids-in-dnd/) (secondhand); [SRC](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/263-how-to-play-hobgoblins-like-perfect-soldiers)
- The best-known published goblin base has a blind at the entrance with two sentries, a kennel, a bridge, a flood trap and a leader's chamber the whole tribe walks through. [SRC](https://rpgindesign.blogspot.com/2019/07/analytical-review-of-lost-mine-of.html) (secondhand review)
- A real army camp dug a ditch, piled the spoil into a rampart and topped it with a stake palisade whose stakes kept side branches to interlock. It kept a clear strip inside the wall, put latrines in or near that strip over drains, kept hospital and workshops in the central band, and rotated sentries. [SRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castra)

**Thrash has the stakes, the sentries and the lookouts. It has no ditch, no walls, no towers and no kennel. It shows its leaders' tastes in one tent.** That gap is the point of the camp. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten tightened after the author's listen-through": the camp was not raided, it was bled]

## 3. What the people do all day

The page only shows evening and night. This schedule is for the writer's reference; mention a daytime detail only when a scene needs it. The game clock and the real clock run differently, so a scene's hour is whatever the story needs. | When | What happens | Who | What you hear and smell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dawn** | Night watch hands over on the stakes. The fire pit is raked and the first pot goes on (ash-cakes, thin broth). Water comes from the stream above the pool, not from the mud. | Stakehands, the cook, Landed | Grey light, cold, steam off the pot. [PROPOSAL] |
| **Early morning** | Wyx drills the Blades on the packed ground before the big tent. Stakehands pair off with club and shield. Sevish trains where the camp can see him. | Wyx, Sevish | Wood on wood, grunts. [CANON Sevish "trains every day"; PROPOSAL scene] |

| **Afternoon** | Foraging on the forest edge, fish traps in the stream, a goblin or two back from a village market with pelts, pitch and scrap. The bone-setter makes rounds. Stake and barricade repairs. | Posts, scouts | Boots, hauling. [CANON village access; PROPOSAL the rest] |

| **Night** | Two on the stakes, two at the lookout, two at the holding tent flap. The fire kept low. Most sleep in fives. The fall's hiss under everything. | The watch | Wind, a snore, a pot ticking as it cools. [PROPOSAL] |

**What they do when nothing is happening.** Arm-wrestle (canon). Cheat at it (canon). Gamble with knucklebones, which suits a race whose racial stat is Fortune. Whittle bone. Mend rags. Tease the runt. Boast about the last March. Sharpen what is already sharp. Tell the old tales at the pot once the bowls are empty. [CANON Chapter 4 for the first two; PROPOSAL for the rest]

**The monthly pulse.** A ship brings new recruits about once a month; the Goblin March, the mass charge on Lunarest, runs on the same cadence; between them the camp is thin. [CANON ledger "RESOLVED, Goblin March cadence"] That pulse is why the grunts are never veterans: nobody has been alive long enough to become one. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`]

## 4. How the camp is defended

### The two walls and the layers behind them

Wyx and Sevish say it on the page: anyone near the outer stakes meets Wyx; if she is passed, or more come and reach the inner barricade, Sevish comes out; only if both fail do they reach the people. "It is how we have held every camp since we left Myrrhwood." [CANON Chapter 10]

| Layer | Where | Held by | Its job | Its limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Eyes** | The east line, the rock shelf, the plank watch hut | Zinka, Kesh, scouts | See the track and the road early | Five of them died in the first hour. [CANON Chapter 9] |
| **Outer stakes** | Along the clearing edge, across the track | Wyx, with the line | Slow anyone who climbs; make them come in a file | A fence, not a wall: burns, can be pulled, ends short of the east cliff. |
| **Inner barricade** | Behind the stakes | Sevish, with the line | Stop whatever passes the stakes | Raised in a hurry from carts, crates and spare timber. |
| **The people** | The tents, the fire pit | Everyone else | Survive | Nothing behind them. |
| **The cliff and the fall** | The back of the valley | Nobody | Closes the valley | There is no retreat: the one track in is the one track out, and the alternative is a roughly 20 meter drop, lethal for most goblins. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`] |

### What the high ground gives and costs

- **Gives:** range for thrown stones and the few bows; sight down the whole track; time, because attackers climb; light on the attackers if the torches are placed to show them. [SRC](https://www.tribality.com/2020/12/29/using-monsters-beyond-their-stat-blocks-goblinoid-tactics/) (secondhand) says goblinoids at home pick chokepoints and hold escape routes; Thrash has the chokepoint but no escape route.
- **Costs:** it is worth nothing the moment the clan goes down to the road, and the camp is a cul-de-sac. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md` "The camp's own defensible terrain becomes the trap"]

### How it lost numbers (the pattern, not a plan)

Sevish's habit was to send goblins down the hill in twos and threes to meet each party. The raiders held the road from a tent that watched the track, cast in bursts, drank, and cast again. Every goblin who fell stayed fallen. The camp was never raided; it was bled. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten tightened after the author's listen-through", Chapter 10] The lesson the writer can use is plain and already on the page: they fought where the raiders were strongest and gave up the place where they were strongest.

### Signals

- Wyx puts two fingers to her mouth and whistles; guards come. [CANON Chapter 6]
- A bone horn: one long, raiders on the track. Two short, everyone inside the stakes. Three, Sevish to the line. [PROPOSAL]
- "Walls first!" from Wyx, "Walls hold!" from the line. [PROPOSAL, from `codex/races/dnd-goblinoid-peoples.md`]
- A pot lid struck on a post is the cook's alarm: fire in camp. [PROPOSAL]

### What the camp cannot do (guard against overreach)

No gate, no towers, no moat, no ditch, no engines, no cavalry, no beast kennel, no casters, no healing magic. Few bows and none well. The camp is a hill, a track, a line of stakes and two very strong people. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten: the formation plan on the page" notes the bows are weak; CANON `codex/races/goblin.md` for the casting gate]

### Weak points the page already shows

- The stakes stop short of the east cliff, where loose rock falls into trees. [CANON Chapter 10]
- The line stands under torches with gaps, and no two shields match. [CANON Chapter 10]
- A stake row can be burned, and widely spaced heavy stakes can be pulled out; a real army camp used smaller, closer stakes so they were harder to uproot. [SRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palisade)
- The only way in is the only way out. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`]

Do not turn any of these into a plan in this file. They are what an observant person could notice; what to do with them is Tatsuya's, and it is held for Chapter 11. [CANON ledger "Chapter Ten: the strategy stays a surprise"]

## 5. Details that fit, and details that break the setting

### Fifteen that fit

1. Tent flaps tied back with sinew cord, or weighted with a stone at the hem. Hide has no hinge. [PROPOSAL]
2. Hide patches of different pale shades on one tent, stitched with dark sinew in uneven knots. [IMAGE]
3. A shield that is plainly a tent door, a barrel lid or a cart panel, still showing the barrel's brand. [CANON Chapter 10]
4. Crate lids and barrel staves as spare shield stock, stacked by the stakes. [PROPOSAL]
5. Stakes with fire-blackened points and stripped bark, pale where fresh, grey where old. [PROPOSAL]
6. A pot lid banged on a post as the cook's alarm. [PROPOSAL]
7. Wet hides on lashed frames, and the reek that goes with them. [CANON smell]
8. Knucklebones scratched on a flat stone, and a free hand sliding under an arm-wrestle table. [CANON Chapter 4; PROPOSAL bones]
9. Bone totems whose carving is blocky and thick, matching Wyx's arms. Bone only, never a person. [CANON]
10. Plank steps cut into the slope, slick when it rains. [IMAGE]
11. Iron cressets and pitch torches hissing in the damp air off the fall. [CANON]
12. Straw bedding crackling in the holding tent, and the lamp's smell of cheap oil. [CANON]
13. Salt pork, hardtack and fish stew from ship stores and stream traps, with a few foraged roots. [PROPOSAL]
14. Banners limp on their poles because the valley air barely moves. [IMAGE]
15. A smith's hammer muffled by the fall, so the forge is heard before it is seen. [PROPOSAL]

### Eight that break it

1. **Doors, hinges, latches or locks on tents.** Hide tents have flaps. Wyx's chest is the only lock in camp and it is a crate hasp. [CANON tent flaps]
2. **Glass, windows, chimneys or stoves.** Smoke leaves through the ridge gap. Light is lamp, torch or fire. [PROPOSAL]
3. **A stone wall, gate, tower or ditch.** The camp has stakes and a hurried barricade only. [CANON ledger on Chapter 10]
4. **Healing potions, priests or cast healing in camp.** Goblins cannot cast, and the bone-setter works with splints and herbs. [CANON `codex/races/goblin.md`]
5. **A wolf or worg kennel, or mounted goblins.** Ash Wolves are wild Class I creatures, not clan animals. [CANON `codex/creatures/class-1.md`]
6. **Plate armour, matched shields, matched uniforms.** Gear is rank-locked and scavenged. Only the two co-leaders and the Blades look equipped. [CANON]
7. **Clean water from the pool, or a latrine upstream.** The pool is stagnant mud. Drinking water is drawn from the stream above it. [CANON Chapter 4]
8. **A large veteran garrison, or any named goblin over level 2 beyond the co-leaders.** The monthly turnover keeps everyone green. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`]

## 7. Open conflicts and decisions

1. **Two smoke trails.** The image shows cookfire smoke plus a darker trail behind the big tent. Treat the dark one as the forge or leave it unexplained. [OPEN]
2. **Hide outside, canvas inside the big tent.** Chapter 6 describes a cream canvas lining. Reconcile as a hide A-frame with a canvas liner the Goblin March looted from a ship. [PROPOSAL]
3. **The holding tent's place.** The image puts a small tent at the right edge; the chapters put it at the east edge. These agree. Keep it there. [CANON]
4. **Animals.** Whether the camp keeps a pony or goat is the author's call. [OPEN]
5. **The name Bloodhurst.** Stays a codex name until the page gives it to Tatsuya. [CANON]
