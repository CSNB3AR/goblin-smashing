# The Barren Lands

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. A contested borderland zone. Forward material.

## Facts

- A stretch of open, poor ground where several holds meet, with no clear owner. It is the standing **contested zone** for Node Wars in the north: guilds fight over it for buffs and minor perks, and bigger guilds with existing numbers move in first (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).
- Poor yields but good sightlines, which makes it a real battlefield, not a farm. Raiding parties and mercenary companies cross it constantly.
- **The funeral and what follows:** once the royal funeral truce ends and the princes' war opens the map, this is where the first large guild clashes are expected.

## Open

- Placement: the north side of Valefrost's southern lowlands is the working read, not final.
