# Eryndal — World Overview

**World name: Eryndal** (never use "Eldora," a superseded old-draft placeholder).

## Structure

Currently one supercontinent under one main crown, one royal family, with named regions spread across it, real internal scale, not a handful of small, uniformly walkable neighborhoods. The six regions below grew organically as the playerbase grew over HGO's roughly two years live, each a substantial province in its own right. Each playable race has one neutral home/starting zone. You can still be killed or PvP'd inside your home zone, but you do NOT drop items on death there. Everywhere outside the home zones is real open-world PvP, death carries a genuine chance of dropping your items. This is the core risk/reward engine of the open world, and the natural seed of both in-game and real-world criminal activity (steal valuable drops, launder or sell for real money, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`).

**Myrrhwood is the one confirmed exception, ship-locked and genuinely separate, consistent with the old draft, not a Crystal League-only change.** No overland route connects it to the rest of the supercontinent, reaching it takes real ship or ferry time (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`'s no-fast-travel note), which is exactly why Thrash clan's eventual return there is a real logistical undertaking, not a short walk. The other five regions below stay part of the directly reachable landmass.

**Crystal League expansion:** formally splits the supercontinent into five full continents, a real territory-ownership and administrative structure landing on top of geography that's already meaningfully separated in Myrrhwood's case, not the first time these regions become distinct or reachable at all. Players can build, own, and defend cities and continents. This is the "new world" Kazuku has been vaguely hyping since launch, most players assumed it just meant bigger tournaments or items, not literal new continents. See `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md` for the launch date.

## The six current regions

- **Lunarest** — central hub, human-dominated. Where Tatsuya's goblin glitch drops him: Lunarest Outskirts.
- **Valefrost** — north, Frostborn and Dwarves. Reachable overland from Lunarest.
- **Myrrhwood** — west, Beastfolk and Goblin clans. Ship-locked, not reachable overland, see above. Where Tatsuya eventually operates and finds his real advantage (not immediately, he starts in Lunarest Outskirts). See the Myrrhwood advantage note below, and the two-landmass structure and accessibility note directly below that.
- **Sunmarch** — south, Orcs. Reachable overland from Lunarest.
- **Wraith Isles** — east, ranked arena and tournament zone. Reachable only by the arena's own teleport-with-zone-lock system (see `codex/arena/overview.md`), unrelated to Myrrhwood's ship requirement, a separate, deliberate exception to normal travel.
- **Azure Expanse** — north peaks, Elves. Reachable overland from Lunarest.

**Myrrhwood advantage:** most players treat Myrrhwood zones as low-value or neutral territory. They are not. Goblin clan players get above-average advantages there that opponents consistently underestimate. Tatsuya discovers and exploits this later in the story, it becomes a real weapon precisely because nobody respects it. See `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`.

**Myrrhwood's internal structure: two landmasses, one organized, one fractured.** Not a single shared territory, Myrrhwood splits into two distinct landmasses within the region, the same shape as a Dominican Republic/Haiti split. One side holds the Beastfolk kingdom, organized, "has their things," a real seat of governance. The other side is goblin clan territory, and it has no true king, no unifying government, the fractured, warring-clan state already locked in `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md` and the whole reason Sevish's own quest exists (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`). Goblin territory itself is dense swamp and forest, dangerous, genuinely avoided by outsiders, not a place casual travelers pass through.

**Never actually player-accessible, until now.** Myrrhwood-the-region is real, known lore, goblins and Beastfolk both trace their origins there, common knowledge any goblin or Beastfolk NPC would take for granted. But no player has ever actually set foot there in HGO's roughly two years live, it's been closed, unopened content this whole time, not a quiet or rarely-visited zone, a genuinely inaccessible one. Whatever the Beastfolk kingdom has or hasn't done toward goblin territory over that time is goblin oral history and rumor as far as any player knows, never confirmed by an actual expedition, see `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`. This is why Thrash clan's planned return there is real, first-of-its-kind news, not a retread of already-mapped territory, brand new to every player in the game, expert veterans included, the moment it actually opens.
