# Eryndal — World Overview

**World name: Eryndal** (never use "Eldora," a superseded old-draft placeholder).

## Structure

**Season Zero (the closed beta) is played on one continent, Lunarest**, under one king, with three princes and a handful of named regions (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). Every race starts there. Each playable race has one neutral home/starting zone. You can still be killed or PvP'd inside your home zone, but you do NOT drop items on death there. Everywhere outside the home zones is real open-world PvP, death carries a genuine chance of dropping your items. This is the core risk/reward engine of the open world, and the natural seed of both in-game and real-world criminal activity (steal valuable drops, launder or sell for real money, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`).

**Myrrhwood is the one region reachable ahead of the world maps, by ship, and it is genuinely separate.** No overland route connects it to Lunarest, reaching it takes real ship or ferry time (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`'s no-fast-travel note), which is exactly why Thrash clan's eventual return there is a real logistical undertaking, not a short walk. **Locked duration: four to five real/in-game days** for the ocean crossing, a boat costs a lot of shillings.

**Hearthfire expansion** (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`): releases the world maps as **five full continents** (Lunarest, Valefrost, Myrrhwood, Sunmarch, Azure Expanse, with the Wraith Isles as the arena zone), a real territory-ownership and administrative structure. Players can build, own, and defend cities and continents, and **Node Wars** across the five continents are the main war content afterward. Each player gets one free teleport at the Lunarest waypoint to a home kingdom, there is no fast travel, and the continents favor certain races (Kazuku's opening ad says so). This is the "new world" Kazuku has been vaguely hyping since launch, most players assumed it just meant bigger tournaments or items, not literal new continents.

**Racial standing differs by continent.** In Lunarest, elves receive special treatment under an elven law, while Beastfolk, goblins and dwarves carry bad infamy. Other continents differ.

## Thornwatch, the border fortress city (Season Zero finale site)

A walled fortress city on the border, on the outskirts of the snowy plains and outside the Kingdom of Lunarest, with a city hall, small houses and bunkers inside the walls. It is a new zone added at the very end of the closed beta and it leads on to a new city beyond it, a port-city type place. The fortress is named Thornwatch (proposed name, OPEN to swap). The only pass between Lunarest and the fortress runs into it, and the plains have deep caverns that offer an unused flank. It is where the Battle of Thornwatch is fought in-world (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).

## Places by region (forward material)

- **Valefrost (north):** the **Ashenveil Forest and Mountains** (`codex/locations/ashenveil-forest.md`) divide the southern lowlands from the northern holds. **Skaldmere** (`codex/locations/skaldmere.md`) is the waypost town on the approach, **Varekpass** (`codex/locations/varekpass.md`) the garrison pass beyond it, and **Varekhold** (`codex/locations/varekhold.md`) the mountain fortress above a cold coast (which coast is OPEN). The **Barren Lands** (`codex/locations/barren-lands.md`) are the contested zone (placement on Valefrost's southern lowlands is the working read, OPEN) where the first big guild clashes land once the map opens.
- **Myrrhwood (west):** **Khen-Mora** (`codex/locations/khen-mora.md`) is the border march on the Beastfolk side facing goblin territory.
- These are node and hall sites for Node Wars (towns, resource sites, forts), not capitals.

**Overland carriage service (OPEN, optional).** Hired carriages with drivers could run overland routes on a continent for a fee, a slow paid alternative to walking. Not decided; there is still no fast travel.

## The regions

- **Lunarest**: central hub, human-dominated, the Season Zero continent. Where Tatsuya's goblin glitch drops him: Lunarest Outskirts. Seat of the royal court.
- **Valefrost**: north, Frostborn and Dwarves. A separate continent that opens with Hearthfire (boat, or the one free teleport).
- **Myrrhwood**: west, Beastfolk and Goblin clans. Ship-locked, see above. Where Tatsuya eventually operates and finds his real advantage (not immediately, he starts in Lunarest Outskirts). See the Myrrhwood advantage note below, and the two-landmass structure and accessibility note directly below that.
- **Sunmarch**: south, Orcs. A separate continent that opens with Hearthfire (boat, or the one free teleport).
- **Wraith Isles** — east, ranked arena and tournament zone. Reachable only by the arena's own teleport-with-zone-lock system (see `codex/arena/overview.md`), unrelated to Myrrhwood's ship requirement, a separate, deliberate exception to normal travel.
- **Azure Expanse**: north peaks, Elves. A separate continent that opens with Hearthfire (boat, or the one free teleport).

**Myrrhwood advantage:** most players treat Myrrhwood zones as low-value or neutral territory. They are not. Goblin clan players get above-average advantages there that opponents consistently underestimate. Tatsuya discovers and exploits this later in the story, it becomes a real weapon precisely because nobody respects it. See `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`.

**Myrrhwood growth route.** A guild can found a camp and grow it into an outskirts and then a town, but that takes players, resources and time (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`). Big guilds with existing numbers take the contested zones first, so Tatsuya's small guild cannot be first. Goblins, fight-hungry by culture, want to join a more dominant Tatsuya for the power it brings, which is what fuels his early expansion here.

**Myrrhwood's internal structure: two landmasses, one organized, one fractured.** Not a single shared territory, Myrrhwood splits into two distinct landmasses within the region, the same shape as a Dominican Republic/Haiti split. One side holds the Beastfolk kingdom, organized, "has their things," a real seat of governance. The other side is goblin clan territory, and it has no true king, no unifying government, the fractured, warring-clan state already locked in `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md` and the whole reason Sevish's own quest exists (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`). Goblin territory itself is dense swamp and forest, dangerous, genuinely avoided by outsiders, not a place casual travelers pass through.

**Never actually player-accessible, until now (a hidden-quest exception ahead of the world maps).** Myrrhwood-the-region is real, known lore, goblins and Beastfolk both trace their origins there, common knowledge any goblin or Beastfolk NPC would take for granted. But no player has ever actually set foot there in the closed beta's eight or nine months, it's been closed, unopened content this whole time, not a quiet or rarely-visited zone, a genuinely inaccessible one. Whatever the Beastfolk kingdom has or hasn't done toward goblin territory over that time is goblin oral history and rumor as far as any player knows, never confirmed by an actual expedition, see `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`. This is why Thrash clan's planned return there is real, first-of-its-kind news, not a retread of already-mapped territory, brand new to every player in the game, expert veterans included, the moment it actually opens.

## World Quests

Global quest lines any player can attempt, actively hunted by the entire playerbase, not just whoever's nearby, and effectively hoardable by whoever gets there first. See `codex/lore/world-quests.md`.

## Crownfall zone (author-stated)

The Crownfall zone (author-stated): a neutral trade zone on a plateau off the coast of Lunarest, reached by ship. It is a massive magical forest, the last 10 to 15 acres off the back end of the kingdom, full of magical beasts, and the only high-magic ancient forest near Lunarest. It leads to the elves' domain: a domain that teleports you there, and the elves welcome you. Rumor says the court mage Corvith came from there. Normally the zone is open to everyone. During the event the forest is replicated, and the zone is locked to guilds and their participants. The rules will be set closer to the date.

OPEN: the event name (the king's fall) and the zone name are both "Crownfall". Not resolved.
