# Eryndal — World Overview

**World name: Eryndal** (never use "Eldora," a superseded old-draft placeholder).

## Structure

Currently one supercontinent, one kingdom, one royal family, with named regions spread across it. Each playable race has one neutral home/starting zone. You can still be killed or PvP'd inside your home zone, but you do NOT drop items on death there. Everywhere outside the home zones is real open-world PvP, death carries a genuine chance of dropping your items. This is the core risk/reward engine of the open world, and the natural seed of both in-game and real-world criminal activity (steal valuable drops, launder or sell for real money, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`).

**Crystal League expansion:** expands the single supercontinent into five full continents. Players can build, own, and defend cities and continents. This is the "new world" Kazuku has been vaguely hyping since launch, most players assumed it just meant bigger tournaments or items, not literal new continents. See `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md` for the launch date.

## The six current regions

- **Lunarest** — central hub, human-dominated. Where Tatsuya's goblin glitch drops him: Lunarest Outskirts.
- **Valefrost** — north, Frostborn and Dwarves.
- **Myrrhwood** — west, Beastfolk and Goblin clans. Where Tatsuya eventually operates and finds his real advantage (not immediately, he starts in Lunarest Outskirts). See the Myrrhwood advantage note below.
- **Sunmarch** — south, Orcs.
- **Wraith Isles** — east, ranked arena and tournament zone.
- **Azure Expanse** — north peaks, Elves.

**Myrrhwood advantage:** most players treat Myrrhwood zones as low-value or neutral territory. They are not. Goblin clan players get above-average advantages there that opponents consistently underestimate. Tatsuya discovers and exploits this later in the story, it becomes a real weapon precisely because nobody respects it. See `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`.

## World Quests

Global quest lines any player can attempt, actively hunted by the entire playerbase, not just whoever's nearby, and effectively hoardable by whoever gets there first. See `codex/lore/world-quests.md`.
