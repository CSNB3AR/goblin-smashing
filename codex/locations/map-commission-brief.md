## The five continents (Hearthfire expansion) plus the arena
1. **Lunarest** [AUTHOR] central hub, human kingdom, one king (Aldemar Vaereth), 12 provinces, three princes. The only continent open in Season Zero. Named places: the city of Lunarest with the royal palace [AUTHOR]; the Grand Library of Lunarest in the city [PAGE, Chapter 3 objective]; Astralum Archive beneath the palace [AUTHOR, forward]; **Lunarest Outskirts**, where Tatsuya drops in [PAGE]; the **Thrash Clan outpost camp** (temporary, unnamed) inside the outskirts [PAGE]; **Thornwatch**, the broken fortress at the edge of **Thornwatch Forest** in the north [AUTHOR, forward]; the **Crownfall zone**, a plateau forest off the coast reached by ship [AUTHOR, name OPEN]. Provinces: Ilyan about 4, Maldren 4, Dorrin 3, one unassigned [OPEN].
2. **Valefrost** [AUTHOR] north, Frostborn and Dwarves only. Think of the Dwarven kingdom Dwargon from That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime. No other places are named. (Ashenveil Forest and Mountains, Skaldmere, Varekpass, Varekhold and the Barren Lands were leftovers from BKC and are NOT in Valefrost; parked for possible later use.)
3. **Myrrhwood** [AUTHOR] west, plateau continent (Australia crossed with Alaska), ship-locked, four to five days by ship. Two halves: the walled **Beast Kingdom** (Ba Sing Se style walls in desert) and the **Black Lands**, many small goblin camps, no kingdom. **Khen-Mora**, the border march on the Beastfolk side [AUTHOR].
4. **Sunmarch** [AUTHOR] south, Orcs. No town names yet [OPEN].
5. **Azure Expanse** [AUTHOR] north peaks, Elves; the elven domain entered through the wisp gate behind Thornwatch [AUTHOR, forward]. No town names yet [OPEN].
- **Wraith Isles** [AUTHOR] east, ranked arena zone, reachable only by the arena teleport.

## Notes for the artist
- Lunarest is one supercontinent with no other continent in Season Zero; the other four open with Hearthfire (one free teleport, boats, no fast travel).
- Thornwatch Forest sits closer to Dwarven and Frostborn land than to Elven land; a Dwarven kingdom and an enclaved kingdom lie on the far side [AUTHOR, forward].
- The goblin land in Myrrhwood has no capital or city.
