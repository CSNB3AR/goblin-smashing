# Eryndal world map (author's picture, free version)

> **Reference only (author, latest):** this map is for the codex so the names and shapes are identifiable; readers are not going to see it. The names on it are the same as the lore ("Kenmore" is the lore's Khen-Mora, "The Wasted Tribal Lands" is the goblin tribal land (the lore also called it the Black Lands; the author wants "The Goblin Tribal Lands")). Lore names win. The conflicts listed below are cosmetic notes, not open questions. The author allows correcting the labels on the graphic.

Images: `codex/locations/reference/eryndal-world-map.png` (original) and `codex/locations/reference/eryndal-world-map-corrected.png` (labels fixed to the lore: "Kenmore" is now "Khen-Mora", "The Wasted Tribal Lands" is now "The Goblin Tribal Lands"; positions unchanged). Use the corrected copy. Parchment style, compass with north up, a legend (Major City, Fort / Stronghold, Camp, Mountains, Forest, Territory Border). No coordinate grid on it yet; the story says the in-game map shows coordinates, so a grid can be added later.

## What the map shows (labels exactly as drawn)
- **Valefrost** (north): mountains, a large walled gate across the top, then **Thornwatch Fort** and **Thornwatch Forest** on the southern forested half.
- **Myrrhwood** (west, its own island): the walled **Beast Kingdom, "Kenmore"** at the top (a ring wall and a keep icon), a territory border across the middle, and **The Wasted Tribal Lands** below with camp icons (tents).
- **Lunarest** (center): **Royal City of Lunarest** in the middle, **Lunarest Outskirts** to the southwest, dotted territory borders cutting the continent into provinces.
- **Azure Expanse** (east): dense forest and mountains, a glowing stone archway on the east side (reads as the elven gate).
- **Sunmarch** (south): mountains and forest across the whole bottom. Orcs and Lizardmen clans (author, latest).
- Small islands are scattered between them. No arena zone is drawn (consistent with the Wraith Isles being removed).

## Conflicts between the map and the written canon (author to decide)
1. **Thornwatch's continent.** The codex puts Thornwatch Fort and Forest in the north of the Kingdom of Lunarest. The map draws them on Valefrost's landmass, below a dwarven wall. Valefrost is Frostborn and Dwarves only, so as drawn it belongs to the dwarves' continent, not Lunarest.
2. **Azure Expanse's direction.** The codex says north peaks. The map has it east. (The elven gate in the codex is "behind Thornwatch"; the map draws the arch far to the east.)
3. **Kenmore versus Khen-Mora.** The map spells it "Kenmore" and attaches it to the Beast Kingdom. The codex has Khen-Mora as a border march.
4. **The Wasted Tribal Lands versus the Black Lands.** Same region, two names.
5. **Provinces.** Lunarest is drawn with border lines; the codex says 12 provinces. The count on the map is not confirmed.
6. **Lunarest Outskirts** matches (southwest of the royal city), where the Thrash Clan outpost camp is.

Use the map as the visual reference. Do not change names or positions in the story until the author rules on the conflicts.
