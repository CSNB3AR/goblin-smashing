# The Ashenveil Forest and Mountains

**Type:** In-game location, Eryndal. A vast ancient forest and mountain belt across south-central Valefrost. Forward material.

## Facts

- A natural barrier that divides Valefrost's southern lowlands from its northern holds, with one major route through it and a long, dangerous alternative.
- **Wilderness zone, Class II to III threat band.** Home to the Greyvast, which locks onto casters over melee attackers, and other large beasts that drop monocrystals (`codex/creatures/`, `codex/materials/monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md`). Missionaries and caravans stop traveling past a certain depth.
- Open-world PvP applies, so ambush parties and PK groups use it too. Its zone karma class (red or yellow, see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) is OPEN; red would mean full loot on death.
- **Node Wars use:** resource site, not a city. Control of the safe route decides what reaches Skaldmere and Varekpass.

## Sensory palette

Pine resin, wet earth, a faint sweet electric scent, dense silver-barked canopy, low resonant calls from deep within, mist between roots, a sound-absorbing forest floor.
