# Staff of the Mist God (working name)

**Type:** Object, in-game, forward plot. One of the **seven world weapons** blessed by patrons, held by the goddess **Seranis** (see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`, owned by another agent). A piece of Shiv's set (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`). The final enchanted name is open, "something more mystical than the mist god."

## Facts

- Buffs and minuses. Not detailed yet.
- **Resurrection:** raises a fallen player once every **seven days** (the dictation also said once a day and every 24 hours, seven days used). It matters because a dead player sits out a 72-hour death lock.
- Lets Tatsuya **stack enchantments faster at lower MP cost**, and is a strong magic tool.
- **Three forms**, chosen by the user: sword (the dictation also said great axe), staff, and a two-handed **energy rifle / gadget gun-blade**. Tatsuya picks the gun-blade, since it lets him shoot and move while casting through the staff. Reference: the King's Avatar gun class, the Albion energy cannon look.
- Distinct from the **Shivbone Staff** (Legendary drop given to Arya in chapter 10).
