# Staff of the Mist God (working name)

**Type:** Object, in-game, forward plot. One of the **seven world weapons** blessed by patrons, Seranis's weapon (she is the goddess of demigods and creation, and of life and resurrection, see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`). The **one item of Shiv's Warset that is a relic** (the Warset is unique items, not seven pieces, see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`). Seranis holds exactly one weapon, this one. The final enchanted name is open, "something more mystical than the mist god."

## Facts

- Buffs and minuses. Not detailed yet.
- **Resurrection:** the staff carries Seranis's domain of life and resurrection, and raises a fallen player once every **seven days** (once a day and every 24 hours were also proposed; seven days is used and the cadence is OPEN). It matters because a dead player sits out a 72-hour death lock.
- Lets Tatsuya **stack enchantments faster at lower MP cost**, and is a strong magic tool.
- **Three forms**, chosen by the wielder: sword (great axe was also proposed, OPEN), staff, and a two-handed **energy rifle / gadget gun-blade**. Tatsuya picks the gun-blade, since it lets him shoot and move while casting through the staff.
- Distinct from the **Shivbone Staff** (Legendary drop given to Arya in chapter 10).

## Cross-links

- The Call of the Bones necklace (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is another unique Warset item but not a relic. The Warset and the seven world weapons both exist; this staff is the one item that is in both.
