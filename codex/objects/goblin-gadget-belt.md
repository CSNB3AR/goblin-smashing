# Goblin Gadget Belt

**Type:** Object, in-game. Common-rarity item, Tatsuya's first real deck-building piece.

## Facts

Dropped in Chapter 3 off the two-goblin kill in Bloodhurst, via the goblin racial passive Long Shot (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`), a Fortune-gated bonus-item roll that happened to proc on the exact kill that also triggered "Call of the Bones" activating for him. Common rarity, not flashy, a satchel belt loaded with smoke bombs and gas bombs.

**What it actually changes:** Goblin Gas (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) has always technically been usable without any gear at all, an ugly, comedic, bodily-function execution he'd rather not lean on. The gadget belt gives him an actual tool-based way to deploy it, thrown gas and smoke bombs instead, letting the ability read as a real combat tool rather than a running gag. He thinks of it, and starts thinking of himself a little, as something closer to a brigand than a caster or a brawler, gadgets and misdirection over raw power.
