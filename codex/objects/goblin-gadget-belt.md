# Goblin Gadget Belt

**Type:** Object, in-game. Common-rarity item, Tatsuya's first real deck-building piece.

## Facts

Dropped in Chapter 3 off the two-goblin kill in Bloodhurst, via Gambler's Gambit (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`), Tatsuya's rare-variant roll ability. Common rarity, not flashy, a satchel belt with slots for smoke bombs and gas bombs.

**Locked/shaded on pickup, not immediately usable.** The belt's abilities show up grayed out in the menu, he needs to gather crafting materials before any of them actually work, not an unlimited free toolkit the moment he equips it. Once unlocked, it gives him an actual tool-based way to deploy Goblin Gas, thrown bombs instead of the bodily-function version (see `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`), letting the ability read as a real combat tool rather than a running gag. He thinks of it, and starts thinking of himself a little, as something closer to a brigand than a caster or a brawler, gadgets and misdirection over raw power.
