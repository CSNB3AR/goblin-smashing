# HGO Access Drive

**Type:** Object, real-world/in-game hybrid. The physical hardware key that carries a player's HGO character.

## Account / character / reset mechanics

- One character per hard drive. Once a character is built on a drive, that's the character.
- Resets ARE possible for most players, but the fee is massive, payable either in a large sum of in-game currency earned through play, or an equivalent real-world payment. Most players who want one simply can't afford it.
- **Tatsuya's situation is unique:** his goblin character was created on a first-gen drive running unsupported software, reached only through his own override, no reset option was ever offered for that drive, not expensive, not available at all. He is permanently locked. This is the central mechanical hook of the entire story. **This is a separate system from the rare-variant roll every character gets at creation** (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), that roll still ran for him same as anyone, and it hit rare. Not being able to reset the character afterward and getting a rare variant at the moment of creation are unrelated systems, one about undoing a character later, one about a bonus at the exact moment it's made.
- The green drive clipped to his belt is his physical HGO account. It can only be properly read and run on first-gen hardware, meaning the two pods at Sakura Arcade are the only place on Earth he can currently play his own character.

## Drive color

An HGO access drive is a neutral silver/clear color before a character is created on it. Once a race is chosen (or, in Tatsuya's case, locked in by the power-blip accident, see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), the physical drive changes color to match, a standard goblin roll comes out a dull, ordinary green, the color most players and NPCs recognize a goblin by at a glance. Tatsuya's drive skips straight past that ordinary green: because his rare-variant roll hit (Gambler's Gambit, see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), his drive reads a distinct **emerald green** from the moment of creation, visibly different from an ordinary goblin's drive even before the Call of the Bones Necklace bonds him to Shiv later and deepens that same bond further.
