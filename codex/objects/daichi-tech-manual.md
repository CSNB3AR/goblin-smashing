# Daichi's Tech Manual

**Type:** Object, real-world. A physical manual Daichi Kazehaya wrote/used for his hardware work.

## Facts

Covers maintenance and repair across Daichi's own hardware lineage, everyday tech like the Haya EB (see `codex/objects/haya-eb.md`) through to first-gen pod hardware (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`). Tatsuya has kept it and still references it to keep the Haya EB running himself, no technician, just the manual and his own trained hands.

Chapter 2: he grabs it and packs it before heading out to search for pod access, not entirely sure why yet, just a hunch. It turns out to be exactly what lets him understand and override the first-gen pods at Sakura Arcade, the concrete link between his father's legacy and the goblin glitch. See `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md` for the override/glitch mechanism this enables.

## The letter (Chapter 1)

Folded inside the manual's controller section is Daichi's letter to Tatsuya, unsigned, marked with a bear paw print (text and meaning in `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`). Tatsuya only finds it because the Haya EB fails on the ride home and he pulls the manual to repair the controller, which is also why he reaches for the manual at all. The note is why he buys into HGO. In Chapter 2 it is still folded in the manual.
