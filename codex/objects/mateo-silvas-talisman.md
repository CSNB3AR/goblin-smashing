# Mateo Silva's Talisman

**Type:** World-tier off-hand item, background texture for London Spire's roster (see `codex/pro-scene/pro-teams.md`). Name a placeholder, easy to rename.

## Facts

An off-hand channeling focus, not a weapon, so it sits alongside whatever Mateo Silva's actually holding rather than competing with it. Grants a flat damage increase and reduces recast/cooldown time on all spells and abilities by a consistent degree, a stat combination that reads as strong regardless of build, ranged, melee, or caster, since it isn't tied to a specific damage type the way most gear is. Part of why it reportedly holds up so well in London Spire's disciplined, patient drafts: consistent, reliable output rather than a single flashy proc.

Like every World-tier item, ranked competitive play applies a real scaling reduction to it (see `codex/lore/world-quests.md`), it isn't running at its raw open-world numbers on the broadcast stage. Even nerfed, it's still genuinely strong, part of the reasoning voiced in-story for why London Spire's mechanical star performs as consistently as he does, real gear doing real work on top of real skill, not a substitute for it.
