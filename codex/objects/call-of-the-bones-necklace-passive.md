# Call of the Bones Necklace: The Passive and the Reason

No dashes of any kind. Tags: [CANON] stated in the repo, [PROPOSAL] mine, for the author to accept.

## The passive: Bone Surge, with a Tally

The necklace's own passive is **Bone Surge** [CANON, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`]: Amplify (a known ability gets bigger, Fortune decides how much) and Reflect (a desperate turn-back that crashes his Energy to 1). Both stay exactly as written. What is missing is a rule that makes it fit an enchanter, so I propose one.

**The Bone Tally [PROPOSAL].** Every debuff or enchantment he lands (Slowing Thread, Ward of Bones, a Tether Rune triggering) adds one bone to a tally of up to 5, shown as pips on the necklace. Amplify spends the tally. Zero bones means a weak Amplify (plus 10 percent), five bones a spike (plus 50 percent and, for an enchantment, it also lands on one more ally). That is why the necklace rewards control first and fire later: his damage grows out of his debuffs.

- **Name and tier:** Bone Surge, passive, Tier 3 effect at level 8, rising one tier per 15 levels to Tier 7 at 60 (World items scale with the wearer).
- **Limiters [PROPOSAL]:** one Amplify per 30 s; the tally decays one bone per 10 s out of combat; Amplify rolls Fortune on a d6 and fizzles on a 1 (a half boost, the tally kept); Reflect costs all Energy (canon) and cannot trigger below 20 percent HP, since he is then too weak to hold the turn.
- **Event cap:** halved in organized events (house rule 7 of the tree file).

## Why it is the world quest's reward [PROPOSAL]

Call of the Bones is a call to the goblin dead and to the living clan. Goblins are barred from the capital and shut out of magic; the quest is the world admitting that was a lie told to them. So the reward is not a sword. It is a vessel that makes a goblin a conduit: the dead lend weight to what the living do. Bone Surge says it mechanically. It cannot do anything alone, it only makes his own moves, and his allies', bigger. A reward that works only through other people suits a quest whose real objective is the clan, and it explains why Shiv gave it to a prisoner with no clan yet.

## What it does at each stage of the quest [PROPOSAL]

The sequence has five stages; Stage 2 is the Chapter Nine bond, later stages are plan.

| Stage | Story point | Necklace state |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Offer | Shiv asks in the tent | Dormant, shown only as a mark |
| 2. Bond (Ch9) | Accept, Shiv Goblin | Bone Surge live, tally at 0 to 2 pips, Wisdom bypass, +5 Wisdom |
| 3. The Camp (Ch10 to 30) | Building the clan | Tally 3 pips. Marked Kin reads on the necklace; clan members near him add a pip when he lands a debuff |
| 4. The Lead | Shiv's equipment in the heart of Lunarest | Tally 4 pips; Reflect no longer drops Energy to 1, only to 1 for ten seconds, repaid by the clan |
| 5. Completion | The Astralum Archive | Full 5 pips and the necklace links to the second Warset piece. Quest closes, the passive stays |

## Tie to the tree
It feeds Flamonta: with a full tally, a Flamonta Form III lands on a second ally as a shield [PROPOSAL]. Unequipping the necklace (canon removability stake) wipes the tally and closes the Veil path.
