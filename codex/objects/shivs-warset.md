# Shiv's Warset

**Type:** Object set, in-game. A 7-piece legendary item set tied to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest arc.

## Facts

Preserved from the old draft as a strong deck-building-flavored long-term collection goal. Piece 1 of 7 is the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), granted by Shiv (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`). The remaining 6 pieces are not yet designed, forward material for later chapters as the World Quest arc progresses.
