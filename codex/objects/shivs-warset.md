# Shiv's Warset

**Type:** Object set, in-game. A 7-piece legendary item set tied to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest arc.

## Facts

Preserved from the old draft as a strong deck-building-flavored long-term collection goal. Piece 1 of 7 is the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), granted by Shiv (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`). The remaining 6 pieces are scattered across the world, not concentrated in Myrrhwood, three are somewhere in Myrrhwood itself, the rest spread across the other continents (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), a genuine long-term, multi-region collection arc rather than a single-region hunt. Individual piece designs are not yet worked out, forward material for later chapters as the World Quest arc progresses.

**Whispers of Shiv is the questline mechanic tied to this hunt.** The passive itself (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`) governs companion recruitment, but the quest chain of the same name advances specifically as Tatsuya gets closer to or finds hints toward each of the remaining 6 pieces, one arc per piece, not a single payoff. The Call of the Bones World Quest itself doesn't resolve until all 7 pieces are found, a genuinely long-running storyline with no near-term wrap, confirmed Chapter 6.

**Recognition and future clan-chief-challenge concept (flagged, not built yet).** Any goblin, NPC or player, can identify a piece of the Warset on sight, the same instinctive recognition that already applies to the necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). Other goblin *players*, not NPCs, could theoretically find and wear their own Warset piece, which opens a future possibility: goblin clan chiefs are player-only positions, and two players each holding a piece could be made to formally challenge each other for it. **Explicitly deferred**, not being developed now, flagged here only so it isn't silently reinvented later.
