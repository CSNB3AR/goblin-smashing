# Shiv's Warset

**Type:** Object set, in-game. A 7-piece legendary item set tied to the "Call of the Bones" World Quest arc.

## Facts

Preserved from the old draft as a strong deck-building-flavored long-term collection goal. Piece 1 of 7 is the Call of the Bones Necklace (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), granted by Shiv (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`). The remaining 6 pieces are scattered across the world, not concentrated in Myrrhwood, at least two are somewhere in Myrrhwood itself, the rest spread across the other continents (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), a genuine long-term, multi-region collection arc rather than a single-region hunt. Individual piece designs are not yet worked out, forward material for later chapters as the World Quest arc progresses.
