# Amulet of Seranis

**Type:** Object, in-game, forward plot. A divine relic tied to the goddess **Seranis**, goddess of demigods and creation (and of life and resurrection), see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`. Not yet on the page in HGO.

## Facts

- **A divine relic.** A relic blessed by a patron, in the family of the world weapons (`codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`). Where it sits among the five patron relics and the seven world weapons is open.
- **Worn at the sternum**, on a chain or mount.
- **Warms during magic use** and **pulses like a second heartbeat** while the bearer works.
- **Amplifies conjuring output.** It boosts projected, outward magic, not only inward body reinforcement.
- **One bearer.** It bonds to a single bearer. Anyone who puts it on without the goddess's blessing **dies on contact**, which is why it is a credential: a bearer who survives it has been chosen.
- **Divine credential.** Allies and factions treat the bearer as touched by the goddess even when the bearer is odd, hated or unexplained. The Church of Seranis can recognize or refuse that claim.
- Written on the page, always "the relic" or "the amulet", never the full formal name in dialogue or narration.

## Overlap with the necklace

It closely echoes the Call of the Bones necklace (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`): worn at the sternum, warm against the chest when power is used, one chosen bearer. Two options are on the table in the patrons doc and neither is chosen: **Option A**, the necklace is Seranis's relic and the amulet is its older name or truth; **Option B**, the amulet is a separate sister relic and Seranis then holds two world items (the amulet and the staff).
