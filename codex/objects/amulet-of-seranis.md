# Amulet of Seranis (retired)

Redirect stub. Seranis holds **exactly one** world weapon, the Staff of the Mist God (`codex/objects/staff-of-the-mist-god.md`), the one relic in Shiv's Warset. A separate Amulet of Seranis would give her a second relic, so it is not used in this world. The Call of the Bones necklace stays a unique Warset item, not a relic (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). See `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md` and `outline/bkc-reuse-map.md`.
