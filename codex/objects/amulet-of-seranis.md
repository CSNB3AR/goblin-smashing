# Amulet of Seranis

**Type:** Object, in-game, forward plot. A divine relic tied to the goddess **Seranis**, Goddess of Life and Resurrection (see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`). Adapted from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles (BKC-Files), where it is also called the Relic of Thalos in older records. Not yet on the page in HGO.

## Facts

- **A divine relic.** One of the five relics in the source's "Five Weapons of Theos" idea, each tied to a patron deity. How it fits HGO's five patrons and seven world weapons is open.
- **Worn at the sternum**, on a chain or mount.
- **Warms during magic use** and **pulses like a second heartbeat** while the bearer works.
- **Amplifies conjuring output.** It boosts projected, outward magic, not only inward body reinforcement.
- **One bearer.** It bonds to a single bearer. Anyone who puts it on without the goddess's blessing **dies on contact**, which is why it is a credential: a bearer who survives it has been chosen.
- **Divine credential.** Allies and factions treat the bearer as touched by the goddess even when the bearer is odd, hated or unexplained. The Church of Seranis can recognize or refuse that claim.
- Written on the page, always "the relic" or "the amulet", never the full formal name in dialogue or narration.

## Overlap with the necklace

It closely echoes the Call of the Bones necklace (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`): worn at the sternum, warm against the chest when power is used, one chosen bearer. Same item, sister relic, or separate is open.

## Not imported

The source's berserk trigger and holy blue-white flame at full berserk are not imported. Add only if the author wants them.
