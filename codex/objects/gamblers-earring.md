# Gambler's Earring

**Type:** Object, in-game. Rare-rarity item, the physical manifestation of Tatsuya's Gambler's Gambit racial ability.

## Facts

Small bronze earring, dice carved into its curve. Granted the moment his rare-variant creation roll resolves (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), but only equips once he's actually in the game world, right after he drops into Bloodhurst in Chapter 3, not at the character-creation screen itself.

Its card lists exactly what it grants, consistent with the rest of the deck-building presentation (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`): **+3 Fortune**, and **Lucky Break**, a once-per-day-after-combat roll for a chance at Rare-or-higher loot, Fortune-scaled. This is Gambler's Gambit's loot-generation half made physical, his real income engine in the early chapters, not something abstract living only in a menu.

Unrelated to the Call of the Bones Necklace's own ability, Bone Surge (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), the two only share a Fortune/gambling theme.

**Death-penalty exemption.** Like the necklace, the earring is exempt from the standard on-death item-drop roll (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`, Player death penalty), a race-roll-origin item, not just any bound item. Everything else Tatsuya carries or wears stays at normal drop risk on death.

## Scaling (author-stated, hidden from Tatsuya for now)

The earring is a **Unique**, and Unique items scale with their user, unlike lower rarities, which cap at a level (even Legendary stays capped around level 80+). That is why the beta's legacy Uniques go for millions. Tatsuya doesn't know the dice scale with him yet; he should find out later. ## Level-up path and the damage amplifier (author-stated, latest)

Unique items level with their user; the earring's dice has its own level. Around **character level 10** (the author said "maybe level 10, or throughout the story") the dice reaches **level 5** and the earring gains a **second ability**, so it has two different abilities, not just the luck roll.

- **Damage amplifier (name TBD).** A passive buff driven by a d6 roll. **A roll of 1 is an automatic failure and always carries a punishment clause** (OPEN: define the punishment). A roll of **2 to 6** gives an enhancement level on his **next ability, or his party's ability, within 15 seconds**. It can work like a field spell, which suits goblins and the troop system (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`): a way to enhance the gambler's ability for a whole group.
- **Design rule: every gamble has a risk.** If there is no chance of a failure there is no point, so there is always a cutoff.
- OPEN: whether the roll number is the enhancement level; what the punishment on a 1 is; the ability's name; exact trigger level; whether it affects only his own next ability or the party's by default.
- Tatsuya does not know the dice scales or that a second ability exists; he finds out when it levels.

## Sellable (author-stated, latest)

The earring is NOT bound or unsellable: he got it from a roll, so he can sell it. The app shows it as `UNIQUE (EQUIPPED)` with no "cannot be sold" tag. (It still has the death-penalty drop exemption noted above, and he is not going to sell it.)

- **Crit-fail punishment, proposed from the author (latest):** when Gambler's Gambit rolls a critical fail, the item he just received breaks. This answers the OPEN punishment question above.
