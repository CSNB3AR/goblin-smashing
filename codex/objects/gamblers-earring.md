# Gambler's Earring

**Type:** Object, in-game. Rare-rarity item, the physical manifestation of Tatsuya's Gambler's Gambit racial ability.

## Facts

Small bronze earring, dice carved into its curve. Granted the moment his rare-variant creation roll resolves (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), but only equips once he's actually in the game world, right after he drops into Bloodhurst in Chapter 3, not at the character-creation screen itself.

Its card lists exactly what it grants, consistent with the rest of the deck-building presentation (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`): **+3 Fortune**, and **Lucky Break**, a once-per-day-after-combat roll for a chance at Rare-or-higher loot, Fortune-scaled. This is Gambler's Gambit's loot-generation half made physical, his real income engine in the early chapters, not something abstract living only in a menu.

Unrelated to the Call of the Bones Necklace's own ability, The Bones Speak (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), the two only share a Fortune/gambling theme.

**Death-penalty exemption.** Like the necklace, the earring is exempt from the standard on-death item-drop roll (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`, Player death penalty), a race-roll-origin item, not just any bound item. Everything else Tatsuya carries or wears stays at normal drop risk on death.
