# Dwarf

**Home zone:** Valefrost (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), north, shared with Frostborn.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 7 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 7 |

Reads sturdy, Courage/Power-leaning.

## Racial ability: Iron Constitution

Reduced Energy loss from sustained (non-burst) damage. Fits the race's Courage/Power-leaning sturdiness.

## Resistances

| Notable resistance | Notable weakness |
|---|---|
| Earth | Storm |

## Magic access

**Fully locked out of magic.** Dwarves cannot open any school at any Wisdom investment, one of two races with a hard lock (the other is Beastfolk, see `codex/races/beastfolk.md`). In exchange, Dwarves carry a strong crafting-flavored racial edge (materials handling, refinement yield, working with the crafting/alchemy system, see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`), the race's actual specialization, not a consolation for the magic lockout. Physical Arts (see `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`) covers the race's combat side.
