# Beastfolk

**Home zone:** Myrrhwood (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), west, shared with Goblin clans.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 8 | 3 | 8 | 4 | 6 |

Leans Power/Speed with a real Wisdom penalty.

## Racial ability: Predator's Instinct

Bonus initiative and a passive read on the nearest real threat in a room. Matches the race's Speed/Power lean.

## Resistances

| Notable resistance | Notable weakness |
|---|---|
| Frost | Fire |

## Magic access

**Fully locked out of magic.** Beastfolk cannot open any school at any Wisdom investment, one of two races with a hard lock (the other is Dwarf, see `codex/races/dwarf.md`). Physical Arts (see `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`) is the race's real, full combat identity, not a fallback.
