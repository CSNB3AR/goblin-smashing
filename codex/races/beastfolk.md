# Beastfolk

**Home zone:** Myrrhwood (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), west, shared with Goblin clans.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 8 | 3 | 8 | 4 | 6 |

Leans Power/Speed with a real Wisdom penalty.

## Racial ability: Predator's Instinct

Bonus initiative and a passive read on the nearest real threat in a room. Matches the race's Speed/Power lean.

## Racial ability: Enrage

A second Beastfolk racial. Below 40% HP, a damage multiplier stacks up to **x12** while the Beastfolk loses HP and defenses in exchange. It rewards a tank who can survive on the edge, which is why wards, heals and enchantments matter so much to a Beastfolk brawler such as Pac_Pac (see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`). Beastfolk carry bad infamy in Lunarest (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`).

## Resistances

| Frost | Fire |

## Magic access

**Fully locked out of magic.** Beastfolk cannot open any school at any Wisdom investment, one of two races with a hard lock (the other is Dwarf, see `codex/races/dwarf.md`). Physical Arts (see `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`) is the race's real, full combat identity, not a fallback.

## Myrrhwood kingdom (new, Act Three forward-plot)

Unlike the goblin clans sharing Myrrhwood's landmass (fractured, warring, no true king, see `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`), the Beastfolk side is an organized kingdom with a real royal family and a genuine seat of governance. Not detailed further here, forward material for `outline/act-three-myrrhwood-expedition.md`, where one of the kingdom's princesses becomes central to Pac_Pac's own arc (see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`).
