# Goblins, Hobgoblins and Bugbear Forms: How the Goblin Race Lives

This replaces the earlier "separate peoples" draft. In HGO there are no three goblinoid nations. There is one playable race, **Goblin**, and its **evolutions**. D&D's goblin, hobgoblin and bugbear are only the model for what each stage feels like. For the table of every race's evolutions see `codex/races/race-evolution-baseline.md`.

Tags: **[CANON]** from the codex or chapters, **[PROPOSAL]** invented here and not for the page until the author accepts it.

## 1. What canon fixes

- Goblin is the weakest base race: stats 4/4/2/7/9/4, no resistances, magic restricted by the Wisdom 18 gate. Patron by affinity: Seranis. [CANON `codex/races/goblin.md`]
- **Hobgoblin is an evolution, not a birth caste.** A goblin becomes one by leveling through combat without dying. Sevish never sends recruits out and the spawn zone has no high level monsters, so almost nobody evolves, and Sevish and Wyx do not know the mechanics. [CANON `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`]
- **Hobgoblins on the page:** Sevish (L80), Wyx (L25), Zinka (L7), Ru (L5 after Shiv's blessing). Goblin Gas becomes Toxic Breath on evolving. [CANON]
- **Tatsuya is a Shiv Goblin,** a separate branch, reached by the Call of the Bones Necklace. [CANON `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`]
- **Feral stigma:** only Thrash goblins may enter villages and settlements. Other clans read as feral. Myrrhwood's goblin side is the Black Lands, many small camps, no kingdom. [CANON `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`]
- Thrash law: "we do not fight each other." Wyx: "there are ranks in this clan. You earn them." [CANON Chapter 6]

## 2. The evolutions as the clan sees them

| Stage | How a goblin reaches it | What the clan sees | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Runt Goblin** | Spawns off the Thrash ship at level 1 | Stuttering, short, a bowl and rags. Ru before Shiv. | CANON |
| **Goblin** | Levels and survives | Rank and file: club, mismatched shield, three to seven word speech. | CANON |
| **Hobgoblin** | Level threshold without dying, or a direct blessing | Human height, markings, formal speech, no contractions. A leader by default. | CANON |
| **Shiv Goblin** | Necklace bond, a world quest | Taller, pale lock, red eyes. Old goblins know the mark on sight. | CANON |
| **Bugbear form** | A Hobgoblin who takes the brute road | Big frame, long arms, few words. Common in feral clans. | PROPOSAL |

The D&D picture gives the feel: goblins are scrappy and numerous, hobgoblins are drilled soldiers and officers, bugbears are big ambush hunters. In Eryndal all of these are the same people at different points in a life. A goblin does not become another people, only a stronger version of itself.

**Why feral clans make more bugbear forms.** [PROPOSAL] A feral clan throws recruits into constant skirmish, so more of them survive long enough to evolve. They evolve rough because nobody drills them. Thrash hobgoblins are formal because Sevish and Wyx run a camp with law.

## 3. Ranks inside the clan (PROPOSAL, informal, never on the page unless accepted)

Wyx says ranks are earned, while the ledger says there are no formal ranks yet. Both hold if ranks are informal and tied to gear. Promotion is by witnessed deed, never time served. Evolution is the one jump the clan does not control.

| Rung | Earned by | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **Landed** | Stepping off the ship; named by the clan lead | Rags, rope belt, one wooden bowl |
| **Stakehand** | Holding a night on the stakes without running | A club and a shield he chooses himself |
| **Blade** | Trusted with a real edge past the stakes | A blade, a belt knot per rally survived |
| **Post** | A skill Wyx confirms: scout, smith, cook, bone-setter | A bone bead per job, a blue thread for Thrash |
| **Hobgoblin** | Evolving | Height, markings, one or two clan techniques |
| **Co-leaders** | Being Sevish and Wyx | The axe, the sabres, the war tent |

**Elder** is a status, not a rank: white hair and the necklace, read as Shiv marked. [CANON ledger]

## 4. Speech [CANON `style-guide/race-dialect-and-voice.md`]

| Tier | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Runt | Short broken words, a stutter | "M-man, you don't have to d-do this." |
| Low Wisdom goblins | Own name as subject, bare verbs, one fact per line | "Kesh bring news." |
| Clan recruits | Full sentences, blunt | "Where's my food, runt?" |
| Hobgoblins | Full sentences, no contractions, formal and dry | "There are four, chief." |
| Shiv | Measured, a little archaic | "Make yours greater than mine." |

Speech rises with Wisdom, and with evolution. Ru moved toward first person after his blessing. A reader learns who has evolved by how a goblin talks. [PROPOSAL for the rule, CANON for the examples]

Rules for third person lines: three to seven words, no subordinate clauses, no more than two in a scene, each with its own body cue. Enforcers in Chapter 4 use contractions, flagged as a later pass.

## 5. Religion

Shiv is not a god. He is a human noble, Shivaris Jutsuin, remade into a goblin by the court, now an ash and smoke spirit whose reach is fading. He got the clans to stop fighting each other with a philosophy and a table, not by conquest. Seranis, goddess of creation and odd bearers, is the race's patron. [CANON]

How it is lived [PROPOSAL]:
1. **Remembered, not worshipped.** Elders say "Shiv said" the way a veteran quotes a grandfather.
2. **Keepers, not priests.** Wyx carries Shiv's notebook. Fireside telling is the liturgy.
3. **Bone totems** on the war tent poles, beast bone only, never people.
4. **Names at the fire.** When a goblin does not get up, his name is said at the pot.
5. **The bowl is the clan.** A stolen bowl is a small sacrilege.
6. **Sayings:** "Teeth at the door, not at each other." "Walls first." "Say the name."
7. **Spirits.** Low Wisdom goblins believe small spirits speak to people ("Spirits do that").
8. **Seranis** has no rites yet. Goblins may swear by "the one who picks the odd ones." [OPEN]

## 6. Food and daily life

One stew pot in camp center, tended by a cook who tastes and adds. Rotten stew in wooden bowls, meat strips, a water barrel. No fields. [CANON Chapter 4 and 6]

- **Staples:** stew of gristle and root, smoked strips, ash-cakes from ship flour, hardtack, stream fish, forest mushrooms. [PROPOSAL]
- **Bowl rule:** every goblin owns one bowl, worn on the belt. Losing it is shame. [PROPOSAL]
- **Pot order:** biggest first, the cook ladles the last to whoever remains. Never blood inside the fence. [PROPOSAL]
- **Never on the page:** farming, bread ovens, feasts. [PROPOSAL]

## 7. How goblins see adventurers

NPCs call players "adventurers." Goblins read them as short lived, loud and rich, and often hostile until someone brings word of Shiv, who then counts as an ally. Players who roll Goblin default into Thrash so they can enter towns. [CANON for the term and Thrash default, PROPOSAL for the attitude]
