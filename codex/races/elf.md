# Elf

**Home zone:** Azure Expanse (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), north peaks.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 3 | 9 | 6 | 5 | 5 |

Leans hard into Wisdom, the highest magic-affinity stat in the game at any race's base line. The natural draw for Tatsuya's original build plan before the goblin glitch (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## Racial ability: Mana Sight

Can visually perceive active magic effects and residual mana traces others can't, a real informational edge in any fight involving casters.

## Resistances

| Notable resistance | Notable weakness |
|---|---|
| Storm | Earth |

## Magic access

Full, unrestricted magic access, no Wisdom gate beyond the standard tier-1 threshold (see `codex/magic/overview.md`). The default "obvious mage" race, the one most players build a caster around by default.

## High Elf and Lunarest's elven law

**High Elf** is a rare Elf variant. In Lunarest, Elves receive special treatment under an **elven law**, while Beastfolk, goblins and dwarves carry bad infamy (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). **High Magister Corvith Ostrane** is a High Elf banished from his own domain in the Azure Expanse and made a royal court mage in Lunarest. Spellforger-leaning elves such as Arya benefit from the standing, though Arya is not a High Elf.
