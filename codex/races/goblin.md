# Goblin

**Home zone:** Myrrhwood (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), west, shared with Beastfolk.

Tatsuya's race, the result of the accidental goblin-glitch selection (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), not a considered pick.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 4 | 2 | 7 | 9 | 4 |

Weakest overall base line in the game, why the race sits dull-red on the selection wheel and most players reroll off it early. The already-established Wisdom penalty, the lowest in the game, offset by the signature high Fortune, also the highest in the game at any race's base line, essentially nobody else builds around it the way a goblin can.

## Racial ability: Scavenger's Eye

Passively identifies which nearby loot is actually worth picking up before it's touched. The race's baseline economic edge, distinct from Tatsuya's own personal rare-variant roll below, every goblin gets this, not just him.

## Resistances

None, deliberately flat/unspecialized across the board, consistent with the race's low-floor, no-hard-walls identity.

## Magic access

Restricted, not hard-locked. Goblins default to Physical Arts (see `codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`) and the race's Wisdom of 2 sits nowhere near the tier-1 casting threshold (Wisdom 8, see `codex/magic/overview.md`) through normal leveling alone. **Goblin isn't locked out of any base class the way this restriction might imply** — no class ceiling stops a goblin player from building toward anything on the class list (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`), it's the weak starting stat line that holds most goblin builds back, a hard climb from a low floor, not a hard wall. This is what Tatsuya works out for himself during Chapter 5's morning research.

**Tatsuya's own exception:** the Call of the Bones Necklace negates the Wisdom restriction outright and unlocks the Shaman's Veil path (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), which is exactly what makes his eventual spellcasting a real, remarked-upon anomaly in-world, a goblin who can genuinely cast is not supposed to happen. This isn't a baseline racial trait, it's specific to his bond with Shiv.

## Tatsuya's personal rare-variant roll: Gambler's Gambit

Not a generic racial trait, a rare-variant roll result specific to Tatsuya (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`), a Fortune bonus and a once-per-day chance after combat to roll for Rare-or-higher loot. Manifests physically as the Gambler's Earring (see `codex/objects/gamblers-earring.md`). Governs loot only, unrelated to the World Quest activation itself (see `codex/lore/world-quests.md`) and unrelated to the necklace's own combat ability, The Bones Speak, beyond a shared Fortune/gambling theme.

## Shiv Goblin (advanced evolution, not a creation-time option)

See `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md` and `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`. Reached only by bonding with the Call of the Bones Necklace during the "Call of the Bones" World Quest, evolves the race entry itself, Goblin to Shiv Goblin, not a cosmetic-only change.
