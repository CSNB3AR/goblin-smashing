# Frostborn

**Home zone:** Valefrost (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), north, shared with Dwarf.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 7 |

Reads sturdy, Courage/Power-leaning, close to Dwarf's profile but with a slightly higher Wisdom floor.

## Racial ability: Cold Endurance

Full immunity to environmental cold/exposure effects. A survival-flavored racial rather than a combat one.

## Resistances

| Notable resistance | Notable weakness |
|---|---|
| Frost | Fire |

## Magic access

Partial access, more elemental/rune-utility flavored than pure arcane, sits between the fully-open races (Human, Elf) and the fully-locked ones (Beastfolk, Dwarf). Clearing the tier-1 casting threshold (Wisdom 8, see `codex/magic/overview.md`) is a real but achievable investment, not the uphill climb it is for a Wisdom-2 or Wisdom-3 race.
