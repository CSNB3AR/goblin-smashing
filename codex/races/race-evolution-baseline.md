# Race Evolution Baseline (author-stated, latest)

Only TWO playable races can evolve: **Goblin** and **Beastfolk**. Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Drakekin and Frostborn have no evolution; they grow through levels, classes, titles and gear, not through a new race entry. This supersedes the earlier agent proposal that gave every race one or two evolutions.

Sources: `codex/races/*.md`, `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`, `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`, `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`.

## The two evolution lines

| Race | Line | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Goblin** | Goblin to **Hobgoblin** to **Goblin Chieftain** | The ordinary line. Hobgoblin is canon (Ru, Zinka, Wyx and Sevish all carry the Hobgoblin tag). Goblin Chieftain is the top step above it, held for later (see Sevish note). |
| **Goblin, unique branch** | Goblin to **Shiv Goblin** | A rare, unique race among goblins. Not everyone can go down it normally and it is not a playable choice. Tatsuya is the only one so far. Entry is through an **Ordeal**: Shiv must accept the candidate personally, and Tatsuya, as Shiv's only disciple right now, has to agree to a candidate before they can enter the Ordeal. |
| **Beastfolk** | Beastfolk to **Beastman** (Beastmen) to **Beast Lord** | The only other race with an upgrade. Stage names are the author's; the triggers and stat changes are proposals (see below). |

All other races: no evolution. A level-ups only path (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Drakekin, Frostborn).

## The goblin line in detail

- **Goblin.** Base stats 4/4/2/7/9/4. Weakest floor in the game, highest Fortune.
- **Hobgoblin (CANON).** Taller, human height, markings, speech without contractions. Goblin abilities evolve (Goblin Gas becomes Toxic Breath). Ru goes from level 1 to level 5 directly by Shiv's blessing (Disciple of Elder Nykri). Zinka is level 7, Wyx level 25, Sevish level 80. Stat deltas are a PROPOSAL: about +2 Courage, +2 Power, +1 Wisdom, Fortune unchanged.
- **Goblin Chieftain (author-stated name, details PROPOSAL).** The leader step above Hobgoblin: a goblin who holds a clan. Gains a clan-wide aura (nearby clan goblins fight steadier), one Command art, and a title that other clans read on sight. Sevish leads Thrash but his tag still reads Hobgoblin; whether he is a Chieftain by tag is left open and not stated on the page.
- **Shiv Goblin (CANON, unique branch).** Reached only by Shiv's Ordeal and the Call of the Bones necklace bond. Tatsuya grows taller, white hair, lighter skin, Shaman's Veil. The mark is legible to older goblins on sight. Others entering the Ordeal need Shiv's acceptance and Tatsuya's agreement while he is the only disciple.

## The Beastfolk line (stage names author-stated, rest PROPOSAL)

- **Beastfolk.** Base race, Predator's Instinct and Enrage.
- **Beastman (Beastmen).** The first step: heavier frame and fur or hide, Enrage starts a little earlier (about 50 percent HP instead of 40), one more Physical Art slot.
- **Beast Lord.** The leader form: a Beastfolk who commands a hunting band or house, an aura that steadies nearby Beastfolk and a command art. Tied to the Myrrhwood kingdom's nobility.

## Rules of evolution in this world

1. Evolution is earned, not selected. Nobody picks it at creation. It costs time, risk or a bond.
2. Only Goblin and Beastfolk evolve. A race that cannot evolve is not weaker for it; it levels, trains and equips.
3. Three triggers exist for the lines that evolve: a level threshold reached without dying, a world quest or item bond, and a direct blessing from a patron or spirit. [PROPOSAL for the grouping, CANON for each example]
4. The Shiv Goblin branch is the exception and the rare case: an Ordeal, Shiv's acceptance, and Tatsuya's agreement while he is the only disciple.
5. A blessing is rare and personal. Shiv's gift to Ru was a one-time act. It does not loosen the normal path for anyone else.
6. Players and NPCs differ. Players follow the stat sheet and the quest. NPC evolution is slower and quieter, and NPCs mostly do not know how it works. Troops evolve through the rank-up system in `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`.
7. Gold-tag NPCs do not level while they are not troops, so their evolved rank is a fixed point.
8. Evolution changes the race entry, not just looks: stats, one or two abilities, and how other people read you. It does not remove the base race's weaknesses outright.
9. Culture reacts. A Shiv Goblin is read as Shiv-marked by old goblins; a Hobgoblin is read as a leader; a Beast Lord is read as nobility.
10. Reputation does not evolve with the body. Goblin and Beastfolk carry their stigma into the evolved form unless worked down through Karma and Affiliation.
