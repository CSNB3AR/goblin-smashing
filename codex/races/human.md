# Human

**Home zone:** Lunarest (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), the central, human-dominated hub.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 |

Flat across the board, no bias, matching Lunarest's "central hub" flavor, the race everything else gets measured against.

## Racial ability: Adaptable

A small bonus to whichever Discipline was last actively trained. Reads as the race's "no real weakness" identity mechanically, not just flavor.

## Resistances

None, flat baseline, no notable resistance or weakness.

## Magic access

Full, unrestricted magic access, no Wisdom gate beyond the standard tier-1 threshold (see `codex/magic/overview.md`). Humans can open any school without a racial penalty to work around.
