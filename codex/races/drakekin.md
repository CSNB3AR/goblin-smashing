# Drakekin

**Home zone:** none, not tied to a starting zone. A rarer, harder-to-unlock achievement race, more of an endgame/milestone race than something browsed at creation alongside the other seven.

## Base stats

| Courage | Power | Wisdom | Speed | Fortune | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 7 | 7 | 6 | 6 | 7 |

Strong across the board, no clean single bias, consistent with it not being tied to a starting zone or a single cultural identity the way the other seven races are.

## Racial ability: Draconic Presence

A minor passive intimidation aura, weaker enemies hesitate half a beat before engaging. Consistent with the race's no-clean-bias, strong-across-the-board identity.

## Resistances

| Notable resistance | Notable weakness |
|---|---|
| Fire | Poison |

## Magic access

Full, unrestricted magic access, no Wisdom gate beyond the standard tier-1 threshold (see `codex/magic/overview.md`), consistent with the race reading strong across every category rather than specializing.
