# Physical Arts — Bow and Arrow

Ranged Physical Arts, Speed/Wisdom-adjacent precision work rather than raw Power. Wyx's category (see `codex/characters/wyx-thrash.md`).

|---|---|

| 1 | **Pin Shot** — a shot aimed at an opponent's limb or gear rather than center mass, disrupts rather than damages. |
| 3 | **Volley Draw** — a rapid multi-arrow release, several shots loosed close enough together to land as one exchange. |
