# Physical Arts — Overview

A distinct skill tree from spellcasting entirely, Power/Speed-driven rather than Wisdom-gated (see `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`), fueled by SP rather than MP (`codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`). This is the real combat identity for races or characters who lean into Physical Arts instead of magic (Orc, Beastfolk, and Goblin by default, see `codex/races/overview.md`), not a lesser consolation tree, a full parallel path to magic.

## Tiers 1-7

Structured the same rough way the magic tier system is (`codex/magic/overview.md`): a single trained technique at the low end, chained multi-technique combos in the middle, battlefield-altering signature moves at the top, earned through repetition and real combat use rather than Wisdom growth. **Tier 7 is the practical ceiling**, one tier below magic's Tier 8, consistent with Physical Arts being an earned-through-practice track rather than a power-scaling-to-godlike one.

- **Tier 1** — a single basic trained technique. Three per weapon category (see the weapon files below) as the standard starting kit.
- **Tier 2-3** — technique refinement, small combo chains of 2-3 moves.
- **Tier 4-5** — real multi-technique combos, a fight-defining sequence rather than isolated hits.
- **Tier 6** — signature techniques, distinctive enough to be recognized by other players/NPCs on sight.
- **Tier 7** — battlefield-altering signature moves, rare, most Physical Arts practitioners never get here.

## Custom techniques: the martial-master rule

A Physical Arts practitioner isn't limited to the named techniques in the weapon files. A character who's genuinely mastered a weapon category (a real, earned threshold, not a level number alone, requiring sustained Power/Speed investment and real combat use of that weapon over time) can develop and name their own technique. This isn't a free-form ability, it has to be built the way any Physical Arts technique is, tiered, SP-costed, and consistent with what that weapon category can actually do. A martial master isn't inventing new physics, they're the first to formalize a combination nobody else has drilled into a repeatable move yet.
