# Goblin Warfare and Tactics (Thrash clan, working level)

**Status:** A working reference for the writer. Every pattern below is a **[PROPOSAL]** unless tagged otherwise. It describes what goblins plausibly do, in the order of how often they do it. It does not contain Tatsuya's Chapter Eleven plan; that stays a surprise. Companion: `codex/locations/thrash-camp-field-guide.md`.

## Ground rules for every pattern

- Goblins are weak, cunning and numerous, and the clan has about 25 at best. They win by position, surprise and numbers, never by a fair fight. [CANON `codex/races/goblin.md`, `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`]
- Goblins cannot cast. Fire, gas and traps come from pitch, oil, hide, rope and herbs, not magic. [CANON]
- Wyx and Sevish are the exceptions. The line does what Wyx whistles and Sevish shouts. [CANON Chapter 10]
- Thrash habit, shown on the page: small groups go down to meet each party. That is the clan's real weakness, not a pattern to copy.

## The eight patterns

### 1. The Hedge Ambush
- **When:** A party on a known track, at dusk or dawn, in trees or tall grass.
- **Who does what:** Scouts lie hidden on both flanks. A bait goblin shows himself and runs. Blades wait at the rear to close the road. A Stakehand or two throws stones to start the panic.
- **Weakness:** It needs patience and silence, and goblins have little of either. A sneeze or a bored boast ends it. A party that looks up sees the second flank.

### 2. The Snare Line
- **When:** Ground the clan holds for days: the track, the stream crossing, the slope under the stakes.
- **Who does what:** Posts lay rope trip lines at ankle height, covered pits with sharpened stakes, and rope tied from tree to tree to snare riders. Scouts reset them each morning. Lines are laid so the goblin path stays clear.
- **Weakness:** Traps need to be remembered. A goblin who forgets where one is falls in it. Anyone who walks the ground by day sees fresh dirt. Traps do not stop an enemy who tests every step.

### 3. Smoke, Pitch and Stink
- **When:** The clan is outnumbered, the target is a tent or enclosed spot, or retreat is needed.
- **Who does what:** Stakehands hurl pitch pots and burning hay. The cook mixes green wood and herbs for choking smoke, with pepper or ash for the eyes. Scouts light the wind side. Everyone else holds the line away from the smoke.
- **Weakness:** Fire follows wind, and the camp is mostly hide, hay and timber. The valley wind comes down off the cliff and can turn it back on the clan. Rain ends it.

### 4. The Plank Wall
- **When:** Holding a narrow place: the track, a lane between tents, a doorway.
- **Who does what:** Shield goblins stand shoulder to shoulder with mismatched shields, overlapped where they can be. Spear goblins stand behind and jab over the top. A Blade sits at the back and drags out anyone who falls.
- **Weakness:** The shields are tent doors and barrel lids. They hold against a club and not against a heavy blow. One broken shield opens a gap, and the line breaks from the gap. It has no reserve and no way to turn.

### 5. The Skirmish Swarm
- **When:** Open ground, against a lone strong enemy, or to wear down a caster.
- **Who does what:** Many goblins attack from every side at once and break off before the enemy can choose a target. Throwers keep up stones and javelins. Blades move in only to finish the wounded.
- **Weakness:** It costs a goblin whenever the enemy hits back. It works only if the enemy tires first. A fast, hard-hitting enemy kills goblins one by one. A caster with area attacks ends it.

### 6. Bite and Bolt (Hit and Run)
- **When:** Raids on supply carts, stragglers and sleeping camps; the clan's oldest habit.
- **Who does what:** A fast group strikes the target, takes what it can carry, and scatters. A second group waits on the retreat route to cover them. Scouts watch the road for pursuit.
- **Weakness:** It needs a way to run, and the camp is a cul-de-sac with one track. It cannot be used when the enemy is already in camp.

### 7. The Ladder (Defence in Depth)
- **When:** Defending the camp, as on the page. Wyx holds the outer stakes, Sevish the inner barricade, the people behind.
- **Who does what:** Eyes on the east line give warning. The outer stakes slow and funnel. The strongest goblin meets the enemy first. If she is passed, the next strongest holds the inner line. [CANON Chapter 10]
- **Weakness:** Each rung is held by few. A strong enemy takes them in order. The layers share one valley, so there is no way out when the last rung falls. The clan leaders are the whole defence, and if one dies the line shakes.

### 8. The Bone Whistle (Shaman Support)
- **When:** Any fight where the clan has a shaman, or something the goblins treat as one. Thrash has no caster, so this is the nearest thing: a leader or bone-setter who rallies and heals by hand.
- **Who does what:** The bone-setter drags the wounded back, binds them and passes them water. A drummer or horn-blower keeps time. A leader shouts names and boasts to hold the line. Where a shaman exists elsewhere, he casts from behind the line.
- **Weakness:** A rally depends on a voice, and the voice can be silenced or drowned out by a louder one. Bone-setting saves one goblin at a time and cannot keep up with a fight. A real shaman is a priority target and dies fast.

## Quick reference

| Pattern | Needs | Fails against |
|---|---|---|
| Hedge Ambush | Cover, patience | Scouts of its own, bright light |
| Snare Line | Time on the ground | Careful walkers, fire |
| Smoke, Pitch and Stink | Wind and dry ground | Rain, reversed wind |
| Plank Wall | A narrow place | Heavy blows, flanks |
| Skirmish Swarm | Open ground, numbers | Area damage, fast killers |
| Bite and Bolt | An escape route | A cul-de-sac |
| The Ladder | Time, strong leaders | A strong enemy taking each rung |
| The Bone Whistle | A voice | A louder voice, a fast fight |

## What goblins do not do

They do not hold ground when they can leave it. They do not fight fair, and they do not follow a plan that depends on every goblin doing what he was told. A plan that needs discipline is the writer's tool for a character who can supply it, not something the clan does on its own. [PROPOSAL]
