# Physical Arts — Hand-to-Hand

Unarmed and improvised-weapon combat. The default category for races or characters without a signature weapon yet, and Ru's own path (see `codex/characters/young-goblin-companion-candidate.md`).

Representative moves, not exhaustive, expandable as needed:

| Tier | Technique |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Guard Break** — a basic strike aimed at forcing an opponent's block open rather than dealing real damage. |
| 1 | **Low Sweep** — a leg strike meant to disrupt footing, sets up a follow-up rather than landing real damage alone. |
| 1 | **Palm Check** — a short, controlled shove-strike, more about creating space than hurting anyone. |
| 2-3 | **Silent Three Fang** — a chained strike technique, three fast hits read by an opponent as one. Drosh's, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`. |
| 4-5 | **Veil-Pierce Gaze** — a mid-tier perception technique, scouting through canvas/thin cover rather than a strike at all, Physical Arts isn't purely offensive. Drosh's, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`. |
