# Tools, Adventuring and Camp Gear, Mounts and Vehicles (D&D baseline for HGO)

**Status:** Research and proposal file, not canon until the author accepts it. It edits no existing file. It translates D&D's tools, adventuring gear, equipment packs, mounts, vehicles and trade goods into HGO, and ties each to a profession, a station, a Gear Tier and the codex's existing camp and travel rules.

**Tags.** SRC (outside source), CANON (in repo), PAGE (in a chapter), PROPOSAL (invented). Core chart: `codex/lore/dnd-gear-baseline.md` section 0.

D&D sources for the tables below: [SRC] https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/adventuring-gear and https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment . The price index is 1 gp roughly 15 shillings at the Common level [PROPOSAL, OPEN]. The hard canon anchors are 10 shillings = 1 HGO Coin = 4 yen [CANON `codex/materials/blacksmithing-and-farm-resources-book-one.md`].

## 1. Inventory, sockets and placed gear

HGO's inventory is a pocket. The flare "materialized in my hand" and claimed items "go straight into the inventory" [PAGE Ch5, ledger]. Dropped items have to be walked over to be claimed ("pickup requires a physical claim" [CANON `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`]). So three layers [PROPOSAL]:

1. **Pocket:** the inventory. Stores anything, ignores container size, but is limited in slot count and weight by Power. Nothing "pinged encumbrance" at Ch11, so the limit is quiet [PAGE draft Ch11].
2. **Sockets:** ready items on the belt, chest harness and boots (pouch, quiver, sheath, whistle, potion loop, gadget slot). The Goblin Gadget Belt is the model, "slots for smoke bombs and gas bombs" [CANON `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`]. D&D containers give the scale: a backpack holds 1 cubic foot or 30 lb, a pouch one fifth of a cubic foot or 6 lb, a quiver 20 arrows [SRC] wikidot gear page.
3. **Placed gear:** tents, pots, barrels, forges, cart. Set down in the world and walked back to. Scouting tents hold a character in place when the player logs off [CANON `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`].

## 2. Tools and the six professions

| Profession | D&D analog | HGO kit | Station | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smithing** | smith's tools | hammer, tongs, files, whetstone, apron | Clan Workbench and Forge, field forge kit | Open to all, mono steel refining is a dwarven house secret [CANON] |
| **Bonecrafting and Leatherworking** | leatherworker's and carver's tools (not priced in the source I read) | awl, needles, sinew, bone saw, carving knives, tanning rack | Clan Workbench, **Portable Campfire Kit** for basic recipes | Goblin-native, "most Bloodhurst gear starts here" [CANON] |
| **Alchemy and Poisoncraft** | alchemist's supplies, poisoner's kit, herbalism kit | mortar, vials, burner, needles, Gas Sac pouch | Campfire Kit (low success above Tier 4) | The gadget line lives here [CANON] |
| **Enchanting** | none | rune chalk, inks, Mountain Silver wire, Spirit Dust | Clan Workbench | Seating costs MP, failure rises with tier [CANON] |
| **Cooking** | cook's utensils | pot, ladle, spice pouch | camp fire | Clanmark Trail Ration, Hearthroot Stew [CANON food file] |

Other kits: healer's kit (5 gp), climber's kit (25 gp, 12 lb), navigator's tools (25 gp) [SRC]. **Signal gear** [PROPOSAL]: whistles, horns and drums. The Scout's Whistle battle item exists [CANON `codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`], and Call of Command (return, scout, call to arms) is Tatsuya's passive [CANON].

Vehicles: cart 15 gp, wagon 35, carriage 100, sled 20, chariot 250, rowboat 50, longship 10,000, sailing ship 10,000. ## 7. Trade goods and services

D&D trade goods [SRC] DDB equipment page: wheat 1 cp per lb, flour 2 cp, salt 5 cp, iron 1 sp per lb, canvas 1 sp per sq yd, copper 5 sp, cotton 5 sp per sq yd, linen 5 gp, silk 10 gp, goats 1 gp, cows 10 gp. Services: unskilled hireling 2 sp per day, skilled 2 gp per day, messenger 2 cp per mile, ship's passage 1 sp per mile [SRC].

HGO tie-ins [PROPOSAL]: trade goods are the regional materials (Clanmark Ore, Ironvein Ore, Myrrhwood Hide, Sunforged Iron, Skyglass Ore, monocrystals, Hearthroot, Redroot Pepper) plus canvas, hay and salt for camps. The goblin trade company's first lines are cheap Myrrhwood-to-Valefrost goods [CANON `codex/subplots/goblin-trade-company-forward-plot.md`]. A Peddler's Permit lets a character sell from a cart in one city, Common and Uncommon goods only [CANON `codex/lore/merchant-license-auction-and-jobs.md`]. A hired scout or porter is an NPC job, priced by the day against the shilling.

## 8. Conflicts with canon and open items

1. **Pocket versus backpack.** The inventory is a pocket [PAGE]. I keep backpacks as socket expanders only. OPEN.
2. **No mount slot.** Eleven slots is canon. Mounts are items. OPEN.
3. **No woodworking or tailoring profession.** Tents, hurdles and soft goods sit under Leatherworking and Smithing in the material map. OPEN.
4. **Ash Wolf, Ironback Boar and Wyvern mounts** are not in the codex. Wyvern Pact mounts are canon, the rest are proposals.
5. **The hired carriage and the Drevhorn** are both flagged optional in the codex.
6. **Price index** (1 gp about 15 shillings) is a placeholder. The real anchors are the shilling conversion and the Book One material bands.
7. **D&D trade goods and services** are D&D economy, not HGO canon.
8. **Healers and the lean-to** were cut from Chapter 10 by the author. The kit lists them because a 25-goblin camp would have them, not because the page shows them.
