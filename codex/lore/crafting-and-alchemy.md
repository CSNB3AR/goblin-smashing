# Crafting & Alchemy

The system standing behind every "requires crafting materials" note already scattered through the codex: the Goblin Gadget Belt's shaded/locked abilities (`codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`), Shaman's Veil's real mastery past the basics (`codex/lore/magic-tier-system.md`), and the future battle items (`codex/lore/battle-items-and-battlegear.md`). None of those unlock for free, all of them route through this.

## The loop

Gather materials in the world (drops, harvested resources, world-quest rewards) → refine or combine them at a crafting station or through a character's own crafting belt/kit → apply the result to unlock a shaded ability, upgrade an item, or build something new outright. Real time and material cost, not a menu toggle, consistent with the house rule that nothing is pure abstraction (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`, pickup requires a physical claim, same underlying philosophy).

Tatsuya's own path into this is a direct extension of his established tinkering instinct, the same thing that keeps the Haya EB running and got a dead first-gen pod overridden (`codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`), carried into the game itself. He's not learning crafting as a new skill so much as finally having a place to actually use the one he already has.

## Crafted constructs, not summons

**A crafted construct is not a summon.** This distinction is load-bearing: the "no summoning magic, ever" rule (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`) is about magic conjuring a creature from nothing. A construct built through this crafting tree is a physical object first, assembled from real gathered materials, that happens to move and act on its own once finished, mechanical and alchemical, not magical in origin. Building one and "activating" it isn't a spell.

**Forward canon: a golem-style construct**, built once Tatsuya's crafting has advanced far enough, a small, built companion rather than a combat-duplicate or a pet in the traditional sense. Ties directly to Daichi's own legacy (a father who built things, including the tech that keeps his son's scooter and, later, a whole arcade's pods running), a real generational throughline rather than a random unlock. Not available early, name and full details not yet decided, flagged here so later chapters build toward it consistently rather than inventing something contradictory.

**Forward canon: a minor time-slow item.** Tatsuya eventually crafts a small-scale item that replicates a limited version of the active freeze-cast Shiv uses in Chapter 6 (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`), nowhere near the zone-wide scale of the real thing. Not available early, no mechanical details worked out yet, flagged here for later chapters.
