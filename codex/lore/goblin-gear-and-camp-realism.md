# Goblin and Hobgoblin Gear, and the Camp Realism Sheet

**Status:** Research and proposal file, not canon until the author accepts it. It edits no existing file. It answers the author's question about Chapter 10: how would a goblin hillside camp have "tent doors" to use as shields? Short answer: it would not. ## 2. The D&D baseline for goblinoids

[SRC] stat blocks: https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/?slug=goblin , https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/?slug=hobgoblin , https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/?slug=bugbear , https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=goblin-boss , https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=hobgoblin-captain , https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=hobgoblin-warlord .

| D&D creature | Armor | Weapons | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goblin | leather armor and shield (AC 15) | scimitar, shortbow | small ambush skirmisher |
| Goblin boss | chain shirt and shield (AC 17) | scimitar, javelins | the one with real kit |
| Bugbear | hide armor and shield (AC 16) | morningstar, javelin | big brute in thick hide |
| Hobgoblin | chain mail and shield (AC 18) | longsword, longbow | disciplined soldier |
| Hobgoblin captain | half plate (AC 17) | greatsword, javelin | officer |
| Hobgoblin warlord | plate and shield (AC 20) | longsword, shield bash, javelin | commander |

Lore. [SRC] https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/263-how-to-play-hobgoblins-like-perfect-soldiers : hobgoblins run a strict rank structure (soldiers, captains who lead up to fifty, warlords over a legion), win by formation and discipline, and a warlord's command post may be a command tent ringed by palisades. [SRC] https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/hobgoblin-5e : goblins are small and sneaky, hobgoblins tall and disciplined. [SRC] https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/humanoids/goblin/ : Pathfinder goblins scavenge their gear, wear leather armor and a light wooden shield, carry a short sword and a short bow, prize fire as a weapon, and live in camps with little drive to build. [SRC] https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/humanoids/hobgoblin/ : Pathfinder hobgoblins wear studded leather and a light steel shield, carry a longsword and longbow, and love engineering and siege craft. [SRC] https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Dogslicer : the goblin dogslicer is a crude single-piece curved blade, often with holes punched in to lighten it, and snaps at the worst moment.

**How HGO differs, and which side wins.** Canon wins every time.
- D&D hobgoblin officers wear mail or plate. **Sevish, a level 80 hobgoblin, wears a vest and a sash and carries an axe.** Wyx wears bone, leather and fur. No plate on either. A Berserker's armor is his edge and his aura. Plate belongs to dwarves, humans and Ironbreakers.
- D&D goblins have leather and a shield. **HGO camp goblins hold a barrel lid and a club, many barefoot.** That is poorer than D&D, and it matches "what they raided with" and "ranks, you earn your gear".
- D&D hobgoblins are drilled legionaries. **The Thrash hobgoblins are two named leaders, a scout, and a lieutenant, not a legion**, and the clan "lives on whatever works" while "the hobgoblins run on a system" [PAGE Ch6 via `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`].
- What I keep from D&D: the **rank ladder** (recruit, soldier, officer, leader), **size**, **spear and shield as the soldier's standard**, **fire as the goblin weapon** (Goblin Flare, gas), **scavenged and crude gear**, and the **dogslicer** idea of a poor blade that can break.

### 3.2 Notes on the table

- **Size.** A goblin is about four feet [PAGE Ch4] and a hobgoblin is human height [CANON Sevish and Wyx files]. [PROPOSAL] The D&D **Heavy** property penalizes Small wielders [SRC] https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/weapons , so a camp goblin does not swing a greataxe, a hobgoblin does. That is why the camp is clubs, spears and shortbows and the two big axes belong to the hobgoblin chief.
- **Common Goblin items are cut for small frames.** Tatsuya's chest fits him "better than it had any right to" because the make is "Goblin" [PAGE Ch10]. A hobgoblin cannot wear it without a refit. See the racial file.
- **Enforcers carry the gadget belt.** The Goblin Gadget Belt drops off the two enforcers [PAGE Ch5], so it is clan-police kit: slots for smoke bombs and gas bombs, "locked until you gather materials" [CANON `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`]. The enforcers had the belts but no recipes, which fits Tatsuya noticing only goblins who know the combination are rare.
- **Bare feet.** Boots are scarce stock in a raider camp. Most recruits are barefoot like Tatsuya.
- **The spiked bone guard is a gauntlets piece**, not a chest piece. The shoulder plate is part of it and is cosmetic. [PROPOSAL] See `codex/lore/dnd-gear-baseline.md` section 4.

## 4. The Common tier and how it relates to the rest

**Common is a rarity, not a clan.** It is the white card, the lowest rung of five [CANON]. **Goblin is the make**: cut for small frames, built at the Clan Workbench, GT1 to GT2. So "Common Goblin Leather Chest" reads rarity, make, material, slot (the naming pattern in the hub file). Nothing in the Common line carries more than one `+1`.
