# Goblin Code and Clan Alignments

**Status:** canon anchors are tagged CANON or PAGE. Everything else is PROPOSAL. Goblins are people with a code or without one, never a nature. "Feral" is the outsiders' word and a stigma (see `goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`). Cell names are in `alignment-framework.md`.

## 1. Thrash's code

Shiv's creed is law in the Thrash camp, and its practice is Warden: steady, fair among its own, slow to look past the camp. These rules are CANON or PAGE unless marked.

1. **We do not fight each other.** Goblin on goblin violence is the cardinal sin, and the sign that Shiv's influence is slipping.
2. **A witness before judgment.** Sevish asks who saw it, and asks the accused to choose words with care. Silence in the crowd counts against the crowd (PAGE).
3. **Whoever brings word of Shiv stands as an ally** (PAGE). The title is granted, then earned.
4. **A fair fight is the only honest argument.** No traps in a duel, no blow to a downed foe (CANON, Sevish).
5. **The dead stay dead.** A goblin who falls does not get up, so every death is counted and owed (PAGE).
6. **The walls rule.** Wyx and Sevish are the walls, and whoever reaches the outer stakes meets her first (PAGE).
7. **A favor is a debt.** Call it a bargain and the clan calls it a debt (PAGE). PROPOSAL: debts are remembered across generations.

The Gap: enforcers take a bowl from a runt while the camp looks away. The code is as strong as whoever enforces it.

## 2. How goblins treat others (PROPOSAL, from the code)

- **The helpless** (children, runts, the wounded, the unarmed): never harmed by a goblin who keeps the code. Mercy here is a rule, not softness. A bully who breaks it loses standing quickly.
- **Prisoners:** held, questioned, and offered a choice. Outsiders who carry no blood on them are ransomed or released with a warning. A prisoner who fought fair is fed. One who farmed the camp is judged at the stakes, with a witness. Nobody is tortured for sport.
- **Enemies:** met in the open where possible. Goblins prefer a duel to a raid, and a raid to a siege. They will use gas, ropes and ambush on those who came to farm them, because farmers earn no duel.
- **Their dead:** names are carried by the one who saw them fall and said at the fire. Gear is shared out only after the names are spoken. Using a dead clansman as a bargaining chip is the worst insult.
- **Humans and others:** a goblin starts at the 20 percent floor in human cities and no goblin is surprised by it.

## 3. How clans differ (PROPOSAL, extending CANON)

Canon says only that the other clans are fractured, warring and barred from villages, and read as feral. Thrash alone enters settlements. The table gives working reads for the author, not facts about places no player has seen.

| Clan type | Cell | What marks it |
|---|---|---|
| **Thrash** | Oathkeeper creed, Warden practice | Shiv's table, the count of the dead, fair fights, a code that lapses at the edges |
| **Table clans** (splinters that kept a code but lost Shiv) | Warden or Unbound | Honor without a unifier. Bargain with outsiders if approached by a goblin who knows their laws |
| **Hunger clans** (raiders and herd takers) | Taker | Take what they can from weaker clans. Respect only strength and food |
| **Broken clans** (dropped both code and table) | Unbound to Taker | Violence by habit, no oaths kept. This is what outsiders mean by feral, and it is a judgment on conduct, not on kind |
| **Ravager bands** | Ravager | None by default. Reserved for scenes that need a true horror |

Gold NPCs in other clans must be written individually: a gentle goblin can live in a broken clan, and a cold one in Thrash. Beastfolk claims about goblin territory stay goblin rumor until a player goes to see.
