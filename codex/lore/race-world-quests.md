# Race World Quests

Every playable race has its own dedicated World Quest line, tied to that race's own origin/culture, distinct from the general World Quest category described in `codex/lore/world-quests.md`. A race's World Quest doesn't exist just because the race does, it has to actually **activate**, and most haven't yet.

## Activation status

- **Humans** — activated. Long since underway, part of why Lunarest reads as the established, developed hub it is.
- **Goblins** — activated as of "Call of the Bones" (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`, `codex/characters/shiv.md`). This is the goblin race's first-ever World Quest activation, which is exactly why `codex/lore/world-quests.md` calls it "the first new one" after a roughly year-and-a-half drought, not a coincidence, this drought and this activation are the same event described from two different angles.
- **Elves, Dwarves, Frostborn, Beastfolk** — not yet activated. No confirmed trigger condition on the page yet for any of them, flagged here so it isn't silently invented later per race.

Humans and Goblins are the two activated races, and both share the same practical reason: they're the two races actually established in and around Lunarest, the one directly reachable supercontinent (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`). Races tied to the currently-inaccessible Myrrhwood region (Beastfolk, and the wider goblin-clan homeland itself) or to regions less central to the playerbase's traffic simply haven't had their line come up yet.

## Why the Thrash clan is in Lunarest Outskirts at all

The Thrash clan didn't start at Bloodhurst. They sailed there from Myrrhwood, real ship time (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`'s no-fast-travel note), specifically because they have an elder, the qualifying condition their own attempt at the goblin race World Quest needed. Myrrhwood itself has never been player-accessible (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`), so the clan's only route to actually attempting the quest was leaving their own homeland and making camp near Lunarest, the one place the quest could actually be attempted at all.

That's what Bloodhurst's hillside camp has actually been for the better part of two years: staging ground for repeated attempts at pushing up toward Lunarest's walls to trigger the quest properly, and repeated defeats at the hands of actual players who read the recurring pattern as nothing more than an easy, predictable farm spot, not a real questline in motion. **This is also the real, grounded reason PK_Wraith's crew treats Bloodhurst as reliable, low-effort prey** (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`): a known fixture that's been losing the same fight in the same spot for two years reads as exactly that, not as a clan on the verge of finally succeeding, which is precisely what Tatsuya's arrival changes.
