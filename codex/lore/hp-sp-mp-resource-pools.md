# HP / SP / MP Resource Pools

Separate from the six Disciplines+Energy stats (see `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`), a character's actual combat resource pools, introduced on the page in Chapter 3.

## Facts

- **HP:** base 50, +3 per level. **Confirmed data point, Chapter 8:** at level 7 (five level-ups past the level-2 baseline), Tatsuya's HP reads 65/65, 50 + 3×5, the formula checked out cleanly against an actual on-page number rather than staying purely qualitative.
- **A second pool splits into SP and MP**, roughly 25/25 at Tatsuya's current level (post level-2), climbing to 32/32 by level 7 alongside the Wisdom investment (+4 Wisdom from his discipline-point spend, see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). **SP** (stamina) fuels physical and item-based abilities, thrown bombs, weapon techniques, anything not pure magic, drains faster when the item or weapon carries a magical augment on top of its base function. **MP** (mana) fuels pure magic, scales with Wisdom.
- **Abilities have real cooldowns and drain these pools.** No spamming, no infinite-use items. Rough pacing: normal physical-tool use barely dents SP, but magically-augmented physical abilities or repeated use drains it meaningfully over the course of a few fights, at which point a consumable refill becomes necessary.
- This is what keeps combat from reading as unlimited ability-spam, real resource management underneath the LitRPG numbers.

**Regen rates** for all three pools (HP Regen, SP Regen, MP Regen) are secondary stats derived from primary disciplines — see `codex/lore/secondary-combat-stats.md`.
