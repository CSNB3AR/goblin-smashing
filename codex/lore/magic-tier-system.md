# Magic Tier System

Not a named hierarchy (no "circles," "ranks," or similar flavor label), just numbered tiers, kept plain and functional. **Tier 8 is the practical ceiling for a player character**, nothing above that is reachable through normal play.

A spell or magic path's tier gates how much a character can actually do with it, independent of whether they have access to the path at all. Access and power are two different gates: Shaman's Veil (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is what grants Tatsuya access to the arcane-bomber path in the first place, since goblins are otherwise locked out of it by the standard Wisdom restriction (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). Tier progression within that path is a separate, later climb, earned through Wisdom growth and his own alchemy/crafting research (see `codex/lore/crafting-and-alchemy.md`), not granted by the bond itself.

## The tiers, functional only

Kept vague/flavor-level rather than an exhaustive mechanical breakdown, per the house style rule against dumping stat blocks (`codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`):

- **Tier 1-2** — a single small, controlled effect. Where Tatsuya starts with Ancient Bone Flame Barrage once Shaman's Veil actually opens.
- **Tier 3-4** — real area/duration control, an effect that lingers or covers ground rather than a single instant, this is where the rune/mine mechanics (channel, arm, detonate) actually become usable.
- **Tier 5-6** — multiple effects chained or triggered off each other. Ancient Bone Flame Barrage's chain-reaction elaboration (already locked in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`) lives here.
- **Tier 7** — effects that reshape the immediate battlefield rather than just hitting something in it, rare, most casters in the world never get here.
- **Tier 8** — the practical ceiling. Reality-bending in scale, vanishingly rare, world-quest/boss-tier casters only, not a target for Tatsuya's arc to reach and blow past, a horizon more than a milestone.

## Two named mechanics

- **Amplify magic** — a caster can spend extra MP to push a known spell's effect temporarily above the tier they've actually unlocked, at real cost and real risk (see `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), a genuine gamble, not a safe stat purchase. The Bones Speak's own Amplify mode (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is a once-a-day, free, Fortune-scaled version of this same general concept, specific to Tatsuya and the necklace, the two aren't the same system but read as the same family of idea.
- **Dual-casting** — maintaining two spells' effects active at the same time, a real Wisdom-gated difficulty spike rather than something any caster can do casually. Out of reach for Tatsuya at his current Wisdom, a real, earned later-arc milestone once he's actually grown into Shaman's Veil.

**Shaman's Veil scales purely on Wisdom.** Fortune has no relationship to it at all, that's a deliberate, locked separation, not an oversight. The Call of the Bones Necklace's own +5 Fortune bonus is a separate, ordinary item-stat grant, unrelated to the magic path it also happens to unlock.

See `codex/lore/magic-affinities.md` for what flavor of magic a caster actually leans into within this tier system.
