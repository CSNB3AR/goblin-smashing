# Magic Tier System

Not a named hierarchy (no "circles," "ranks," or similar flavor label), just numbered tiers, kept plain and functional. **Tier 8 is the practical ceiling for a player character**, nothing above that is reachable through normal play.

A spell or magic path's tier gates how much a character can actually do with it, independent of whether they have access to the path at all. Access and power are two different gates: Shaman's Veil (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`) is what grants Tatsuya access to the arcane-bomber path in the first place, since goblins are otherwise locked out of it by the standard Wisdom restriction (`codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). Tier progression within that path is a separate, later climb, earned through Wisdom growth and his own alchemy/crafting research, not granted by the bond itself.

**Shaman's Veil scales purely on Wisdom.** Fortune has no relationship to it at all, that's a deliberate, locked separation, not an oversight. The Call of the Bones Necklace's own +5 Fortune bonus is a separate, ordinary item-stat grant, unrelated to the magic path it also happens to unlock.
