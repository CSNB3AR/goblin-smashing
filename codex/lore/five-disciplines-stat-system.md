# The Five Disciplines + Energy (The Glory Codex)

Every creature, player character, and named NPC in Eryndal runs on the same six numbers. These are also the stat line printed on any deck-building card representation of a creature or hero (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`).

- **Courage** — two faces on the same stat, both real, neither optional. The first is willpower, bravery, fear resistance, stabilizing risky gambles, drives "save the skill" checks and defensive challenges. The second is a genuine charisma-adjacent component: how a character actually talks to NPCs, the register and confidence behind it, and how NPCs read that back. This is distinct from the separate roleplay-register/Affiliation system (see `codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md`), which governs whether an NPC's own reaction actually lands, a low-Courage character can still say the right in-character thing and move Affiliation, Courage governs how convincingly/confidently they say it. No seventh "Charisma" stat exists, this social half of Courage covers that ground directly.
- **Power** — raw physical strength, damage output, intimidation, breaking things. Primary stat for physical attacks and Power Challenges. Explicitly covers real PvE-relevant physical capability too, not just a combat-damage number: can this character actually lift, carry, or force something heavy. A low-Power character (Tatsuya sits at a flat 4) genuinely can't do much heavy manual labor, a real, felt limitation, not a soft-locked one. Eryndal runs a genuine in-world economy with real jobs players and NPCs can work, Power is one axis of employability/capability among several, not the only one that determines whether a character is functional in the world.
- **Wisdom** — perception, knowledge, cunning, analytical/deliberate perception (distinct from Speed's reflexive perception below), magical affinity, resisting tricks or mental effects. Wisdom scales magic damage and power output the same way Power scales physical output, on top of its separate, already-locked role as the magic-access gate (the Wisdom-18 casting threshold, see `codex/magic/overview.md`), access and raw output are two different things Wisdom governs. Governs skill-learning speed generally, and specifically joins with Speed below on a named joint check: absorbing a crafting manual or technique for real (not just holding the item) requires a combined Wisdom+Speed threshold, since reading it takes analytical perception and picking up the physical technique takes reflexive perception, fall short on either and the manual doesn't "take" (see `codex/crafting/overview.md`, cross-reference `codex/lore/mastery-system.md`).
- **Speed** — agility, reflexes, evasion, movement, initiative in many situations, plus reflexive/reactive perception, quick-reaction awareness, dodge timing, distinct from Wisdom's analytical/deliberate perception above. A goblin favorite for hit-and-run play. Joins Wisdom on the crafting-manual comprehension check above.
- **Fortune** — luck, probability, critical hits, loot quality, random-event success, and every Gambler-path effect. Higher Fortune makes gambles safer and better, and is the gate for goblin signature mechanics (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). Fortune Challenges cover high-variance moments.
- **Energy** — the depletable resource pool: HP plus fuel for big abilities. Damage reduces it; zero means defeated. In the story, this reads as stamina/mana that regenerates between fights or slowly during them.

**A character's own invested points versus gear bonuses are shown separately, never folded into one number**, format `[STAT: subtotal] (+X)` where the subtotal is what a character actually built with their own discipline points and `(+X)` is whatever currently-equipped gear happens to be granting, removable/replaceable independent of the character's own progression (see `codex/lore/equipment-and-gear-slots.md`).

**Resolving checks in prose:** opposed checks or checks against a difficulty are handled narratively, only surfaced on the page when the tension calls for it. Margin of success can add damage or a bonus effect. Author judgment and narrative weight drive most outcomes; a check is only shown as a number when a gamble is genuinely live.

**House style reminder** (see `style-guide/craft-rules.md`): mechanics are shown sparingly and always filtered through what Tatsuya perceives, small HUD notifications in his vision, brief inner-monologue math, the occasional stat callout at a real milestone. Never a dumped stat block mid-fight. Never narrator-voice exposition of a rule Tatsuya wouldn't be actively thinking about in the moment. Brains-over-brawn: the story rewards game knowledge and tactics (see the Tactical Cartographer title in `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`) over raw numbers, in keeping with the King's Avatar governing principle.
