# Timeline, Ages & the Beta

The real-world and in-game calendar the story sits inside. Sequence and rules for the expansion itself live in `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`. Ages and school here override older figures elsewhere in the codex.

## The window

The whole story, from the Season Zero finale broadcast to the Hearthfire launch and Node Wars opening, fits inside roughly **eight to nine months**. The closed beta ran about that long in real time. "Tonight" in Chapter 1 is **Sunday, December 10th, 2045**, the Season Zero finale. Crownfall (Season One opener) is December 25th. Hearthfire is January 1st.

## Beta structure

- **Closed beta (Season Zero):** about **5,000 players**, from roughly **870,000 pre-orders**. One server, one continent. Access was a mix of invitation and pre-order draw.
- **Open beta:** opens after the Season Zero finale. All players and businesses can enter, and the access drive **and the helmet rig** are discounted 50 percent for 72 hours, which undercuts the Tabuchis' Gen 2 pods. Tatsuya's character is an open-beta character (see `codex/objects/hgo-access-drive.md`).
- **The season restarts at open beta.** Open beta leads into Season One (Kazuku explains this at the start), so open-beta seasonal points roll into Season One. Individuals can earn them, but only guilds enter Crownfall, the Season One opener (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`, "Seasonal points and Arc One").
- **New-game tone.** Closed-beta names (Dosuke AI, Eren and others) are established. Tatsuya is new, and after the global stream everybody is watching him.
- Closed-beta characters freeze at Hearthfire. Open-beta characters are permanent (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).
- **Lumi** is the cute system guide who walks players through character creation. She glitches when Tatsuya's goblin-glitch selection fires (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## Dad's two accidents (separate events)

- **The childhood crash, about eleven years ago.** It left Tatsuya the scar over his right brow and **paralyzed Daichi**, who then went all in on VR as a way to make a world where the body was not the ceiling: the Haya pod, the Haya EB, his hybrid Haya Sedan with hand controls. Its cause is not stated.
- **The fatal accident, about a year ago, months before HGO and the third-gen pods released.** Author-side truth, suspicion only on the page: the Tabuchis hired yakuza to sabotage Daichi's **Haya Sedan** (a **hybrid that still ran on gas**; only the Haya EB is fully electric). Its breakdown caused the accident that **killed him**. The Tabuchis used it to attack the business, and every Haya product, the EB included, was pulled from the market. They expected the family to go broke while Ohema was in the hospital. Chiyo returning was not in their plans, Kazuto's court plan for the business and schematics has not worked because Chiyo has her own money, and she has hired lawyers (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`). Closed casket, never fully explained (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`).
- **Daichi's handoff.** He and Kazuku were building the third-gen pods. Before he died he handed off some notes; that stake would have paid off Ohema's debt, which is another reason his letter says to find the bear.
- **Order of events.** Daichi is killed about a year ago, months before HGO and the third-gen pods released. Tatsuya lasts about three months of his first year at Tochi before the Eren betrayal over the stolen build, and has been out a full school year. He starts at WattsUp about a year ago. The closed beta opens roughly eight to nine months before the December 2045 finale. OPEN: whether Daichi died before or during Tatsuya's roughly three months at Tochi. The only author-stated anchors are that Daichi was killed about a year ago, months before HGO and the third-gen pods released, and that Tatsuya lasted about three months of his first year at Tochi before the Eren betrayal pushed him out and he has been out a full school year.

## Eren

- **Eren Tabuchi** is Kazuto's messenger and is **blind to the schematics**: he knows his father wants something from Tatsuya's family, not what or why.
- His guild missed the Season Zero top eight because **Arya's small guild took the last spot** (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`, Fire Festival). He hates her for it, and the rivalry with Tatsuya is layered with that (see `codex/subplots/tatsuya-pro-league-arc-and-eren-rivalry.md`).

King Aldemar Vaereth dies around the start of Season One (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). The December 25th event is named **Crownfall** for it. ## Backstory block (author-stated, kept off the page for now)

- **Tatsuya's nature.** Very homebound. He loves his siblings and his family. He knew his mom was already ill.
- **HGO and Daichi.** Daichi was under an NDA because he was on the HGO development team, one of its creators or early patent holders (not a developer of the game itself), based on his first-gen pod schematics. This is found out much later.
- **Pod generations.** VR1 (the first VR pod, like Meta's, for convenience, business and daily life, not full dive, one or two games, though Daichi did produce games for it) has been out four to five years. VR2 has been out about two years; the Tabuchis invested in VR2, which is when games became patent-driven. New now: HGO and the third-gen pod (lighter, not a full-body rig).
- **Daichi's company and the manual.** Kazuhaya Tech (the author wrote "Haya Interply"; read as the company). His line was two vehicles (the Haya EB and the Haya Sedan) and a VR pod. The manual holds his early work and schematics and helps repair the EB and, if it is ever running, the Sedan. He had a lot invested in Tochi.
- **Tochi.** Tochi is the one place in Japan where the prime minister has no influence: family and backing mean nothing and you enter on merit. Tatsuya got in on merit with his dad's brain and could have gone in on athletics but chose neither. His parents have Tochi ties: Ohema worked there (before she got sick she was the national volleyball team's coach, went to nationals three years and won twice, and is an alumna); Chiyo is an alumna on the council with a stake; Daichi was an alumnus who worked there; the Tabuchis also have a stake. OPEN: the earlier wording that Tochi is "connected to all politics" is superseded; OPEN: "his dad finally got him in" versus merit only; OPEN: Ohema "former player" versus "national team coach"; OPEN: first-year versus "maybe second year".
- **The courtyard and Eren.** At Tochi Tatsuya talked to nobody. One day in the courtyard, on his tablet, he was watching HGO trailers, three or four months before release. He watched because Dad loved gaming, and the ads were everywhere; HGO was a mindful distraction. He did not know what his dad was working on. Eren walked by, saw it, and the friendship grew over a few months. Eren did not know Tatsuya was Daichi's son; Tatsuya did not know his dad knew Eren's dad. The kids were not involved until Eren's dad got involved and turned greedy. Later Tatsuya hates HGO, until he gets in the game himself.
- **The loan.** Daichi sold everything and his business money and royalties went to his wife's hospital bills. As a last resort he took a loan from a loan shark he did not realize was Tabuchi Enterprises, believing her last surgery would cure her; she relapsed, costing more. The Tabuchis pressed him for three to four months. Eren's dad then said that since he could not pay, he should give them the designs and patents of VR1 as collateral, because he knew VR3 was coming.
- **Why he left Tochi.** The betrayal and the mocking, not wanting to be at Tochi because of his family ("why am I here?"), "I can go my own route," needing to make money, worrying about his younger siblings and about Chiyo covering everything, knowing the Tabuchis want more than money now. Chiyo has money and wants him in school.
