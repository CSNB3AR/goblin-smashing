# Timeline, Ages & the Beta

The real-world and in-game calendar the story sits inside. Sequence and rules for the expansion itself live in `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`. Ages and school here override older figures elsewhere in the codex.

## The window

The whole story, from the Season Zero finale broadcast to the Hearthfire launch and Node Wars opening, fits inside roughly **eight to nine months**. The closed beta ran about that long in real time. "Tonight" in Chapter 1 is **Sunday, December 10th, 2045**, the Season Zero finale. Crownfall is December 25th. Hearthfire is January 1st.

## Beta structure

- **Closed beta (Season Zero):** about **5,000 players**, from roughly **870,000 pre-orders**. One server, one continent. Access was a mix of invitation and pre-order draw.
- **Open beta:** opens after the Season Zero finale. All players and businesses can enter, and access drives are discounted. Tatsuya's character is an open-beta character (see `codex/objects/hgo-access-drive.md`).
- Closed-beta characters freeze at Hearthfire. Open-beta characters are permanent (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).
- **Lumi** is the cute system guide who walks players through character creation. She glitches when Tatsuya's goblin-glitch selection fires (see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`).

## School and ages

- Tatsuya lasted about **three months** of his first year at Tochi Academy, then dropped out. He has been out a **full school year**. He returns as a **first-year**.
- **Eren and Arya are second-years.**
- Tatsuya's age is **16 to 17**. No chapter states it. The older "18" in `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md` is superseded.
- He has worked the WattsUp register for roughly a year, since dropping out (see `codex/locations/wattsup.md`).
- Taji and Momo are the twins, and **they do not know** about Dad's connection to the game (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`).

## Dad's crash and death

- When Tatsuya was a child, a **car crash** left him the scar over his right brow. It also **paralyzed Daichi**, who then went all in on VR as a way to make a world where the body was not the ceiling: the Haya pod, the Haya EB, an adapted car.
- Daichi died about **a year ago**. Closed casket, never fully explained (see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`).
- Order of events, reconciled with `bible/writing-bible.md` (Eren's entry): Tatsuya starts Tochi High School, Daichi dies within his first month (about a year ago), and the Eren betrayal over the stolen build lands in the same stretch, before the beta opens (Eren had Tabuchi second-gen pod access ahead of launch). About three months into the school year Tatsuya drops out, which falls around the closed beta's launch roughly eight to nine months ago. Exact dates are left unpinned.
- What the reader is given is suspicion, never proof. The author-side truth sits in `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`.

## Eren

- **Eren Tabuchi** is Kazuto's messenger and is **blind to the schematics**: he knows his father wants something from Tatsuya's family, not what or why.
- His guild missed the Season Zero top eight because **Arya's small guild took the last spot** (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`, Fire Festival). He hates her for it, and the rivalry with Tatsuya is layered with that (see `codex/subplots/tatsuya-pro-league-arc-and-eren-rivalry.md`).
