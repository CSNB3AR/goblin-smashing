# Lunarest Royal Court

The court of Lunarest, the one continent playable during Season Zero. This is the political backdrop of the closed beta, Shiv's history, and the reason goblins are the state's enemy. See `codex/characters/shiv.md` for Shiv's own account and `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md` for how Season Zero was run.

## The royal house

Royal children carry the **queen's family name**, not the king's.

- **King Aldemar Vaereth.** About **97 years old**, kept alive by elixirs that are failing. Rules Lunarest. Doubts that Prince Dorrin is his own son.
- **Queen Maravel Solenne.** The official queen and mother of Ilyan and Maldren.
- **Isaveil Thandrel.** The first queen. Killed about **45 years ago**. She loved Shiv. She is unlisted in the royal rolls, and the Astralum Archive keeps the truth (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`).

## The princes

- **Prince Dorrin.** No surname, which is itself hidden. Titled "First Shield of the Crown." About 45 years old, born of the first queen's line. Brute-force fighter in heavy armor, currently injured. A likely ally for Tatsuya's side of the story. The king doubts he is his son.
- **Prince Ilyan Solenne**, the **Silver Prince.** About 25. An agile mage, apprentice to High Magister Corvith Ostrane. Lives in the royal city.
- **Prince Maldren Solenne**, the **Gilded Prince.** About 19 to 20. Cunning, cruel, the most powerful of the three. Wants a new order.

During Season Zero the three princes each raise an army. Twelve guilds qualify for the finale, four attached to each prince's army, and city lords hire guilds as mercenaries.

## High Magister Corvith Ostrane

A **High Elf** (a rare Elf variant, see `codex/races/elf.md`) banished from his own domain in the Azure Expanse and made a royal court mage in Lunarest. Papal in authority, he speaks to the king directly. He is the one who cursed Shiv. He makes the king's elixirs from material he brought out of Azure, and **that material is running low**, which is the real reason the king is declining.

## The king's fall and the funeral

Corvith's elixirs fail as his Azure material runs out, and King Aldemar's decline is the reason the Season One opener is called **Crownfall** (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`). He dies around the start of Season One.

- **The funeral** is a royal event. A truce holds in the royal lands, and no battling is allowed there while it lasts. Length and scope (Lunarest only or the whole map) are **open**.
- **After the funeral** the princes Dorrin, Ilyan and Maldren move against each other. The map starts moving and Node Wars open.
- **Timing:** the Crownfall Siege on December 25th runs in a neutral zone outside royal jurisdiction, so it is not stopped. The Thrash Clan's return to Myrrhwood falls near the same time.

## Shiv, additions to the Chapter 7 account

Existing facts stay: Shivaris Jutsuin, a noble of the court, loved the Queen, was "corrected" into a goblin hybrid, unified the clans. Added:

- The queen he loved was **Isaveil**, and she loved him back.
- The king **killed her** and told the realm she died ill.
- To bury it, the court told the world that Shiv was a **hobgoblin shaman who cursed her**, and made goblins the state's enemy.
- This all happened about **45 years ago, not a century**. Any "over a century" line is wrong (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`).

## Racial standing in Lunarest

- **Elves** receive special treatment under an elven law.
- **Beastfolk, goblins and dwarves** carry bad infamy.
- Other continents differ (see `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`).
- **Myrrhwood** is the neutral land of the Beastfolk and goblins, split into two zones. Unifying it is the big arc.
- PK_Wraith's group and Eren's group stay in Lunarest, where a faction hating Shiv grows.
- NPCs never express naive surprise about their own race's standing (see `style-guide/craft-rules.md`).

## The Fire Festival and "The Second Dawn"

The Fire Festival is a guild event during Season Zero. **Arya's small guild won it** with the quest **"The Second Dawn"**: it saved Prince Dorrin from a setup. A player guild had been running a quest with Prince Maldren to remove him, and Arya's guild used intel from other players to stop it. The win took the **eighth seed** in the championship. Arya trades in information, and some players call her a traitor for leaking one guild's plan to an NPC prince. Infighting, the 72-hour death locks and the departure of Sana and Nao then sank her guild in the finale week (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).

## Restricted zones

After Season Zero the main story is frozen except for hidden quests, and the princes are held off-screen in **restricted zones**. Yuna Yunaile has restricted-area access but remains in Lunarest, and no player holds a sigil (see `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md`). Restricted zones are one more reason ordinary players cannot walk up to the court.

- **Access to power in Lunarest (author-stated).** A guild is not given access to nobles or the kingdom's NPCs just because it is a guild. Players have to get comfortable with the nobles first. Once they have power they can keep following their prince or choose their own path.

## Faction map and why Thornwatch matters (author-stated)

- The Kingdom of Lunarest is one supercontinent, the only continent on the map for now, with **12 provinces**. All three princes are still under the king's banner, but tension has built and there have been skirmishes.
- **Prince 2 (Ilyan)** runs the king's army while the king is sick, under his father and the court mage (Corvith). He is the king's faction, the true court. About 4 provinces. OPEN: his count is inferred from the author's "the other two princes have 4".
- **Prince 3 (Maldren)** has done his own thing. 4 provinces.
- **Prince 1 (Dorrin)** is the outcast. He set himself up far from the capital cities and holds 3 provinces. OPEN: 3 + 4 + 4 = 11 of 12; the twelfth province (perhaps the royal city) is not assigned.
- The power struggle is roughly equal. Until now the conflict has been espionage, missions and seasonal points. **Thornwatch is the first major skirmish out in the open**: two guilds fighting for two different princes over a city. Thornwatch is a border fortress at the edge of the kingdom and the gate to a new port city. Early in the year trailers teased a potential expansion with five continents, so players read the border as important. Yuna only knows the trailers; she does not know what the expansion is.
