# Tournament Ecosystem & Guilds

Beyond the World Championship and Winter Bash (`codex/lore/competitive-pvp-and-esports-structure.md`) and the 1v1 Open Ladder:

- **GvG (Guild vs. Guild) Wars**, seasonal, guild-scale fights for city/territory control, winning guilds can tax and set rules for their zones.
- **World Boss Raids / Server Firsts**, PvE guilds race to clear new content, first clear earns a cash prize, exclusive loot, and a permanent mark on server history.
- **Streamer/Influencer events**, sponsored "all-star" or meme-build exhibition weeks, PR-driven.
- **National/City Championships**, country- or city-branded teams backed by real sponsors and governments, think an eWorld-Cup-meets-metaverse-Olympics framing, top players can be drafted, traded, or signed to multi-year contracts.

## Guilds (background roster, for future-chapter use)

Guilds sit below pro teams on the prestige ladder but drive most of the game's actual economy and world content (GvG territory control, World Boss Raids). A guild leader doesn't need pro standing to matter in-world.

| Guild | Identity |
|---|---|
| **Shadowspire** | Rogue/assassin, PvP-focused, controls several open-world PK hotspots. |
| **Arcane Sanctuary** | Mage/cleric, world-boss specialists, holds several server-first raid clears. |
| **Warlord's Court** | Heavy armor, siege focus, the guild most other GvG factions organize against. |
| **Nova Market** | Merchant/crafter guild, controls a meaningful share of the player-driven trade economy rather than fighting for it. |
| **Wyvern Pact** | Mount specialists, controls most of the map's fast-travel and aerial-mount trade. |
| **Crimson Static** | Arya's guild, all-girls, PvP-focused (see `codex/characters/arya.md`). Small now after a roster split cost her most of the team, but still holds a World Championship berth this cycle through a guild-event win rather than competitive qualifying, she's an established guild leader in her own right even before she has a scene on the page. |
