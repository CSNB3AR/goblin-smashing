# Battle Items & Battlegear

Original to HGO, described functionally only, not modeled on or naming any outside reference. Battle items sit alongside a player's base class kit and equipped gear, consistent with the deck-building pillar (`story/premise.md`), a limited number of equippable slots keeps loadouts meaningful rather than a player stacking every effect they own at once.

## Category structure

- **Common/generic battle items** — background texture, the kind of gear competitive players are shown carrying without it being a plot point. Rare-or-higher versions of these are what Tatsuya notices on the auction house in Chapter 5 and reasons he can't afford yet. Worked examples, all genuinely common-tier, nothing build-defining:
  - **Momentum Charm** — a small stat trinket, brief Speed bump on landing a hit, the kind of thing half the ranked ladder wears without thinking about it.
  - **Blink Chalk** — a thrown consumable, marks a short-range spot a player can reposition to once, a positioning tool, not a teleport-in-combat escape.
  - **Second Wind Draught** — a minor SP-refill consumable, the standard answer to the resource-drain pacing already locked in `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`.
  - **Nameplate Ward** — a cheap defensive trinket, shaves a small percentage off the first hit taken after combat starts, competitive players run it as a baseline habit more than a real edge.
  - **Scout's Whistle** — a utility item, pings visible allies within a short radius on the mini-map, the kind of low-stakes coordination tool that shows up in every ranked match without being a story beat.
- **Signature battle items** — rarer, build-defining gear tied to a specific playstyle rather than generic stats. Tatsuya's pieces below are this tier.

## Tatsuya's signature battle items (forward canon, not acquired yet)

- **A doppelganger ring.** Creates a real combat-duplicate of Tatsuya for a limited window, not an illusion or a scouting decoy, actual double-damage-style output in a fight. This is the future combat-duplicate ability already flagged in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, now given a concrete form: a battle item he picks up or earns later, unrelated to Shiv's blessing, unrelated to Shaman's Veil, unrelated to the necklace entirely. No summoning involved, the duplicate is him, not a conjured creature.
- **A steampunk sidearm.** Held two-handed, fed by tubes running to a small tank on his back, not a conventional gun. Functions as an ammo-type switch rather than two separate weapons: loaded with Goblin Gas, it fires a caustic gas shot; loaded with Flare, it fires a fire shot that amplifies his existing Flare attack rather than replacing it. Requires him to wear a mask while using it, he's not immune to his own gas and never has been (see Goblin Gas's existing self-damage rule, `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`). A tool-based extension of his existing kit, not a new magic source, fits the steampunk-gadget visual identity already locked for his archetype.
- **A back-mounted mobility rig.** Steampunk thruster pack, short controlled bursts rather than sustained flight, real mobility/repositioning tool consistent with his Speed-leaning build (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`). Fuel/charge-limited, not a permanent flight solution, a tool for disengaging or closing a gap fast, not a way to skip positioning entirely. Fits the same visual identity as the sidearm, tubes and tank included, reads as one coherent gear set rather than three unrelated gadgets.

None of the three items are acquired on the page yet. This file exists so future chapters introducing any of them stay consistent with this design.
