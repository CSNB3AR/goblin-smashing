# Battle Items & Battlegear

Original to HGO, described functionally only, not modeled on or naming any outside reference. Battle items sit alongside a player's base class kit and equipped gear, consistent with the deck-building pillar (`story/premise.md`), a limited number of equippable slots keeps loadouts meaningful rather than a player stacking every effect they own at once.

## Category structure

- **Common/generic battle items** — background texture, the kind of gear competitive players are shown carrying without it being a plot point: stat-buff trinkets, minor utility consumables, positioning tools. Rare-or-higher versions of these are what Tatsuya notices on the auction house in Chapter 5 and reasons he can't afford yet.
- **Signature battle items** — rarer, build-defining gear tied to a specific playstyle rather than generic stats. Tatsuya's two pieces below are this tier.

## Tatsuya's signature battle items (forward canon, not acquired yet)

- **A doppelganger ring.** Creates a real combat-duplicate of Tatsuya for a limited window, not an illusion or a scouting decoy, actual double-damage-style output in a fight. This is the future combat-duplicate ability already flagged in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, now given a concrete form: a battle item he picks up or earns later, unrelated to Shiv's blessing, unrelated to Shaman's Veil, unrelated to the necklace entirely. No summoning involved, the duplicate is him, not a conjured creature.
- **A steampunk sidearm.** Held two-handed, fed by tubes running to a small tank on his back, not a conventional gun. Functions as an ammo-type switch rather than two separate weapons: loaded with Goblin Gas, it fires a caustic gas shot; loaded with Flare, it fires a fire shot that amplifies his existing Flare attack rather than replacing it. Requires him to wear a mask while using it, he's not immune to his own gas and never has been (see Goblin Gas's existing self-damage rule, `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`). A tool-based extension of his existing kit, not a new magic source, fits the steampunk-gadget visual identity already locked for his archetype.

Neither item is acquired on the page yet. This file exists so future chapters introducing either one stay consistent with this design.
