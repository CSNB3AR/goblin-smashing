# Battle Items & Battlegear

Original to HGO, described functionally only, not modeled on or naming any outside reference. Battle items sit alongside a player's base class kit and equipped gear, consistent with the deck-building pillar (`story/premise.md`), a limited number of equippable slots keeps loadouts meaningful rather than a player stacking every effect they own at once.

**Every item's activation ties back to the existing SP/MP split (`codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`), no exceptions.** An item is either a tool with a Physical Arts identity (`codex/combat/physical-arts-overview.md`, Power/Speed-driven, draws SP) or a genuine magic effect (draws MP, scales with Wisdom, ties to the tier and school systems, `codex/magic/overview.md`). An item can also be a tool with a magical augment layered on top, in which case it still draws SP as its base cost but drains it faster, per the already-locked rule that a magical augment on a physical/item-based action accelerates SP drain rather than pulling from MP. Nothing gets a cost invented from scratch, it has to map onto one of these three cases.

## Category structure

- **Common/generic battle items** — background texture, the kind of gear competitive players are shown carrying without it being a plot point. Rare-or-higher versions of these are what Tatsuya notices on the auction house in Chapter 5 and reasons he can't afford yet. Worked examples, all genuinely common-tier, nothing build-defining, each tagged with what it actually draws on:
  - **Momentum Charm** — a small stat trinket, brief Speed bump on landing a hit. Passive, no activation cost, a plain Physical Arts-adjacent stat item, nothing to drain.
  - **Blink Chalk** — a thrown consumable, marks a short-range spot a player can reposition to once, a positioning tool, not a teleport-in-combat escape. Draws a small flat SP cost on use, pure item-based Physical Arts action, no magic involved.
  - **Second Wind Draught** — a minor SP-refill consumable, the standard answer to the resource-drain pacing already locked in `codex/lore/hp-sp-mp-resource-pools.md`. Restores SP directly rather than drawing from either pool itself.
  - **Nameplate Ward** — a cheap defensive trinket, shaves a small percentage off the first hit taken after combat starts. Passive, no activation cost, same category as the Momentum Charm.
  - **Scout's Whistle** — a utility item, pings visible allies within a short radius on the mini-map. Draws a small flat SP cost on use, pure item-based Physical Arts action, no magic involved.
- **Signature battle items** — rarer, build-defining gear tied to a specific playstyle rather than generic stats. Tatsuya's pieces below are this tier.

## Tatsuya's signature battle items (forward canon, not acquired yet)

- **A doppelganger ring.** Creates a real combat-duplicate of Tatsuya for a limited window, not an illusion or a scouting decoy, actual double-damage-style output in a fight. This is the future combat-duplicate ability already flagged in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, now given a concrete form: a battle item he picks up or earns later, unrelated to Shiv's blessing, unrelated to Shaman's Veil, unrelated to the necklace entirely, no MP involved anywhere. **Physical Arts identity, SP-based:** the ring itself carries a magical augment (an enchanted item creating a real physical duplicate, not a cast spell), so per the established rule it draws from SP rather than MP, just at an accelerated drain rate because of that augment, a real cost that scales with how long the duplicate stays active. No summoning involved, the duplicate is him, not a conjured creature.
- **A steampunk sidearm.** Held two-handed, fed by tubes running to a small tank on his back, not a conventional gun. Functions as an ammo-type switch rather than two separate weapons: loaded with Goblin Gas, it fires a caustic gas shot; loaded with Flare, it fires a fire shot that amplifies his existing Flare attack rather than replacing it. Requires him to wear a mask while using it, he's not immune to his own gas and never has been (see Goblin Gas's existing self-damage rule, `codex/lore/preserved-misc-mechanics.md`). **Physical Arts identity, pure SP:** Goblin Gas and Goblin Flare are both already-established tool/physical abilities, not spells, so the sidearm draws SP at the same baseline rate its unaided abilities already do, no magical augment layered on, no MP anywhere in the chain. A tool-based extension of his existing kit, not a new magic source, fits the steampunk-gadget visual identity already locked for his archetype.
- **A back-mounted mobility rig.** Steampunk thruster pack, short controlled bursts rather than sustained flight, real mobility/repositioning tool consistent with his Speed-leaning build (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`). Fuel/charge-limited, not a permanent flight solution, a tool for disengaging or closing a gap fast, not a way to skip positioning entirely. **Physical Arts identity, pure SP:** a mechanical fuel/charge system, not magic in any way, straightforward SP draw per burst, no augment, no MP. Fits the same visual identity as the sidearm, tubes and tank included, reads as one coherent gear set rather than three unrelated gadgets.

None of the three items are acquired on the page yet. This file exists so future chapters introducing any of them stay consistent with this design.
