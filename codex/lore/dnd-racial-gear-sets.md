# Racial Gear Sets: a D&D-style baseline kit for every playable race

**Status:** Research and proposal file, not canon until the author accepts it. It edits no existing file. For each of the eight playable races (human, elf, dwarf, orc, beastfolk, goblin, frostborn, drakekin) it gives a starting kit, a typical kit by role (weapon, armor, shield, tools, camp gear), the materials and craft traditions the race would use from `codex/materials/`, racial fit rules (size, tail, horns, wings, cold and heat gear), and what the race's NPC guards, scouts, soldiers and civilians wear. Every item stays inside our system: Way-appropriate weapons, the eleven gear slots, the Common to World ladder, the six professions, and the Gear Tier ladder (GT1 to GT8).

**Tags.** SRC (outside source), CANON (in repo), PAGE (in a chapter), PROPOSAL (invented). Core chart: `codex/lore/dnd-gear-baseline.md` section 0.

**Sibling files.** `codex/lore/dnd-gear-baseline.md` (rules, weapons per Way, armor, shields, rarity), `codex/materials/dnd-gear-material-map.md` (materials, GT ladder, professions), `codex/lore/goblin-gear-and-camp-realism.md` (the full goblin and camp treatment), `codex/lore/dnd-camp-gear-tools-and-mounts.md`, `codex/lore/dnd-class-dress-and-casting-foci.md`.

## 1. Shared rules

### 1.1 Starting kit, for everyone

[CANON/PAGE] A new character starts in spawn clothes with **no armor in any slot** and a **Newcomer Mystery Box** (a weapon, a skill line, an item or a passive, with big pulls rare) [PAGE Ch4]. Tatsuya's spawn kit is a patched shirt, ragged trousers, a rope belt and bare feet. In Season Zero every race starts in Lunarest [CANON `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`], and each race has a neutral home zone where you do not drop items on death. [PROPOSAL] So the "starting kit" for every race is **Spawn Rags in the race's own cut** plus the Mystery Box. A drakekin is an achievement race, not picked at creation, so its starting kit is a milestone kit (OPEN).

### 1.2 Fit rules

[SRC] D&D has a variant rule that gear sized for the wrong body must be tailored, costing 10 to 40 percent of the item's price, and a **Heavy** weapon property that gives Small creatures disadvantage (https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment and https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/weapons). In the SRD, dwarves and elves are Medium and halflings and gnomes are Small (https://api.open5e.com/v1/races/). HGO fit rules [PROPOSAL]:

1. **Frame classes.** **Small** (a goblin, about four feet [PAGE Ch4]), **Standard** (human, elf, hobgoblin, drakekin), **Broad** (orc, frostborn, and an OPEN reading for the dwarf's stout build), plus **Ports**.
2. **Ports.** Tail slits, ear and horn ports, claw gaps and wing slits are part of fit. A helm without horn ports cannot be worn. A chest piece without a tail slit chafes (a small penalty). Ports are added at a bench.
3. **Wrong frame.** A piece for another frame can be **refit** for 10 to 40 percent of its price, or worn with a small penalty to Evasion or SP Regen.
4. **Adjustable gear** (laces, toggles, buckles) tolerates one frame step. That is why Tatsuya's Common Goblin Leather Chest fits a goblin who has just grown ("I pulled the straps tight myself" [PAGE Ch10]).
5. **Heavy weapons** cost Small frames Edge and reach. A goblin who evolves to a hobgoblin changes frame class and can wield what a goblin cannot. That is why Sevish carries a two-handed axe and the camp carries clubs.
6. **Resistance and gear stack.** A race's resistance is a free slot. Its weakness is a hole the kit must cover (the Debuffs framework, [CANON] `codex/crafting/debuffs-and-resistances.md`).

## 2. Human (Lunarest)

The baseline race. "The material base every other region's specialties get compared against" [CANON overview]. Standard frame, no ports, no climate trouble.

**Materials and craft tradition.** Ironvein Ore (the default starting smithing metal), Hearthroot, Common Spirit Dust, Beast Leather [CANON overview and organic]. [PROPOSAL] Lunarest city smith and tanner guilds, garrison armorers, guild-stamped steel, royal-colour livery. Hallmarked Steel and Mono Steel arrive from dwarven contacts. Humans run the broadest GT1 to GT5 production base, and the least exotic.

| Role | Weapon (Way) | Armor by slot | Shield | Tools | Camp gear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting** | whatever the box gives | plain wool tunic, rope belt, soft shoes | none | none | none |

| **Crafter or civilian** | hammer, knife, cleaver | wool, leather apron, work boots | none | trade tools | market stall cart |

## 3. Elf (Azure Expanse)

Light, long and fine. Power 3 means medium and heavy kit cost real points, so an elf builds light. Weak to Earth, resists Storm.

**Materials and craft tradition.** Skyglass Ore ("a light, naturally Wisdom-friendly metal, favored for staves"), Moonpetal, Prism Dust (the top common enchanting reagent) [CANON overview]. [PROPOSAL] Elven craft is light and fine: Skyglass fittings on leather, Moonpetal-dyed linen, Prism-seated insignias. Elves get "special treatment" under Lunarest's elven law [CANON `codex/races/elf.md`], so an elven guard in Lunarest looks more official than a goblin or beastfolk guard.

**Fit.** Standard frame, slim. Long ears need helm ports, long hair needs hoods that do not bind. [SRC] D&D elves train with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow and longbow (https://api.open5e.com/v1/races/elf/), which is a good default for an HGO elf: Blade and Bow.

| Role | Weapon (Way) | Armor by slot | Shield | Tools | Camp gear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting** | box | linen shift, soft shoes | none | none | none |
| **Duelist** | Needle-blade or Sabre (Blade) | Riveted Leather (needs no Power floor), light cap, leather bracers | buckler | whetstone | light tent, bedroll |
| **Ranger** | Warbow (Bow), a few javelins | Tanned Jerkin or Riveted Leather, hooded cloak, quiet boots | none | climber's kit, quiver socket | cloak tent |
| **Caster** (the elf default) | **Skyglass-capped staff** or wand and orb, Book-matched focus | Enchanted Thread robe (soft goods), cap, sash with sockets | none | rune chalk, ink, scroll case | tent with lamp, bedroll, tea kit (Moonpetal Infusion [CANON food file]) |
| **Crafter or civilian** | knife | fine linen, apron | none | scribe's and weaver's tools | none |

## 4. Dwarf (Valefrost)

The heavy-gear race, and the crafters. Locked out of magic, with "a strong crafting-flavored racial edge" [CANON `codex/races/dwarf.md`]. Weak to Storm, resists Earth.

**Materials and craft tradition.** Rimeiron, Glacial Marrow, monocrystals and **Mono Steel**, which dwarven houses refine and which "takes a dwarven contact or standing with a house" [CANON overview and `codex/materials/monocrystals-and-mono-steel.md`]. **Hallmarked Steel Bar**, "stamped with its mark" [CANON Book One file]. [PROPOSAL] House marks on every piece, riveted heavy kit, wooden handles and leather linings. A dwarf smith builds the best base but "cannot seat a rune" (the locked magic gate), so dwarven gear is stout and plain and wants a goblin or elf enchanter to finish it [CANON Book One file].

**Fit.** Stout frame (broad shoulders, short legs). Beards need helm shaping. [SRC] D&D dwarves train with the battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer and warhammer, and with smith's, brewer's or mason's tools, and resist poison (https://api.open5e.com/v1/races/). Good HGO defaults: Axe Way and Smithing tools. **Storm weakness gear** [PROPOSAL]: leather-lined and wood-hafted pieces, a grounded undercoat, and a "storm-ward" insulating charm. Bare iron in a storm is the bad case.

| Role | Weapon (Way) | Armor by slot | Shield | Tools | Camp gear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting** | box | leather apron over wool, work boots | none | none | none |
| **Frontline** | War axe or Hammer (Axe), Fighting axe | Mail Hauberk or **Splint Harness** (Heavy, Power floor met), iron great helm, steel gauntlets, sabatons | round board or **Tower board** | smith's tools, whetstone | forge kit, canvas tent |
| **Ranged or wall** | Lever or Siege crossbow (Bow, mechanical) | Mail Shirt, iron cap | **pavise** | repair kit | tent, bedroll |
| **Support** (no magic) | hammer, mace | Cuirass or Mail Hauberk | round board | **engineer's tinker kit, smith's tools** | field forge |
| **Crafter** | hammer, tongs | thick leather apron, gloves | none | smith's, mason's, brewer's | forge, workbench |

## 5. Orc (Sunmarch)

Power 9, the strongest base line. Weak to Frost, resists Poison, restricted from magic.

**Materials and craft tradition.** **Sunforged Iron** ("high-heat-tolerant ore, favored for Book of Embers gear and any weapon meant to carry a burn enchant"), Redroot Pepper, Cracked Bloodstone [CANON overview]. The Ironback Boar is a Sunmarch beast [CANON class-2]. [PROPOSAL] Orcs forge heavy, simple, hot-tempered gear: big hides, boarhide, tusk trim, spiked pauldrons, fire-hardened edges. Their gear stays loud and Noisy.

**Fit.** Broad frame, tusks need helm ports, long arms need long sleeves and bracers. **Frost weakness gear** [PROPOSAL]: fur linings, and **Rimeiron fittings imported from Valefrost** (which "carry an innate minor Frost resistance" [CANON overview]). A Sunmarch kit with a Rimeiron belt buckle is a natural trade-company product.

| Role | Weapon (Way) | Armor by slot | Shield | Tools | Camp gear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting** | box | hide wraps, tusk-trim belt | none | none | none |
| **Frontline** | War axe, Sledge-maul, Fighting axe (Axe) | Thickhide, Scale Coat or Ring Hauberk, spiked pauldron (chest piece), horned or tusk-ported helm | light round board or none (Unbroken carries a stagger) | whetstone, hammer | hide tent, fire kit |
| **Ranger or scout** | javelins (Spear, thrown), horn shortbow | Thickhide, fur cloak | none | rope, flint | hide lean-to |
| **Caster** (restricted) | Sunforged-capped rod or staff (Embers) | hide robe, scale trim | none | rune chalk | tent |
| **Crafter or cook** | hammer, cleaver | leather apron | none | smith's tools, Redroot spices | forge, cookpot |

## 6. Beastfolk (Myrrhwood)

Power 8 and Speed 8, the brawler race. **Locked out of magic**, and Physical Arts "is the race's real, full combat identity" [CANON `codex/races/beastfolk.md`]. Enrage: below 40 percent HP a damage multiplier stacks up to x12 while the Beastfolk loses HP and defenses, so heavy armor wastes the build and "wards, heals and enchantments matter" (Pac_Pac, `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`). Resists Frost, weak to Fire. Beastfolk carry bad infamy in Lunarest [CANON].

**Materials and craft tradition.** Myrrhwood Hide ("tougher and poison-resistant, a regional specialty"), Bonewood, Clanmark Ore [CANON overview and organic]. [PROPOSAL] Bone-and-hide harnesses, leather straps over bare fur, fist-guards, claw-capped gauntlets, ward charms in accessory slots to cover the Enrage window. The organised Beast Kingdom is the "walled half" of Myrrhwood, a city ringed by great walls and set in desert [CANON `codex/locations/eryndal-world-overview.md`], so its civilians and soldiers dress for dry heat.

**Fit and ports.** The codex gives no physical description of beastfolk beyond stats, so tails, ears, claws and possibly digitigrade legs are **[PROPOSAL, OPEN]**. Fit rules: **tail port** on belt, chest and legs, **ear ports** on helms, **claw gaps** in gauntlets, **paw-fit boots** that do not bind. Armor made for a Standard frame needs ports added at a bench or it chafes.

**Climate and weakness.** Fire weakness [PROPOSAL]: wet-hide aprons, fire-ward charms, no fur trim near flame. Frost resistance already covers the cold. Desert heat kit for the Beast Kingdom: light linen over leather straps.

| Role | Weapon (Way) | Armor by slot | Shield | Tools | Camp gear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting** | box | patchwork wraps, a strap harness | none | none | none |
| **Brawler or tank** (Pac_Pac) | **Hand wraps, Fist-guards, Great cudgel** (Fist), or spear | Thickhide or Scale Coat with tail port, bone guards, ward charm accessories | buckler at most | none | hide bedroll, fire kit |
| **Scout** | Dart-spear, Herder's sling | light harness, soft paw boots, cloak | none | rope, bone knife | lean-to |
| **Support** (no magic) | mace, charm kit | Thickhide, ward charms, healer's kit | round board | healer's kit, **Charged Ward** tokens [CANON gadget line] | tent |
| **Crafter** | bone knife | apron, leather straps | none | carver's knives, leatherworker's kit | bench |

**Beastfolk NPCs (the Beast Kingdom).** Civilians in light linen and strap harnesses. **Guards**: long spear, round board, Scale Coat or Thickhide with ear-ported caps. **Scouts**: light, fast, javelins and slings. **Soldiers**: spear and board lines, layered like their walls. All [PROPOSAL], the codex has no Beast Kingdom NPC kit yet.

## 7. Goblin (Myrrhwood, and the Thrash clan in the Lunarest outskirts)

The full treatment is in `codex/lore/goblin-gear-and-camp-realism.md`. In brief.

Goblins have **no resistances**, "deliberately flat", so gear and consumables are the only lever [CANON `codex/races/goblin.md`, `codex/crafting/debuffs-and-resistances.md`]. They have Scavenger's Eye (loot worth taking is outlined) and Nimble Hands (an arcane-tech and gadget proficiency). Goblin clans in Myrrhwood fractured and are barred from villages, except Thrash [CANON `codex/lore/goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`].

**Materials and craft tradition.** Beast Leather, Sturdy Beast Bone, Clanmark Ore ("cheap to acquire, quietly useful"), Bonewood, Ashroot Fungus, scrap iron and canvas [CANON]. Goblins default into Bonecrafting/Leatherworking and the gadget branch of Alchemy [CANON `codex/crafting/professions.md`]. **Fit:** Small frame before evolution, Standard as a hobgoblin. Heavy weapons penalize the Small frame.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Crafter or cook** | hammer, ladle | apron | pot-lid buckler | Clan Forge, Campfire Kit | forge |

**Goblin NPCs.** Civilians do not exist as such, the clan is the warband and the camp. **Guards and defenders**: club and camp board, vest, bare or booted feet. **Scouts**: dark wrap, rope, sling. **Soldiers**: hobgoblin spearmen in Thickhide with a round board. **Gas and toxin gear:** Barrow Bear Hide and Clearquartz mask lenses answer the zero resistance and the user-not-immune rule on Goblin Gas [CANON Book One file].

## 8. Frostborn (Valefrost)

Courage 8, Energy 7. **Cold Endurance** gives full immunity to environmental cold and exposure [CANON `codex/races/frostborn.md`]. Resists Frost, weak to Fire, partial magic with an elemental and rune-utility flavor.

**Materials and craft tradition.** Rimeiron (frost-veined ore with innate Frost resistance), Glacial Marrow, Frostbloom, monocrystals and Mono Steel shared with the dwarves [CANON overview]. [PROPOSAL] Fur-lined hide, bone and horn, Rimeiron fittings, white and grey dyed hides. Their cold kit is for others: a Frostborn camp lends warm gear to allies, since the race itself is immune to the cold.

**Fit.** The codex gives no height, so I propose a **Broad frame**, tall. OPEN. Horns and tusks, if any, are not canon, so I add no ports. A fur cloak is common and burns fast, so it is a Fire-weak design problem.

**Weakness gear (Fire)** [PROPOSAL]: **Sunforged Iron fittings** imported from Sunmarch ("Sunforged Iron gear runs the same way for Fire" [CANON debuffs file]), wet-hide linings, and no loose fur near flame.

| **Scout or ranger** | Warbow, Dart-spear | white-dyed hides, snow cloak, wide-sole snow boots | none | snowshoes, rope | lean-to, sled |
| **Caster** (partial magic, rune-leaning) | rune mallet or staff, Frost Book focus (Rimeiron-capped) | hide coat, fur collar | none | rune chalk | tent |

## 9. Drakekin (achievement race, no home zone)

Strong across the board (7, 7, 7, 6, 6, 7) with **Draconic Presence**, resists **Fire**, weak to **Poison**, full magic access [CANON `codex/races/drakekin.md`]. [SRC] D&D's closest cousin, the dragonborn, has damage resistance tied to ancestry and is Medium (https://api.open5e.com/v1/races/).

**Materials and craft tradition.** No canon region. [PROPOSAL] Because Fire resistance suits Embers and Sunforged gear, a drakekin often buys from Sunmarch, and as a roaming achievement race it trades widely. Scale Coat and Half-Plate suit the aesthetic. The **Wyvern Pact** controls most of the mount and aerial-transport trade [CANON `codex/pro-scene/guilds.md`], so a Wyvern ride is the likely link, OPEN.

**Fit and ports.** The codex gives no tail, horn or wing details. **[PROPOSAL, OPEN]:** Standard frame, tail slit on belt, chest and legs, horn ports on helms, and **wing slits** only if the author gives drakekin wings (D&D dragonborn have none, so I assume none). Scale-fit gauntlets for clawed hands.

**Weakness gear (Poison)** [PROPOSAL]: Barrow Bear Hide (Toxin ward, [CANON Book One file]), Clearquartz mask lenses, antitoxin vials as a belt consumable. Heat is a bonus, not a problem: vented, light kit for hot zones. No canon weakness to cold, so no special cold kit.

## 11. Conflicts with canon and open items

Canon wins.

1. **No physical canon for beastfolk, frostborn or drakekin.** Tails, ears, claws, horns, wings, height and frame are all proposals. The author decides, and the Ports rule adapts.
2. **Dwarf frame.** Stout (my reading) versus Broad. D&D dwarves are Medium [SRC], and the codex does not say.
3. **Magic-locked races.** Dwarves and Beastfolk have no caster rows. Their "support" role is items and craft only. A dwarf smith "cannot seat a rune" [CANON Book One file], so enchanting needs a partner.
4. **Racial weapon defaults** (dwarf axes, elf blades and bows, and so on) are D&D flavor [SRC], not HGO canon.
5. **Orc mounts, Beast Kingdom NPC kit, Frostborn skis and sleds** are not in the codex. The Drevhorn is flagged optional there.
6. **Lunarest's elven law and infamy.** Elves get special treatment, while beastfolk, goblins and dwarves carry bad infamy [CANON `codex/races/elf.md`]. A goblin or beastfolk guard in a Lunarest city would not be seen, so these kits are for home zones and clan camps, and Thrash goblins are the only goblins allowed into settlements [CANON goblin culture file].
7. **Drakekin starting kit.** An achievement race has no creation-time start, so "milestone kit" is OPEN.
8. **Tatsuya's frame change.** He is about four feet before Shiv's blessing and grows after [CANON `codex/races/goblin.md`], while his chest still fits. I explain it with adjustable straps. If the author prefers, the Common Goblin line could be tagged "one step adjustable".
9. **Guard, scout, soldier and civilian dress** for every race except the Thrash goblins is invented. No canon NPC outside Thrash and the raiders is described.
