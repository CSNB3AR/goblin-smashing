# Preserved Mechanics From the Old Draft

These carry forward unchanged from `bible/old-draft-chapter-bible.md`, flagged there as worth keeping.

- **Skill combo system** — Goblin Gas + fire = Goblin Flare; toxin effects boost Ancient Boneflame Barrage's damage/radius. Reusable and mechanically satisfying, fits the deck-building pillar.
  - **Goblin Gas:** a fart-based radius attack, genuinely comedic, genuinely a radius, Tatsuya can hurt himself if he's standing in his own cloud when it matters. He doesn't know where it comes from the first time he uses it, that's part of the reveal in Chapter 3. Applies a nauseous debuff that stacks up to 3-4 times. Once he picks up the goblin gadget belt (see `codex/objects/goblin-gadget-belt.md`, materials required to unlock its abilities), he gets a real, tool-based alternative, thrown gas bombs instead of the bodily-function version.
  - **Goblin Flare:** presents as a distress/signal flare, shoot-in-the-air SOS. Its tooltip also lists incidental fire damage (3-4), a detail he notices and decides to test rather than something he walks in already knowing.
  - **The combo, corrected:** Tatsuya does not know gas and fire combine into an explosion going in. In Chapter 3 he uses gas while fleeing two pursuers, it trails behind him, they run into their own cloud chasing him (he's already put distance between himself and it by the time it matters), the nauseous stacks land and stun them, and he throws the flare at the stunned pair thinking he's just adding fire damage on top. The resulting explosion killing both is a genuine surprise to him in the moment, not a plan paying off.
- **Karma/Alignment system** — killing players outside sanctioned PvP shifts alignment and visibly flips the nameplate from white to red ("Blood Tinted"). A strong, visible consequence system for open-world PvP, and per `codex/lore/player-owned-economy-and-living-world-npcs.md`, this now extends to mistreating NPCs and a region's people too, not player-kills alone.
- **Seven-day death lock** — dying carries a real week-long penalty, not just a respawn timer. See `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md` for the open flag on how this relates to the pass-out limit.
- **Physical Arts** — a distinct skill category from spells (Drosh's Veil-Pierce Gaze, Silent Three Fang, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`), giving goblins/hobgoblins a combat identity separate from caster classes.
- **Tactical Cartographer title** — earned for drawing up a battle plan, not just fighting. Rewards the brains-over-brawn playstyle the story leads with.
- **In-game time dilation** — time moves faster inside HGO than in the real world. Flagged as needing pacing care: any quest timer stated in-game (e.g., a three-day ultimatum) needs to be checked against how it lands against Tatsuya's real-world schedule before it's used in a chapter.
- **Live streaming as an organic exposition tool** — chat reactions doing the work of flagging "this is unusual" on-screen, without narration or an announcer stating it outright. A deliberate craft technique, not just a mechanic, reuse it the same way.
