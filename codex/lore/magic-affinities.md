# Magic Affinities

Separate from the tier system (`codex/lore/magic-tier-system.md`, how *much* a caster can do) and separate from race (which governs *whether* a character has magic access at all, `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`). Affinity is *what kind* of magic a caster actually leans into: **Fire, Frost, Storm, Earth, Light, Dark, and Arcane-neutral** (no elemental lean at all, straight raw-magic generalists).

## How a caster gets one

Not a hard racial lock, no race is pre-assigned an affinity. It comes from background, temperament, and who actually trains a character, if anyone, once they pursue a school of magic seriously. A caster raised in a temple leans Light almost by osmosis. A battlefield-taught caster with no formal school often lands Arcane-neutral, generalist by necessity, nobody around to specialize them into anything else. Affinity isn't chosen from a menu at character creation, it emerges from how a character actually comes into their power.

## Tatsuya's affinity: Dark

Tatsuya's is **Dark**, tied directly to Shiv and the spirit-magic path Shaman's Veil opens (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`), not a separate choice layered on top of it. Shiv himself reads as a spirit/death-adjacent figure, an echo, not a living caster, and the magic he passes down carries that same texture. This is consistent with the archetype's already-locked warlock-adjacent flavor (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`, Shaman's Veil "leans sorcerer/warlock-flavored, not priest/cleric"), Dark affinity is the concrete name for that flavor, not a new direction.

Ancient Bone Flame Barrage itself reads Fire on the surface (it's a literal flame effect), but the *path* delivering it, and anything Tatsuya grows into past it, carries the Dark affinity underneath, spirit-touched fire rather than a pure elemental Fire caster's version of the same spell. The distinction matters for how NPCs and other players read him: a Fire-affinity caster and Tatsuya can throw visually similar effects and still register as completely different kinds of caster to anyone who actually understands magic.
