# Leveling & Progression

- Level starts at 1 (or backstory-appropriate).
- XP comes from quests, fights, exploration, and world events.
- **Stat points, confirmed formula:** on level up, a character gets **2 stat points**, spent by the player across the six Disciplines+Energy (see `codex/lore/five-disciplines-stat-system.md`), not an automatic +1 to everything. **Each stat caps at 75.** This is a deliberate scarcity mechanic, not a curve that eventually maxes out a character.
- **Level 150 is the "first major" milestone.** Reaching it is rare and genuinely hard, not a routine waypoint. At 150, a character has 300 total stat points to have spent, enough to push roughly **three of the six stats to their 75 cap**, with the rest spread thin. Nobody reaches a fully maxed stat tree, real specialization is forced by the math itself.
- **A further, much later expansion raises the ceiling toward level 300** (600 points at that rate), not reachable with any current in-story content, no one in the game has gotten anywhere near it. Not a hard design target yet, just the acknowledged theoretical ceiling.
- Stat points are narrated through action (training, quests, "the bones warmed at his throat"), never a dry allocation screen. Level-ups also occasionally grant a new Codex Entry (skill or minor item) from class/race lists or story rewards.
- **Racial/Class growth:** Goblins grow Fortune faster than other races (an extra point or two per level, or a bias in free stat points), consistent with the underdog/luck-based arc the goblin race already carries. Other races grow more balanced or lean into their own strengths. See `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md` for each race's starting base stats and magic-access restrictions.
- **Codex Entries** (skills, feats, gear) carry a rarity tier from the ladder in `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md` (Common through World), introduced organically, two to four per major arc, never as an upfront catalog dump. Scaling comes from higher tiers and better gear, not bigger numbers pasted onto the page.
