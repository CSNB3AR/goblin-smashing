# Quest Types and Categories

Eryndal sorts every quest a player can pick up into one of six categories. A quest's category shows up as the bracketed tag on its notification (e.g. `**[BREACH QUEST: DOMAIN DEFENSE]**`), and more than one category can genuinely apply to the same quest chain at once, categories describe scope and stakes, not a single mutually-exclusive bucket.

## The six categories

- **Legacy Quest** — personal, main-story progression tied to a specific character's own arc. Not shared, not hoardable by anyone else, it only exists because of who the player is and what they've already done.
- **World Quest** — large-scale, region- or world-affecting, open to the entire playerbase, actively hunted, effectively hoardable by whoever gets there first. See `codex/lore/world-quests.md` for the full mechanics and rarity payoff (World-tier gear, the top of the rarity ladder).
- **Racial Quest** — tied to a specific playable race, only that race's players can attempt it.
- **Clan Quest** — tied to a specific clan or bloodline within a race, narrower than a Racial Quest. Thrash Clan quests fall here (the clan name is **Thrash**, not "Thraz," already locked, see `codex/characters/sevish-thrash.md` and `codex/characters/wyx-thrash.md`).
- **Breach Quest** — a territory or settlement under active attack. **Domain Defense** is its main subtype: a real-time notification that a specific camp, town, or holding is being invaded right now, with a live defender count and countdown timer. Stakes texture for whoever's present, not automatically a scope change to the surrounding narrative.
- **Contract Quest** — standard NPC or quest-board jobs, day-to-day work with no larger category attached. Not yet used on the page.

## "Call of the Bones," a three-way overlap

"Call of the Bones" is locked as genuinely three categories at once, not a single tag that changed over time:

- **World Quest** — its original framing, open to the whole goblin race, why PK_Wraith and other outside players raced for it (see `codex/lore/world-quests.md`).
- **Racial Quest** — goblin-only.
- **Legacy Quest** — evolving into this once it became personally, permanently tied to Tatsuya via Shiv's blessing and the necklace bond (see `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`).

All three tags are simultaneously true. It doesn't graduate out of being a World or Racial Quest just because it also became personal.

## Chapter 8's invasion notification is a Breach Quest, not part of the Call of the Bones chain

The Bloodhurst invasion notification (`**[BREACH QUEST: DOMAIN DEFENSE]**`, `[INTRUDERS INVADING BLOODHURST]`, `[THRASH CLAN DEFENDERS: 50/50]`, a live countdown timer) is a separate category from Call of the Bones. It's real, in-fiction confirmation of the already-locked ~50-goblin warband scale (see `codex/locations/bloodhurst.md`), concrete stakes texture for the tent-ambush resolution in Chapter 9, not a World Quest scope change and not a stage of the Call of the Bones chain itself.
