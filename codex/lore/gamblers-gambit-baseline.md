# Gambler's Gambit: baseline (PROPOSAL, extend as we go)

Builds on `codex/objects/gamblers-earring.md` and the ledger entry "Tatsuya's build, Flamonta, the Gambit dice". The earring is a UNIQUE item that scales with its user.

## The rule
Gambler's Gambit rolls a d6 on loot, enchantments and buffs. A roll of 1 is a FAILED ROLL and breaks the item the roll was made on (the byproduct and the cost). A crit fail also costs power for a time, silences him or applies a similar short debuff. A 6 or a good number gives the boost.

## The d6 table (baseline)
| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Failed roll. The item the roll was made on breaks. A debuff follows (see below). |
| 2 | Weak result. The effect lands at 100 percent, no boost. |
| 3 | Normal result. 110 percent of the base effect. |
| 4 | Good result. 125 percent. |
| 5 | Strong result. 140 percent. |
| 6 | Crit. 160 percent and a bonus effect (a free extra stack, one cooldown refunded, or one rarity step up on a loot roll). |

## What breaks
The item the roll applies to: loot received (a roll on a drop turns it to scrap), an enchanted weapon or shield (it loses the enchant and drops one durability tier, repairable by a smith), a buffed ally item (the buff fails and the item is unharmed). Bound and Unique items are never destroyed by a failed roll, they lose the enchant and take a short lock instead. The earring itself never breaks.

## The crit fail debuff menu (pick one, 20 to 60 seconds)
- Power loss: minus 25 percent damage dealt.
- Silenced: cannot cast.
- Fumble: the next Physical Art fails and its stamina is spent.
- Hexed: minus 20 percent resists.

## The upgrade ladder (every 10 levels until 80)
| Character level | Dice tier | Modifier | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plain die | none | The loot gamble only. |
| 10 | Worn die | +1 | A second use: a damage amplifier on the next ability, or the party's, within 15 seconds. |
| 20 | Bronze die | +2 | Rolls of 2 no longer fail to boost. |
| 30 | Silver die | +3 | Failed rolls break only common gear. |
| 40 | Gilt die | +4 | Crit fail debuff halved. |
| 50 | Brass-and-bone die | +5 | A 6 also grants a short shield. |
| 60 | Bone die | +6 | Reroll one failed roll per day. |
| 70 | Gold die | +6 | The boost table moves up one step. |
| 80 | Gambler's die | +6 | Cap: one chosen bonus effect on every 6. |

The modifier adds to the roll before the table is read (a 1 still fails on the natural die). The next real upgrade, at level 10, is the power boost the author asked for.
