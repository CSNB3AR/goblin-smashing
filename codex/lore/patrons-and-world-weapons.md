# Patrons and World Weapons

Forward canon. The patron system and the seven world weapons are set up here so chapters and other docs stay consistent. Final names and details are open (see Open).

## Patrons

- There are **five patrons**. At the start of the game a player **chooses a patron to follow**, the way a race has a home zone. Dwarves follow a patron, goblins follow a patron, and so on.
- **Seranis** is a goddess (working name, "something more mystical than the mist god") who **blesses demi-humans**. She is the **goblins' patron**. She is not only a patron of one race: her blessing reaches demi-humans as a group.
- The other four patrons stay unnamed for now.
- Where it shows up: the character-creation walkthrough (Lumi, see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`) and later the character sheet.

## The seven world weapons

- **Seven weapons** in the world are blessed by patrons. Five patrons, seven weapons, so a patron can hold more than one or none. **Seranis holds one.**
- They are World-tier items (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`) and are globally significant gear, tied to World Quests (see `codex/lore/world-quests.md`).

## The Staff of the Mist God (Seranis)

A piece of **Shiv's set** (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). Final enchanted name open ("Staff of the Mist God" is the working title).

- **Three forms**, chosen by the wielder: a **sword**, a **staff**, and a **two-handed energy rifle / gadget gun-blade** (an energy-cannon look, like the energy-cannon weapon in Albion Online; the gunner / gun-blade class idea from King's Avatar). Pick one form at a time.
- **Tatsuya picks the gun-blade form.** It lets him shoot and move while still conjuring his abilities through the staff, which suits his Arcane Bomber build (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`).
- **Resurrection.** The blessing of Seranis lets the wielder **raise a fallen player once every seven days**. That matters against the 72-hour death lock, since a player on lock can be brought back.
- **Enchanting.** It lets him **stack enchants faster at a lower MP cost**, and it is a strong tool for his magic.
- **Buffs and minuses.** It has drawbacks as well as benefits. The biggest benefit is the resurrection.
- **Distinct from the Shivbone Staff.** The Shivbone Staff (Legendary) dropped from Spellweaver_Lyn in Chapter 10 and given to Arya is a different item.
