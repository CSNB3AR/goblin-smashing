# Patrons and World Weapons

Forward canon. The patron system and the seven world weapons are set up here so chapters and other docs stay consistent. Final names and details are open (see Open).

## Patrons

- There are **five patrons**. At the start of the game a player **chooses a patron to follow**, the way a race has a home zone. Dwarves follow a patron, goblins follow a patron, and so on.
- **Seranis**, **Goddess of Life and Resurrection**, **blesses demi-humans**. She is the **goblins' patron**. She is not only a patron of one race: her blessing reaches demi-humans as a group. Life and resurrection is her domain, which is why her blessed staff can raise a fallen player (see below). Name and domain adapted from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles (see Provenance).
- The other four patrons stay unnamed for now. The source material names none of them.
- Where it shows up: the character-creation walkthrough (Lumi, see `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`) and later the character sheet.

## The Church of Seranis

An NPC faith that can exist in Eryndal, a faction of the living world and not a player guild. Light touch, available when a scene needs it.

- Led by a **Pope** of Seranis. The church ranks **beside** the nobility, not under it, so a Pope answers to the goddess before any king or prince. That independence makes the church a political pressure point rather than a vassal.
- Its doctrine is built on **life and resurrection**: healing, warding and bringing the fallen back. Its healers' arts (light guidance, targeted healing and cleansing, protective wards) are the kind of healer-class abilities the world already allows, and they are doctrine-bound, so they cost the caster something each time and can fail if the caster is weak, interrupted or compromised.
- Its tie to relics: a church holds relics of its patron and can refuse or grant a bearer's legitimacy, which matters when a ruler wants divine endorsement (compare the royal court, `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`).
- Goblins and other demi-humans may treat Seranis as a patron without belonging to the church. The church and the patron are linked, not the same.

## The five relics and the seven world weapons

- **Five divine relics, each tied to a patron deity** (the source calls them the Five Weapons of Theos). The idea carries over: a relic is a weapon or token blessed by a patron, bonded to a single bearer, and treated as a **divine credential**, since people accept an odd bearer once the relic proves a god chose them.
- HGO's own canon says **five patrons and seven world weapons**. The five-relic idea and the seven-weapon count do not match yet (see Open).
- **The Amulet of Seranis** is the source's relic for Seranis (`codex/objects/amulet-of-seranis.md`). It echoes the Call of the Bones necklace (`codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`).

- **Seven world weapons** in the world are blessed by patrons. Five patrons, seven weapons, so a patron can hold more than one or none. **Seranis holds one.**
- They are World-tier items (see `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md`) and are globally significant gear, tied to World Quests (see `codex/lore/world-quests.md`).

## The Staff of the Mist God (Seranis)

A piece of **Shiv's set** (see `codex/characters/shiv.md`, `codex/objects/call-of-the-bones-necklace.md`). Final enchanted name open ("Staff of the Mist God" is the working title).

- **Three forms**, chosen by the wielder: a **sword**, a **staff**, and a **two-handed energy rifle / gadget gun-blade** (an energy-cannon look, like the energy-cannon weapon in Albion Online; the gunner / gun-blade class idea from King's Avatar). Pick one form at a time.
- **Tatsuya picks the gun-blade form.** It lets him shoot and move while still conjuring his abilities through the staff, which suits his Arcane Bomber build (`codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`).
- **Resurrection.** The blessing of Seranis lets the wielder **raise a fallen player once every seven days**. That matters against the 72-hour death lock, since a player on lock can be brought back.
- **Enchanting.** It lets him **stack enchants faster at a lower MP cost**, and it is a strong tool for his magic.
- **Buffs and minuses.** It has drawbacks as well as benefits. The biggest benefit is the resurrection.
- **Distinct from the Shivbone Staff.** The Shivbone Staff (Legendary) dropped from Spellweaver_Lyn in Chapter 10 and given to Arya is a different item.

## Provenance

Seranis, the Church of Seranis, the five-relic idea and the Amulet of Seranis are adapted (recreated, not pasted) from the author's abandoned Beast King Chronicles (the BKC-Files repo). Only Seranis is named as a deity there, so no other patron is imported.
