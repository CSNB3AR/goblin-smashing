# Prestige & Ranking Ladder (resolved)

Named tiers: **Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster**, then a regional **Top 500** cap above that, not everyone can reach it, the game is global with millions of players across many countries. This replaces an earlier, superseded set of numeric MMR placeholders entirely.

**Ladder rank and pro/tournament status are related but distinct tracks.** A player can be Grandmaster without being Top 500, and can still carry a pro marker on their profile from actually qualifying into real tournaments, ladder rank alone doesn't confer that marker. Contenders-tier players (the development league below the Pro League) generally sit around Masters. Haruto Murayama (see `codex/characters/haruto-murayama.md`) is the clean illustration: an early Grandmaster, not Top 500, but carries a pro marker from real tournament qualification.
