# Alignment Framework for Eryndal

**Status:** PROPOSAL, codex only. Nothing here is printed on the page. Placements for the cast are in `codex/characters/alignment-and-character-trees.md`, the blank card is in `codex/characters/npc-personality-template.md`, and goblin culture is in `codex/lore/goblin-code-and-clan-alignments.md`.

## 1. Two questions

HGO has no alignment screen. The author uses two questions to place anyone, borrowed from the D&D idea of law and good.

- **The Road:** how do they pursue what they want? **Straight** (oath, law, clan code, contract), **Wandering** (what the day asks), or **Wild** (conscience, instinct, loyalty to people over paper).
- **The Hand:** what do they want for others? **Open** (gives, shields, spares, at real cost), **Level** (fair, tends its own), or **Cold** (takes or hurts for gain, order or pleasure, and is unbothered by it).

"Wild" is not "feral." Feral is an outsider's insult for goblins and demi-humans (see `goblin-clan-culture-and-feral-stigma.md`). A Wild goblin can be gentle and reasoned.

## 2. The nine, in HGO terms

| D&D | HGO byname | Road and Hand | How an NPC might say it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lawful Good | Oathkeeper | Straight + Open | "Word-true, and he stands between you and the blade." |
| Neutral Good | Hearthkeeper | Wandering + Open | "Her door is never barred." |
| Chaotic Good | Freeheart | Wild + Open | "Wild as a gale and warm as a hearth." |
| Lawful Neutral | Warden | Straight + Level | "By the letter, and fair to all alike." |
| True Neutral | Wayfarer | Wandering + Level | "Goes where the day goes." |
| Chaotic Neutral | Unbound | Wild + Level | "No man's dog, no one's enemy until crossed." |
| Lawful Evil | Ironhand | Straight + Cold | "The law is in his fist and the debt is in his ledger." |
| Neutral Evil | Taker | Wandering + Cold | "Smiles while he empties your purse." |
| Chaotic Evil | Ravager | Wild + Cold | "A burning wolf: no bargain, no end." |

The Tree is not a ladder. Lunarest's law is Straight and rotten, and the Church of Seranis is Straight and kind. A cell says what a deed is, not whether a faction welcomes it. Gods notice patterns of deeds by trait, not by good versus evil.

## 3. What is shown, and to whom

- **NPCs: hidden.** No label, no bar, no menu. NPCs have no word for alignment and never say one. They speak of deeds, roads and hands ("he keeps his word," "that one counts you as coin"). The inspect panel fills only with what the NPC has shown the player, and unknown rows stay question marks. Nameplate colors (white, yellow, red, gold) stay as canon sets them.
- **Players: expressed, never labeled.** A player has no alignment readout. What they do shows up in two canon meters:
  - **Karma:** global and punitive, never rises on its own. Killing players outside sanctioned PvP or mistreating NPCs lowers it. Goblins start about minus 5. Arrest and the Blood Tinted red nameplate begin around minus 15 to minus 20.
  - **Affiliation:** 0 to 100 per faction and per NPC, shown as a one line tick on the page. Nobody starts below about 20. Goblins start about 40 to 50 with goblin camps and about 20 in human cities. Bands: Hostile below 0, Wary 0 to 24, Friendly 25 to 49, Trusted 50 to 79, Allied 80 to 100.
- **Fame** is a third public meter and tracks how widely known, not how good.

## 4. How actions shift it

1. **Deeds, not declarations.** NPCs react to in-world speech and action. A claim with no deed does nothing, and gamer slang does nothing.
2. **Witness rule.** A deed counts for those who saw it, heard it from someone they trust, or found the evidence. News travels along clan, guild and city lines and arrives weaker.
3. **Cost test.** Open deeds count only if they cost the giver something real. Free kindness is polite, not Open. Gifts alone cannot carry Affiliation past Friendly. Shared risk carries it to Trusted.
4. **Pattern rule.** One deed is a deed. Three consistent deeds before different witnesses is a nature. Gods and gold NPCs revise their read only on a pattern.
5. **Each faction ticks by its own values.** The same deed can raise one meter and lower another. Sparing a prisoner pleases Thrash and annoys a Lunarest guard.
6. **Who started it.** A death in answer to an attack is not a Cold Hand deed. The page already flags the aggressor.
7. **Zones change the law, not the deed.** In a red zone kills carry no Karma, but witnesses still judge them.
8. **Sizes (PROPOSAL).** Affiliation ticks: small 1 to 2, notable 3 to 4, landmark 5 to 8, betrayal about twice a landmark with the offer closed until repaired. Karma: petty offense minus 1, serious offense minus 2 to minus 3, killing an unflagged player or protected NPC minus 5, massacre or a pattern minus 8 or worse. Repeats of the same act earn less, because gold NPCs remember.
9. **Drift.** Only gold NPCs change cell, and only through an arc: a pattern plus a landmark deed. Mob NPCs never drift.

Intent and outcome both count. A mercy that gets innocents killed still reads Open for the one who forgave, and costs standing with whoever paid.
