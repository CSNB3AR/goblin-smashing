She alone "actually played it like real competition (best kill/point ratio on the team by a wide margin)." Sana and Nao chased "viewership and stream moments over the actual matches" and after the loss "turned on her." Sana ran the damage: publicly blamed Arya's leadership and her relationship for the loss. Nao was a follower, never badmouthed her publicly, left with Sana. The guild fell to roughly three who stayed.
- Jiro Amano, old member with trade connections (auction contact), does Arya one last favor then leaves. Keep separate from the unnamed auction house specialist she later recruits.
- Author's points edge: Crimson Static "scaled high in seasonal points even though it was dysfunctional." - Leon sponsored her guild when they were top eight on seasonal points. He walked away from sponsorship after "two guildmates framed her, the guild fell apart, she is trolled on every stream." Leon: her reputation in HGO "is not great and she has had a rough run."
