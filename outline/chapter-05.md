# Chapter 5 — The Bones Speak

**Status: Scene plan locked, not yet drafted.** Ends at Scene 7, the rest of the dictated material continues in `outline/chapter-06.md`. Given the amount of ground covered, this chapter may need to run past the standard 3,000-4,000 word target rather than cut content; flagged, not yet decided.

## Scene-by-scene

1. **Getting home, still angry.** Direct continuation off Chapter 4's collision ending. He's already texted Chiyo but hasn't opened whatever she's sent back, doesn't even check. Decides: shower, calm down, don't respond until morning. The withheld truth about Ohema recontextualizes everything, this is now entirely his decision to make on his own terms.
2. **Morning, the Chiyo exchange.** She's glad to hear he's reconsidering Tochi, asks why the sudden change. He gives a partial truth: found some of Dad's old notebooks, started tinkering with the Haya EB, realized parts need real upgrading, decided to get back on track. Hides the actual reason (fixing the Sakura Arcade pods) entirely. Mentions a vague "second part-time job," reassures her school stays the priority.
3. **The note, and the friend code.** He looks at Yunaile's note again (dated December 23rd, two days before Winter Bash, see `chapters/chapter-04.md`), adds her code. It resolves to a full character profile, stats and all, a real "wait, what" beat, she's clearly not an ordinary player. Her account normally requires exactly this kind of private code to even send a request, a public encounter alone wouldn't have been enough, she's locked down like actual HGO staff. He doesn't get her real name from any of this, just the profile.
4. **Morning research** (roughly an hour, has to be at Sakura Arcade by 9). Official goblin guides are thin, but he clocks the real insight: goblin is functionally an unspecialized class, low base stats but few hard restrictions, real build freedom most races don't get. He starts actually designing his build here: the trap-bomber brigand archetype (already seeded in `codex/subplots/tatsuya-trap-bomber-brigand-archetype.md`), high Fortune for crit/loot, high Speed for maneuverability and evasion, squishy but fast, objective-control playstyle over pure kill-chasing (a direct callback to his own read on the Aogiri match in Chapter 1). Also digs up what little exists on Shiv, a goblin spirit-sorcerer, and starts imagining what that magic path might eventually look like: a Spectral Wolf ability (ninja/shadow-clone-flavored) and an "Ifrit" spell that functions as an arcane bomber, runes and glyphs used as traps and mines, a magical extension of his existing gadget kit. He notices auction-house gear that could shortcut some of this, but reasons he can't spend money right now when the whole point is making it.
5. **Back at Sakura Arcade.** Leon's in genuinely good spirits, energized. Arya's there too, and Leon deliberately keeps her out of the meeting, doesn't want her around the pod he's trying to commercially position given she's an HGO player herself, a real conflict of interest. This is what plants her curiosity (matches her established arc in `codex/characters/arya.md`, multiple visits before she ever actually approaches Tatsuya). Leon and Tatsuya's actual meeting: a tech's coming later today to move the second pod toward real clearance, Tatsuya mentions he's already got some leads on parts from Dad's manual and can dig up more, Leon's relieved to have any direction at all.
6. **Back in-game, the Call of the Bones necklace.** Shiv appears, the meeting plays out, Tatsuya bonds with the necklace. Real physical transformation: his hair goes white, and the camp immediately reads him differently, descendants of Shiv are treated as elders without needing to prove anything, an unearned status that lands on him the moment the bond takes. Lands as genuinely disorienting, he doesn't fully understand what just happened to his own character.
7. **Deciding not to wait.** Still nominally confined, he decides sitting three days out is pointless and sends word through the young goblin companion that he's ready to accept now. Sevish and Wyx move fast, release him, and specifically ask that the young goblin come with him too, real language, not a quest-log popup.

**Chapter ends here.** See `outline/chapter-06.md` for Sevish's status update onward.
