# Act One: The Winter Bash Arc

Picks up right after Chapter 9 ("Silence and Fire," see `outline/chapter-09.md`), starting with the not-yet-drafted Chapter 10. A sequence of story beats, not a chapter map, some of these will need more than one chapter to actually land, that gets sorted during drafting, not here.

Full context and the "new decisions" this arc locks in (Pac_Pac's race, Arya's school, the Haya Storm name, the Souma Ikeda name, the Inter-High/Tochi-representative tournaments, the 14-hour downtime revision) live in the relevant codex files linked below, not duplicated here.

## Story beats, in order

1. PK_Wraith's blacklist fallout, real-world monitor fix, the camp's near-wipe down to ~15 survivors while Tatsuya's offline, Sevish and Wyx setting the Myrrhwood deadline/rendezvous. (This is Chapter 10, not yet drafted.)
2. The breathing-room stretch: Chiyo returns from her Kyoto trip and confronts the gap in her own knowledge, she's seen the Ohema medical payments land, knows Tatsuya's talked to Gato, knows he's applied to Tochi, and doesn't buy the vague "extra shifts" explanation, but she isn't angry, she's realistic, this tracks with who she already is.
3. Tatsuya sits the Tochi entrance exam.
4. Winter Bash gets formally announced in-game alongside the first wave of Kazuku's patch notes (PvP-heavy in detail, PvE deliberately glossed, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`).
5. Momo's Yuna fixation surfaces at home, first plant of the "Heart on You" song thread (see `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md`).
6. **Split leveling schedule, since Arya's in school 9-to-3.** Mornings/school hours, Tatsuya fills the gap grinding at Luxor Lakes with a different in-game friend, one who goes by "Yuna," not obviously the broadcast persona at first (see `codex/characters/yuna-yunaile.md` for the full reveal mechanism, they've already traded real names once in person, this is where they each start connecting that person to who they're actually playing with). After 3PM once Arya's out of school, that block shifts to PvP training with her instead, splitting the day cleanly.
7. Winter Bash qualifiers. Pac_Pac lands as the random third teammate, first real team-chemistry beats, working out how a hardpoint-DPS goblin, a fog/silence control mage, and a Beastfolk brawler actually combo on a King-of-the-Hill map (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`).
8. Qualifiers close on the 8-2 record, 8th seed, barely through.
9. **New:** Arya tells Tatsuya, unprompted, something like "not trying to add pressure, but my mom and dad have been fighting Leon over custody again. I'm counting on a lot going into this," real stakes landing on the page without needing to explain the full situation (see `codex/subplots/leon-arya-custody-battle.md`).
10. The tournament itself begins, the physical-attendance requirement lands as a real identity-exposure fear for Tatsuya before it's a triumph.
11. **New, mid-tournament: PK_Wraith's Luxor Lakes deception.** Unable to move against Yuna openly (too publicly prominent within HGO), PK_Wraith's crew ambushes Tatsuya at Luxor Lakes on fresh/legacy accounts, and he taunts Tatsuya mid-fight, falsely thanking "Yuna" for tipping off his location. Yuna's genuinely blindsided when it reaches her, her shock reads as guilt to Tatsuya in the moment, a real, painful misunderstanding, not an actual betrayal (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`). Creates a real standstill between them running alongside the tournament itself.
12. Top 32 vs. Eren's team, and Eren's partner is PK_Wraith (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`), not a random pairing, Haruto's public exit left Eren without anyone strong willing to team with him, and PK_Wraith takes the open slot chasing a real shot at T-Drag, his own separate rivalry, not borrowed from Eren's. Neither of them can actually guarantee the matchup, Top 32 seeding factors in qualifying record alongside the draw, so it's genuine coincidence when Haya Storm does land across from them, not something either side engineered. Lands the identity-reveal beat (crowd sees T-Drag's face, Eren sees Tatsuya's) and PK_Wraith's own revenge beat together. Haya Storm wins.
13. Advancing through Top 16 and Quarterfinals, public attention building on the underdog story.
14. Semifinal loss to Haruto Murayama/Dosuke AI/Souma Ikeda, a real, earned loss, not a throwaway (see `codex/pro-scene/winter-bash-tournament.md`).
15. Fallout: Eren's spiral after watching Tatsuya beat him in public, pushing the debt/schematics pressure harder, the "unfinished conversation with Daichi" bait lands here.
16. Chiyo's reaction to the public name reveal, not angry, "this is what you chose, but you went back to school for it too," her own realistic read on him.
16a. **New:** somewhere in this stretch, Tatsuya researches replacement parts for the first-gen pod itself (distinct from Leon's already-resolved monitor-rig parts lead) and gets real sticker shock at the cost, planting why the sponsorship/placement money starts mattering as more than just debt relief.
16b. **New, Act One's close:** sponsorship, placement, and loot-drop income lands, enough to clear the family's hospital debt and get Ohema's surgery scheduled for real (see `codex/characters/ohema-kazehaya.md`), the financial half of the six-month clock resolved, the mind-recovery half still open. In the same stretch, the pod itself finally gives out from accumulated wear, right as Tatsuya has the money to actually fix it properly for the first time (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`), the concrete trigger behind Act Two's pod-building/parts thread. The Tabuchis' quiet debt-collection pressure stops working now that Tatsuya's a public star with real money and standing, pushing them toward more overt tactics (see `codex/subplots/tabuchi-schematics-and-family-debt.md`).
17. **New, closes the PK_Wraith/Yuna rift:** by the end of Act One, Tatsuya works out what actually happened at Luxor Lakes, that it was PK_Wraith's deception, not a real betrayal, and he and Yuna start trying to make up. The reconciliation itself is what carries into Act Two, not the rift.
18. **Ties directly into Act Two:** the semifinal loss is a real, live stake for Arya specifically, not just a competitive disappointment, her mother's push to America was already real, and she'd privately been ready to concede and go if the run didn't prove something. It doesn't end in a win, but reaching the semis as an 8th-seed underdog is real enough that it doesn't close the door either, left open rather than resolved, pulling straight into Act Two's opening beat (see `codex/characters/arya.md`).
19. Arc closes with Tatsuya confirmed heading into Tochi for real, debt and Eren pressure both left open, not resolved, carrying forward into the next arc.
