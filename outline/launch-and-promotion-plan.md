## Audience

LitRPG first and heaviest, then high fantasy, then science fiction. (Tags and categories to use on the platform: LitRPG, high fantasy, science fiction.)

## Shout-out plan: the "10-10-10" process

The author cannot run a 20-20-20 (that is the Twitter version, which needs about 60 chapters), so the plan is 10-10-10 across the first ten to thirty chapters:

1. **Ten swaps with authors at the author's own level**: similar stats, same genre. This is the biggest and easiest group (the "under 10K" range, around 20 to 30 chapters in).
2. **Ten with mid-sized authors** (written as "10 to 40K"; the exact follower bands are OPEN).
3. **Ten with the top authors** (100K or higher; the author wants to try these within the first ten chapters). Written once as "10 to 90K" for the middle band, which is why the bands are OPEN.

Rules: approach the bigger authors early, but only once there is a firm launch date, since a swap has to be simultaneous and on date ("tit for tat"). Do not approach anyone without a date.
