# Chapter 2 — Into the Game (~2,000-2,500 words)

**Status: Drafted.** See `chapters/chapter-02.md`. This file is kept as the scene-by-scene reference, still accurate to the drafted chapter, with one location correction applied after drafting: the drive pickup happens at the Nexus Enterprises flagship store in Ginza (see `codex/locations/nexus-enterprises-flagship.md`), not at WattsUp.

## Scene 1 — Picking up right where Chapter 1 ends

No new material needed. Chapter 1 already ends on Tatsuya standing in the empty apartment, the Nexus Enterprises pickup text just landed, Chiyo's note already read (gone six days to Kyoto, back around December 16th). Chapter 2 opens directly from that beat, same moment, no time skip. No repeated Chiyo dialogue or text in this chapter, the deadline pressure carries forward from Chapter 1 without restating it.

## Scene 2 — HGO TV, the pro-scene shakeup

He grabs the remote, flips to HGO TV (Kazuku's official channel, see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`), the same channel that plays at WattsUp. Coverage of the competitive scene reacting to the 5v5 shift: pro teams scrambling for players since hybrid builds are about to lose their edge, veteran players cashing in Legacy Points to rebuild fresh, Dosuke AI's team named as one of the teams in flux (see `codex/characters/dosuke-ai.md`). Filtered entirely through Tatsuya's own insider recognition, not a narrator info-dump, per the standing POV rule.

## Scene 3 — Research and build planning

He researches the deck-building/item-set side of things, market prices, zone-specific loadouts, landing on wanting an **Elf**, leaning caster, a real departure from the old Warden/hybrid-mage build he and Eren once theorized together on paper back at Tochi (a pointed, bitter callback, "hate that I gave my first idea to that jackass"). See `codex/lore/deck-building-rarity-and-classes.md` for the new per-race base stats, Elf's high Wisdom is exactly why he wants one. Eventually concludes research has a ceiling, this is full-dive VR, no amount of reading substitutes for actually being in the game, and pivots to action.

## Scene 4 — Numbers check

A quick, visible beat: his bank balance and his Loop follower count, both bracketed per house on-screen-text formatting. Meant to read as trackable stakes the reader watches move over the story (see the new "numbers going up" craft note in `style-guide/voice-technique.md`), not just flavor.

## Scene 5 — The booking search

Third-gen cafes across Tokyo booked solid (Winter Bash hype). Second-gen pods booked out for months because of the expansion. Real frustration, "this is what I was worried about." He's never been outside Shibuya ward (see `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md`), so the search stays hyperlocal by nature, not a citywide odyssey. Scrolling further, a stray recommendation mentioning someone running HGO on a first-gen pod catches his eye, his one real lead, tied directly to his father. Notices "free after 5pm" on Sakura Arcade's listing. Grabs Daichi's tech manual (see `codex/objects/daichi-tech-manual.md`) before heading out, not yet sure why, just a hunch.

## Scene 6 — Picking up the drive at WattsUp/Nexus Enterprises

Quick beat: the pickup counter, tournament highlight reels playing, other people also picking up drives, genuine crowd energy from the expansion hype. Clock pressure, it's closing in on 5pm, he needs to move to make Sakura Arcade's free window.

## Scene 7 — Sakura Arcade, meeting Leon

Cross-ward trip to the far side of Shibuya, the arcade reads retro and a little cut off (see `codex/locations/sakura-arcade.md`). Leon mentions business being slow, a pressure/opening beat of his own. Confirms Tatsuya's here for the pods, warns they're first-gen and don't play well with newer titles, mentions he's already turned other HGO hopefuls away since the announcement hit. Signs him in, asks his name, "Tatsuya Kazehaya" lands with a flicker of nostalgic recognition (see `codex/characters/leon.md`), Leon doesn't fully connect it yet, that pays off after the blackout below, not in the moment.

## Scene 8 — The override, the blackout, the glitch

Tatsuya uses what he knows from Daichi's manual to override the pod, the deliberate technical act already established in `codex/lore/playable-races-and-character-creation.md`. Running HGO on hardware never built for it draws more power than the arcade's system was built to handle, causing a real blackout, not a coincidental one. Leon panics over the liability of a kid losing pod contact mid-blackout, rushes to check the backup generator. The pod's own external monitor (meant to let an attendant see what a player's doing inside VR) has been dark and unrepaired for a while, so Leon can only reach him over comms, not see him, asks if he's alright, whether he's running anything unusual. Tatsuya downplays it, uncertain whether Leon can actually see anything (confirmed he can't). This is the exact moment of the goblin lock: mid character-creation, paused on Goblin out of dry curiosity, the reboot mid-blip locks it in. Leon, none the wiser about the specific mechanical cause, is satisfied things look fine and tells him he can keep playing.

**Chapter ends** on the goblin lock landing, first login imminent, continuing into the already-outlined Chapter 3 camp arc (see `outline/chapter-03.md`).
