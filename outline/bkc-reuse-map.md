# BKC Reuse Map

Single home for provenance. **Beast King Chronicles** (the author's abandoned story, repo BKC-Files) is source material only. Nothing in the codex depends on it. HGO canon wins any conflict, only gaps were added, and everything added was re-edited for Eryndal. BKC kingdoms, politics and characters were not imported.

## Kept as HGO's own (BKC equivalent already existed)

- Six of the seven BKC schools map to existing books: fire to Embers, wind and lightning to Tempest, light to Radiance, earth to Stone, dark to Veil. BKC school names (Ascentio, Ventara and the rest) are an optional naming note only.
- Augmented versus conjuring magic (same split as Physical Arts versus Wisdom-gated casting), named arts at tier 7 and up, the tier ladders (HGO tiers 1 to 8 stay unnamed), court mages versus northern augmenters.
- Wire coil and whistle (gadget belt, Scout's Whistle), generic armor, camp trade goods, arcana-tech rules, healing costing the healer (cleanses stay healer-only).

## Skipped

- Valdenmere, Ardentia, Norr-Thane, noble, church and military hierarchies, province law (clash with the Lunarest court).
- The Albrgard Cycle calendar (HGO runs on real dates), the tier titles (Recruit to Warbrand), Port of Skiff-Haven and Ellas (never built).
- The Amulet of Seranis (BKC's relic): retired, because Seranis holds exactly one world weapon (the Staff of the Mist God), so a second relic would contradict that. `codex/objects/amulet-of-seranis.md` is a redirect stub.
- Character-bound material: Gale Draw, the berserk state, Essentia versus raw mana, the relic as the party's divine credential, the silver chain, tin cups, generic Ashenveil beasts and the relic's bird symbol.
