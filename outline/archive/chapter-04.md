# Chapter 4 — Real-World Anchor (No VR)

**Status: Drafted, see `chapters/chapter-04.md`.** Target length 3,000-4,000 words (see `style-guide/craft-rules.md`).

**Deliberate craft call:** entirely real-world, no in-game scenes at all. After three chapters building the game world, this chapter stays grounded so the story doesn't over-index on the fantasy side this early.

## Scene-by-scene

1. **Coming out of the pod.** Direct continuation from Chapter 3's ending. Real world reasserts itself, nighttime, Leon's arcade around him.
2. **HGO Companion App, the character screen.** Links his drive to his phone (a chip on the back of the drive, tapped against the phone while the app's open, auto-authenticates). Sees his goblin card: Goblin Gas and Goblin Flare are innate racial abilities, no card needed. Actual item inventory: the Goblin Gadget Belt (locked/shaded), two Minor Elixirs, the Gambler's Earring (character-bound, unsellable). Sells the two elixirs, ¥16,800 hits his bank balance, a real "numbers going up" beat.
3. **Loop.** Updates his profile, real bio this time, pro-player ambition reframed around his actual income plan now. Scrolls the feed: World Quest chatter spreading, PK_Wraith and his crew reacting loudly (see `codex/characters/pk-wraith.md`), a nod to Dosuke AI or London Spire's captain, kept organic. Sees established players already switching to post-reset characters and cashing in Legacy Points, some guild leaders hesitating since it means starting at level 1 again (new-region cap sits at 25 for Winter Bash), a real "I'm already ahead" feeling since his own character was never anything to retire. Starts thinking ahead to where he could grind efficiently, Luxor Lakes surfaces as a place he's read about (see `codex/locations/luxor-lakes.md`), not visited.
4. **The math problem.** Showing up at 5pm nightly only nets him about four real hours a session, not enough. He clocks the second, dead pod next to the one he's been using.
5. **The pitch to Leon.** Leon opens up first about the arcade struggling financially, that's the opening. Tatsuya pitches fixing the second pod using Daichi's manual, openly admitting he already got HGO running illegally on the first one. They strike a deal: guaranteed access for Tatsuya, the second pod goes into commercial rotation once fixed and VRSA-cleared (see `codex/lore/vrsa.md`), Leon brings a tech in the next morning, Tatsuya agrees to come back and help, and to hold off talking publicly about working HGO pods until both are genuinely done. Leon's backstory lands here: he and Arya inherited the shop from their grandfather, their parents want Arya back overseas, she won't leave HGO behind, the arcade's struggles are part of why (see `codex/subplots/technical-arc-leon-and-sakura-arcade.md`). Closing beat: Tatsuya connects doing this right to needing what Tochi can actually teach him, his own reason to go back, not just Chiyo's ultimatum.
6. **Ride to the hospital.** Short transition on the Haya EB.
7. **Visiting Ohema.** Comatose, he talks to her anyway, sincere, unguarded, shares the day's real win. The one place his "doesn't monologue pain" default cracks.
8. **Overhearing the nurses.** Leaving, overhears Ms. Sugiyama and a colleague (see `codex/characters/ms-sugiyama.md`): Chiyo asked them to keep quiet, without real progress in six months Ohema might not have a mind left to come back to (see `codex/characters/ohema-kazehaya.md`). He's furious, specifically at the secrecy, not just the news. Recontextualizes everything, the Gambit income plan is the priority now.
9. **The collision.** Leaving, still furious, he physically collides with a girl carrying an armful of things, papers scatter. His HGO Companion App is still open mid-setup (because of the glitch, his account never had a username), and this is the on-page origin of **TDRAG** (see `codex/characters/tatsuya-kazehaya.md`), created in this exact flustered moment. She catches it, teases him over it, doesn't give her own code verbally, writes it on a scrap of paper with a date and tucks it into his pocket, says she has to go, hurries off before he gets a real look at her or the note. Chapter ends right there, papers on the ground, eye contact, unresolved, not the fuller established "hospital meeting" scene (still reserved for later). This is Yunaile/Yuna, unnamed to him and the reader in the moment.

**Not this chapter:** going home, resting, and waking up is Chapter 5's opening, along with his morning research turning up that goblins aren't stat-capped out of scaling Wisdom the way other magic-restricted races are.
