# Chapter 6 — Cover Blown

**Status: Scene plan locked, not yet drafted.** Continues directly from `outline/chapter-05.md`, Scene 7.

## Scene-by-scene

8. **Sevish's status update.** Skirmish parties have been probing the enemy group camped at the base of the hill, can't move the rally point while they're sitting on it. Tatsuya privately clocks that Sevish is basically feeding them XP, doesn't say it out loud.
9. **The no-metagaming rule, made explicit as a real mechanic.** NPCs never break character or reference anything player/meta-related, full stop, and that's not just flavor, it's the actual mechanism for building real trust/affinity with them (a percentage-based relationship system, unlocked by talking to them like real people, not like game objects). Tatsuya starts to intuit this is what Kazuku's "living world" pitch actually meant.
10. **The goblin squad companions, actual introduction.** Tatsuya proposes a scouting run with just the young goblin; Sevish agrees but insists on backup, this is where he actually meets the established squad (Grukk/Krok, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh, see `codex/characters/goblin-squad-companions.md`) for the first time on the page.
11. **Real-world interruption.** The tech/inspector arrives to work on the second pod, forcing a break right before he'd have engaged the enemy group at the base of the hill.
12. **Arya's discovery.** She ends up monitoring his pod during this break, a legitimate excuse (VRSA rules require someone watching a player in a full pod, and Leon can't be at the desk and monitoring at once, see `codex/lore/vrsa.md`). She sees him actively leading NPC allies in real combat, something she's never actually seen a player do before, clocks that he's also the World Quest holder, and starts recording, not deviously, documenting something genuinely unprecedented. This is the on-page start of her already-seeded Loop-discovery arc (`codex/subplots/aryas-loop-discovery-and-reputation.md`) and the first spark of her eventually wanting to team up with him for Winter Bash's duo mechanic.
13. **Chapter ends.** A second break, he steps away to clear his head before the actual engagement. Comes out to find Arya waiting, relaxed, direct: something like "are you going to go? I'm excited to see what happens." His cover's blown, not through any big confrontation, just her already knowing.
