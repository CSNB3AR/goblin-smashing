### B1. "Hey, Nykri" and the guild offer (350)
- Who and where: Tatsuya in the upstairs bathroom doorway, phone braced against the sink then in his hand, head down on shield wall pages. Arya comes up the hall behind him. Leon and the inspector are downstairs with the pod.
- He sees: his own phone. Hears: footsteps, then her. No other source.
- Body cue: his thumb freezes mid scroll, head snaps up into her eyes.
- Must be there: "Hey, Nykri." / "How do you know that name?" She watched the stream and confirmed it is him (gray eyes, white bun, ink to the wrists, half smirk, as drafted).
- Her first move is the guild proposal, not a team slot: "Would you create a guild with me?" (Drafted version had her ask to join his team; same idea, now a guild.) She adds the 'low on men, four players, NPCs as scripts' pitch here.
- Keep it not rude: she is blunt, not sneering. Drop the "Come on. You really do need me" line.

- She keeps calling him Nykri as he walks. "Hey man, I'm talking." "Please listen." "Why are you ignoring me?"

### B3. The rumor, the second no, the plea, the yes (400)
- Who and where: foot of the stairs, voices kept low so the inspector does not listen.
- He shrugs it off politely: "That's good to hear. Not interested, thanks." (first no)
- She plays the card: she has information about what will happen at Thornwatch, and it will be guild oriented. Sources, unconfirmed, rumors only. He does not repeat the rumor aloud; he only says no a second time (the rumor is why: a guild shaped event means a guild is a liability or a prize he cannot yet judge).
- Then she cracks: "Please, just hear me out. I wouldn't ask someone random to form a guild with me. I've been watching your stream. When I noticed my brother was helping you... I'm sorry if I came off rude. I'm in a really bad spot. I was texting my mom when you got here." The honest reason: if she does not make it in this game she has to leave her brother and everything here; they both rely on HGO.

- PROPOSAL: the guild itself is not founded on the page. He says yes to the guild idea in principle and to her joining the assault now; Thornwatch details wait for Chapter Twelve.

### B4. Her build, the mist kit, the whistle code (350)
- Who and where: pod room corner, Arya with her own J-Link slot twenty minutes away. If the codex files `codex/characters/arya-build-and-mist-kit.md` and `codex/magic/hushveil-mist-mechanics.md` exist when drafting, they win over everything below (they were not present when this sheet was written).
