# Restructuring Notes — What Changed From the Old Draft, and Why

This supersedes the old draft's chapter breakdown for the opening stretch (see `bible/old-draft-chapter-bible.md` for what it replaces and why). Canon facts live in `codex/characters/`, `codex/locations/`, `codex/objects/`; game mechanics live in `codex/lore/`; voice guidance lives in `style-guide/voice-technique.md`. Chapter 1 is drafted in `chapters/chapter-01.md`; everything past it is beats only, one file per chapter in this `outline/` folder.

## Three restructuring decisions driving this pass

1. **Compress the "find a pod" search.** The old draft spent a full chapter (old Chapter 3) on Tatsuya physically riding his moped from cafe to cafe across Tokyo looking for an open VR pod slot, only landing on Sakura Arcade as a last resort. The new direction moves the *search* online and compresses it to a single paragraph inside Chapter 2, keeping the Haya EB for exactly one deliberate trip: Sakura Arcade.
2. **Get him into the game early, and keep the real-world half of Chapter 2 trimmed hard.** Momentum matters more than thoroughness here: too long a real-world wait before login kills the pull into the game. The ride home closes out Chapter 1; Chapter 2's opener, and the pod search, are compressed so login lands around the chapter's midpoint, not its final page, leaving real room for the first camp scene to actually breathe.
3. **Don't log him out the instant he gets the ultimatum.** The old draft has Tatsuya/Kaiseki get Sevish's three-day ultimatum and essentially leave the game world immediately after. The new direction wants him to live in camp for a stretch first, texture, a taste of goblin culture, the buildup toward accepting the "Call of the Bones" World Quest.

**Superseded, Chapter 3 planning pass:** the "chained up" beat no longer waits for a later chapter after a real-world return trip. Killing two camp goblins in the roast-turned-fight draws real consequences immediately, he's taken into custody by the end of the same first-camp chapter, even while being offered the World Quest in the same breath. Chapter 4 becomes the real-world processing/decision chapter (Loop reaction, the Tabuchi/Chiyo threads, the return decision), and Chapter 5 picks up still in custody, formally accepting the quest and starting the proving-trial trust arc from there. See `outline/chapter-03.md` through `chapter-05.md` for the current shape.

See `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md` for the fuller in-game arc this feeds into.
