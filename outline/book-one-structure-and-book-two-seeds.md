## In Book One: Arya, Chiyo and the household

- **Arya is the babysitter.** She plays a big part as the person who looks after Taji and Momo. She starts as the temp Chiyo hired for the Nakamura Grand front desk (Chapter Eight shows her at the desk with her back to the door).
- **Chiyo and Arya.** Chiyo starts off present at the hotel. She notices that Arya and Tatsuya have become friends and sees it as a way to work her own relationship with Tatsuya back.
- **Tatsuya stays cold.** He takes this stretch to hold himself back until he and Chiyo are on talking terms again. He is not rude or disrespectful. He is hurt, and he is very cold. (This sits inside the anger that runs to the end of Chapter Nine.)
- **Chiyo leans on Arya for the twins.** She goes to Arya for help with Taji and Momo as the court dates start. Taji and Momo's reactions are different from Tatsuya's.
- **Court dates against the Tabuchis.** A legal case builds, which strains the household and takes Chiyo away for periods. There are court cases she has to appear at, so the family needs constant transport. Timing across Book One versus Book Two is OPEN.

## Book Two seeds

- **Arya goes to Tochi in Book Two ("season 2"). There is no separate Arya storyline in Book One.** Her Tochi admission is mostly a sign to her that her brother has connections. The reasons she gets in: (1) a **recommendation**, she is very smart and just wants to be a professional gamer; (2) her **connection to the guild**: Tochi ends up sponsoring the guild, and Leon sets one condition: if Tatsuya does this, he has to **bring Arya on**.
- **What Tochi gives them.** Full access if Arya is a Tochi student: Leon has aged out and could not go, but she can, so the team gets lab and equipment access it would not have otherwise, and can play a lot more. It also gives Leon the means to run the **two pods** at Sakura Arcade fully.
- **Leon stays the manager** and the voice on the other side, still coming to them. Arya has to attend school during this.
- **Sakura Arcade becomes a high-tech arcade.** They upgrade it with high-tech gear, and it becomes their base. It is also how Tatsuya gives back to Leon. The author likes this a lot.
- **The Haya Sedan.** Because the court dates need constant transport, Tatsuya upgrades the **Haya Sedan**, the four-door his dad was driving in the accident, into a **family vehicle**. Arya (or Chiyo, flagged) can then drive the kids. He keeps riding the Haya EB. OPEN: whether this is the wrecked car from the impound (which carries the tampering evidence, see `codex/characters/daichi-kazehaya.md`) or another unit of the model; the author said "he still had one model".
- **Myrrhwood expands** on top of the family drama and the court dates, feeding into the guilds. See `outline/act-three-myrrhwood-expedition.md`.
