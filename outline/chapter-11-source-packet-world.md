## 6. Style rules that apply

craft-rules.md, cue-bank.md and ledger standing rules:
- No em dashes, en dashes, spaced hyphens or double hyphens, anywhere. No ellipses in narration; dialogue "..." only for a true trail off, at most two per chapter. Exempt: hyphens inside words and bracketed bold system text.
- Thoughts: quotation marks and italics, present tense, short, in his voice: `*"Damn, she has me cornered."*`. Plain narration stays narration. Spoken dialogue is quotes without italics. Tatsuya swears casually; keep it.
- One speaker or thinker per paragraph. Every line of dialogue paired with a visible action, posture or tone cue (a bare tag is a floating quote). No isolated one-sentence paragraphs for emphasis. Full sentences, no action fragments.
- Cues: signature tell is scratching the thin goatee when weighing a decision, once or twice a chapter. Capped crutches (jaw set, stomach drop, pulse, breath out, shoulders, knee bounce, hands flat, throat tight, heat up the neck, blinking hard) at most once each per chapter and not repeated from Chapter Ten. In goblin form use the body (cold, long fingers and nails, smaller frame, green skin). Return to the body every two or three paragraphs of information. One or two perceptions per beat.
- Leon's channel: every time Leon comes in over the voice channel the page opens with `**[DIRECTOR IN BOOTH: VOICE CHANNEL]**`, consistently. Leon speaks only about what shows on his monitor. Only Leon has voice. No party chat, no voice chat in HGO [AUTHOR]; Arya whistles (three slow whistles for low mana; three at 10 MP is a ten second timer) and everyone else talks face to face.
- No metagaming: Tatsuya knows only what he sees, hears or is told; he guesses and hedges, rough estimates not exact numbers. Exposition only where cause leads to effect. Show the plan through others' pieces; do not over explain. No teleporting, short transition beats. Do not restate a beat after it lands. Every detail mentioned must be used. Real world elapsed time shown indirectly. Combat shows real damage and kill numbers. Rune Priming shows no dice text. Chapter target 3,000 to 4,000 words; a revision replaces rather than adds.
- NPCs never express naive surprise about their own species' place in the world. Hobgoblins (Ru, Zinka) speak in fuller sentences; plain goblins like Kesh speak in third person.
