# Arc One: Open Beta to Crownfall

**Status:** Forward outline, a sequence of beats and not a chapter map. Written from the author's dictated arc plan. Open items are flagged at the bottom.

## Shape

**Arc One:** Open Beta, then **Crownfall** (the Season One opener for seasonal points, which introduces the Siege battle royale), then **Hearthfire** (Season One starts, the expansion and the map-wide world). Mini sub-plot: the **Battle of Thrash Hill**. See `outline/act-one-winter-bash.md` for the beat-by-beat Crownfall run and `codex/pro-scene/crownfall-siege-event.md` for the event.

## Season rules this arc runs on

- The season restarts on open beta, and open beta leads into Season One. Kazuku says so in his opening ad. Seasonal points earned from the first night of open beta roll into Season One.
- Raids, world quests and guild activities all award seasonal points. An individual can earn them, but **only a guild can enter Crownfall**. That is why Tatsuya founds his own guild after he and Arya escape.
- This is a new game. Closed-beta names (Dosuke AI, Eren and others) are established. Tatsuya is not, and after the global stream everyone is watching him.

## Beats

1. **Open beta night.** Tatsuya shrugs off the ad, finds Dad's letter, buys the drive, and enters the game (Chapters 1 to 3).
2. **The World Quest and the camp.** Shiv, the necklace and the Thrash Clan (Chapters 3 to 9).
3. **The Battle of Thrash Hill** (Chapter 10). The raid on PK_Wraith's early legacy crew, with Arya, Ru, Rott and Nab. The first legacy drops come out of it. (Whether "one of the holders" of a world weapon also fights there is not set.)
4. **The split.** After Thrash Hill the group separates from the Thrash Clan. The clan heads for the boat. Tatsuya, Arya and the first recruits head for Lunarest while avoiding bigger guilds.
5. **The Archives.** They reach roughly floor one, the public floor, which is heavily guarded. The royal guard watches the goblin the whole time. Deeper floors are out of reach for now (see `codex/locations/astralum-archive.md`).
6. **Selling the drops.** Some legacy items are useless to his build. Arya's brokering and contacts are vital to moving them quietly.
7. **Blackmail and framing.** PK_Wraith has real influence and his crew just died on a global stream, so Tatsuya is blackmailed and framed at the start of a new game. That pushes him to get streaming running much sooner.
8. **Building the engine.** Levels, world quest progress, the stream, and relationships with Leon and Arya, all while he works the money problem (Ohema's deadline does not die because Dad's letter matters more now).
9. **The guild.** To enter Crownfall he founds a guild, gathers seasonal points and recruits gradually.
10. **The troop edge.** He is the first to fill guild slots with NPC troops (see `codex/lore/companion-troop-system.md`). Because troops do not follow the player system, his enchants multiply on them and last longer than they do on players.
11. **Crownfall.** The Siege battle royale debut: 5 guilds per session, 20 players each, camps to forts, 60 lives, 3 hours, a neutral zone (see `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`).
12. **The king falls.** Around the start of Season One the king of Lunarest dies, about when the Thrash Clan heads back to Myrrhwood (see `codex/lore/lunarest-royal-court.md`). This is why the event is called Crownfall: HGO named it for the coming fall, a wink the reader catches later. Nobody in the story knows yet.
13. **The funeral.** A royal event and a truce: no battling in the royal lands while it runs.
14. **The map moves.** After the funeral the princes go after each other, Node Wars open, and the expansion opens up. Hearthfire stays the expansion's name; Crownfall is the arc and event.
15. **Hearthfire.** Season One starts and the map opens.

## Tatsuya's drivers

- **Money** first: the surgery deadline and the debt.
- **Dad's letter** second. The Tabuchis want what is in the manual. Only the bear, Dad's business partner, has the proof, and he is reachable inside HGO. Daichi also handed Kazuku some notes while they were building the third-gen pods; that stake would have paid off Ohema's debt, another reason the letter says to find the bear.
- **Getting the bear's attention.** He cannot go in doing nothing. A Dad invention brought into the game, a top guild, a Crownfall run: any of these might get Kazuku to notice. He also wants the compensation (tech sold in the game plus other earners).
- **Dad's name.** Dad could not walk, and he helped build a world that does not know his name.

## Later items (forward only)

- **The Staff of the Mist God (Seranis).** The one relic among the unique items of Shiv's Warset, and one of seven blessed world weapons (see `codex/lore/patrons-and-world-weapons.md`). It raises a fallen player once every seven days, stacks enchantments faster at lower MP cost, and has three forms: sword, staff, and a two-handed energy rifle / gun-blade gadget. He will favor the gun-blade form so he can shoot and move. It is distinct from the Shivbone Staff given to Arya in Chapter 10.
- **Patrons.** Five patrons. A player picks one at the start. Seranis blesses demi-humans.
