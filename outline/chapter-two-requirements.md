## What the author asked for

1. **Opening.** Start on the beat right after the name: his reaction, the bell, the two of them crossing the lot. Do not repeat the emblem line ("I knew that emblem like I knew the scar over my own eye") as the first thing, because the reader has just left the car.
2. **The riddle must be earned.** In the letter he reads "talk to the bear". He must stop and question it ("What does he mean, bear?"), go through who it could be, and only then click: bear, HGO, that mascot. His first reaction is reluctance ("That damn mascot?"). The click should not be instant and should not be solved in a few lines with no inner monologue. The letter text and the message stay as written; they are good.
3. **No fully formed plan that night.** He ends the scene with a thread he cannot confirm and a decision to buy, not a finished strategy (the gadget, the guild and the ten million are later).
4. **Inner thoughts in quotation marks AND italics**, throughout. Narration stays narration. This is the author's format and does not change.
5. **Do not repeat that Chiyo is a Japanese volleyball star.** Say it once (the lobby line) and cut the second mention (the couch line, "a retired national-team legend").
6. **Cleanup:** the ride paragraph repeats "empty" three times ("emptier", "nearly empty", "an empty stretch"). Fix.
7. **Keep:** the letter, the paw print, "Bring your business here", the Nexus purchase, "I didn't remember closing my eyes."
8. Keep Tatsuya Black, 6'3" with his mother's build (set in chapter one). His mother is from Africa; the page names no country.
