# Act Three: The Myrrhwood Expedition Arc

Picks up only after `outline/act-two-haya-storm-rise.md` closes. Myrrhwood stays fully dormant through Acts One and Two (locked in that file's throughlines section); this is the arc where it finally activates. A sequence of story beats, not a chapter map, some of these will need more than one chapter to actually land, that gets sorted during drafting, not here.

## Locked for this arc

- **The "elder" payoff here is distinct from Chapter 8's.** Chapter 8 already has Tatsuya addressed as "elder" on the page (Wyx, Ru, the `[WAR TENT – ELDER T-DRAG]` nameplate), explicitly unearned, granted the moment the necklace bond took. This arc's payoff is Sevish and Wyx coming to treat the same title as genuinely earned, mechanically expressed as Tatsuya's Affiliation with the Thrash clan collectively (not just Ru's individual reading) crossing into Allied band (80-100, see `codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md`). Same word, explicitly different status, worth calling out as such when it lands on the page rather than reading as a repeat beat.
- **The recurring monthly rally is already fully locked**, not new territory (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`): Sevish's "three times" was always his own limited, persistent POV, he and Wyx survive every attempt, only rank-and-file goblins reset. The real count is dozens, closer to a hundred-plus, since it's been recurring monthly since launch, almost two years. Chapter 4 already planted the seed on the page ("Three times. He's saying that like it's a specific number that matters"). This arc is where Tatsuya actually pieces that together.
- **Pac_Pac and Arya's builds stay undetailed here**, per the user's own call to cover that separately later. Nothing below commits to mechanics for either of them.
- **A second elder NPC travels with the rally party to the coast, undefined for now.** A real character, not a placeholder to be silently dropped, just not built out yet.

## Story beats, in order

1. Tatsuya finally infiltrates the Grand Library of Lunarest's restricted floors, the still-pending mission Sevish gave him back in Chapter 4, real capture/jail risk if caught. He finds a lead: three of Shiv's Warset's remaining six pieces are in Myrrhwood specifically (see `codex/objects/shivs-warset.md`), a real worry given how many pieces are scattered elsewhere, but also real hope, since it means real progress is possible close to home.
2. Piecing together library records and his own accumulated experience, Tatsuya realizes "Call of the Bones" was never a one-off, it's a recurring, monthly, world-level quest that's failed for almost two years, and Sevish's "three times" was only ever his own limited memory. A real, earned on-page realization, not an info dump, the actual payoff of the Chapter 4 seed.
3. With Tatsuya actually present, and having genuinely triggered the quest rather than lucking into it, the rally finally succeeds. Through the Affiliation growth built across this arc, Sevish and Wyx's collective read on him crosses into Allied band, and his "elder" status shifts from the necklace's unearned title into something they now treat as actually earned. Explicit on the page that this is a real shift, not the same beat as Chapter 8.
4. Payoff: the surviving rally party reaches the coastline and boards a ship, alongside the second, undefined elder NPC who traveled with them.
5. Wyx's already-flagged background interest in Tatsuya (see `codex/subplots/world-quest-call-of-the-bones-camp-arc.md`) becomes more overt here, a narrative beat, not a formal mechanic (see the light note added to `codex/lore/affiliation-and-reputation-system.md`).
6. Before departure, Arya has to separately convince the group to let her come, "she helped us in the fight, she's my ally," her place on the ship isn't assumed or automatic (already locked, same subplot file).
7. Ship departs. A real, multi-day ocean crossing, four to five days, no fast travel (see `codex/lore/hgo-timeline-and-real-world-stakes.md`). This is also the in-fiction reason any distant crew or allies took real time reacting to Bloodhurst's Chapter 9 losses, if that timing's ever needed on the page.
8. New mechanic lands here practically: an idle/offline character doesn't teleport to safety, they go idle in place, defenseless. A true safe zone protects them; a contested/red zone (open ocean, the Outskirts) doesn't. The concrete reason guards matter during the crossing, and the reason they mattered during Tatsuya's past log-offs too.
9. Arrival at Myrrhwood's clan docks. Pac_Pac, playing Beastfolk, simply spawns in directly on the next server restart, since it's his race's home region, no ship required, a real mechanical asymmetry between him and the rest of the group worth landing on the page.
10. Arya, playing a mage with no racial or narrative tie to Myrrhwood, has no natural route there at all. A real, unresolved problem, left open here to seed the arc's larger "bring your business here" travel-solution theme, not solved in this same beat.
11. Myrrhwood's goblin side: already-locked fractured, warring clans, no true king, dense swamp/forest. This is where the Thrash clan's reunification push under Shiv's legacy actually plays out on the page for the first time.
12. Myrrhwood's Beastfolk side: an organized kingdom, a real royal family (new, see `codex/races/beastfolk.md`). One of the kingdom's princesses takes real interest in Pac_Pac, whose growing public profile, real-world rap career plus genuine in-game Beastfolk warrior reputation, has earned him real standing there.
13. Pac_Pac becomes the first player ever to marry an NPC, a real, headline-making first for HGO.
14. Myrrhwood's actual unification doesn't resolve as one side "giving over" to the other. Tatsuya's work reunifying the goblin side under Shiv's legacy, and Pac_Pac's marriage into Beastfolk royalty, together provide the leverage for a cooperative council spanning both sides, with the wider guild structure (Haya Storm or a broader guild presence) sitting over it. New lore, no "council" concept exists anywhere in the repo yet.
