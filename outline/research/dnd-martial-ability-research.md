### 1.11 DM resources on unique martial NPCs and boss action economy
- Foe Foundry, "No More Boring Martial NPCs": https://foe-foundry-stage.onrender.com/blog/2025_07_09_martial_npcs/ (fetched). Plain martial NPCs feel dull. Add drama-triggered abilities (bloodied rage, a once per day defiant roar), team abilities (phalanx, counterattack when an ally misses), telegraphed big actions, defender abilities (interception, taunt), a duel ability ("Challenge": single out one creature, gain advantage against it, others have disadvantage attacking you), and pace power with recharge and once-per-day limits.
- Sly Flourish, making monsters interesting: https://slyflourish.com/making_monsters_interesting.html (fetched). Wrap changes in flavour, borrow existing monster features, change gear, and vary terrain and objectives. (The "12 plus half CR" DC tip came from a search summary, not the page I fetched, so treat as unverified.)
- Legendary actions: https://www.nerdsandscoundrels.com/legendary-actions-5e/ (fetched). Typically three legendary actions per round; used at the end of other creatures' turns; the pool refreshes at the start or end of the creature's own turn (sources vary); stronger options cost two or three; legendary resistance is usually three uses per day. A solo boss has one action per round against many player actions, which is the root balance problem.
- Boss pacing: https://pages.roll20.net/dnd-boss-fight and https://koboldpress.com/?p=45721 (search summary). Use phases, add reactions and bonus actions.

## PART 2. PALETTES

Shared stamina frame [MINE]: each NPC has a stamina pool (call it 100). Arts cost 10 to 30. Stamina regenerates slowly (about 5 per second out of combat actions, 2 per second in fights). At zero stamina the NPC cannot use arts and attacks at base only. This one rule is the main balance lever: it makes cooldowns and costs matter even for a level 80.

### 2.1 WYX THRASH, L25 Blade Dancer, "the wall that dances"

Core identity [MINE]: two real blades, one real strike per blade per swing. Everything else that feels like extra strikes is an ECHO: a delayed ghost hit from an afterimage that lands one beat later from a different angle. The player can see it coming and counter it. Echoes are fuelled by TEMPO, a 0 to 5 counter.

**Physical arts (active, stamina, cooldown)**

1. **Afterimage Cut** (the three-strike move)
   - Does: one real sabre cut, then two afterimage cuts land from the flanks a beat later, so one swing reads as three strikes.
   - Cost: 20 stamina, 12 second cooldown.
   - Balance: each echo does about one third of a normal hit; a parry or sidestep of the first cut cancels both echoes; she must be adjacent.
   - Derived from: Irelia Flawless Duet (the second blade arriving from another direction), Battle Master Sweeping Attack, Virtuoso/Mesmer stored hits. Adapted by me.

2. **Crossing Cuts** (the Duet)
   - Does: she marks a spot with one sabre and, a beat later, her other sabre crosses it from the opposite direction. A foe in the line takes a brief stagger, not a stun.
   - Cost: 15 stamina, 18 second cooldown.
   - Balance: telegraphed by a bright line on the ground; staggers for under a second; cannot be used on someone who is already staggered.
   - Derived from: Irelia E, minus thrown blades. MINE for the "sabre crossing" form.

3. **Counter Flurry** (Riposte)
   - Does: when a melee attack misses or is parried, she answers with a fast three-cut counter (one real, two echoes).
   - Cost: 15 stamina plus her reaction; once per round, and only while in her stance.
   - Balance: only against melee misses; damage low; players can bait it.
   - Derived from: Battle Master Riposte, Parry.

4. **Defiant Whirl** (wall stance)
   - Does: she holds a spinning guard for about three seconds (reduces damage from the front by half and cannot be knocked back), then releases a sweeping cut that hits everyone adjacent.
   - Cost: 25 stamina, 30 second cooldown.
   - Balance: she cannot advance during the guard; release damage is capped at a fixed value; a ranged enemy or a flank bypasses the guard.
   - Derived from: Irelia W, Bladesinger Song of Defense, Battle Master Parry.

5. **Slip-Through Step** (Bladesurge and Fancy Footwork)
   - Does: dash past or through an enemy line, giving a free cut to one target and avoiding opportunity attacks from anyone she hit.
   - Cost: 10 stamina, 8 second cooldown.
   - Balance: reset only on killing blow, and at most once per fight [MINE cap]; only short range.
   - Derived from: Irelia Q, Swashbuckler Fancy Footwork.

6. **Pirouette Dodge** (Evasive Footwork)
   - Does: reaction to avoid a single hit entirely and reposition about ten feet.
   - Cost: 15 stamina, 20 second cooldown.
   - Balance: once per round; does not work against area attacks; leaves her out of line.
   - Derived from: Battle Master Evasive Footwork and Parry.

7. **Cue the Line** (Dance refresh, Commander's Strike)
   - Does: she points with a sabre and a nearby Thrash ally immediately gets a follow-up attack or step.
   - Cost: 10 stamina, once per fight, 30 second cooldown within it.
   - Balance: no healing; no stat buff; only one ally; she skips one of her own attacks that turn.
   - Derived from: Fire Emblem Dance, Battle Master Commander's Strike. Adapted by me.

8. **Five-Beat Finale** (the five-strike move)
   - Does: at full Tempo, a spiral of two real strikes and three echoes on one target in about two seconds.
   - Cost: all five Tempo, 30 stamina, once per fight.
   - Balance: total damage about 1.5 times a normal pair of cuts per action (see 3.1); if she is staggered during the opening beat, it fizzles; she is exposed for a second afterwards.
   - Derived from: Irelia R and Q combined with Virtuoso/Mesmer shatter (consume stored hits). MINE in form.

9. **Kit skill: Challenging Howl** (keep)
   - Per the brief she keeps this. Tabletop shape: Panache plus Goading Attack. Enemies in earshot choose her as target for a short time.
   - Suggested bounds [MINE]: wisdom-style save for players; a successful save gives 24 hour immunity (from Intimidating Presence); duration capped around six seconds; cannot be used again for a minute.

10. **Kit skill: Toxic Breath** (never used)
   - Keep as an unused skill. Suggest it exists only as a character beat (she has it, chooses not to use it).

**Passives (always on, small and bounded)**

11. **Tempo (momentum)**
   - Each landed strike or successful dodge gives one Tempo, up to five; Tempo decays after six seconds without action; being staggered drops Tempo to zero.
   - Effect: each Tempo gives a small attack speed bonus; the first echo is unlocked at Tempo two, the second at four.
   - Derived from: Irelia Ionian Fervor.

12. **Dancer's Footing (Bladesong-lite)**
   - While wielding two one-handed sabres with no shield and light outfit, she gets a small bonus to evasion and movement speed.
   - Balance: gone if she picks up a shield or two-handed weapon, or is staggered.
   - Derived from: Bladesinger Bladesong.

13. **Fancy Footwork**
   - After she hits a foe, that foe cannot take an opportunity attack against her for the rest of that turn.
   - Derived from: Swashbuckler Fancy Footwork.

14. **Strategist's Read (Studied Target)**
   - After watching one foe for a few seconds, the first strike against that foe has a small bonus; only one studied foe at a time.
   - Derived from: Slayer Studied Target, Rakish Audacity.

**Spell flag:** none. If the author wants one beat of "magic", it should be game-system cosmetics only (visual trails), not an in-world spell. [MINE]

### 2.2 SEVISH THRASH, L80 Berserker, "calm and heavy"

Core identity [MINE]: very high damage, very slow and readable. He takes damage as a resource. He picks an honourable fight and holds himself back unless someone earns more.

**Physical arts**

1. **Reckless Swing** (Reckless Attack)
   - Does: he drops his guard on purpose; his attacks are more accurate for a few seconds but everyone else hits him more easily.
   - Cost: no stamina, 20 second cooldown, lasts about eight seconds.
   - Balance: a visible stance change (he opens his stance and laughs); players can exploit the open guard.
   - Derived from: Barbarian Reckless Attack.

2. **Frenzy Chain** (Frenzy)
   - Does: an extra axe swing after each main swing for about ten seconds.
   - Cost: 25 stamina, 45 second cooldown.
   - Balance: after it ends he is winded for five seconds (slower, no Howl, reduced guard); it only fires below 75 percent HP.
   - Derived from: Berserker Frenzy.

3. **Reaping Arc** (Sweeping Attack)
   - Does: a wide axe cleave that hits everyone in front of him.
   - Cost: 20 stamina, 10 second cooldown.
   - Balance: long wind up, wide telegraphed arc, a half second opening after.
   - Derived from: Battle Master Sweeping Attack.

4. **Earthsplitter** (heavy overhead strike, Brutal Critical feel)
   - Does: a very large single hit, with armour against interruption while he winds up.
   - Cost: 30 stamina, 30 second cooldown.
   - Balance: three second wind up with a loud audio cue; two second recovery; a perfect dodge makes him miss and open.
   - Derived from: Dark Souls wind up, poise and punish windows, Brutal Critical.

5. **Answering Blow** (Retaliation)
   - Does: instant counter swing against anyone who just hit him in melee.
   - Cost: his reaction, once per round, 10 stamina.
   - Balance: only against melee; limited damage; not usable while winded.
   - Derived from: Berserker Retaliation.

6. **Honour Challenge** (Challenge, Goading, Panache)
   - Does: he points his axe at one opponent; for the length of the challenge he fights only that opponent, and others cannot be targeted by him.
   - Cost: once per fight; ends when the challenger yields, is downed, or after about thirty seconds.
   - Balance: he never strikes a downed opponent; he will not use arts that hit the whole group during it.
   - Derived from: Foe Foundry Challenge, Swashbuckler Panache. The honour twist is MINE.

7. **Kit skill: Challenging Howl** (keep)
   - Per brief he has the roar that pinned L2 to 3 players. Bound it: save-based, 24 hour immunity for anyone who passes the save or stands up (from Intimidating Presence), and the NPC laughs when someone stands. Cooldown one to two minutes.
8. **Kit skill: Toxic Breath** (long unused)
   - Keep as an unused skill; treat as a character beat. If used at all, it should be a deliberately dramatic last resort.

**Passives**

9. **Pain Is Proof** (Bloodied Rage)
   - Below 50 percent HP, his attacks hit harder and he moves faster by a small, fixed amount.
   - Balance: he also takes extra damage from critical hits and loses his guard; the bonus is capped.
   - Derived from: Foe Foundry Bloodied Rage; Berserker.

10. **Stand Again** (Relentless Rage)
   - Once per fight, if he would be dropped, he stays on his feet with a sliver of HP; any next use is not available.
   - Derived from: Relentless Rage.

11. **Hyper Armour** (poise)
   - He ignores small hits mid-swing but is staggered by a heavy blow, a crit, or a burst of hits in a short window.
   - Derived from: Dark Souls poise.

12. **Fearless** (Mindless Rage)
   - Cannot be frightened or charmed while in a stance; cannot be pulled by Howl-style effects.
   - Derived from: Mindless Rage.

13. **Measure of the Bold** (my own idea)
   - Fights at the opponent's measure unless provoked, by hitting a fraction of full strength against much lower-level foes, unless they are in a duel; honour forbids crushing someone beneath him.
   - Own idea (Kenpachi-style self-restraint, not from a tabletop source).

**Spell flag:** none.

## PART 3. DESIGN TRICKS

### 3.1 Making two blades feel like three to five strikes [MINE unless marked]
1. Real hits versus echoes. Only two blades, so only two real strikes per action. Echoes are afterimage hits that land a beat later. [Concept from Irelia E, Mesmer clones, Virtuoso blades.]
2. Delay the echo by one beat so the player can read it; echoes should arrive from a different angle than the first cut.
3. Counter-based escalation. Use a Tempo counter capped at five. Three-strike feel at Tempo two; five-strike feel at Tempo four. [From Ionian Fervor and the Mesmer cap of three.]
4. Echoes are small. Give each echo a third of a normal hit so the total stays inside the damage budget (about 1.5 times a two-blade pair per action).
5. Echoes die with the first cut. Parry or dodge of the real strike cancels the echoes. This gives players a clear counter.
6. Stored hits. Hits that land bank a blade or echo for later; a finale pays them out. [Virtuoso blade stocking; Mesmer shatter.]
7. Rhythm. Use a three-beat cadence for the small combo and a five-beat cadence for the finale; give the beats a sound cue (a chime or a cloth snap) so players can learn them.
8. Visual signature: trailing ribbons of her sash, a faint shimmer after each cut, and a performer's step pattern. In HGO, these can be skill effects rendered by the game, not magic.
9. Never stack more than two echo sources in the same round (cap).
10. Tie echoes to movement. Dash, spin, then cut, so the afterimage seems earned by footwork rather than free.

### 3.2 Making Sevish dangerous without being a god
1. Big hits are slow and telegraphed (Dark Souls wind up).
2. Every big move leaves an opening afterwards.
3. Resource limits: a stamina pool and cooldowns; at zero stamina he is base attack only.
4. Frenzy has a crash (Barbarian exhaustion).
5. Thresholds: Pain Is Proof only below half HP; Frenzy only below 75 percent; Stand Again once.
6. Voluntary downsides: Reckless Swing gives players advantage on him.
7. Honour as limiter: he fights one opponent at a time; he does not hit downed opponents; he fights at the measure of the opponent (my idea).
8. Immunities that keep the fear fair: pass the Howl save once and you are immune for a day.
9. Do not give him a lot of defences. Use hit points and poise rather than damage immunities.
10. Make danger legible: reading his stance is the game. Players at level 5 to 8 should be able to survive by reading, not by stats.

## PART 4. BALANCE PRINCIPLES FOR BOSS AND UNIQUE NPCs

1. Boss action economy (Nerds and Scoundrels, https://www.nerdsandscoundrels.com/legendary-actions-5e/): one boss gets one action per round against many players; legendary actions (usually three per round, more costly options cost two or three) restore parity. For this camp, the NPCs are not enemies by default, so consider using the budget as a hard cap on art use: Wyx spends at most two stamina-arts per round, Sevish at most one, plus reactions. [Application is MINE.]
2. Legendary resistance is usually about three uses per day; it protects the boss from being locked down. For Sevish the Mindless Rage immunity does this job; no need for a separate resistance.
3. Pace power with recharges and limited uses (Foe Foundry). Every art in the palettes has a cooldown or a stamina cost.
4. Tie abilities to drama (Foe Foundry): bloodied thresholds, once per fight defiant moments, honour duels.
5. Telegraph big actions (Foe Foundry and Dark Souls): players remember a clear tell more than a big number.
6. Give unique abilities flavour first (Sly Flourish): wrap mechanics in sound, sight and character.
7. Unique ability rule of three [MINE]: each NPC has one signature move (Wyx: the echo; Sevish: the stand-and-laugh), one defensive gimmick, and one limiter, and no more.
8. Level gap rule [MINE]: with level 5 to 8 players, a L25 and L80 NPC should never use full output on a player. Use the Measure of the Bold passive for Sevish and a skill-contest cap (about half output) for Wyx unless she is in a real duel, so the numbers do not need to be tuned for each player level.
9. Damage ceilings [MINE]: define a "non-lethal tier" in which NPC attacks cap damage below a typical player max HP, so a mistake does not delete a player; reserve the lethal tier for a real duel the player opted into.
10. Avoid HP inflation; use stagger thresholds and exposed windows to let low level players contribute (Dark Souls poise and punish windows).
11. Do not give either NPC both strong defence and strong offence at once; Wyx is defensive with moderate damage; Sevish is offensive with moderate defence.
12. Reaction cap: one reaction per round each (Retaliation, Riposte), so they cannot chain counters.
