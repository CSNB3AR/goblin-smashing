## The hill, seen from below [AUTHOR]

## The tent assault [AUTHOR]
- Order: Hushveil goes up first; when it drops the first time, the raiders try to get out; that is when the gas comes in. Ru and Zinka are on the sides of the tent with Toxic Breath. Resisters (Dwarf, Human) throw up on the first one or two procs; the fourth proc forces them out. They hold their breath, then the last proc hits, they are stunned in the tent for about eight seconds. That is the window for the strike unit to move, and for Arya to recover mana.
- The rune is drawn in the sand in front of the tent entrance (hand drawn, base arcana rune, triangle of jagged claws, points not joined), with a stun and a debuff, so anyone who comes out is stunned again. It must be a rune with stun, because the stun is what lets Ru and Zinka get clear. Each attempt costs MP; he misdraws twice (10 MP each, PROPOSAL), the third lands inside Arya's ten second whistle window.
- Hushveil stage 2 adds the deafen. The mist then gives three debuffs: blind, silence (no spells, no arts), deafen. Stage 2 costs three points, which is why Arya objects; Tatsuya knows why it is needed and does not explain. Allies are not debuffed.
