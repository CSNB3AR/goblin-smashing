# Act Two: The Haya Storm Rise Arc

Picks up immediately where `outline/act-one-winter-bash.md` (the Crownfall arc)'s beat 16 leaves off. A sequence of story beats, not a chapter map.

## Locked for this arc

- **Haya Storm is a guild, not a permanent trio.** The 3-person core from Crownfall, Tatsuya (Arcane Bomber), Arya (fog/silence control, detonator), Pac_Pac (Beastfolk brawler/tank with Enrage), stays together and is who this arc builds around, with troops filling the Crownfall side and real players added as earned. The other two roster slots get filled somewhere across this arc, real characters worked out once we're actually drafting that far, not placeholders invented now.
- **The Tochi representative tournament is real and escalates the Eren rivalry to a genuine climax.** Tatsuya and Eren both vying for the same intra-school representative slot (or facing off inside it) is what finally forces a real confrontation, not just Loop-post secondhand tension like Act One. This can genuinely be Eren's full crash-out, the rivalry subplot's own file already frames the "real" Eren payoff as happening on the competitive stage eventually, this tournament is a legitimate candidate for that reckoning rather than a placeholder skirmish, though it doesn't have to be the final, permanent resolution of the rivalry, just the real confrontation this arc has been building toward.
- **The Inter-High tournament follows**, third-party-licensed (see `codex/pro-scene/inter-high-tournament.md`), cross-school eligibility, this is where the roster's cross-school makeup (Tochi/local school/homeschooled) actually gets to compete together on the page, and where Haya Storm's public profile starts growing past "Crownfall underdog story" into a real, ongoing scene presence.

## Story beats, in order

1. Tatsuya starts at Tochi as a first-year, behind Eren, the re-entry itself is its own real adjustment (per the already-locked "not ahead of Eren" detail, see `codex/characters/eren-tabuchi.md`). He and Yuna are still reconciling off the back of Act One's PK_Wraith deception.
2. Pod-building/parts logistics thread continues, the concrete trigger being the first-gen pod finally giving out at the end of Act One right as he has the money to fix it properly (see `codex/objects/pod-hardware.md`), the reason he came back in the first place.
3. Haya Storm starts training seriously as a trio, working out its actual identity past "the team that beat Eren once," Pac_Pac and Arya's own school lives folded in around practice scheduling.
4. The Tochi representative tournament (see `codex/pro-scene/tochi-representative-qualifier.md`): **Eren's team holds the incumbent representative slot**, Tochi-backed, Tabuchi-funded. Tatsuya and Eren on a collision course for it, PK_Wraith still teamed with Eren into this fight, a real continuation of Crownfall's pairing.
5. Escalates past Loop-post distance into a real, direct confrontation, the rivalry's real climax, debt and the "unfinished conversation with Daichi" bait either surface here directly or detonate right after. (How far Eren's own spiral goes stays open for when we're actually drafting.)
6. **Haya Storm wins**, taking Tochi's representative slot from Eren's team.
7. On the strength of that win, Tochi extends real **scholarships** (not just a sponsorship gesture) to both Pac_Pac and Arya, enrolling them at the school itself.
8. For Arya specifically this is the real turn: it demonstrates she earned this on tournament merit, not charity, and it's the actual legal linchpin in her and Leon's reopened custody fight (see `codex/subplots/leon-arya-custody-battle.md`), not just a reason to resist her mother's push to America.
9. Real pushback follows, family and/or their own lawyers showing up to contest it, and Leon steps in, flatly stating he'll bring in lawyers of his own if it comes to that, a real, dramatized conflict, not settled quickly or quietly.
10. **New:** Tatsuya's own leverage from the win, he negotiates to route some of Tochi's backing toward Sakura Arcade instead of requiring the team be based on campus, a real financial boost for Leon's business (see `codex/characters/leon.md`).
11. **New:** Sana Domoto and Nao Fujita, the two Crimson Static guildmates who turned on Arya after the guild's finale-week collapse (see `codex/characters/arya.md`), resurface now that she's a real, publicly visible player, fresh esports-scene drama, callouts and rumor-mongering, consistent with what already cost her the guild once.
12. **New:** Pac_Pac's own real-world rap career starts taking off alongside the team's public profile, drops a single (see `codex/characters/pac-pac.md`).
13. The Inter-High tournament itself (see `codex/pro-scene/inter-high-tournament.md`): cross-school eligibility lets the full roster compete under one banner, public attention grows past the Crownfall underdog story into a real ongoing scene presence.
14. Somewhere across this stretch, Haya Storm's roster actually grows to a five-player representing squad for Tochi, the two new players introduced and folded into the team's established synergy rather than replacing it, and per the roster-composition rule (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`) these two, not Pac_Pac or Arya, are the ones who need to actually attend Tochi. **Ryo Tachibana** (see `codex/characters/ryo-tachibana.md`), a quiet, unconventional Kansai Black Lotus-era teammate of Eren's and Haruto's who attends Tochi, is a live candidate for one of these two slots, keeps the roster co-ed alongside Arya. The second slot stays genuinely open.
15. **New, flagged, not detailed:** once Tatsuya's making real money, Momo and Taji end up discovering his T-Drag identity (see `codex/characters/momo-kazehaya.md`, `codex/characters/taji-kazehaya.md`).
16. **The Siege Pro League is an option, not Tatsuya's path.** It is active from the start of Season One and Kazuku mentions it for players who prefer that route to the open-world wars. Siege, introduced at Crownfall as a battle royale, draws crowds, and a pro scene forms around it. Tatsuya reacts through money, Dad's letter and Eren, never as a fan. Format and league notes: `codex/lore/hearthfire-expansion-and-seasons.md`.
17. By the end of this arc, Haya Storm is a real, popular breakout guild, not full pro status yet, positioned for Season One (see `codex/pro-scene/haya-storm.md`).

## Running throughlines, not resolved this arc

Ohema's debt continuing to shrink through Tatsuya's quiet payments, Yuna's slow-burn advancing in parallel (her family situation actively darkening now, running alongside but distinct from Ohema's own six-month clock, see `codex/subplots/yuna-family-inheritance-plot.md`) without taking over the team-focused chapters, the Tabuchi pressure not fully resolved even after the Eren confrontation, since the family debt itself is a separate lever from Eren's personal rivalry. **Myrrhwood stays fully dormant through both acts**, nothing moves there until after the tournament/expansion arc closes, a deliberate hold, not an oversight.
