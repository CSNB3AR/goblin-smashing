## 3. RULES CHAPTER ELEVEN MUST OBEY

- No metagame, for every character. Name the source before anyone acts on a fact.
- Leon's voice: **[DIRECTOR IN BOOTH: VOICE CHANNEL]** bracket every time it opens in game, no bare "Leon said" from nowhere.
- No em dashes, en dashes, spaced hyphens or double hyphens. No ellipses in narration. In dialogue "..." only on a true trail off, two per chapter at most.
- One speaker per paragraph, every line with a real body, posture or tone cue.
- Thoughts in italics and quotation marks, short, present tense. Body cue first, inner thought only for a real inference. Do not use a thought where a visible reaction does it.
- Show every arrival and exit; nobody teleports. Add a short travel beat for each move (holding tent to center of camp, and the climb down the east face).
- Keep Tatsuya in the scene with body cues while others talk. Return to the body after tactical or system passages. One or two perceptions at a time.
- NPCs call players "adventurers", not "players". No "top dog" or "alpha" labels. No gamer slang to NPCs (no "XP", "loot", "buff", "meta").
- NPCs speak in a medieval register. Third person for Kesh, Rott and Nab only.
- No fond Eren memory. Capped crutches (jaw, stomach, pulse, breath, shoulders, knee, throat, blink) once each per chapter and not repeated from Chapter Ten.
- Numbers must mean something and arrive through a person (Leon, Wyx, a panel), not a lecture. No lore dumps, no roster math.
- No self explanation after a beat. Do not explain the plan to the reader before Tatsuya teaches it; let Sevish and Wyx react.
- A revision replaces, it does not add.
