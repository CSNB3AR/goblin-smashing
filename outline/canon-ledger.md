## Game structure

- Hearthfire is the expansion (January 1st, five continents, Season One). Open beta rolls into Season One and open-beta seasonal points count. Crownfall opens Season One and introduces Siege.
- Crownfall is named for the king's fall (nobody on the page knows yet). Hearthfire stays the expansion's name (OPEN if the author wants it renamed).
- Siege is a battle royale: 5 guilds per session, 20 players each, a camp that upgrades to level 3 and becomes a fort, a guild flag, 60 lives in 3 waves, 3 hours. Winners earn shillings and Ember cores. Crownfall is guild-only, in a neutral zone, ten million yen. Node Wars are the main war after Hearthfire and last weeks and months.
- The Siege Pro League is active from the start of Season One as an option Kazuku mentions. Tatsuya stays on the open-world path.
- The king (Aldemar Vaereth) dies around the Season One start. The royal funeral is a truce with no battling in the royal lands (scope and length OPEN). After it the princes (Dorrin, Ilyan, Maldren) move against each other and the map moves.
