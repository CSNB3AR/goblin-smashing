## Chapter-by-Chapter Summary

### Chapter 1: No Respawns in Shibuya
WattsUp closing shift. Chiyo's text about Tabuchi pressure and the Tochi option. Eren and his butler/driver arrive to charge their car; Eren threatens Rin over the debt in front of Haruto (customer) and the butler. Winter Championship broadcast plays throughout. Ends with the Crystal League expansion reveal and Rin buying the key with almost his last yen.

### Chapter 2: The Cost of a Gamble
(Referenced in project files but not included in the batches read, covers the scooter ride and picking up the drive, per earlier discussion in this project.)

### Chapter 3: The World Tilts Sideways
Morning after. Full family breakfast scene, Chiyo, Momo, Taji, genuinely warm, well-written domestic chapter. News broadcast covers Kazuku/Nexus acquiring Southeast Asian oil and gas reserves, market panic, UN emergency session (this is the real-world-stakes approach now superseded, see Structural/Craft Notes below). Chiyo mentions the Tochi application deadline (Jan 4th). Rin does a full-day odyssey trying to find an open VR pod slot in Tokyo, every cafe is booked solid because of Christmas Bash hype, before landing on Sakura Arcade as a last resort. First meeting with Leon and the pods. The "Kazuhaya" name hits him hard seeing it on the pod. First login attempt fails (game not compatible, system update required), he manually overrides the pod himself, and the second attempt drops him in as a goblin in Lunarest Outskirts.

### Chapter 4: Three Days to Bleed
First moments as a goblin. Two Outlaw NPCs (Gornak, Vesh) pick a fight; Rin/Kaiseki uses "Goblin Gas" and it accidentally combos with an NPC's fireball into a "Goblin Flare" explosion that kills both. Wyx Thrash (chieftain's daughter) and Sevish Thrash (goblin chieftain, Level 80) intervene. Sevish gives him three days to prove himself or be treated as an enemy, bring intel on a way into Lunarest. He picks the in-game name Kaiseki.

### Chapter 5: Lanterns in the Dark
Held in the war tent. Accepts the World Quest "Call of the Bones" via a UI prompt. The ghost king Shiv appears in vision form and gives cryptic quest direction (seek shrines, gather bones, three nights to prove his name). Logs out. Returns to Sakura Arcade, extended, strong scene with Leon, who recognizes the manual pod override as something only someone trained by "Ikko/Daichi Kazehaya" could do, and strikes a deal: fix the arcade's second dead pod, get playing privileges. TV news continues the oil/gas real-world plot thread. Heads to the hospital to visit his mother, warm, detailed hospital-staff relationship (Ms. Sugiyama, Dr. Inoue). Meets Yuna/Yunaile in the hallway (she drops a box of fan mail/pastries), full meet-cute, she invites him to a "Goblin XP event" at Luxor Lakes on the 22nd. Dr. Inoue reveals a real deadline on Ohema's surgery. Gets a threatening message from the Tabuchi family retainer referencing "closed caskets."

### Chapter 6: Borrowed Time, Broken Roads
Mostly transitional, riding to the hospital, Loop feed shows "Kaiseki" trending after the World Quest activation (pro players and community reacting without knowing his real identity). Repeats/extends the hospital and Yuna material from Chapter 5. Fires back at the Tabuchi retainer with a direct threat of his own.

### Chapter 6.5: No Light Without Shadow
**POV chapter from Eren's perspective, breaks the "locked to Tatsuya" rule that's now non-negotiable for the redraft.** Eren is summoned by his father, Kazuto Tabuchi, who is cold, controlling, and furious about the team's financial losses and lack of results. Kazuto explicitly references knowing Eren "leaned on" Rin for the original build and hints at deeper, darker knowledge about what happened to "the man who owed too much and tried to fight back," implying real complicity in Daichi's death, not just debt pressure. Kazuto strips Eren of financial control, hands it to the family butler Shinji Takamura. Later, Haruto Murayama, Kansai Black Lotus's star tank and Rookie of the Year, a much bigger character in the old draft than the minor customer he became in the new Chapter 1, confronts Eren and quits the team on principle, publicly calling out the stolen build and Eren's toxic leadership on Loop, hinting the "real creator" should "come out and play." Chapter ends with Eren vowing revenge.

### Chapter 7: My Name Is Kaiseki
Rin gets home. Chiyo's note reveals she's filed a restraining order against the Tabuchis and referenced a "Prime Minister Yazuri" law protecting students at top academies from family-based targeting. Rin decides to make a clean break, deletes his old, barely-used Loop account and creates @Kaiseki_HGO, follows HGO-adjacent accounts including Yuna, who follows back immediately. Sets an alarm, plans to grind hard with the twins away for three days.

### Chapter 8: The Secrets of the Goblin Sorcerer, Part I
Quiet morning alone in the apartment. Chiyo's message confirms she suspects Tabuchi involvement in Daichi's death directly. **Rin decides to quit WattsUp entirely to focus on the game** (flag, see Structural/Craft Notes). Heads to Sakura Arcade and meets Arya for the first time (spelled "Aria" here), Leon's younger sister, red hair, street-biker aesthetic, her own silver HGO drive, sharp and guarded. She clocks his green drive and internally judges the goblin race choice, confirming the drive-color-reveals-race mechanic already lived in this draft. She starts researching him almost immediately (this pacing is now superseded, see Structural/Craft Notes).

### Chapter 9: The Secrets of the Goblin Sorcerer, Part II (The Spirit of Shiv)
Major lore chapter. In-game time moves faster than real time (he's been gone almost two full in-game days). The goblin who was imprisoned alongside him was sent to the front line in his place and died, a real, weighty consequence, and confirms a seven-day death lock penalty for dying in this game. Shiv appears properly: a spectral goblin spirit, ash-and-smoke, wide-brimmed hat, burning green eyes, genuinely well-written mystical character. Grants Kaiseki a legendary bound item, the first piece of "Shiv's Warset" (1 of 7): the Call of the Bones Necklace, which negates the goblin INT penalty and unlocks the Shaman's Veil spirit magic path. Also grants the "Ancient Boneflame Barrage" ultimate, an AoE trap spell that gets bonus damage/radius when combined with toxin effects (a deliberate combo system with Goblin Gas). Reveals worldbuilding: goblins are historically barred from the capital; there's a hidden "Astralum Archive" beneath the palace that Shiv wants Kaiseki to eventually reach. Rin visibly changes appearance somewhat after bonding with the relic, taller, sharper features, and other goblins start treating him with more deference. He starts a public stream mid-quest; chat reactions do a lot of organic "this is unusual" work without any character having to explain it outright.

### Chapter 10: Call of the Boneflame, Part III
Rin/Kaiseki, now visibly changed, returns to the war camp. Sevish and Wyx treat the Shiv relic as a real honor. Sevish assigns him a squad for a tactical strike: Grukk/Krok (thick, grim), Tikka (small, wiry, pyromaniac energy), Muzzle (steady, burn-scarred, controlled), and Drosh (hobgoblin lieutenant, disciplined, tattooed). **These four are very likely the exact companions referenced in earlier feedback about the story assigning companions too fast without earning the relationships**, worth preserving the character designs, but their introduction needs a complete pacing rework so trust is built gradually rather than assigned in one scene. Establishes the "Goblin Rally" passive (stat bonus scaling with nearby allied goblins) and a "Tactical Cartographer" title earned for drawing up the ambush plan, a nice mechanical reward for game-knowledge/strategy over raw stats, which is exactly the King's Avatar energy the new direction wants. Chat shows "AriaSky" watching his stream, a soft, workable way she could track him from a distance before ever meeting him in person.

### Chapter 11 / 11.5: The Circle in the Mist, Parts IV–V
Extended combat setpiece. Drosh's "Veil-Pierce Gaze" lets Rin scout an enemy player camp through canvas walls, reveals a four-player guild party (New Covenant tag) affiliated with a named PvP faction leader, PK_Wraith, using cashed-in Legacy Point gear. Rin plans and executes a coordinated ambush: two goblins gas the tent from both sides, stacking a "Nauseous" debuff toward an "Overload" stun, while Tikka baits the party out and Rin's runed Boneflame Barrage (boosted by the toxin combo) wipes three of four instantly. Drosh finishes the fourth with a physical art after their racial survival skill saves them once. Triggers a Karma/Alignment system, killing players outside sanctioned PvP flips his nameplate from white to red ("Blood Tinted"), a strong, reusable consequence mechanic for open-world PvP that reinforces real stakes.

## Mechanics Worth Preserving From the Old Draft

- **Skill combo system:** certain abilities combine for bonus effects when used together (Goblin Gas + fire = "Goblin Flare"; toxin effects boost Ancient Boneflame Barrage's damage/radius). Reusable, funny, and mechanically satisfying, fits the deck-building pillar well.
- **Karma/Alignment system:** killing players outside sanctioned PvP shifts alignment and visibly changes your nameplate color (white to red). Strong, visible consequence system for open-world PvP.
- **Seven-day death lock:** dying carries a real week-long penalty, not just a respawn timer. Reinforces genuine stakes.
- **In-game time dilation:** time passes faster inside HGO than in the real world. Useful but has real pacing implications, flag for confirmation before reusing, since it affects how a "three day quest timer" interacts with his real-world schedule.
- **Live streaming as an organic exposition tool:** chat reactions ("is this really Kaiseki," "why is that NPC planning with him") did real work explaining why something on-screen was unusual, without narration or an announcer having to state it. Strong craft technique worth reusing deliberately.
- **Prestige/ranking ladder:** numeric tiers (Platinum 40K, Diamond 75K, Master 120K+) as a visible competitive ladder, good LitRPG texture if Bobby wants a concrete ranking system.
- **Physical Arts as a distinct skill category** (e.g., Drosh's Veil-Pierce Gaze, Silent Three Fang) separate from spells, gives goblins/hobgoblins a distinct combat identity from caster classes.
- **"Shiv's Warset"** — a 7-piece legendary item set tied to the world quest. Strong deck-building-flavored long-term collection goal.
- **Tactical Cartographer title** — an earned bonus for drawing up battle plans, not just fighting. Mechanically rewards the brains-over-brawn playstyle the new direction wants front and center.

## Structural / Craft Notes — What Not to Repeat

- **The Eren POV chapter (6.5) breaks the now-locked "tight third-person on Tatsuya always" rule.** Any content from it that's worth keeping (Kazuto's dialogue, Haruto's exit) needs to reach the reader through Tatsuya's own observation or secondhand knowledge, not a direct scene cut to Eren's world.
- **Real-world stakes were built around Kazuku buying Southeast Asian oil and gas reserves, UN emergency sessions, and market collapse headlines.** This has been explicitly superseded, the new direction keeps real-world stakes vague and esports/economy-focused rather than geopolitical-thriller-flavored.
- **Companions (Grukk, Tikka, Muzzle, Drosh) were all introduced and assigned as a squad in a single scene.** This is the exact pacing problem flagged earlier, trust with goblin companions needs to build gradually, one relationship at a time, likely over multiple chapters, not handed out by chieftain's decree.
- **Arya/Aria clocks and starts researching Tatsuya almost immediately on his second arcade visit.** Now explicitly superseded, the confirmed direction wants multiple visits and organic noticing (business changing, being turned down from pod access herself) before she ever approaches him.
- **Yuna is met and fully introduced within the first week, in Chapter 5.** The new direction wants her identity withheld until later, with only Momo's fandom and the broadcast voice planting her earlier. If any of this hospital meet-cute material is wanted, it needs to be resequenced much later in the timeline.
- **Rin quits his job at WattsUp entirely to focus on the game.** Superseded, the new direction has leaned on Gato's store as an ongoing grounding, working-class anchor throughout the early chapters, which is in tension with dropping the job this early. Tatsuya keeps the job.
- Chapter 1 of the old draft has an author's note breaking the fourth wall mid-chapter (asking readers to vote on chapter length preference). Not a story issue, just noting it's not part of the narrative and won't carry forward.
- **The old draft's Chapter 3 "full-day odyssey"** (Rin riding his moped to cafe after cafe across Tokyo looking for an open pod) is also superseded as of this pass, see `outline/chapter-outline.md`. The search-for-a-pod beat is compressed into an online booking search plus one deliberate trip to Sakura Arcade, and the "three days to prove himself" ultimatum no longer triggers an immediate log-out, Tatsuya lives in camp for a stretch first.
