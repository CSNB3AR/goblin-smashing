# Chapter Four: Into Bloodhurst II

Heavy footsteps behind him. Multiple sets, moving with purpose. He turned fast, weight already shifting back into a ready stance before he'd consciously thought about it, his body responding on instinct.

Three goblins in real armor stepped into view, not the rags everyone else was wearing but actual fitted leather and metal plates, weapons drawn but not raised yet. White tags floated above their heads, bright and clear.

"You're coming with us," one of them said, voice flat and professional. 

Another one peeled off toward the supply crate where the smaller goblin was still pressed against the wood, eyes wide. "You too, kid."

They didn't bind either of them or even touch them, just fell in on either side and started walking, a clear flanking position that made it obvious running wasn't an option. Goblins throughout the camp stopped what they were doing to watch as they passed, some of them whispering to each other, a few pointing. The woman with the wild green hair and copper rings fell a few steps behind them.

They stopped in front of a tent bigger than all the rest, wide enough to fit a dozen people inside if they packed in tight. Bone totems were carved into the posts on either side of the entrance flap, intricate patterns that caught the firelight and threw strange shadows across the canvas. One of the guards pulled the flap open and gestured for them to go inside.

Tatsuya ducked through, the kid right behind him, and the temperature dropped immediately. The air inside was cooler, darker, lit only by a single oil lamp hanging from the center pole that threw uneven, flickering light up the canvas walls. A scarred goblin sat behind a crude table made from stacked crates and a plank of wood, a bone-carved staff leaning against the side of his chair within easy reach. His eyes were sharp, taking in both of them in one sweep, and when the woman with the copper rings stepped in behind them and took up a position at his shoulder, something in the air shifted, became more formal.

**[SEVISH THRASH - CHIEFTAIN - LEVEL 80]**

The woman's nameplate appeared a second later when Tatsuya's eyes tracked to her.

**[WYX THRASH - LEVEL 25]**

*"Eighty and twenty-five. I'm level two. I’m dead if I say the wrong thing here.”*

Sevish's eyes went from Tatsuya to the kid and back, taking his time before he spoke. The silence stretched, broken only by the faint crackle of the oil lamp and the distant sounds of camp life continuing outside.

"So," Sevish finally said, voice deep and rough, the kind of voice that didn't need to be loud to carry weight. "You two causing trouble in my camp."

The kid opened his mouth, about to speak, but Tatsuya cut in before he could get a word out. "They came at him first. Two on one over so I stepped in."

"And killed two of my men while doing that," Sevish said, leaning forward slightly, elbows resting on the makeshift table. 

"They came at me after I got involved," Tatsuya said, keeping his hands flat on his thighs "I did what I had to do to still be standing."

Sevish leaned back, the wood of his chair creaking under his weight, and something that might have been approval flickered across his scarred face, eyes still locked on Tatsuya's. "Not bad."

Tatsuya kept his breathing steady, his posture as non-threatening as he could manage while still being ready to move if this went sideways. "Would've been peaceful if they'd let it. But I'm not about to watch goblins waste time on each other when there's clearly bigger shit to deal with." 

The moment the words left his mouth, Sevish went completely still. Beside him, Wyx's sharp eyes locked onto Tatsuya's face with an intensity that hadn't been there a second ago, her whole body language shifting into something more alert. Both of their gold tags flickered at the edges, not changing color to yellow or red, just flickering like a notification trying to fire and being held back by something.

*"What now!?  Did I say something wrong?"*

Sevish let out a short laugh, the sound genuine and a little surprised. "Bigger problems than fighting each other." He glanced at Wyx, and something passed between them in that look, a whole conversation Tatsuya couldn't read but could feel the weight of. When Sevish looked back at him, his expression had shifted into something more serious, more considering. "That's the kind of thinking we've been missing around here."

A purple notification bloomed at the edge of Tatsuya's vision, the color immediately drawing his attention.

**[WORLD QUEST DETECTED]**

**[CALL OF THE BONES – STAGE 1 ACTIVATED]**

*"The world quest is back? “*Did I really just advance it?”

Sevish stood up from his chair, the movement slow and deliberate, and walked around the table with heavy, purposeful steps that made the packed dirt floor creak slightly under his weight. He stopped a few feet away from Tatsuya, close enough that Tatsuya had to tilt his head back slightly to maintain eye contact, and crossed his arms over his broad chest.

"You killed two of my men," Sevish said, voice carrying absolute certainty. "Justified or not, that makes you a threat. I can't just let you walk out of here without knowing more" He paused, letting that sink in, then continued. "You have  shown more in one fight than most of this camp has shown in weeks. Outside my sister." A nod toward Wyx, who was still watching Tatsuya with that sharp, evaluating gaze. "So here's what I'm offering you."

He laid it out plain and rough, no sugarcoating, just the facts.

"Myrrhwood is hell right now. Gets worse every year. Shiv's been gone for over a century and the clans have never found their footing again without his leadership. That's the real reason Wyx and I gathered people and came all the way out here to find what Shiv was hiding before he disappeared, and maybe bring that knowledge back to our people so we can actually survive what's coming."

"We've tried the rally three times now," Sevish continued, his tone matter-of-fact, not defensive. "Failed all three times. Couldn't get past the city guard and their allies.”

—

*"Three times. He's saying that like it's a specific number that matters.'"* Tatsuya filed the detail away, not sure why it felt important but trusting his instinct that it was.

"Tried the Grand Library by force," Sevish said, gesturing toward the map on the table. "Doesn't work but what you did back there moving through instead of fighting head-on, using terrain that's not something anyone here can do. Could be what we need." 

"The library," Tatsuya repeated, making sure he understood correctly. "You want me to get into the Grand Library of Lunarest."

"The restricted floors, specifically," Sevish clarified, uncrossing his arms and letting one hand rest on the pommel of the blade at his belt, a casual motion that still carried weight. "Public floors won't have what we're looking for. You find anything on Shiv up there records, journals, anything that tells us what he knew or where he went you bring it back here."

"And if I do this," Tatsuya said slowly, working through the implications even as he spoke, "What does that actually mean?"

"You complete this, you're under Thrash protection. You're ours, and we're yours. Anyone comes for you, they're coming for the clan."

*"He's offering real, long-term backup. That's not nothing, especially if higher-level players start hunting the World Quest. Can't just throw that away."*

"And if I don't?" he asked, needing to hear the other side spelled out just as clearly.

Sevish's gold tag flickered again, and so did Wyx's, both of them reacting to the question in a way Tatsuya couldn't fully read but could definitely feel.

*"Stupid question. I know what the answer's going to be before he says it."*

"Then you stay here," Sevish said, and there was no heat in his voice, no threat, just the plain statement of fact delivered like he was commenting on the weather. "I don't let someone who can drop two of my enforcers walk off into the world without knowing which side they're actually on. And understand this if you take the job and don't come back, we're leaving. If we cross paths after that, you're an enemy. Simple as that."

Wyx spoke for the first time since they'd entered the tent, her voice dry and carrying a hint of warmth that didn't quite match the sharpness in her eyes. "He was entertaining, at least. That's worth something."

Before Tatsuya could formulate a response to any of that, the time display in the corner of his vision caught his attention, the numbers sitting there quiet and unobtrusive but impossible to ignore once he'd noticed them.

9:41 PM.

*"I logged in late because I was fixing the pod and trying to figure out the override. Damn."*

Leon's voice crackled through the pod's external speaker a second later, distant and tinny, the sound cutting in from somewhere outside the world Tatsuya was currently standing in. "Hey, heads-up, we're closing in about thirty minutes. Just wanted to give you a warning."

He blinked, trying to reconcile the two realities overlapping in his head, a goblin chieftain's tent on one side and Leon's voice reaching in from somewhere outside it on the other. 

"I'll be off shortly," he said out loud, the words coming out directed at Leon even though Leon couldn't hear Sevish's side of this conversation and had no idea what Tatsuya was in the middle of.

"No worries, man," Leon's voice came back through the speaker, sounding relaxed and unbothered. "You've been in there a while. Hope the pod didn't give you any more issues after that initial hiccup."

"Nah, it's been good," Tatsuya said, keeping his voice casual even though his heart was racing from everything happening inside the game.

Sevish and Wyx were both looking at him strangely now, their expressions shifting from serious evaluation to mild confusion.

*"Right. They can't hear Leon. From their perspective I'm just talking to empty air like I'm losing it."*

"I need a few days," Tatsuya said out loud, directing the words at Sevish this time, knowing he had to log out soon but also knowing he needed to at least establish the timeline before he left. "Three days to think about this and figure out my next move."

"You can have them," Sevish said, and his tone made it clear that wasn't a negotiation, it was a decision he'd already made. "But you're staying in camp while you think."

Two guards stepped forward from where they'd been standing just inside the tent entrance, moving into flanking positions on either side of Tatsuya without being explicitly told to, their movements smooth and practiced.

**[YOU ARE NOW A PRISONER OF THE THRASH CLAN]**

**[MOVEMENT RESTRICTED TO CAMP BOUNDARIES]**

**[DURATION: UNTIL RELEASED BY CHIEFTAIN SEVISH]**

**[WORLD QUEST ACTIVATION: CALL OF THE BONES]**

**[STAGE 1 UNLOCKED]**

**[OBJECTIVE: INFILTRATE THE GRAND LIBRARY OF LUNAREST – RESTRICTED FLOORS]**

**[REWARD: UNKNOWN]**

**[TIME LIMIT: 7 DAYS IN-GAME]**

The guards walked him back out of the tent and through the camp, past the arm-wrestling match that had fully broken up now, people dispersing back to their fires and their own business, past the older goblin who'd gone back to mending that same torn shirt by the light of a small lamp, needle moving in the same patient rhythm. The stew pot was down to nothing but coals now, one last goblin scraping the very bottom of it with a wooden spoon, the metallic sound of wood on metal carrying across the quieter parts of camp.

They stopped at a tent near the eastern edge of the camp, away from the central fires but still close enough that Tatsuya could hear the low murmur of conversation and the occasional burst of laughter from goblins still awake and moving around. One of the guards pulled the tent flap open, canvas rustling. "Inside.”

Tatsuya ducked through the entrance, his shorter frame making it easier than it would have been in his real body, and the noise from outside dropped off almost immediately, muffled by the thick canvas walls. The tent was small, barely tall enough for him to sit upright in without his head brushing the peaked ceiling, lit by a single oil lamp hanging from the center pole that threw uneven, flickering shadows up the walls every time the flame guttered. A bedroll took up most of the available floor space, thin and patched in multiple places but looking relatively clean, and a wooden bowl sat beside it filled with water that smelled faintly metallic when he leaned closer to it.

He sat down on the bedroll and the straw stuffing inside crackled loudly under his weight, the sound sharp in the sudden quiet of the enclosed space. The fabric of the bedroll under his hands was rough, woven from something coarser than cotton, and when he reached out to touch the tent wall itself it felt thick and heavy, oiled or waxed to keep out rain and wind. The ground underneath the bedroll was hard-packed dirt, uneven in places, and he could feel the cold seeping up through the thin layer of padding even through his boots and the rags he was wearing.

*"This is where I'm staying until I decide to give them an answer."* He let himself process that, sitting there in the dim lamplight. *"Can't leave camp boundaries until Sevish releases me. But that's fine.."*

Outside the canvas walls, the camp kept moving around him, life continuing whether he was part of it or not. Footsteps passed close to his tent, boots scuffing on the packed earth.

Farther out, past the immediate camp sounds, a dog barked once and went quiet, answered a moment later by another bark from a different direction. Wind moved through the tree line beyond the camp boundary, branches creaking under the weight of it, and underneath that he could hear the distant sound of something larger moving through the underbrush, probably some kind of wildlife he couldn't see from here.

He pulled out the two green vials from the pouch at his belt and turned them over in his clawed hands, watching the way the lamplight caught in the glass and made the liquid inside seem to glow faintly. The number stamped beneath each one was clear and easy to read: ¥8,400.

*"Sixteen thousand eight hundred yen total. Plus whatever else I can farm with Gambler's Gambit once per day."* He set the vials down carefully on the ground beside the bedroll, making sure they wouldn't roll away. *"First real money this game's ever paid me. Not a bad first login."*

*"Once I am back online I will just accept his mission so I can get things moving."* He thought about Chiyo waiting at home, about Tochi sitting there as an option he'd been avoiding for months, about his mom in the hospital with a six-month window he hadn't known about until tonight. 

The camp kept breathing around him outside the canvas, fire and low voices and the smell of woodsmoke still thick in the air even here. It all felt real in a way he hadn't been prepared for, the way it kept existing whether he was paying attention or not.

In the corner of his vision, barely noticeable until it moved, the chat interface he hadn't looked at since spawning suddenly lit up with activity, messages scrolling past faster than he could read individual lines. Then a bell sound rang out, clear and loud enough that it felt like it should have echoed through the actual camp even though nobody else reacted to it.

**[SYSTEM ANNOUNCEMENT: WORLD QUEST | CALL OF THE BONES HAS BEEN ACTIVATED]**

His eyes shot up to the chat window and watched it erupt. Messages flooding in so fast the text was almost a blur, player names he didn't recognize all reacting at once, questions and theories and arguments breaking out in real time.

*"Damn. That's not good."* He reached for the logout button and his vision of the world went dark.

**[END CHAPTER FOUR]**
