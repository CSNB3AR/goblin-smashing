## The process that makes a final draft

1. Author writes or dictates the chapter. 2. Claude checks it against the canon and the style guide. 3. Walter smooths it, in sections under 1,500 words (split files are made with a `--------` line every 1,400 words or so). 4. Claude repairs what the humanizer broke (meaning, dashes, ellipses, thoughts, capitalization) and keeps Tatsuya's and the characters' voices. 5. The author runs the detector. When it is low, the chapter is a final draft until it is published or an editor takes it.

Rules that still hold: Tatsuya's and every character's voice and the meaning never change to chase a detector score; the unhumanized original is kept in `../archive/` so there is always a version to check against.
